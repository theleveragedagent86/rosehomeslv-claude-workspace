## DESCRIPTION
A pigeon with a war medal, a tiny wooden leg, and a permanent spot in the Smithsonian. This is the true (and slightly ridiculous) story of Cher Ami, the carrier pigeon who flew through machine gun fire to reach the trapped Lost Battalion in 1918.

We cover what actually happened, what the legend got wrong, and why the U.S. Army was so determined to make a bird a hero.
