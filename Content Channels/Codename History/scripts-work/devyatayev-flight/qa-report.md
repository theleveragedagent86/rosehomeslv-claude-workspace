## Issues to fix (if any)

None requiring a cut. The two fragile items (intelligence value, Korolev role) were flagged in advance and the script deliberately attributes them ("here is where we have to be careful, because a lot of this comes from Soviet-era accounts," "how decisive that intelligence really was, we honestly cannot verify to the same standard"). That is the correct, honest treatment and it is exactly what the channel's accuracy rules demand. No claim in the script rests on a single source; every significant claim has 2+ independent sources, with Tier 1/strong-secondary anchoring the date, aircraft, escaper count, guard death, and medal.

## Math check

- No arithmetic in the script beyond the year span 1945 to 1957 = twelve years. Re-checked: correct.
- Survivor count deliberately left as "only a few" to avoid picking a disputed number (five died in action per Wikipedia and labrujulaverde; one Wikipedia-cited source says only three survived the war). No single figure asserted, so no conflict shipped.
