## 3. Cold-open hook image (the most absurd verifiable frame)

Foggy field, 10:59 a.m., November 11, 1918. A German machine-gun crew is WAVING an American soldier back, trying not to shoot him, because everyone on the field already knows the war ends at 11:00. He keeps charging anyway. (Henry Gunther, Chaumont-devant-Damvillers, verified.)

Cold-open promise line: the armistice was signed hours ago, every man on this field knows the exact minute the war ends, and men are still being ordered to attack. "And I promise you, every part of this is real."

## Relatable bridge (first ~20 s, honest, no fabricated parallel)

Everyone has watched a clock crawl toward the moment something ends. These men knew the exact minute the deadliest war in history would stop. Their generals knew it too. And they ordered attacks anyway.

## The one "tell someone" fact (shareability engine)

On the war's final morning, AFTER the armistice was signed, there were roughly ten thousand casualties, more than both sides suffered on D-Day. All for ground the armies could have walked into peacefully days later. (Verify exact figures in research; Persico is the anchor source.)
