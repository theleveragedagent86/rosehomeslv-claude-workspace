## Issues to fix (if any)

None. All significant claims carry 2+ independent sources with at least one Tier 1/authoritative anchor for numbers, dates, the quote, and the corrections. No single-sourced significant claim. No unresolved conflict.

Notes on the two apparent conflicts, both resolved in-text honestly:
- Survivor counts: "about 40 pulled from the sea" (Wikipedia) vs "31 survived" (NHSA) are not a conflict; they describe different moments (pulled from water vs ultimately survived after 6+ died of wounds). Script states both correctly.
- Detachment date (Wikipedia "7 April" vs NHSA man-overboard "6 April"): script does not pin an exact detachment date, avoiding the conflict. Action date 8 April is unambiguous and confirmed.
