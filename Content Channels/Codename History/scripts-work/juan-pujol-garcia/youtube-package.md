## YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION

A failed chicken farmer invented 27 spies who did not exist, and Nazi Germany believed every one of them for four years.
Then Hitler personally authorized him an Iron Cross, while he was sitting in a house in north London working for MI5.

This is the real, documented story of Juan Pujol Garcia, the Spanish poultry farmer who talked his way into German intelligence with no training, no passport and no plan, then reported on Britain for nine months without ever setting foot there. He built his fake Britain out of a tourist guidebook, a map of England and an out of date railway timetable, and the Third Reich stamped it all as verified.

We also cover the parts that usually get cut. What the deception cost Araceli, his wife, who wrote the fake agents' life stories and got written out of the memoir. What it cost the people of Croydon when the V-1 barrage was deliberately pushed short. And where historians push back on the D-Day story, because accuracy is the whole point of this channel.
