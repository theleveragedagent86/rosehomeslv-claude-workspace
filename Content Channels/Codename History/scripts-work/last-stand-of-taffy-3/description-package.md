## YOUTUBE DESCRIPTION (paste-ready below this line)

---

On 25 October 1944, the most powerful surface fleet Japan had left ran into thirteen small American ships it could have destroyed in an afternoon. It logged them as fleet carriers doing thirty knots, and two and a half hours later it turned around and left while it was still winning.

They were escort carriers, destroyers and destroyer escorts. No armor, one gun each, about eighteen knots on a good day. This episode walks the Battle off Samar the way the records actually have it: the empty strait, the misidentified targets, the shells that failed because they were too good, the men in the water for over fifty hours, and the padded radio message that has been blamed for the wrong thing for eighty years. Where the popular version and the archives disagree, we say so on screen.
