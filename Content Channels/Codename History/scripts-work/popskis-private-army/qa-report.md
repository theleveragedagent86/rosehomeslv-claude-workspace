## Issues to fix

**Gating issues, all must be fixed:**

1. **"His entire force present was nine men in three jeeps."** (Act 6) - This is memoir-only, asserted as fact, and it is directly contradicted by his own 1945 Historical Summary, which says the garrison "surrendered to 12 men in 5 jeeps." The script silently picks the bigger-sounding memoir version, which is the exact failure mode the episode exists to expose. Fix: attribute and show the conflict, e.g. "His report said twelve men in five jeeps. His book said nine men in three." That is a better beat than the one currently there.

2. **"In his own report, the Germans kept asking when the real troops would arrive."** (Act 6) - Two faults. The source says "when **the troops** would arrive"; the word "real" is inserted into a near-quote. And "his own report" points at the 1945 summary, which gives the twelve-men figure the script just contradicted. Fix: cut "real", and place this line after the conflict is shown rather than after the memoir figure.

3. **"Then Caneri talked at the German envoy for five hours, concealing one detail."** (Act 6) - Memoir-only, asserted as fact. Fix: add an in-narration attribution ("In the book, Caneri talked..." or "He wrote that Caneri talked...").
