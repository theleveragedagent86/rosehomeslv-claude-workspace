# PACKAGE LOCK — Red Baron episode (locked before script, per virality playbook)

**Working title (locked):** Who Really Killed the Red Baron?
- <=8 words, mainstream topic, evergreen search demand, true curiosity gap (the official story is wrong: RAF credited Roy Brown, forensics point to a ground gunner).
- Alternates to carry through packaging: "The Red Baron Broke His One Rule" / "80 Kills. One Bullet." / "The Red Baron's Last 60 Seconds"

**Thumbnail concept (locked):** Bright red stick-figure triplane flying LOW over brown trenches, huge dotted bullet-trajectory line rising from the ground toward it, giant white "?" at the shooter end. Text (marker caps, <=4 words): "ONE BULLET." High contrast: sky-blue top, dirt-brown bottom, red plane center.

**Cold-open hook image (locked):** The red triplane at treetop height chasing a fleeing British plane, while the narrator says the most feared pilot alive is, right now, breaking the one rule he himself wrote down. Freeze mid-chase. "To understand why he's doing this, we have to go back."

**Promise the script must deliver:** By the end, the viewer knows (1) exactly what happened in the last 60 seconds, minute by minute, (2) who almost certainly fired the real shot and why the official credit is wrong, and (3) why the best pilot of the war broke his own rule that morning (the head wound thread).
