A brand new Nazi U-boat, first war patrol, sunk by its own toilet. It never sank a single enemy ship.

Here is the true story of U-1206, and how the internet gets it wrong.

On its first and only patrol in April 1945, the German submarine U-1206 was lost off the coast of Scotland after a crewman opened the wrong valves on its high-tech toilet, flooding the batteries and filling the boat with chlorine gas. This video walks the real chain of events, from the flush to the surface to the bombing, and separates the popular legend that the captain flushed it himself from what Kapitanleutnant Karl-Adolf Schlitt actually wrote in his own report.
