## Issues to fix (if any)

The "3 drowned" half of the breakdown is now double-sourced (Wikipedia + Buchan Divers), but the "illness the day before" detail rests on Wikipedia alone. It is not contradicted by any source, so this is a sourcing-thinness flag, not a fabrication or an unresolved conflict. RECOMMENDED (non-blocking): add a light hedge so the narration does not assert a single-sourced specific as flat fact, e.g. "By the record, one man had already died of illness the day before the toilet ever failed." The aggregate 4 dead / 46 captured is rock solid and multiply sourced, so this does not undermine the episode; it is a polish item consistent with the accuracy brand.

No must-fix (gate-blocking) issues. Nothing is fabricated, no significant claim is wholly unsourced, no source conflict is left unresolved, the myth is corrected rather than asserted, and the NUMA cover-story claim is excluded.
