Twenty-five prisoners in a castle above Florence. A dozen of them generals. Six months later they went out through the floor, and they dug it with two stolen carving knives and a croquet set.

This is the story of Campo P.G. 12, Castello di Vincigliata, and the tunnel that a Victoria Cross Royal Engineer designed while an air vice marshal sat on a bunk whittling knife handles. The Italians had assumed senior officers would not stoop to breaking out. The popular version of this story is about one famous, instantly recognisable general, and he is in it, but he is recaptured within a week. The two men who actually got clear of Italy were two New Zealand brigadiers you have never heard of. This is the record, not the legend: the real toolkit, what happened to the men who dug and stayed behind, and what Adrian Carton de Wiart was actually sent to Lisbon for.

This episode discusses a death by suicide. If you are struggling, help is available: the International Association for Suicide Prevention keeps a directory of crisis centres worldwide at https://www.iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres/ and you can also find a helpline for your country at https://findahelpline.com
