West Henderson Fieldhouse Renamed "Henderson Sport & Social"

The big sports building going up in West Henderson finally has a name. On June 2, 2026, the City of Henderson announced that its new $70 million fieldhouse will be called Henderson Sport & Social. The project broke ground on May 21, 2026, and the city expects the doors to open in the fall of 2026. So if you have been driving past the construction near St. Rose Parkway and Maryland Parkway wondering what it is, now you know.

Henderson Sport & Social is a large indoor sports complex. It will cover about 160,000 square feet across two levels. That is a huge amount of space, roughly the size of three football fields stacked into one building. The plans include courts that can change shape for different sports, hybrid turf fields, and family entertainment areas. The goal is to make it a place where kids play in tournaments, families spend a weekend afternoon, and the community comes together.

The project is what people call a public-private partnership. That means the city is working with a private company instead of building and running everything on its own. The company is KemperSports, a well-known operator of sports and recreation facilities. The city helps pay for and back the project, and the private partner helps build and run it. This new name and the recent groundbreaking are big steps for one of the fastest growing parts of the Las Vegas Valley.

What Happened

For more than a year, locals had been calling the project the West Henderson Fieldhouse. That was a working name, kind of like a nickname builders use before the real name is picked. On June 2, 2026, the City of Henderson made it official. The complex will be known as Henderson Sport & Social. The new name is meant to show that the building is about more than just sports. The word "social" points to the family fun, food, and gathering spaces that will be part of the experience.

The announcement came just under two weeks after the official groundbreaking on May 21, 2026. A groundbreaking is the event where leaders dig the first shovels of dirt to start construction. From that point, crews began the real work of turning empty land near St. Rose Parkway and Maryland Parkway into a major facility.

Here is what the city has shared about the building itself. Henderson Sport & Social will be about 160,000 square feet and built on two levels. It will have convertible courts. That means the same floor space can be set up for basketball one day, volleyball another day, and other indoor sports after that. This kind of flexible design lets the building host many different events without wasting space.

The complex will also include hybrid turf fields. Hybrid turf is a mix of natural grass blades and artificial fibers, or a high-quality artificial surface built to play like grass. These fields are good for indoor soccer, flag football, lacrosse, and similar sports. Because they are indoors, games can happen even when it is over 110 degrees outside in the Las Vegas summer. That is a real advantage in our desert climate.

What the city has made clear is that the goal is a destination where families can spend several hours, not just drop off a player and leave.

The total cost is around $70 million. The project is a public-private partnership with KemperSports, a company that runs sports and recreation venues across the country. In a partnership like this, the public side usually provides land, funding help, or financial backing, while the private side brings building experience and day-to-day management. The expected opening is fall 2026, which is a fast timeline for a building this size.

Background and History

West Henderson has changed dramatically over the past decade. What was once open desert land on the southwest edge of the city is now home to thousands of new houses, major employers, and growing retail centers. The area got a big boost when large companies and a professional sports training site chose to plant roots there. That growth created strong demand for the kinds of amenities that make a place feel complete, including recreation centers, parks, and entertainment.

The City of Henderson has long marketed itself as a family-friendly community. It runs a well-known parks and recreation system and has earned a reputation as one of the better places to live in the Las Vegas area. Building a large indoor sports complex fits that brand. As West Henderson filled in with rooftops, city leaders looked for ways to give those new neighborhoods the services they needed without making residents drive across the valley.

Public-private partnerships have become a common tool for projects like this. Building and running a 160,000-square-foot sports facility is expensive and complex. By teaming up with an experienced operator like KemperSports, the city can share the cost and the risk while bringing in expertise it might not have on its own. KemperSports manages many sports and recreation properties around the country, so the city is partnering with a group that knows how to keep these venues busy and well maintained.

The journey from idea to groundbreaking took planning, funding decisions, and public discussion. The May 21, 2026 groundbreaking marked the moment the project moved from paper to dirt. The June 2 naming announcement was the next step in shaping the facility's public identity. Together, these milestones show a project picking up real momentum after years of West Henderson growth set the stage for it.
