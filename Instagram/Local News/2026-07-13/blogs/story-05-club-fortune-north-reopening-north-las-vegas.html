Former Poker Palace Sets Reopening Dates as Club Fortune North

The old Poker Palace in North Las Vegas is coming back, and now we know exactly when. Reno-based Truckee Gaming, the company that bought the longtime locals casino for $20 million, has set a soft opening for Sunday, July 19, 2026, and a grand opening for Wednesday, July 22, 2026. The building will not open as the Poker Palace, though. It will open under a new name: Club Fortune North. That is a big deal for a spot that has been dark for months while crews worked inside.

If you have lived in the north valley for a while, this news probably hits you right in the memory. The Poker Palace was one of those places that regular people knew. It was not on the Strip. It was not fancy. It was a neighborhood casino where locals grabbed a cheap meal, played a few machines, and knew the staff by name. So when a spot like that closes, folks worry it will sit empty or turn into something they do not recognize. This time, the answer is different. The owners spent big money to fix it up and reopen it.

Below, I will walk you through what is changing, what is staying, and one surprise that longtime fans may not love. I will also share why I think this reopening matters far beyond the casino floor, especially if you own a home nearby or you are thinking about buying in North Las Vegas.

What Happened

Here are the facts, straight from the reporting. Truckee Gaming, a gaming company based in Reno, bought the Poker Palace for $20 million. After the sale, the company started a remodel that was planned to take a while and then grew even bigger in scope. In plain words, the project got larger than the owners first expected. That kind of thing happens when crews open up an older building and find more work to do than they planned for.

The remodel took about nine months. Now it is close to done. The company announced a soft opening on July 19, 2026, and a grand opening on July 22, 2026. A soft opening is like a practice run. The doors open to the public, but the business uses those first days to test everything and work out any bumps before the big celebration. The grand opening a few days later is the official party, when the place is fully ready and the owners want a crowd.

The renamed casino, Club Fortune North, is getting a real upgrade, not just a coat of paint. The reporting says the revamped locals casino adds expanded restaurant and kitchen space. That means more room to cook and more room to sit and eat. It also gets a redesigned bar, so the place where people grab drinks got a fresh new look. On the gaming side, the casino is adding new slot machines. And it is adding a Boomer's Sportsbook, which is a place where people can bet on sports events like football and basketball.

Now here is the part that may surprise loyal fans. Even though the old place was called the Poker Palace, the new Club Fortune North will not have a poker room. It will not have table games either. So there will be no live poker tables, no blackjack, and no roulette. The name Poker Palace is gone, and so is the poker. The casino is leaning into slot machines, food, drinks, and sports betting instead.

Why would a place called the Poker Palace drop poker? The reporting does not spell out every reason, so I will not guess at private business math. What I can say is that live poker rooms and table games need a lot of staff, a lot of floor space, and steady crowds to make money. Many smaller locals casinos across the valley have moved away from table games over the years and put their focus on slots and sports betting, which are easier to run. Club Fortune North looks like it is following that same path. [NOT VERIFIED: the exact business reasons for dropping poker were not stated in the source.]

It is also worth pointing out what the remodel says about the owners' plans. Spending nine months and a large budget on a bigger kitchen, more dining space, a redesigned bar, new machines, and a sportsbook tells you they are building for the long haul. This was not a quick flip. It was a careful, top-to-bottom refresh of an aging building. When a project grows in scope like this one did, it usually means the crews wanted to do it right the first time instead of patching problems later. That is good news for anyone who plans to visit, because it should mean a cleaner, more comfortable, and more modern space than the old Poker Palace offered.
