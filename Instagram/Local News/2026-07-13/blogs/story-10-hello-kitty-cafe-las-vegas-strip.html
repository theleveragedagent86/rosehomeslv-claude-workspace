Hello Kitty Cafe Bringing Third Location to the Las Vegas Strip

Hello Kitty is getting ready to open a third cafe in the Las Vegas valley, and this one is landing right in the middle of the Strip. Sanrio, the company behind the famous white cat with the red bow, is building a new Hello Kitty Cafe at the Forum Shops at Caesars Palace. The project is expected to cost around $1.6 million, and it is set to open in summer 2026. That timing means the doors could swing open before this year is even over.

This will not be a small grab-and-go window like some fans might picture. Reports say the new spot will offer elevated dining, an expanded menu, tea service, and an immersive Sanrio retail space where shoppers can buy Hello Kitty gear. In plain words, it is meant to be a full experience, not just a place to grab a cookie. That is a big step up from the smaller Hello Kitty stops many people already know around town.

If you have lived in Southern Nevada for a while, you already know Hello Kitty has fans here. The brand runs a cafe at The Park, which sits between New York-New York and Park MGM, and another one inside the Fashion Show mall. Adding a third location shows the company still sees strong demand in this market. Below, I will walk through what we know, why it matters to the people who live here, and what to keep an eye on as the opening gets closer.

What Happened

Here is the short version. Sanrio, the Japanese company that owns Hello Kitty, is opening a new Hello Kitty Cafe at the Forum Shops at Caesars Palace, located at 3500 South Las Vegas Boulevard. This will be the third Hello Kitty Cafe in the Las Vegas valley. The news was reported this spring by What Now Las Vegas and by the Las Vegas Review-Journal's Neon dining section.

The project carries a price tag of roughly $1.6 million. That number covers the build-out, which is the work of turning empty or old retail space into a finished cafe. A build-out of that size points to a real sit-down concept with a kitchen, seating, and a store attached, not a tiny kiosk. The plan is to finish the work and open in summer 2026.

What can guests expect inside? Based on the reports, the new cafe will feature four main things. First, elevated dining, which means a step above the usual quick sweets. Second, an expanded menu, so there should be more food and drink choices than the smaller shops offer. Third, tea service, which fits the cute and cozy style Hello Kitty is known for. And fourth, an immersive Sanrio retail space, where fans can shop for Hello Kitty and other Sanrio character items in a setting built around the brand.

The Forum Shops is one of the busiest shopping centers on the Strip. It sits next to Caesars Palace and pulls in a heavy mix of tourists and locals every single day. The mall is known for its Roman theme, its winding walkways, and its long list of stores and restaurants. Dropping a photo-ready cafe like Hello Kitty into that space is a smart fit, because the Forum Shops already draws people who like to walk, shop, snack, and take pictures.

It is worth being clear about what has not been shared yet. As of now, the exact opening date, the full menu, and the final list of prices have not been made public. Why It Matters to Las Vegas Residents

You might wonder why a cat-themed cafe deserves attention from a local real estate guy. The answer is simple. The health of the Strip shapes the health of the whole valley, and that includes home values, jobs, and the money that flows through our neighborhoods. When a well-known brand spends over a million dollars to open on the Strip, it is placing a bet that visitors will keep coming and keep spending. That bet is good news for the people who live here.

Tourism is the engine of Southern Nevada. When more people visit, more workers are needed for hotels, restaurants, shops, and shows. Those workers need places to live, which supports demand for homes and apartments across the valley. A single cafe will not move the market on its own, but a steady stream of new openings like this one is a sign that the Strip economy is still growing. That matters for anyone who owns a home or hopes to buy one.

There is also a local angle that often gets missed. Places like the two existing Hello Kitty Cafes are not just tourist stops. Locals visit them for birthdays, for date nights, and for family outings. A third location adds one more spot where residents can enjoy the Strip without needing a hotel room. That kind of everyday value helps make life here feel a little richer, and it gives families another easy option close to home.

For parents, this is a friendly, low-pressure place to take kids on the Strip. For young adults and fans of the brand, it is a fun and photogenic hangout. And for the many people in our community who work in hospitality, it means new jobs. A build-out of this size usually needs cooks, servers, cashiers, retail staff, and managers once the doors open. Every new hire is a paycheck that gets spent right here in the valley, at our grocery stores, gas stations, and local shops.

Finally, think about the ripple effect on the Forum Shops itself. A buzzy new draw can lift foot traffic for the stores nearby, which helps those businesses stay open and keep their own workers employed. Healthy shopping centers on the Strip help keep the wider economy strong, and a strong economy is the foundation that home demand is built on.

Background and History

Hello Kitty first appeared in 1974 as a Sanrio character, and she has grown into one of the most recognized brands in the world. Over the years, Sanrio has stretched the character far beyond toys and stickers. Today you can find Hello Kitty on clothing, home goods, theme park rides, and yes, cafes. The cafe idea leans into the brand's cute and cheerful style, turning food and drinks into something fans want to photograph and share.

Las Vegas has been a strong market for Hello Kitty for years. The first Hello Kitty Cafe in the valley opened at The Park in July 2019. The Park is the outdoor space between New York-New York and Park MGM, and it draws a steady crowd of visitors walking between resorts and the arena. That location proved there was real hunger for the brand in this town.

The second valley location opened in July 2022 inside the Fashion Show mall, which sits on the north end of the Strip across from Wynn Las Vegas. Adding a mall location showed the brand wanted to reach both resort walkers and shoppers. Now, with the Forum Shops project set for summer 2026, Sanrio is filling in a third piece of the map, right in the heart of the Strip near Caesars Palace.

The Forum Shops has its own long history. It opened in 1992 and has expanded more than once over the years. It is famous for its faux sky ceiling that shifts from day to night, its statues and fountains, and its blend of luxury stores and popular restaurants. The mall has always mixed high-end names with crowd-pleasing spots, so a fun and affordable cafe fits the pattern of giving visitors a range of choices under one roof.

As more of these projects open, we get a clearer picture of how strong the tourism recovery is and how that strength feeds back into the local job market and housing demand.
