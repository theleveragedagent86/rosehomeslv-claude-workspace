Nacho Daddy Opens Fourth Southern Nevada Location at AREA15

Nacho Daddy, a restaurant that got its start right here in Las Vegas, just opened a brand new spot, and it is already one of the most talked about food stories in town. On Friday, July 3, 2026, the company opened its fourth Southern Nevada location inside AREA15, the immersive entertainment complex near the Strip. The new restaurant sits at 3040 Rigel Ave., Suite 120. It is nearly 5,000 square feet, seats more than 200 people, and comes with a full bar, bright and colorful murals, and one very strange centerpiece. That centerpiece is an eight-foot-long scorpion. Yes, eight feet long. It is the kind of thing you have to see in person to believe.

This opening is a big deal for more than one reason. Nacho Daddy is now the only Las Vegas founded restaurant operating inside AREA15. That means a homegrown brand, one that started small and grew here, now has a home inside one of the most unusual and popular attractions in the whole city. For locals who have watched Nacho Daddy grow over the years, this feels like a hometown team making it to the big stage. In this article, we will walk through exactly what happened, why it matters to people who live here, the history behind the brand, and what all of this could mean for the neighborhoods around it.

What Happened

Let us start with the simple facts. On Friday, July 3, 2026, Nacho Daddy opened the doors to its newest restaurant inside AREA15 in Las Vegas. This is the fourth Nacho Daddy location in Southern Nevada. The address is 3040 Rigel Ave., Suite 120. That puts it just west of Interstate 15, close to the Strip but not right on it, in the same building that holds AREA15's many art displays, bars, and attractions.

The space itself is large. It measures nearly 5,000 square feet, which is a good size for a sit down restaurant. It can seat more than 200 guests at one time. That is a lot of tables, and it means big groups, families, and friends can all find room even on a busy night. The restaurant has a full bar, so guests can order margaritas, beer, and other drinks along with their food. Nacho Daddy has always been known for its drinks as much as its food, so a full bar was a must.

Then there is the look of the place. The walls are covered in colorful murals. Murals are large paintings done right on the wall, and they help give the restaurant a fun, lively feeling that fits well inside AREA15. But the real showstopper is the eight-foot-long scorpion. This giant scorpion acts as the centerpiece of the whole room. It is a bold choice, and it is exactly the kind of eye catching detail that AREA15 is famous for. People love to take photos in AREA15, and a giant scorpion gives them one more reason to pull out their phones and share the moment online.

The opening also comes with a title that Nacho Daddy can be proud of. With this new location, Nacho Daddy becomes the only Las Vegas founded restaurant operating inside AREA15. AREA15 already has many food and drink options, but most of them come from brands based somewhere else. Nacho Daddy is different. It was born in Las Vegas. So when locals eat there, they are supporting a company that started in their own backyard. That local connection is a big part of the story, and it is one reason the opening has become a trending topic across the city's food scene.

Background and History

To understand why this opening feels special, it helps to know a little about where Nacho Daddy came from. Nacho Daddy is a Las Vegas born brand. That means the company started here in this city, not somewhere else. It grew up alongside the community, building a name for itself with bold Mexican food, creative drinks, and a fun, casual feeling that locals connected with. Over the years, it built a loyal following of people who come back again and again.

The new AREA15 spot is the fourth Nacho Daddy location in Southern Nevada. Growing to four locations is a real accomplishment for any restaurant brand. Many restaurants never make it past their first location. Reaching four means the food, the service, and the overall experience have kept customers happy long enough for the company to keep expanding. That kind of steady growth does not happen by accident. It happens when a business earns the trust of the community it serves.

AREA15 itself is worth understanding too. It is an immersive entertainment complex, which is a fancy way of saying it is a place packed with art, lights, games, bars, and experiences meant to surround you and pull you in. It opened near the Strip and quickly became one of the more unusual attractions in the Las Vegas area. It draws both tourists and locals who want something different from the usual casino floor. Because AREA15 is so focused on wild, colorful experiences, a giant eight-foot scorpion and a wall of murals fit right in. Nacho Daddy did not just copy its other restaurants. It leaned into the AREA15 style and made a space that belongs there.

Put those two stories together, a homegrown food brand and a bold local attraction, and you get an opening that means more than just another place to eat. It is a piece of local pride finding a home inside a place built to amaze people.
