Henderson Silver Knights Announce 2026-27 Regular Season Schedule

The Henderson Silver Knights have a full plan for next winter, and hockey fans in Southern Nevada now know exactly when to show up. On July 9, 2026, the team put out its 2026-27 regular season schedule. It is a 72-game slate in the American Hockey League. The season kicks off at home at Lee's Family Forum in Henderson on October 3 against the Bakersfield Condors. This is the team's seventh season, and it will be the first one led by new Head Coach Joel Ward.

The schedule spreads 36 home games across the season, which gives local families a lot of chances to catch pro hockey close to home. The team will face every one of its nine rivals in the Pacific Division. It also picked up a rare treat this year: two teams from the Central Division, Texas and Iowa, will come to town. For anyone deciding where to plant roots in the Las Vegas valley, a steady flow of family-friendly game nights is one more reason Henderson keeps drawing new neighbors.

What Happened

The Silver Knights shared their 2026-27 regular season schedule on July 9, 2026. The plan covers 72 games in all. Half of those, 36 games, will be played at home at Lee's Family Forum in Henderson. The other 36 will be road games in other cities. That even split means fans get a real season of home hockey, not just a handful of nights.

The season opens on October 3. The first game is at home, and the visiting team is the Bakersfield Condors. Opening night is often one of the best nights of the year at any rink. The building is loud, the ice is fresh, and everyone is excited to see the new roster take the ice for the first time.

The Silver Knights play in the AHL's Pacific Division. Next season they will face all nine of their division rivals. Those teams are the Bakersfield Condors, the Abbotsford Canucks, the Calgary Wranglers, the Coachella Valley Firebirds, the Colorado Eagles, the Ontario Reign, the San Diego Gulls, the San Jose Barracuda, and the Tucson Roadrunners. Some of these teams are close by, and some travel in from as far as Canada. That mix keeps the schedule fresh from week to week.

This year the schedule also brings in two teams from the Central Division. The Silver Knights will play four games each against the Texas Stars and the Iowa Wild. Those crossover games do not happen every year, so they add something new for fans who like to see faces they do not usually see. FOX5 Vegas noted that these two Central Division matchups are one of the standout parts of the new slate.

The biggest change of all is on the bench. The 2026-27 season will be the first for new Head Coach Joel Ward. Ward is 45 years old, and this is his first job as a pro head coach. He is a familiar name to hockey fans because of his long playing career, and now he gets to run the show in Henderson. A new coach often means a new style of play, so fans will be watching closely to see how the team looks under him.

That kind of planning is a small thing, but it is the sort of small thing that makes a place feel like home.

It also helps to think about the type of crowd these games draw. AHL hockey tends to bring out families, youth teams, and longtime fans who care more about a fun night than a fancy one. That mix makes for a friendly building. Kids can wear their own jerseys, chase promotions between periods, and stay excited without anyone minding the noise. Background and History

The Henderson Silver Knights are still a young team, but they have a clear story. The team is the AHL affiliate of the Vegas Golden Knights. That means the Silver Knights are where many young Golden Knights prospects go to grow before they reach the NHL. When you watch a Silver Knights game, you might be watching a future NHL star in his early days.

The team came to Southern Nevada in 2020. The Golden Knights bought a franchise that had been playing in San Antonio, Texas, as the Rampage, and they moved it to the Las Vegas area. On May 28, 2020, the club revealed its new name: the Henderson Silver Knights. The name honors Nevada's nickname, the Silver State. The team even took on the slogan "Home Means Henderson," which plays off the state song "Home Means Nevada." The first head coach was Manny Viveiros, hired later that same year. The team's home, Lee's Family Forum, opened its doors in March 2022. It sits in Henderson and was built as a modern sports and events venue. The arena went by a different name at first. When the naming rights changed hands, Lee's Discount Liquor stepped in, and the building was renamed Lee's Family Forum. The name is a tribute to the company's founder, Hae Un Lee, and his son Kenny Lee. Today it is one of the newer arenas in the valley and a key part of the Henderson sports scene.

In its short life, the team has already sent players up to the NHL and put strong squads on the ice. Silver Knights players have earned AHL honors, and the club has built a loyal local following. Each new season adds another chapter, and the 2026-27 season is a fresh one because of the coaching change. Fans who have followed the team since 2020 get to see how a new voice behind the bench builds on what came before.

The tie to the Golden Knights is a big part of what makes the team fun to follow. When the Golden Knights have an injury or want to test a young player against tougher NHL competition, they often turn to the roster in Henderson. That means the Silver Knights lineup can change based on what the NHL club needs. For fans, it adds a layer of drama. You never quite know when a standout in Henderson will get the call to Vegas, and you can say you saw him play before he made it. That connection turns a minor league game into a window on the future of the whole Golden Knights organization.

What Happens Next

The next big date is October 3, 2026. That is opening night at Lee's Family Forum against the Bakersfield Condors. Between now and then, the team will hold training camp and set its roster. Because the Silver Knights work so closely with the Golden Knights, the roster can shift right up until the season starts. Some players will be sent down from Vegas, and some will be called up. That back-and-forth is normal at this level.

Fans who want to go should keep an eye on ticket news. Single-game tickets and season plans usually go on sale in the weeks before the opener. Popular games, like opening night and games against nearby rivals such as the Coachella Valley Firebirds or the Ontario Reign, can sell out faster than others. If you have a game in mind, it is smart to grab seats early rather than wait.

The two crossover games against Texas and Iowa are worth circling on the calendar too. Since those teams do not visit every year, those nights carry a little extra buzz. Dates and start times for every game are part of the full schedule the team released, so you can plan your fall and winter around the games you care about most.

It is also worth watching how the team plays early under Coach Ward. The first month of any season under a new coach tells you a lot. Fans will look for signs of the style he wants: a fast attack, a strong defense, or a focus on developing young talent. By the time the holidays roll around, the shape of the season should be clear.
