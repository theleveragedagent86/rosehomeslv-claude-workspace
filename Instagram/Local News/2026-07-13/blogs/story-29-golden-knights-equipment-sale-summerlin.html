Golden Knights to Host Annual Battle-Worn Authentics Equipment Sale

The Vegas Golden Knights are bringing back one of the most fun events of the summer for hockey fans. The team announced its annual Battle-Worn Authentics Equipment Sale, and this year it happens again at City National Arena in Summerlin. This is the place where fans can buy real gear that players used or that the team handed out. We are talking about sticks, skates, gloves, jerseys, and helmets. Some of these items still carry the marks and wear from real games and practices.

If you have ever wanted to own something that a Golden Knights player actually held or wore, this is your chance. The sale is open to the public for a set window of time. Fans line up, walk around the rink, and shop tables full of team gear. The most special and rare items are kept in two spots called Locker Room 5 and The Arsenal. There are also online-only deals for people who cannot make it in person.

For folks in Las Vegas and Summerlin, this is more than a shopping trip. It is a community event that brings neighbors together around a team many people love. Below, we will break down what is happening, why it matters to local families, and how you can take part.

What Happened

The Golden Knights shared news of their yearly Battle-Worn Authentics Equipment Sale. The event takes place at City National Arena, the team's practice home in Summerlin. The public shopping hours are set for Rink A, which is the main ice sheet inside the building. During the sale, the whole area around the rink turns into a giant store filled with team gear.

Here is what fans can expect to buy. The sale offers player-used and team-issued items. That list includes sticks, skates, gloves, jerseys, shoes, helmets, jackets, shirts, sweatshirts, and hats. Some pieces come straight from real games. Others were issued to players and staff for practice or travel. Prices change from item to item based on how rare or special each piece is.

The team keeps its best and most valuable items in two special rooms. Premium gear can be found in Locker Room 5 and in The Arsenal, which is the arena's team store. These spots hold the pieces that serious collectors want most, like game-worn jerseys tied to certain players or moments.

According to reports, the sale is open to the public from 3 to 6 p.m. Before the public window, Season Ticket Members get a series of early shopping chances. This gives the team's most loyal fans a first look at the gear. On the day of the sale, The Arsenal also offers 30% off purchases for Season Ticket Members all day long.

Fans who cannot show up in person are not left out. The team runs online-only offerings at VegasTeamStore.com. Those online deals are open from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. on the day of the sale. That means someone at work or out of town can still shop for gear from a phone or computer.

Background and History

The Golden Knights have held game-used and team-issued gear sales for several years. These events grew out of a simple idea. Teams end up with lots of equipment and apparel that they no longer need. Instead of tossing it or storing it, the team sells it to fans. That gives supporters a chance to own real pieces while the team clears space and gives back to the community.

City National Arena has been the home base for these sales. The arena opened in 2017 as the team's official practice facility. It holds two ice sheets and is open to the public for skating, youth hockey, and team practices. Because it is where players train every day, it is the perfect spot to sell the gear they have used. The building already means a lot to local hockey families, so the sale fits right in.

Over the years, the format has grown more organized. The team added special rooms, like Locker Room 5 and The Arsenal, to hold premium items apart from the general tables. It also added early access for Season Ticket Members as a thank-you for their loyalty. And it built out online-only offerings so fans who cannot attend still get a chance. Each year, the setup gets a little smoother and easier for shoppers.

Hockey has also grown fast in Southern Nevada since the team arrived. More kids are playing, more rinks are busy, and more families are learning the sport. Sales like this one help feed that growth by putting real gear into the community. Each year the crowd seems to include more new fans who moved to the valley and picked up the team along the way.

The Battle-Worn Authentics name ties the gear back to real hockey. "Battle-worn" points to equipment that has been through the fight of a real season. That story is a big part of the appeal. A stick with a scuff or a jersey with a repair tells fans that a player used it in a real moment. For collectors, that history is worth just as much as the item itself.
