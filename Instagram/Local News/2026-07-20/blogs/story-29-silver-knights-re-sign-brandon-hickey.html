Henderson Silver Knights Re-Sign Defenseman Brandon Hickey for Sixth Season

The Henderson Silver Knights are bringing back one of their most familiar faces. The team re-signed defenseman Brandon Hickey to a one-year contract extension for the 2026-27 season, and it will mark his sixth year with the club. The announcement came in early July 2026.

For Henderson hockey families, this is welcome news. Hickey has been part of the Silver Knights since the team's early days. He is a steady, physical defenseman who fans in Green Valley and across Henderson have watched grow into a veteran leader. Keeping him around gives the roster some much-needed continuity.

Roster news like this may seem small, but it is exactly the kind of local story that keeps a community connected to its team. When a familiar player comes back year after year, fans feel a sense of ownership and pride. That bond is a big part of what makes a growing city like Henderson feel like home, and it is worth understanding what this signing means for the season ahead.

What Happened

The Henderson Silver Knights announced the signing in the first week of July 2026. Brandon Hickey, who is 30 years old, agreed to a one-year American Hockey League contract extension that keeps him in Henderson through the 2026-27 season. It is the sixth straight season he will wear the Silver Knights sweater.

Hickey is a defenseman, and his game is built on defense first. In the 2025-26 season, he played in 37 games for Henderson. He recorded one goal and six points, along with 17 penalty minutes. Those are not flashy scoring numbers, and that is by design. His value comes from blocking shots, clearing the crease, and playing hard minutes against the other team's top forwards.

That physical, defense-first style is exactly what a development team needs. The Vegas Golden Knights use Henderson to season their young prospects, and those prospects need steady partners who can cover for mistakes and set an example. A grinding veteran defenseman fills that role. He may not light up the scoreboard, but coaches count on him in the toughest moments of a game, like protecting a one-goal lead late in the third period.

Over his time with the Silver Knights, Hickey has appeared in 131 career games with the club. In those games he has collected seven goals and 22 points. He has been with Henderson since the 2021-22 campaign, which was only the team's second year of existence. That makes him one of the longest-tenured players in franchise history.

Across his full pro career, Hickey has played in 222 American Hockey League games. Those games came with the Silver Knights, the Abbotsford Canucks, the Chicago Wolves, and the Rochester Americans. In total he has 11 goals and 37 points at the AHL level. A native of Leduc, Alberta, Hickey was a third-round draft pick of the Calgary Flames back in 2014.

A one-year deal is standard in the American Hockey League, where contracts tend to be short and rosters turn over quickly from season to season. That makes it even more notable that Hickey keeps coming back. Six straight years with one club at this level is a sign that the coaching staff trusts him and that he fits what the organization wants to build. In a league where players are always chasing a shot at the National Hockey League, staying put and staying valued says a lot about a player's reliability.

Background and History

The Henderson Silver Knights joined the American Hockey League for the 2020-21 season. They are the top minor-league affiliate of the Vegas Golden Knights, which means the players you see in Henderson are often one call away from the National Hockey League. The team was created so the Golden Knights could develop their young prospects close to home.

When the Silver Knights started, they played their first games at the Orleans Arena while their permanent home was being built. The team then moved into its new downtown Henderson arena, which opened in 2022 and now goes by Lee's Family Forum. The practice facility on Water Street, Lifeguard Arena, opened in November 2020 and gave the organization a full-time base in the heart of the city.

Brandon Hickey arrived for the 2021-22 season and has been a fixture ever since. He came into pro hockey with real pedigree, having been drafted by the Calgary Flames and having played college hockey before turning pro. Over the years he bounced through a few AHL organizations, but Henderson is where he settled in and became a trusted, everyday defenseman.

His long run with the team mirrors the growth of hockey in this valley. When Hickey first signed, the Silver Knights were a brand-new franchise still finding their identity. Six seasons later, the team is an established part of the Henderson sports scene, and Hickey is one of the veterans who helped build that foundation.

It is worth remembering how new all of this is. Southern Nevada had no major pro sports teams at all until the Vegas Golden Knights arrived in 2017. That team's surprise run to the Stanley Cup Final in its first season turned casual fans into diehards almost overnight. The Silver Knights were born out of that momentum, giving Henderson its own team and giving the Golden Knights a nearby place to develop talent. Players like Hickey have been part of the valley's hockey story from nearly the very beginning.

What Happens Next

The 2026-27 American Hockey League season will get underway in the fall of 2026. Training camp usually opens in September, and the Silver Knights will start building their roster around returning veterans like Hickey and the fresh prospects the Vegas Golden Knights send down. Expect more signings and roster moves to roll out over the summer as the team fills out its lineup.

Hickey's role should look familiar. As one of the older and more experienced players on the blue line, he is likely to log steady defensive minutes and help mentor the younger defensemen. In a development league, that veteran presence matters just as much as goals and assists. Young players learn how to be pros by watching guys like him.

The team will also keep selling the game-night experience that has made Henderson hockey a hit. Silver Knights home games are known for being affordable and family-oriented, with promotions, giveaways, and a lively arena atmosphere. That formula has helped the team build a loyal following in just a few years, and returning veterans give longtime season-ticket holders another reason to renew.

Fans who want to see him in action can start planning now. The full home schedule at Lee's Family Forum will be released before the season, and single-game tickets and season packages typically go on sale in late summer. If past seasons are any guide, opening night in Henderson will bring a strong crowd ready to cheer on a familiar group.

There is also a bigger picture to watch. Because the Silver Knights feed the Vegas Golden Knights, injuries and call-ups at the NHL level can reshape the Henderson roster during the season. A veteran defenseman like Hickey provides stability when younger players get promoted to Las Vegas. Keep an eye on how the team rounds out its blue line in the coming weeks, and on whether more familiar names return alongside him.
