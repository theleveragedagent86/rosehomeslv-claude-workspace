Station Casinos to Open 24/7 Seventy Six Tavern in North Las Vegas

Station Casinos is opening its sixth Seventy Six tavern in the Las Vegas Valley, and this one is coming to North Las Vegas. The new 24/7 tavern is set to open at 976 W. Dorrell Lane, near Revere Street, on July 30, 2026. The company plans a 9 a.m. ribbon-cutting on opening day, then a public grand-opening party at 7 p.m. the next night on July 31.

This is a big deal for a growing part of town. Seventy Six is Station Casinos' neighborhood tavern brand. It is built for locals who want a casual place to eat, drink, watch sports, and play a few casual games close to home. You do not have to drive to the Strip or fight traffic to a big resort. The idea is simple. Bring the food, the drinks, and the fun right into the neighborhood where people already live.

For folks in North Las Vegas, the opening says something bigger than just a new bar. When a company like Station Casinos chooses to plant a tavern in your area, it is betting that the neighborhood is growing and that more rooftops are on the way. Below, I will break down what is opening, what it means for homeowners and renters nearby, and why this fits a pattern I have watched play out across the valley.

What Happened

Station Casinos confirmed that its newest Seventy Six tavern will open on July 30, 2026, at 976 W. Dorrell Lane in North Las Vegas. The spot sits near Revere Street, which puts it close to homes and everyday errands rather than out by the resort corridor. This is the sixth Seventy Six location in the Las Vegas Valley, so the brand is clearly growing fast.

The plan has two parts. First, there is a ribbon-cutting at 9 a.m. on opening day, July 30. That is the official start. Then, the company is throwing a public grand-opening party at 7 p.m. the next evening, July 31. The party is open to the community, so neighbors can come check out the space, grab a bite, and see what the buzz is about.

Like the other Seventy Six taverns, this location runs 24 hours a day, seven days a week. That means you can stop in early in the morning, late at night, or anytime in between. For shift workers, night owls, and anyone with an odd schedule, an always-open spot in the neighborhood is a real perk.

The tavern features 15 bartop machines. These are the small gaming screens built right into the bar, where you can play slots, video poker, blackjack, and keno while you sit and relax. At the other Seventy Six taverns, these machines offer more than 50 game options, so there is plenty to pick from. This is limited, casual gaming, not a giant casino floor. It fits the neighborhood-tavern feel.

Food is a big part of the draw. Seventy Six serves comfort food and classic bar fare. Think burgers, salads, flatbreads, fish and chips, and steak sandwiches. The brand is known for wings that are made fresh in-house rather than frozen, and locals have praised them as some of the best around. On the drink side, the tavern offers handcrafted cocktails along with the usual beer and other options. Add in big-screen TVs for sports, comfortable seating, and casual games, and you have the full package the brand is going for.

The location itself is worth a closer look. Dorrell Lane runs through a residential part of North Las Vegas, and Revere Street is a main road that ties nearby neighborhoods together. That is a very different setting from a tavern parked next to a giant resort. By choosing a spot surrounded by homes, Station Casinos is putting the tavern within a short drive of thousands of everyday customers. It is the kind of location you pass on the way home from work or errands, which is exactly what a neighborhood brand wants.

It is also worth noting how fast this brand has moved. The first Seventy Six opened in late 2024, and by the summer of 2026 the company is cutting the ribbon on number six. That is a quick pace for a brand-new concept. Companies do not roll out locations that fast unless the early ones are doing well and the demand is clearly there. For North Las Vegas, being chosen for one of these fast-moving new spots is a good sign about how the area is trending.

Background and History

The Seventy Six name is not random. It points back to the year Station Casinos was first established in Las Vegas, 1976. The company started with a single property and grew into one of the biggest operators of locals-focused casinos in Southern Nevada. The Seventy Six tavern brand is a smaller, more neighborhood-friendly version of that idea, built to serve people right where they live.

The first Seventy Six tavern opened in North Las Vegas in the fall of 2024 on North Lamb Boulevard. It was designed as an American-style tavern aimed at neighborhood customers who wanted food, drinks, sports, and a little casual gaming in a relaxed setting. The concept caught on, and the company kept building. By mid-2026, the brand had grown to several locations across the valley, and this new Dorrell Lane spot marks the sixth.

This kind of expansion follows a long pattern in the Las Vegas Valley. For decades, big gaming companies have understood that locals want places close to home, not just tourist spots on the Strip. Neighborhood taverns, small casinos, and locals casinos have long been part of Southern Nevada life. What is newer is how quickly a fresh brand like Seventy Six has spread, and how much of that growth is pointed toward North Las Vegas.

North Las Vegas itself has changed dramatically over the past couple of decades. Once seen as a smaller, quieter neighbor to Las Vegas, it has become a fast-growing city with new master-planned communities, warehouse and industrial jobs, and a steady stream of new residents. Companies have noticed. The arrival of a sixth Seventy Six tavern, with this one landing in North Las Vegas, fits right into that story of a city on the rise.
