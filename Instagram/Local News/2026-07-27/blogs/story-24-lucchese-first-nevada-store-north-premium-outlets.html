Lucchese to Open First Nevada Store at Las Vegas North Premium Outlets

Lucchese, the heritage Western boot brand, is expected to open its first dedicated store in Nevada in July 2026 at Las Vegas North Premium Outlets. This will be the well known bootmaker's first storefront in the state, and it is part of a larger wave of new and first to Nevada retailers announced for the outlet center this spring.

For anyone who loves cowboy boots, this is a big deal. Lucchese has spent more than a hundred years building a name for careful leatherwork and handmade quality. Until now, if you wanted to try on a pair in person, you had to travel to Texas or another state. Soon you will be able to shop the brand right here in Las Vegas, just minutes from the Strip and the downtown area.

The news also says something about our city. Brands do not pick a spot at random. When a company like Lucchese chooses Las Vegas for its first Nevada store, it is betting that shoppers here will show up. That kind of confidence tends to follow people, jobs, and homes. In this article we will walk through what was announced, why it matters to people who live here, the history behind the brand and the mall, and what you can do if you want to be part of it.

What Happened

In May 2026, Nevada Business Magazine reported that Las Vegas North Premium Outlets was adding a group of new stores. The report described the changes as a mix of fashion and footwear brands. One of the names that stood out was Lucchese, the Western boot company. The article said Lucchese is expected to open in July 2026, and that it would be the brand's first dedicated store in Nevada.

Las Vegas North Premium Outlets is an outdoor shopping center. It sits at 875 South Grand Central Parkway in Las Vegas, just north of the Strip and close to downtown. The mall is owned and run by Simon Property Group, one of the largest mall operators in the country. It is easy to reach from Interstate 15, and it draws both tourists and locals looking for name brands at outlet prices.

A Lucchese store fits the mix at this center. The outlets already carry a long list of well known names in clothing, shoes, and accessories. Adding a heritage boot brand gives shoppers something they could not get there before. It also adds a Western touch that feels right at home in Nevada, a state with deep ranching and rodeo roots.

The word "first" is the key part of this story. A first to Nevada store means the brand did not have a full storefront anywhere in the state before. Shoppers who wanted the real thing had to buy online or drive out of state. Now they can walk in, look at the leather up close, feel the weight of a boot in their hands, and get help finding the right fit. That in person trial matters a lot with boots, since sizing and shape can be hard to judge from a screen.

It is worth noting that the report described a wave of openings, not just one. Lucchese was one of several brands named as part of the refresh at the center. That tells us the mall is not making a single small change. It is working to bring in fresh names and pull in more foot traffic. When a shopping center adds several new tenants at once, it usually means the owners see strong demand and want to keep the property fresh and busy.

As of this writing, the exact opening day within July 2026 had not been shared in the report we reviewed. Store hours, the full product list, and any grand opening events were also not spelled out. If those details change or get confirmed, they may be posted on the mall's own website or the brand's channels. We will treat the July 2026 timing as expected, since that is how the announcement framed it.

Background and History

Lucchese has a long story behind it. The brand traces its roots to 1883 in San Antonio, Texas, when the Lucchese family, who came to America from Italy, began making boots. Over the years, the company built a strong name for handcrafted leather footwear. It became known for careful stitching, quality hides, and a fit that many wearers swear by. For a lot of boot fans, the name stands for the high end of the Western market.

Because the boots are made with so much hand work, they usually cost more than mass produced footwear. That premium price is part of the brand's story. People who buy Lucchese often see the boots as an investment meant to last for years, not a quick throwaway purchase. Trying a pair in person helps a buyer feel more sure about spending that kind of money, which is one reason a physical store matters so much for a brand like this.

The mall has its own history. Las Vegas North Premium Outlets opened on August 1, 2003. It is an open air center spread across roughly 40 acres near downtown Las Vegas. Over the years it has grown into a busy shopping stop with around 175 stores. It carries a wide range of designer and name brand outlets, and it pulls in both locals hunting for deals and tourists taking a break from the Strip.

Simon Property Group runs the center as part of its Premium Outlets chain. Simon is one of the biggest names in American shopping centers. Its outlet malls are known for bringing well known brands together in one place at outlet prices. Over the years, the North outlets have refreshed their store list many times to keep up with what shoppers want. The 2026 wave of new tenants, including Lucchese, is the latest chapter in that ongoing effort to stay current and busy.
