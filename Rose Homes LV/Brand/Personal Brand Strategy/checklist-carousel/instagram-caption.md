## Caption (primary)

The friendly person at the model home works for the builder. Not for you.

Buying new construction in Las Vegas feels safe. Clean walls, a warranty, a smiling face at the sales desk. But that face is paid to protect the builder, and the contract was written by the builder's lawyers.

Here are 5 things every new-build buyer should check before they sign:

1. Bring your own agent on visit one. The builder usually pays for it, so it costs you nothing.
2. Read the delay and cancellation clauses in the contract.
3. Ask about SID and LID bonds. These can add $500 to $3,000 or more a year on top of your property taxes.
4. Don't take the builder's lender at face value. Compare at least one outside quote on rate and fees.
5. Inspect the brand-new home. About 9 out of 10 have a real issue at inspection.
