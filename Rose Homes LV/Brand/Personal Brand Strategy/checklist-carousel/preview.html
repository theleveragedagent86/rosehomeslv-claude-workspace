Representation
03 / 08
01
Bring your own agent on day one.
Most builders pay your agent, so it costs you nothing. But walk in alone and you can lose the right to bring one later. Register with representation on visit one.

The contract
04 / 08
02
Read the contract the builder wrote.
Their lawyers wrote it to protect them. Find the delay clause, the cancellation terms, and whether tariff increases can be passed to you. Get every promise in writing.

@rosehomeslv
Hidden costs
05 / 08
03
The big Vegas one
Ask about SID and LID bonds.
These assessments are billed on top of your property taxes. Often $500 to $3,000+ a year, for 10 to 20 years. They transfer to you and can lead to foreclosure if unpaid. Get the number before you fall in love.

Financing
06 / 08
04
Don't take the builder's lender at face value.
The incentives are usually tied to their preferred lender. Get their full Loan Estimate, then compare one outside quote on rate and fees. Know what a buydown becomes after it ends.

@rosehomeslv
Inspections
07 / 08
05
Inspect the brand-new home.
About 9 out of 10 new homes have a significant issue at inspection. Get a pre-drywall inspection before the walls close, and an independent final inspection before closing. New is not flawless.
