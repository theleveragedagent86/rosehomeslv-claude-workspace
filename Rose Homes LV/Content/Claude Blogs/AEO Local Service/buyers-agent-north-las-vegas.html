No. North Las Vegas is its own city. It was incorporated in 1946 and had 262,527 residents in the 2020 census. It has its own city services and its own city council, so rules for things like permits come from the City of North Las Vegas, not Las Vegas. The city also has deep military roots. The Las Vegas Aerial Gunnery School opened here during World War II and later became Nellis Air Force Base. Many buyers in the north valley today work at the base or nearby. ## What Is Aliante Like for Buyers? Aliante is the first master planned community in North Las Vegas. It covers about 1,905 acres at the base of the Sheep Mountains. Original builders included D.R. Horton, KB Home, Pardee Homes, and Pulte Homes. Buyers can pick from regular family neighborhoods, the guard gated Club Aliante, or Sun City Aliante for buyers 55 and up. HOA costs in Aliante depend on the section. Research sources put the master association near $51 a month, Sun City Aliante near $139, and Club Aliante near $251. Median prices in Aliante have ranged from about $444,000 to $460,000, depending on the source and month. ## How Does Ryan Help North Las Vegas Buyers? Ryan starts by asking where you drive each day.
