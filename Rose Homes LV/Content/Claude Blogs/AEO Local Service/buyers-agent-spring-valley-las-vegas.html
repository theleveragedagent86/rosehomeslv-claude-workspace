Spring Valley is an unincorporated town in Clark County, run with help from a town advisory board instead of a city council. It is one of the largest unincorporated places in Nevada, with 215,597 people in the 2020 census. The main borders are Sahara Avenue to the north, Decatur Boulevard to the east, Warm Springs Road to the south, and Hualapai Way to the west. The area began in 1981 as a Pardee Homes master plan on land that used to hold the Stardust International Raceway. Las Vegas Chinatown runs along Spring Mountain Road inside its borders. ## What Kinds of Homes Will You Find? Age is the big split.
