Summerlin is big, and the rules change from village to village, so it helps to have someone on your side who reads the fine print. ## How Big Is Summerlin, and Why Does It Matter? Summerlin covers about 22,500 acres along the western edge of the valley, next to Red Rock Canyon. It began in 1990, when the $22 million Summerlin Parkway opened to link the area to US 95. Today it is split into Summerlin North, Summerlin South, and Summerlin West, plus 55+ villages like Sun City Summerlin and Siena. Here is a detail many buyers miss. Most of Summerlin sits inside Las Vegas city limits, but Summerlin South is in unincorporated Clark County. That changes which local government handles things like permits and code questions for your home. ## What Costs Should Summerlin Buyers Check? Summerlin uses two layers of HOA. You pay a master association fee plus a fee to your own neighborhood association. Ryan's research found typical totals of about $100 to $200 a month for a single family home, but gated villages and condos can run higher. Some newer parts of Summerlin West also have SID assessments. These are paid on the property tax bill, not to the HOA, and they pass to the next owner. Ryan asks for the payoff balance before you write an offer, so it never shows up as a surprise at closing. ## How Does Ryan Work With Summerlin Buyers? Prices vary a lot by village.
