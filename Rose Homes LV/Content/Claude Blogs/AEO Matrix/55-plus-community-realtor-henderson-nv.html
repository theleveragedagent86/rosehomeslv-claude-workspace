Age rules come from the community, not from the MLS. Federal law lets a community qualify as housing for older persons when at least 80 percent of occupied units have at least one occupant aged 55 or older, and the association verifies ages by survey at least every two years. The remaining 20 percent is a ceiling on exceptions, not a slot a buyer can request. Rules for a younger spouse, an adult child, or grandchildren staying over live in that association's CC and Rs. Ask the HOA to confirm your exact household situation in writing before your contingencies expire. ## What Should You Check Before Writing an Offer? Read the resale package early.
