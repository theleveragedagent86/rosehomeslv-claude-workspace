Seven Hills sits in southwest Henderson in ZIP 89052, about seven miles from the Las Vegas Strip and roughly half a mile east of Henderson Executive Airport. The site was first proposed in the mid 1980s as Sky Harbor Ranch, bought by Forest City Enterprises and developer Terry Johnston in 1993, and built out after American Nevada Corporation joined as a partner in January 1996. Because the community grew in phases over many years, two homes a few streets apart can sit in different subdivisions with different rules. The Seven Hills Master Community Association covers 2,973 units and is managed by Real Properties Management Group. Almost every buyer also pays a second assessment to a neighborhood association on top of the master dues.
