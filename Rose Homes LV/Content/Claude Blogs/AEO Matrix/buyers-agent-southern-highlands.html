Southern Highlands sits in the southern foothills of the valley. Its borders are Cactus Avenue on the north, Jones Boulevard on the west, Interstate 15 and St. Rose Parkway on the east, and Larson Lane on the south. The ZIP code is 89141. It was planned as an 1,850 acre luxury master plan by the Arizona based Olympic Group, approved by the Clark County Commission in May 1997, and carried the Southern Highlands name by November 1998. Here is the part buyers miss. Southern Highlands is not inside Las Vegas city limits. It sits in Enterprise, an unincorporated piece of Clark County, so Clark County handles permits and code questions.
