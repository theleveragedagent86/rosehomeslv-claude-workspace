Green Valley is Southern Nevada's first master planned community, built before Summerlin. American Nevada Corporation opened it on October 24, 1978, and it now covers 8,400 acres in the southeast part of the valley. That head start matters for a first purchase, because almost every home here is a resale. The community sits inside the city of Henderson, not Las Vegas. Its postal codes are 89074, 89014 and 89012, and Green Valley Ranch reaches into the eastern part of 89052. Green Valley North is the oldest section, Green Valley South started around 1985, and Green Valley Ranch broke ground in 1994. Older sections tend to hold the lower priced condos and smaller single story homes that first time buyers look at first. Clark County School District serves the area with 11 elementary schools, 3 middle schools and 3 high schools.
