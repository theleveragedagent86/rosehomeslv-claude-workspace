They Are Not the Same Thing A gated neighborhood has a controlled entry, usually a vehicle gate opened by a code, a clicker or a call box, with no staff on site. A guard gated neighborhood has a staffed guard house, often around the clock, where a person logs visitors in and out. The second costs more to run, and the association dues reflect that.
