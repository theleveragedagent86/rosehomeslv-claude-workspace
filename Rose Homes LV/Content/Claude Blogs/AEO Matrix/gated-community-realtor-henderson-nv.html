A gated neighborhood has an automatic vehicle gate on a code, a clicker or a call box, with nobody on site. A guard gated neighborhood pays staff to sit at the entry, log visitors in and out, and call the home before anyone is admitted. That staffing is the single biggest reason two Henderson homes of similar size can carry very different monthly dues. Some Henderson communities also run a roving patrol on top of the gatehouse.
