A guard gated neighborhood has a staffed gate around the clock. A person checks in visitors, logs deliveries, and controls who drives through. An auto gated neighborhood uses a remote or a code with no guard on site. Both limit traffic, but only one screens people. Seven Hills has five guard gated neighborhoods: Terracina, Venezia, Rapallo, Renaissance, and Presidio. Terracina and Venezia share a connected guard gated entry at the top of the community. Renaissance is the one guard gated neighborhood built by a production builder, with 217 semi custom homes from Pardee Homes ranging from about 2,800 to 4,600 square feet. The auto gated group includes Avalon, the condominium community, The Terraces townhomes, Palazzo Monte, Villa Villagio, and Majestic Hills. Neighborhoods such as Crescendo, Naples, Palermo West, and Meridiana are open access, though the master plan's cul de sac layout and defined entries already keep through traffic down. ## What Do the HOA Dues Actually Cover?
