The VA says a VA purchase loan requires no down payment as long as the sales price is not higher than the home's appraised value, and no private mortgage insurance or mortgage insurance premium. Most buyers pay a one time VA funding fee instead. That combination is why a VA offer can be strong even when cash for closing is thin. The appraisal is where these deals get tense. A VA appraisal includes minimum property requirements, and on an older resale that can surface items a seller has to address before closing.
