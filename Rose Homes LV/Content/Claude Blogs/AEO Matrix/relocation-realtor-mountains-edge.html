Mountain's Edge is not one neighborhood. Focus Property Group started building it in February 2004, and the master plan covers 3,500 acres in the unincorporated town of Enterprise, on the southwest rim of the valley. The original plan called for 14,500 homes across more than 22 neighborhoods. By the end of 2025 roughly 12,500 of those homes were built. That matters when you are clicking through listings at night in Ohio. Two houses at the same price can sit two miles apart and feel like different towns. One backs to a paseo near Exploration Peak.
