Ryan Rose | Real Broker, LLC is a Las Vegas Realtor who walks homes on video, drives the commute for you, and helps you narrow the neighborhood before you ever land at Harry Reid. ## Why Is Providence Hard to Shop From Out of State? Providence is not one neighborhood. It is a Focus Property Group master plan on the upper west side of the valley, mostly in the 89166 ZIP code, covering 1,200 acres and planned for roughly 7,500 residences across 30 neighborhoods. The product mix runs from condominiums to estate homes. That is why listings look interchangeable from a laptop in Chicago. Two homes at the same price can sit a mile apart in different sub neighborhoods, with different dues, different build years and very different streets. Photos flatten all of it, and a map app will not tell you which one backs a park and which one backs a wall. Schools work the same way. Henry and Evelyn Bozarth Elementary School is on Egan Crest Drive inside the community, but Clark County School District assignments go by address, so the school your kids attend depends on which house you pick. ## How Does Ryan Handle a Providence Relocation?
