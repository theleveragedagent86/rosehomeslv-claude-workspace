Seven Hills is a master planned community in southwest Henderson, ZIP 89052, sitting in the foothills about seven miles from the Las Vegas Strip and roughly half a mile east of Henderson Executive Airport. Henderson is its own city, not a Las Vegas neighborhood, which matters for your permits, utilities and city services. Harry Reid International Airport is a short drive north, and St. Rose Parkway plus Interstate 215 connect the area to the rest of the valley. The community is built around the Serket Golf Club, an 18 hole par 72 course designed by Rees Jones that opened in 1997 and carried the Rio Secco name for most of its history. It is also home to the Butch Harmon School of Golf. Sloan Canyon National Conservation Area lies to the south and east, which keeps open desert at the edge of the community. Inspirada sits on the 1,940 acre parcel southwest of Seven Hills, sold in 2004 and developed as a separate master plan. Relocation buyers often tour both, along with Green Valley and Anthem, before deciding, and Ryan lays out the differences rather than pushing one. ## How Does Ryan Work With Out of State Buyers?
