Southern Highlands occupies the southern foothills of the Las Vegas Valley, bounded by Cactus Avenue on the north, Jones Boulevard on the west, Interstate 15 and St. Rose Parkway on the east, and Larson Lane on the south. The ZIP code is 89141. That southeast corner is the practical selling point for people relocating, because Interstate 15 and St. Rose Parkway put you on the freeway quickly for the airport, the south Strip, and Henderson. One thing to know before you file anything. Southern Highlands is in Enterprise, an unincorporated part of Clark County, not the City of Las Vegas. Your mailing address says Las Vegas, but county offices handle permits and code. ## What Should a Relocating Buyer Know About Schools? Public schools here are Clark County School District. Three district elementary schools sit inside the community: Charles and Phyllis Frias, Dennis Ortwein, and Evelyn Stuckey. Lois and Jerry Tarkanian Middle School and Desert Oasis High School serve the area from outside the community, and Southern Highlands Preparatory School, a private campus, opened in August 2003.
