The best new construction communities in Las Vegas right now include Summerlin, Cadence in Henderson, Skye Canyon, Sunstone, Lake Las Vegas, and the new Sandstone at Tule Springs in North Las Vegas. Which one is best for you comes down to your budget and your daily drive. Published starting prices across these plans run from the $300,000s to well over $1 million. I'm Ryan Rose | Real Broker, LLC, and I help buyers compare builders across the valley. Below is what each plan offers, using the developers' and builders' own published numbers. Prices change with every release, so treat them as a starting point. ## Which Master Plans Have the Most New Homes for Sale? **Summerlin.** This is the valley's best known master plan, on the west side near Red Rock. In July 2026, Summerlin said it had 12 new neighborhoods selling, priced from the mid $400,000s to over $2 million. Building is in the newer western areas, like Grand Park, Kestrel, Kestrel Commons, and La Madre Peaks. Builders include Toll Brothers, Lennar, Pulte, Taylor Morrison, and KB Home. **Cadence in Henderson.** Cadence covers 2,200 acres off Lake Mead Parkway, with up to 12,250 homes planned. Its FAQ lists prices from the $300,000s to the $700,000s. It also says there are no SID or LID fees, which is worth a lot over time. Seven builders sell there, including Beazer, Century Communities, D.R. Horton, and Woodside Homes. The nearly 50 acre Central Park sits in the middle. **Skye Canyon.** This plan sits in the northwest, off US 95. The developer lists three builders today: Century Communities, Toll Brothers, and LGI Homes, with homes from the $400s. Residents get Skye Fitness, a community center, parks, and a Smith's Marketplace close by. ## Where Are the Newest and Most Affordable Options? **Sandstone at Tule Springs.** KB Home opened this North Las Vegas plan on September 4, 2026, at North 5th Street and the 215 Beltway. It covers nearly 300 acres and plans more than 1,500 homes. Two neighborhoods are open now, and two are coming soon. KB lists homes from the $300,000s. A new plan also means years of building next door, so expect some dust. **Sunstone.** This northwest plan is newer too. Lennar lists five collections there, from $383,990 for Axel to $859,990 for Alia Estates, as of September 2026. ## Which Communities Fit Buyers 55 and Older? Two stand out. Trilogy Sunstone by Shea Homes is a 55 and older community inside Sunstone, with homes from $399,990 and a resort style club. Lake Las Vegas, on the far east side of Henderson, lists Del Webb homes from the mid $400,000s. Lake Las Vegas also has luxury options, with Blue Heron homes from the low $1 million range. ## How Do I Pick the Right One? Start with your daily drive. Drive the route at rush hour before you fall for a floor plan. Next, ask each builder for the full price in writing. That means the lot premium, design center upgrades, HOA dues, and any special assessment. Then check the exact address on the CCSD zoning search, since builder school lists are not zoning. Bring your agent to your first visit. Many builders will only work with an agent who registers with you that day.
