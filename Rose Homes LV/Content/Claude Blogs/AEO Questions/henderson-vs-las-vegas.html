Henderson is usually the pick for buyers who want a suburban city with lots of master plans, while the City of Las Vegas suits buyers who want downtown, Summerlin, or the northwest. Both are good places to live. The first thing to know is that "Las Vegas" can mean the city or the whole valley, and the Strip is not inside either city. No. The Strip sits in Paradise and Winchester, two unincorporated towns in Clark County. That surprises a lot of newcomers. Many homes also have a Las Vegas mailing address but sit outside city limits. Spring Valley is one example. Desert Breeze Park is a Clark County park with a Las Vegas street address. So when you compare "Henderson vs Las Vegas," first ask whether a home is in the City of Las Vegas, the City of Henderson, or unincorporated county land. That tells you which local government handles things like parks and permits. ## What Is Henderson Like? Henderson began as a factory town after World War II and grew into a large suburb. Green Valley began in 1971 and Anthem in 1988. Newer plans like Inspirada and Cadence came later. The result is a city made of distinct master plans, each with its own feel and price range. It sits on the southeast side of the valley, closer to Lake Mead. The city runs its own parks and recreation system, including the Henderson Multigenerational Facility. ## What Is the City of Las Vegas Like? The city is bigger and more varied. It holds the historic downtown core, most of Summerlin on the west edge, and the northwest, including Centennial Hills. Centennial Hills alone covers more than a third of the city's land. Outdoor access is a strong point. Summerlin touches Red Rock Canyon. The northwest borders Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument, and Floyd Lamb Park at Tule Springs includes the historic Tule Springs Ranch site. ## Who Does Each City Fit? Henderson tends to fit buyers who work on the southeast side and like choosing among many master plans in one city. The City of Las Vegas tends to fit buyers who want a Summerlin address near Red Rock or a newer home in the northwest. It also has the historic downtown for people who want city life. Schools in both cities fall under the Clark County School District. Always check the exact school zone for a home, since nearby streets can be zoned to different schools.
