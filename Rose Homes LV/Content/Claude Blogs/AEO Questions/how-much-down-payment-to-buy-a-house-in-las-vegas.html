The less you put down, the more you pay in interest and fees over the life of the loan. It also says that with less than 20% down you will likely pay mortgage insurance. On the other side, cash you put into the house is cash you can't use for an emergency. Many buyers choose a smaller down payment so they keep a cushion. That is a fair choice as long as the monthly payment still fits. Don't forget closing costs. The CFPB says they typically run 2% to 5% of the purchase price, on top of your down payment.
