To buy a median priced single family home in Henderson, most buyers need a household income of roughly $110,000 to $145,000 a year. Henderson costs more than the Las Vegas valley as a whole, so the income bar sits higher. Your down payment, your debts, and your rate decide where you land in that range. This answer comes from Ryan Rose | Real Broker, LLC, a Las Vegas area Realtor who helps Henderson buyers match a home to a real budget. The math below is an estimate, not a loan quote. ## What Does a Home Cost in Henderson? Nevada Real Estate Group, citing Las Vegas REALTORS data, reports a median existing single family price of $540,000 in Henderson for July 2026. That was 5.4% higher than a year earlier. For the whole Southern Nevada market, Las Vegas REALTORS reported a median of $475,000 in August 2026, per News 3 Las Vegas. So a typical Henderson house runs about $65,000 above the valley median. That gap adds a few hundred dollars to the monthly payment. ## How Much Income Does $540,000 Take? Lenders compare your monthly debts to your gross monthly income. Experian reports that FHA loans usually look for a mortgage payment at or below 31% of income, with total debts at or below 43%. Some loans allow more. Here is the example. Assume a $540,000 home, a 30 year fixed loan, and the 6.95% average rate Freddie Mac reported on September 17, 2026. With 3.5% down ($18,900), the loan is about $521,100. Principal and interest are about $3,449 a month. At 31% of income, that points to about $133,500 a year. With 20% down ($108,000), the loan is about $432,000. Principal and interest are about $2,860 a month. At 31% of income, that points to about $110,700 a year. Those payments leave out property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, and HOA dues. Many Henderson neighborhoods sit inside master planned communities with an HOA, so ask for the dues on any home you like. Add those costs and the income you need goes up. ## How Can You Buy in Henderson on Less? Start with the home type. Condos and townhomes cost less across the valley. Las Vegas REALTORS put that median at $299,900 in August 2026, per News 3 Las Vegas. Henderson has its own mix of both. Next, look at help with cash. The Nevada Housing Division's Home Is Possible program lists down payment assistance of up to 4% of the loan amount for eligible first time buyers who meet its credit and income limits.
