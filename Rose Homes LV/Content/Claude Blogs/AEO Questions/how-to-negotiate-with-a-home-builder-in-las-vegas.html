Yes, you can negotiate with a home builder in Las Vegas, but most of the give is in incentives and upgrades, not the base price. Ask for closing cost help, a rate buydown, a design center credit, or a better lot, and get every promise written into the contract. Your strongest spot is usually a finished home the builder wants to close soon. I'm Ryan Rose | Real Broker, LLC, and I represent buyers with builders across the valley. Here is how builder deals really work, and what to ask for. ## Why Won't Builders Just Cut the Price? A builder sells many homes in the same neighborhood. If it drops the base price on one plan, that new price becomes the comp for every home it still has to sell. So most builders would rather add value through credits than lower the sticker. Some do cut prices. In its August 2026 survey, the National Association of Home Builders said 35% of builders nationwide cut prices, and the average cut was 6%. The same survey found 63% used sales incentives of some kind. That is a national number, so ask what your builder is doing this month. ## What Can You Ask a Builder For? Here are the usual levers. Not every builder offers each one, and terms change often. **Closing cost credit.** The builder pays part of your closing costs. This is often tied to using the builder's own lender. **Rate buydown.** The builder pays to lower your mortgage rate. Some buydowns are permanent. Others lower the rate only for the first year or two, then it goes back up. Read which kind it is. **Design center credit.** Money toward upgrades. This fits a home that is not built yet, while you still have choices to make. **Lot premium.** Better lots cost more. KB Home, for example, notes on its Landings at Sandstone page that a homesite premium may apply. On a lot the builder has had trouble selling, you can ask whether that premium can come down. ## When Do You Have the Most Room to Negotiate? Finished homes are the best spot. A builder pays to carry a completed home, so it often wants it closed. Homes near the end of a phase can be similar. Your own prep matters too. Be preapproved before you visit, so the builder sees a real buyer. Tour more than one builder, and let each one know you are comparing. ## How Do You Make Sure the Deal Sticks? Put everything in writing. A promise from the sales office means nothing if it is not in the contract or an addendum. That includes the credit amount, the upgrades, the lot, and the estimated finish date. Compare lenders on total cost. The biggest incentive can come with a higher rate or more fees. Ask an outside lender for a quote on the same loan and compare the two side by side. Ryan connects buyers with trusted lenders and down payment assistance programs. For loan terms, confirm the details with your lender, and ask a Nevada attorney if any contract language is unclear. ## How Ryan Handles Builder Negotiations Bring Ryan to your first visit, since many builders require your agent to be there that day. No. Builders expect questions about incentives. Ask politely and clearly. ### Should I use the builder's lender to get the incentive? Only if the total cost beats your outside quote. Compare both. ### Can I negotiate upgrades on a finished home? The finishes are already in. Ask for a closing cost credit or a rate buydown instead. ### Do builder incentives change? Yes, often monthly. What a friend got last spring may not be offered now. ## What Homes Are Selling For in the Las Vegas valley Here is what actually closed.
