Nevada's Constitution, in Article 10, Section 1, says "No income tax shall be levied upon the wages or personal income of natural persons." That means no state income tax on a pension or Social Security. Federal taxes still apply. Property taxes have a built in brake too. Clark County explains that NRS 361.4723 applies a 3% cap on the yearly tax bill for an owner's primary residence. Homes that are not owner occupied can see a cap of up to 8%. One catch: a brand new home does not get any cap in its first fiscal year. The 3% cap starts the next year. Another detail from the Clark County Assessor: recording a new ownership document, like moving your home into a trust, removes the 3% abatement until you return a new postcard. Watch your mail after July 1. Tax rules change and every situation is different, so confirm your plan with a CPA or tax attorney. ## What Is the Weather Really Like?
