Here is what the latest numbers say, and how to decide for yourself. ## What Do the Latest Las Vegas Numbers Show? Las Vegas REALTORS reported a median price of $475,000 for existing single family homes sold in August 2026. That was down 1% from August 2025. It was also below the record of $490,000 set in May and June of this year. Supply is the bigger story. About 7,590 single family homes were listed without offers at the end of August, up 5.3% from a year earlier. That works out to a little over 4.5 months of supply. In January 2026, supply was nearly five months, up from 3.5 months a year before. More listings mean less of a rush for buyers. Homes are also taking a bit longer to sell. In August, 74.8% of single family homes sold within 60 days, down from 77.5% a year earlier. George Kypreos, the LVR president, said buyers are taking more time to make decisions. ## Why Are Mortgage Rates the Main Hurdle? Freddie Mac reported the average 30 year fixed rate at 6.95% on September 17, 2026. That was up from 6.76% the week before and 6.26% a year earlier. Here is a rough example. Say you buy a home at $475,000 with 5% down. Your loan would be about $451,250. At 6.95%, principal and interest come to roughly $2,987 a month. Property taxes and insurance are extra, plus any HOA fee. This is an estimate, not a quote, so ask a lender to run your real numbers. ## How Do You Know If It Is a Good Time for You? The market does not pick your timing. Your life does. These questions help more than any headline: - Do you plan to stay at least five years? Short stays make it hard to cover buying and selling costs. - Is your job steady, with savings left after closing? - Does the full monthly payment fit your budget today, without counting on a future rate drop? - Do you qualify for help? Ryan connects buyers with trusted lenders and down payment assistance programs. If you can say yes to most of these, a calmer market can work in your favor. You may have room to ask for repairs or seller credits, which is harder to do when supply is tight. ## What Does Ryan Tell Buyers Right Now? Ryan tells buyers to shop the payment, not just the price.
