Northgate is a compact, built out gated community, while Centennial Hills is the broad, growing northwest submarket that surrounds it. Northgate holds roughly 208 residents in single story homes from about 1995, with no new construction left inside its gates. Centennial Hills, by contrast, spans several ZIP codes across the northwest valley and still sees active new home building, with roughly 18 to 22 new homes reported across the wider area. In short, Northgate offers small enclave quiet, and Centennial Hills offers scale and newer inventory. ## What Do Northgate and Centennial Hills Share? Northgate and Centennial Hills share the same everyday amenities along the Ann Road corridor.
