Things to do near Northgate in Las Vegas include hiking at Lone Mountain Regional Park, skating and dog parks at Craig Ranch Regional Park, picking produce at Gilcrease Orchard, and dining and bowling at Santa Fe Station casino. ## What Are the Best Things to Do Near Northgate? The best things to do near Northgate center on the northwest valley's parks and open space. Lone Mountain Regional Park, about 4 to 5 miles west, spans roughly 120 acres with a 3 mile loop trail, a steeper summit trail, an equestrian arena, and pickleball, basketball, and tennis courts. Craig Ranch Regional Park, roughly 3.5 to 4 miles away, covers about 170 acres and features a 65,000 square foot skate park, three dog parks, an amphitheater seating around 8,000, a lake, and about 5 miles of paved trails. These two regional parks give residents a wide range of activities within a short drive. One leans toward desert hiking and horseback riding, while the other delivers courts, skating, and shaded event space for families and groups. ## Outdoor and Family Attractions Near Northgate Families have plenty of variety close to home. Gilcrease Orchard, about 3.5 to 4 miles northwest at 7810 N Tenaya Way, is a working orchard where visitors pick their own produce and enjoy hayrides plus fall cider and donuts. Santa Fe Station casino, near Rancho Drive and US 95, adds bowling, movies, and several restaurants roughly 4 to 5 miles from the neighborhood. For bigger outings, Centennial Hills Town Center serves as a nearby retail and dining hub, and Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area is about 20 to 25 minutes west for scenic drives and desert hiking. Together these spots give Northgate residents a mix of everyday recreation and weekend adventure without a long trip across the valley. Dining and entertainment round out the choices. Santa Fe Station casino pairs its bowling alley and movie theater with sit down restaurants, while the Ann Road corridor closer to home offers casual spots for a quick meal.
