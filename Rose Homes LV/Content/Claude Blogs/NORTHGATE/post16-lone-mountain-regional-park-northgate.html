Lone Mountain Regional Park sits about 4 to 5 miles west of Northgate and covers roughly 120 acres of open desert recreation at 9825 West Lone Mountain Road in Las Vegas. It is one of the largest and most versatile parks near this gated community. ## What Will You Find at Lone Mountain Regional Park? Lone Mountain Regional Park anchors the northwest valley with about 120 acres of trails, sports courts, and open space at the base of a prominent desert peak. The park draws families, hikers, and horse owners from across Las Vegas, and Northgate residents can reach it in roughly 10 to 15 minutes by car. Its location off West Lone Mountain Road keeps it close to the Ann Road and Decatur corridor while still feeling tucked against the mountain. That balance of easy access and real scenery is a big part of the appeal. The park offers a full slate of courts and facilities beyond its walking paths. Visitors find two pickleball courts, a basketball court, and tennis courts, plus shaded picnic areas and gazebos that work well for birthday parties and group gatherings. The equestrian side includes a riding arena and round pens, a feature that is rare this close to a gated pocket like Northgate. The mix makes the park useful for a quick evening workout or a full day outing. ## Is Lone Mountain Regional Park Good for Views? Yes, the park is well known for its wide valley views and its two contrasting trails. A gravel loop of about 3 miles circles the mountain base at an easy to moderate grade, welcoming joggers, cyclists, and families with strollers. A steeper summit trail climbs roughly 1.3 miles round trip with about 600 feet of elevation gain, rewarding hikers with 360 degree views of the Las Vegas valley. Leashed dogs are welcome on the trails, which adds to the draw for pet owners who want an easy place to roam. Picnic gazebos, open turf, and desert landscaping round out the grounds, so families can pair a hike with a shaded lunch. The park generally follows dawn to dusk hours, making early mornings the coolest and quietest time to visit during the warm months.
