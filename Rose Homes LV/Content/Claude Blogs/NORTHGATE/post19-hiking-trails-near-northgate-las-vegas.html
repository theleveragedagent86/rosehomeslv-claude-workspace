The two standout hiking trails near Northgate both start at Lone Mountain, and they suit very different fitness levels. The Lone Mountain Loop runs about 3 miles of packed gravel around the base of the peak, with only minimal elevation change. It is graded easy to moderate, which makes it friendly for joggers, cyclists, families with strollers, and leashed dogs. Most walkers finish the full loop in a little over an hour at a relaxed pace. The Summit Trail is the harder of the two and rewards the effort with a payoff few close in hikes offer. It climbs roughly 1.3 miles round trip over rocky dirt, gaining about 600 feet, with a few short scrambles near the top. From the summit, hikers take in 360 degree views of the Las Vegas valley, which is why sunrise and sunset are the most popular windows. Sturdy shoes with good grip help a lot on the loose, rocky sections. ## What Should You Bring Before Hiking Near Northgate? Bring more water than you think you need, sun protection, and grippy footwear, since the desert sun and loose rock are the two biggest challenges on these trails. Leashed dogs are welcome on both trails, making them a favorite for Northgate pet owners.
