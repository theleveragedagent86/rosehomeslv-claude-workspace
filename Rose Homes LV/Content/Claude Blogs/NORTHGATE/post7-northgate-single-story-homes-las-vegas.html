Single story homes in Northgate appeal strongly to retirees and downsizers, offering step free living on quiet gated streets in northwest Las Vegas, with everyday errands and two hospitals close by. ## What Makes Single Story Homes in Northgate Good for Retirees? Single story homes in Northgate mean no stairs, which is the feature downsizers and retirees ask about most. The community is made up entirely of single story tract homes built around 1995, typically three bedroom, two bathroom layouts near 1,244 to 1,300 square feet. That compact, all on one level design is easy to maintain and easy to age in place. Homes on a single level also reduce fall risk, a practical benefit for aging in place. Northgate is gated, all ages, and quiet, so it suits retirees without being an age restricted 55 plus community. Buyers get the calm of a settled neighborhood without the rules or higher dues that come with active adult developments. ## How Close Is Northgate to Hospitals and Errands? Northgate sits within easy reach of medical care, a priority for many older buyers. A freestanding Valley Vista ER on North Decatur is about 2 miles away, and Centennial Hills Hospital Medical Center, a full service facility with 262 beds, is roughly 5 miles northwest. That short drive to both a walk in ER and a full hospital gives peace of mind to retirees managing health needs. Everyday shopping is even closer, with grocery and pharmacy options at Ann Road and Decatur within about a mile. The low monthly HOA, reported near $45, keeps fixed costs modest for buyers on a set income. ## What Do Northgate Homes Offer Downsizers?
