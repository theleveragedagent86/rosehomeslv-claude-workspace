# Research Data: Northgate (Las Vegas)

## Which Northgate (disambiguation)

**Researched community:** Northgate is a small **gated residential subdivision in the City of Las Vegas (not the separate City of North Las Vegas), ZIP 89130**, in the northwest valley / southern Centennial Hills area.

- **Location / cross streets:** Just **north of W Ann Road, near N Decatur Blvd** (Google Maps places the neighborhood at approx. 36.2604, -115.2086). Internal streets include **Minturn Ave** (e.g., the 4800 block: 4804–4856 Minturn Ave). The HOA's registered address is **4816 Minturn Ave, Las Vegas, NV 89130**.
- **Builder / era:** Tract homes built **circa 1995** (sample home at 4848 Minturn built 1995). Original builder NOT FOUND by name in available sources (portal pages that would list it — REMAX/Zillow/Redfin — returned 403/blocked). Mark as "NOT FOUND — verify."
- **Gated status:** **Yes, gated.** Single-story homes.
- **Size:** Very small pocket community — Nextdoor/Census cite ~**208 residents**.

**Important caveats to prevent conflation:**
1. Several directory sites label it "N Las Vegas / North Las Vegas," but ZIP 89130 is inside the **City of Las Vegas**. The border with the City of North Las Vegas runs along/near Decatur here, so listings on both sides get mixed. The residential HOA itself is the Las Vegas 89130 community.
2. There is also a **"Northgate" business/retail context** and a shopping node near Ann & Decatur — do NOT confuse the retail area with the residential HOA.
3. "Northgate" also exists as a Reno, NV neighborhood — several search results were Reno; those were excluded.
