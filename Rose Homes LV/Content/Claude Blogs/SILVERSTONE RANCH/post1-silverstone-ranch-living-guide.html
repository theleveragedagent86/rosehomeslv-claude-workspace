Living in Silverstone Ranch means settling into one of northwest Las Vegas's most established master planned communities, an all ages neighborhood of roughly 1,500 homes tucked against the Sheep Mountains beside Floyd Lamb Park. ## What is it like living in Silverstone Ranch? Living in Silverstone Ranch feels calm and residential, a welcome contrast to the busier valley core. Pulte Homes built the community between 2003 and 2009 across nine neighborhoods, among them Amberly, Clairbrook, Eastpoint, Greenfield, Parkfield, Somerset, Tuscany, the Cottages, Pinehurst, and the guard gated Palms. Three of those neighborhoods sit behind gates, and the Palms adds staffed 24/7 entry for extra privacy. Housing options stretch from cozy Pinehurst townhomes to roomy two story estates, so buyers at many budgets find a match. More than half the homes front the fairways of the former Silverstone Golf Club, which closed in 2015 and remains a topic residents follow closely. Wide streets, mature desert landscaping, and green corridors give the neighborhood a settled, lived in character that newer subdivisions cannot copy. ## What amenities does Silverstone Ranch offer? Residents enjoy a generous slate of shared amenities that keep daily life close to home. The community includes lighted tennis courts, basketball courts, bocce ball lawns, three putting greens, and several playgrounds. A central family park of roughly six acres anchors gatherings, while walking and fitness trails wind through landscaped greenbelts. Picnic ramadas, reservable cabanas, and a clubhouse you can book for events round out the list. Seasonal community happenings give neighbors easy reasons to meet, which helps Silverstone Ranch feel more connected than a typical bedroom community. ## Where is Silverstone Ranch located? Silverstone Ranch sits in the Centennial Hills area at ZIP 89131, near the base of the Sheep Mountains and next to Floyd Lamb Park. The Las Vegas Strip is roughly 25 to 35 minutes away, downtown about 22 to 25 minutes, Downtown Summerlin near 18 minutes, and Harry Reid International Airport close to 32 minutes. Centennial Hills Hospital Medical Center is the nearest hospital. Shopping and dining cluster nearby at Skye Canyon Marketplace and Centennial Center, both a short drive from most front doors. Because transit is limited, this is a car dependent area, so plan on driving for work, school runs, and errands. ## Local Insight Ryan Rose, a Las Vegas Real Estate Expert with Rose Homes LV, knows Silverstone Ranch pod by pod.
