Silverstone Ranch was built by Pulte Homes, the national homebuilder that developed the northwest Las Vegas community roughly between 2003 and 2009. ## Who Built Silverstone Ranch and When? Pulte Homes built Silverstone Ranch, delivering plans such as the Princeville Collection along with other designs across the community. Construction ran roughly from 2003 to 2009, producing somewhere between about 1,471 and 1,526 homes spread across nine neighborhoods. The homes reflect the range you would expect from a large Pulte build out. Sizes run from around 1,348 to 3,853 square feet in single story and two story layouts, from townhomes in the Pinehurst section to larger estate homes. Three car garages and backyard pools are common features here, along with open floor plans and covered patios. When people ask who built Silverstone Ranch, the short answer is Pulte Homes, and the neighborhood still carries that consistent quality today. Across those nine neighborhoods you will find everything from smaller townhomes to spacious estate homes, all set within one master planned community at the base of the Sheep Mountains. This is an all ages community in the 89131 ZIP code, close to Floyd Lamb Park and the wider Centennial Hills area, which is a big part of why the Pulte homes here have held their appeal. ## Is There New Construction in Silverstone Ranch? No. Silverstone Ranch is fully built out, so there is no new construction inside the community itself. Buyers who want a brand new home usually look to nearby Skye Canyon or other parts of Centennial Hills, where builders remain active. One point worth clearing up: Pulte built the homes, while the separate Silverstone golf club was designed by architect Robert Cupp, named Golf World Architect of the Year in 1992. The two are often confused, but they had different creators. So the builder question and the golf course question have distinct answers, even though both shaped the character of Silverstone Ranch.
