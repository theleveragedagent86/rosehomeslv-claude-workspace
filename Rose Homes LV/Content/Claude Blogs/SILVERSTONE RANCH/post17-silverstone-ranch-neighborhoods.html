Silverstone Ranch is organized into nine distinct neighborhoods, or pods, spread across roughly 1,471 to 1,526 homes in northwest Las Vegas. Each pod has its own character, gate status, and homeowners association dues. ## What Are the Nine Neighborhoods of Silverstone Ranch? The nine neighborhoods of Silverstone Ranch include Amberly, Clairbrook, Eastpoint, Greenfield, Parkfield, and Somerset, along with the Tuscany, Cottages, Pinehurst, and The Palms pods. Different sources name slightly more than nine because a few villages overlap or were marketed under more than one label, so treat this list as the recognized sub neighborhoods of the community. Each pod was built by Pulte Homes between roughly 2003 and 2009. Together they hold somewhere between 1,471 and 1,526 homes, depending on the source you check. That range covers everything from starter friendly floor plans to larger estate style homes, so buyers at many price points can find a fit within the same community. ## Which Silverstone Ranch Neighborhoods Are Gated? Three of the nine Silverstone Ranch neighborhoods sit behind gates. The Palms is the standout, offering a staffed guard gate that manages entry around the clock. The Tuscany and Cottages pods round out the gated group with controlled access. The remaining villages are open, yet they still belong to the same master association and share every community amenity. Pinehurst is worth calling out for a different reason. It is the townhome section of Silverstone Ranch, so buyers looking for lower maintenance, attached living tend to start there before touring the single family pods. ## How Do HOA Fees Differ Between the Neighborhoods? Fees vary by pod. Every owner pays the master association fee of about $158 a month, billed quarterly, and then a sub association fee is layered on. Open villages total near $200 a month, The Palms sits around $240, the Tuscany and Cottages pods run about $252, and the Pinehurst townhomes are highest at roughly $286. The gated and townhome pods cost more because they cover extra upkeep and, at The Palms, gate staffing.
