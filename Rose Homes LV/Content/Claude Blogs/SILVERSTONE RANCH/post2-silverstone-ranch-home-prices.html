Silverstone Ranch home prices center on a median sale price of about $562,000 as of late 2025, with values up roughly 3.5% year over year across this northwest Las Vegas community. ## What are Silverstone Ranch home prices in 2026? Silverstone Ranch home prices landed at a median of about $562,000 in September 2025, according to Redfin, up around 3.5% from a year earlier. Price per square foot ran near $262, a gain of roughly 6.5% year over year. Homes sold in about 49 days on average, and 14 properties changed hands that month. Some valuation sites list higher whole community estimates near $873,000, but that reflects all homes on paper, not what buyers actually pay, so the $562,000 median is the number to trust for real transactions. These figures shift with the seasons, so treat them as a recent snapshot rather than a fixed rule. ## What price range can buyers expect in Silverstone Ranch? Buyers see a wide spread here. Entry level townhomes and smaller plans start in the low $300,000s, while larger five and six bedroom homes reach the low $700,000s and beyond. Living space ranges from roughly 1,348 to 3,853 square feet, so the price you pay tracks closely with size, lot, and whether the home fronts the former golf course. Condition matters too; an updated interior and a private pool can move a home to the top of its bracket. Many homes add pools, three car garages, and covered patios, features that lift value within the community. ## Are Silverstone Ranch home prices going up? Recent data points to steady, modest growth rather than sharp swings. With the median up about 3.5% and price per square foot up near 6.5% over the year, Silverstone Ranch home prices have held firm even as the broader valley cooled. Limited supply helps, since the community is fully built with no new construction inside its borders. Nearby Skye Canyon and Centennial Hills carry the new build inventory instead. For sellers, that scarcity can mean solid demand when a well kept home hits the market at the right price.
