# Floyd Lamb Park at Tule Springs Near Silverstone Ranch Silverstone Ranch sits right next to Floyd Lamb Park at Tule Springs, a regional green space of about 680 acres where residents fish, picnic, and stroll past historic ranch buildings just minutes from home. ## What Is Floyd Lamb Park Near Silverstone Ranch? Floyd Lamb Park at Tule Springs is a regional park of roughly 680 acres located at 9200 Tule Springs Road, adjacent to Silverstone Ranch. The park centers on four stocked ponds surrounded by shady lawns, mature trees, walking paths, and picnic areas you can reserve for gatherings. Peacocks, ducks, and geese roam freely, giving the grounds a relaxed, almost rural feel that stands apart from the surrounding desert. The historic Tule Springs Ranch buildings sit on site and add a layer of Las Vegas history to any visit, offering a glimpse of the working ranch that once operated here at the base of the Sheep Mountains. ## Things To Do at Floyd Lamb Park There is plenty to fill a morning here. Families cast lines at the four fishing ponds, walk or jog the looping paths, and set up at reservable picnic areas stocked with BBQ grills. The park also offers horse trails, volleyball, and horseshoes for anyone who wants a little friendly competition. Entry runs about $6 per vehicle, and the gates are generally open from 8am to dusk. Because it borders Silverstone Ranch, many residents treat Floyd Lamb Park as an extension of their own backyard, dropping in for a sunrise walk or an afternoon with the kids. The variety is what keeps people coming back. One weekend you might reserve a shaded spot for a birthday party, and the next you might simply wander the ponds watching the resident peacocks show off. ## Getting There From Silverstone Ranch Reaching the park could hardly be simpler. Silverstone Ranch sits right at the edge of Floyd Lamb Park in the 89131 ZIP code, so residents can leave home and be watching the water within minutes, no freeway required. That short distance makes it one of the easiest outdoor escapes in the northwest valley, and it is a big reason the community feels connected to nature even though the Strip is only a 25 to 35 minute drive away. ## Local Insight Ryan Rose, a Las Vegas Real Estate Expert, often points buyers toward the northwest valley for exactly this reason.
