# Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument Near Silverstone Ranch Silverstone Ranch borders Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument, a roughly 22,650 acre preserve just north of the community where Ice Age mammoths, sloths, and camels once roamed the desert floor. ## What Is Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument? Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument protects about 22,650 acres of northwest Las Vegas desert directly north of Silverstone Ranch. Managed by the National Park Service, the monument preserves Pleistocene fossils from the last Ice Age, including Columbian mammoths, ground sloths, ancient camels, bison, and early horses. Scientists have studied this ground for decades, and it remains one of the richest fossil sites in the Southwest. For residents, it is a rare chance to live beside a piece of deep natural history that most Las Vegas neighborhoods can only read about. ## Hiking and Visiting Tule Springs Fossil Beds The main way to explore is the Aliante Loop Temporary Trail, a dirt path of about 3.25 miles rated easy to moderate. Virtual interpretive stops along the route explain the fossils beneath your feet, while wide views open toward the Las Vegas Range. Facilities are limited and undeveloped, so bring water and sun protection, since shade is scarce along the way. The monument is open for day use from dawn to dusk, and its quiet trails feel a world away from the city even though Silverstone Ranch sits minutes down the road. Because the land stays largely natural, every visit feels like a small expedition. It is ideal for curious kids, weekend hikers, and anyone who loves open desert with a story to tell. ## Why It Appeals to Silverstone Ranch Residents For people who live at the edge of the monument, this open space is a daily amenity, not just a bucket list stop. Morning walkers and photographers head out early to catch soft light on the Las Vegas Range, and families use the trail to teach kids about the animals that once lived here. Having 22,650 acres of protected desert as a permanent neighbor also means the northern horizon stays open, which is part of what gives Silverstone Ranch its calm, edge of the valley feel. ## Local Insight When Ryan Rose shows buyers around the northwest valley, the monument is a talking point that surprises people.
