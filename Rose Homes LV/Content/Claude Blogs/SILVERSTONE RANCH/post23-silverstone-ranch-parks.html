# Silverstone Ranch Community Parks & Playgrounds Silverstone Ranch community parks give residents a central family park of about 6 acres plus pocket parks and greenbelts, all filled with playgrounds, sport courts, and shaded walking trails close to home. ## What Silverstone Ranch Parks Offer Silverstone Ranch parks are maintained by the HOA and anchored by a central family park of roughly 6 acres. Around it sit several community pocket parks and landscaped greenbelts that connect the neighborhoods. Families find playgrounds, picnic areas, and open lawns for weekend play, plus walking and fitness trails for daily strolls. The layout keeps green space within easy reach no matter which part of the community you call home, which is one reason Silverstone Ranch has stayed popular with buyers who want everyday recreation built in. ## Sports and Recreation in Silverstone Ranch Active residents have plenty of choices. The parks include lighted tennis courts, basketball courts, bocce ball and lawn bowling, and three putting greens for golfers who want to sharpen their short game. These amenities make Silverstone Ranch parks a hub for both quiet mornings and social afternoons. Note that the community's former golf course is closed and is not part of these park amenities, so plan your recreation around the maintained parks, courts, greens, and trails instead. Together these spaces support an active, connected lifestyle. Whether you want a playground for the kids or a court for a pickup game, the community keeps it all a short walk away. ## Parks for Families and Daily Walks The everyday value of Silverstone Ranch parks shows up in the small routines. Parents push strollers along the greenbelts, neighbors meet for morning laps on the fitness trails, and kids burn off energy at the playgrounds after school. Because the pocket parks are spread throughout the community rather than tucked into one corner, most homes are only a short walk from grass, shade, and a place to sit. That walkability is a genuine draw for families weighing Silverstone Ranch against other northwest Las Vegas neighborhoods. ## Local Insight Buyers frequently ask Ryan Rose which northwest neighborhoods deliver everyday amenities without a long drive.
