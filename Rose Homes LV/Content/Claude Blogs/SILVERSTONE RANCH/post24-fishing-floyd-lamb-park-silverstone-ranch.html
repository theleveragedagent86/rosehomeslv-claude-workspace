# Fishing at Floyd Lamb Park Near Silverstone Ranch Fishing at Floyd Lamb Park near Silverstone Ranch means casting for rainbow trout at four stocked ponds inside a 680 acre regional park, one of the few urban fishing spots in the northwest valley. ## Fishing at Floyd Lamb Park: What To Know Fishing at Floyd Lamb Park centers on four ponds stocked with rainbow trout, seasonally restocked by the Nevada Department of Wildlife. The ponds sit inside a park of about 680 acres at 9200 Tule Springs Road, right beside Silverstone Ranch. Adults aged 16 and older need a valid Nevada fishing license to cast a line, which you can arrange through the Nevada Department of Wildlife. Entry to the park runs about $6 per vehicle, and gates are generally open from 8am to dusk, so a quick after work trip is easy to pull off. ## Why Anglers Love This Spot This is one of the few urban fishing spots in the northwest valley, so residents rarely have to drive far for a quiet morning on the water. The stocked trout make it beginner friendly, and the shaded lawns, picnic areas, and resident peacocks turn a fishing trip into an easy family outing. Because it sits next to Silverstone Ranch, you can pack the gear, cast a line, and be home for lunch without much planning. For families teaching kids to fish, the calm ponds and short distance from home are hard to beat. It is the kind of amenity that makes northwest living feel a little more like a getaway. ## Planning Your Fishing Trip A little preparation goes a long way at these ponds. Because stocking happens seasonally, the trout action tends to be strongest in the cooler months, so check the Nevada Department of Wildlife for current stocking updates before you load the truck. Bring your license, a valid form of identification, and a few basic setups suited to trout, since the fish here are a manageable size for new anglers. Pack water and shade for warm days, and plan around the park hours so you are not rushing at dusk.
