# Pet-Friendly Living in Silverstone Ranch Silverstone Ranch is a pet friendly community where dog owners enjoy greenbelts, walking and fitness trails, and community parks for daily walks, plus the adjacent 680 acre Floyd Lamb Park for longer outings. ## Is Silverstone Ranch Pet Friendly? Silverstone Ranch is a genuinely pet friendly, dog friendly community in northwest Las Vegas. Residents describe it as an easy place to own a dog, thanks to landscaped greenbelts, walking and fitness trails, and several community parks that make daily walks simple. The connected green space means you rarely run out of room to stretch your dog's legs. For longer adventures, the roughly 680 acre Floyd Lamb Park sits right next door with wide open lawns and shaded paths, giving pets a change of scenery without a long drive. ## Tips for Dog Owners in Silverstone Ranch A few practical notes help pet owners settle in. Standard HOA pet rules apply, so keep dogs leashed on the trails and clean up after them to keep the greenbelts pleasant for everyone. If you own a specific breed or plan to have several pets, verify any number or breed limits directly with the HOA before you move. There is no dedicated fenced dog park confirmed inside Silverstone Ranch, so most owners head to the nearby open space at Floyd Lamb Park when they want a bigger run. Even so, the everyday trails and parks keep most dogs happy close to home. For many buyers with pets, this balance of on site greenbelts and a huge park next door is exactly what they are looking for. ## A Typical Day With Dogs in Silverstone Ranch Life with a dog here settles into a comfortable rhythm. Early risers walk the fitness trails before the desert heat builds, then loop back through the greenbelts that thread between the neighborhoods. Afternoons might mean a shady rest at one of the community parks, and weekends often call for a longer outing at Floyd Lamb Park, where the ponds and open lawns give energetic dogs plenty of room to explore. Because so much green space sits within the community itself, owners can keep most walks short and simple on busy days, then save the bigger adventures for when there is time to roam. ## Local Insight Pet owners are some of Ryan Rose's most common clients in the northwest valley.
