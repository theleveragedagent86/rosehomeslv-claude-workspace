Silverstone Ranch is a family friendly, master planned community in northwest Las Vegas, built for all ages with a central family park, playgrounds, sports courts, and quiet streets next to Floyd Lamb Park. ## Why is Silverstone Ranch good for families? Silverstone Ranch is built as an all ages, family friendly community, and that shows in the daily details. It sits in Centennial Hills, one of the safer parts of the city, with quiet streets that suit kids on bikes and evening walks. Some neighborhoods within the community are gated, adding a layer of privacy that many families appreciate. The master planned layout keeps parks, trails, and schools woven through the area rather than tacked onto the edges. ## What parks and amenities do families get in Silverstone Ranch? Families in Silverstone Ranch get a lot of shared space. The community centers on a roughly 6 acre family park, backed by multiple pocket parks and playgrounds spread across the neighborhoods. Active households can use lighted tennis and basketball courts along with bocce, and greenbelts and walking trails link it all together. Just outside the gates, the 680 acre Floyd Lamb Park adds fishing ponds and open lawns for weekend outings, giving Silverstone Ranch families even more room to roam. ## What schools serve Silverstone Ranch families? Silverstone Ranch families are served by Clark County School District campuses nearby.
