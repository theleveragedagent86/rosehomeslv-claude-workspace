# Silverstone Ranch vs Providence Silverstone Ranch vs Providence comes down to established versus newer. Silverstone Ranch is an older, fully built out community defined by its closed golf course, while Providence is a newer, larger community still adding homes nearby in northwest Las Vegas. ## Silverstone Ranch vs Providence: which community is newer? The clearest difference in Silverstone Ranch vs Providence is age and stage of growth. Silverstone Ranch was built mostly between 2003 and 2009 by Pulte Homes and is now fully built out, with roughly 1,471 to 1,526 homes across 9 neighborhoods. There is no new construction inside the community, so every purchase is a resale. Providence, by contrast, is a newer master planned community developed largely from the mid 2000s onward, and some sections have continued adding homes. If you want a settled, established neighborhood with mature landscaping, Silverstone Ranch leans that way. If newer construction and the option of a more recently built home appeal to you, Providence is worth a close look. For current Providence pricing and inventory, contact Ryan Rose, since those numbers shift and this post sticks to verifiable structural differences. ## How do amenities compare between Silverstone Ranch and Providence? Amenities tell the second half of the Silverstone Ranch vs Providence story. Silverstone Ranch was built around the Silverstone golf course, which closed in 2015. Its clubhouse burned in 2021, and the course sold at auction in May 2025 but remains closed with no approved redevelopment. On the plus side, the community sits next to Floyd Lamb Park, carries a master HOA of about $158 per month, and includes three gated neighborhoods, one of them guard gated. Providence takes a different approach, anchored by parks such as Huckberry and Knickerbocker along with community centers that host neighborhood life. Rather than a golf course, Providence leans on active parks and gathering spaces. Which mix fits you depends on whether you value proximity to open space like Floyd Lamb Park or a network of community parks and centers. ## Which community should you choose? There is no single winner in Silverstone Ranch vs Providence. Choose Silverstone Ranch for an established, built out setting, resale homes in a quiet pocket, and easy access to Floyd Lamb Park. Choose Providence for newer homes and a parks and centers lifestyle.
