# Silverstone Ranch vs Skye Canyon Silverstone Ranch vs Skye Canyon is a choice between established and brand new. Silverstone Ranch is a built out early 2000s community with a closed golf course, while Skye Canyon is newer, still building, with heavy amenity programming. ## Silverstone Ranch vs Skye Canyon: established resale or new construction? The heart of Silverstone Ranch vs Skye Canyon is new versus established. Silverstone Ranch is an early 2000s, master planned community built by Pulte Homes between about 2003 and 2009. It is fully built out, so every home is a resale, with about 1,471 to 1,526 homes across 9 neighborhoods and sizes from roughly 1,348 to 3,853 square feet. Skye Canyon, by contrast, is a newer master planned community with ongoing new construction from multiple builders, which means modern floor plans and the option to buy a recently built home. If you want mature streets, established value, and a home you can tour today, Silverstone Ranch fits. If new construction and current design trends matter more, Skye Canyon is built for that. Location and amenities separate the two in the Silverstone Ranch vs Skye Canyon matchup. Skye Canyon sits at a slightly higher elevation near the 215 and US 95, and it leans hard into an active lifestyle with Skye Canyon Park, the Skye Center fitness facility, and community events such as Skye and Vine. It also has its own Skye Canyon Marketplace anchored by a Smith's Marketplace grocery store. Silverstone Ranch offers a quieter, more established feel. It was built around the Silverstone golf course, which closed in 2015 and remains closed after selling at auction in May 2025. The community sits next to Floyd Lamb Park, carries a master HOA of about $158 per month, and includes three gated neighborhoods, one of them guard gated. So the trade off is programmed events and new amenities in Skye Canyon versus open space and an established layout in Silverstone Ranch. ## Which community is right for you? Silverstone Ranch vs Skye Canyon ultimately depends on your priorities. Pick Silverstone Ranch for established resale homes, proximity to Floyd Lamb Park, and a settled, lower key setting. Pick Skye Canyon for new construction, modern floor plans, and a busy calendar of community events.
