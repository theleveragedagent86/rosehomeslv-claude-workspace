## Golf Course History & Current Status
The **Silverstone Golf Club** was a 270-acre, 27-hole championship course (three nines: Desert, Mountain, Valley) at 8600 Cupp Drive, designed by noted architect **Robert Cupp** (Golf World Architect of the Year, 1992). It opened in the early-to-mid 2000s as the anchor of the master-planned community; more than half the homes front the fairways.

Timeline and status (source-cited):
- **2002:** A recorded agreement/covenant requires the land be operated as a 27-hole golf course unless at least **75% of Silverstone Ranch homeowners** approve an alternative use. (source: https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/buyers-name-revealed-for-shuttered-las-vegas-golf-course-3381175/ ; https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/nv-supreme-court/2001546.html)
- **September 1-4, 2015:** Desert Lifestyles LLC acquired the course and shut it down (widely cited close date Sept 4, 2015), turning off the water. Homeowners sued in 2015. The closure triggered more than a dozen lawsuits. (source: https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/shuttered-las-vegas-golf-course-sold-at-auction-after-history-of-bankruptcies-3376573/ ; https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g45963-d265578-Reviews-Silverstone_Golf_Club-Las_Vegas_Nevada.html)
- **2016-2020:** Stoneridge Parkway LLC bought the course and later filed for bankruptcy; liens accumulated and the grounds deteriorated to a "semi-wilderness state" (residents reported snakes, mice, trash). Stoneridge Parkway LLC v. Silverstone Ranch Community Association reached the Nevada Supreme Court (2019). (source: https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/nv-supreme-court/2001546.html ; https://www.reviewjournal.com/local/local-las-vegas/homeowners-press-to-reopen-silverstone-golf-course-in-las-vegas/)
- **November 19, 2021:** The ~34,000 sq ft clubhouse was destroyed by an arson fire (est. ~$5M damage; two juveniles arrested). (source: https://www.silverstoneranchhomes.com/golf-course-status ; https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/shuttered-las-vegas-golf-course-sold-at-auction-after-history-of-bankruptcies-3376573/)
- **September 2023:** An arbitration panel / judges ruled that Stoneridge was in breach of its contractual obligation to maintain the course and had failed to pay to restore water and never had the financial ability to maintain it. (source: https://www.reviewjournal.com/local/local-las-vegas/silverstone-ranch-golf-course-owners-broke-contract-judges-rule-2908655/)
- **May 8, 2025:** The property sold at auction for approximately **$2.8 million** (minimum bid), despite more than **$12 million** in recorded liens, to Las Vegas land investor **Khusrow "K" Roohani** (partnered with Dr. Jaswinder Grover; deed recorded under the name Imran Malik). Roohani said he has no plans unless he and his partner agree on a concept and pledged to consult the community before submitting any project. (source: https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/buyers-name-revealed-for-shuttered-las-vegas-golf-course-3381175/ , article dated June 4, 2025)
- **Most recent status (late 2025):** Course **remains closed and unmaintained; no redevelopment application has been filed** with the City of Las Vegas or the HOA. The 75% homeowner-approval deed restriction still governs any change of use. (source: https://www.silverstoneranchhomes.com/golf-course-status , dated Nov 2025)

CAUTION: One HOA-fee page phrases it as the golf course having "closed May 2025 pending redevelopment" - that conflates the May 2025 auction sale with the actual 2015 closure and is misleading. Use the 2015 closure / 2025 sale framing above.

## Real Estate Overview
- **Price Range:** Roughly low $300Ks (townhomes/entry) up to low $700Ks+ for larger 5-6 bedroom homes. Older marketing lists "low $200Ks" but that is dated. (source: https://www.brownellteamrealtors.com/northwest-las-vegas-nv/silverstone-ranch/ ; https://neighborhoodsinlasvegas.com/silverstone-ranch/)
- **Median Price:** **$562K** median sale price, September 2025, up 3.5% YoY; median $262/sqft (up 6.5% YoY); ~49 days on market; 14 homes sold that month. (source: https://www.redfin.com/neighborhood/108284/NV/Las-Vegas/Silverstone-Ranch/housing-market - Sept 2025, most recent found). CONFLICT: NeighborhoodScout lists a "median real estate price" of **$873,354** (an estimated all-homes value metric, not recent sale price). Use Redfin's $562K sale figure as the authoritative transactional number; note it is ~10 months old, so refresh from live MLS/Redfin before publishing. (source: https://www.neighborhoodscout.com/nv/las-vegas/silverstone-ranch)
- **Home Styles:** One- and two-story single-family homes, townhomes (Pinehurst), and larger estate homes; open floor plans, 3-car garages common, many with pools/covered patios/built-in BBQs. Sizes approx **1,348-3,853 sq ft** (Pulte plans); other sources cite 1,559-3,448 and up to ~4,000 sq ft.
- **Lot Sizes:** Not found (specific figures) - many fairway-facing lots; verify per listing.
- **Year(s) Built:** ~**2003-2009** (Pulte build-out).
- **Builders:** Original developer/builder **Pulte Homes** (Princeville Collection and others), 2003-2009. No current large-scale new construction inside Silverstone Ranch itself (it is essentially built out); new-construction activity is in adjacent Centennial Hills / Skye Canyon (e.g., Century Communities). (source: http://www.act4u.com/newhomes/newerhomes/Silverstone_Ranch_Princeville.html ; https://therealestateguylv.com/communities/las-vegas/silverstone-ranch/)
- **New Construction:** No - resale community, built out. Nearby new-build options in Skye Canyon/Centennial Hills.
- **Gated:** Partial - **3 of 9 neighborhoods are gated** (one, The Palms, is guard-gated). Overall ~1,471-1,526 homes across 9 neighborhoods (Amberly, Clairbrook, Eastpoint, Greenfield, Parkfield, Somerset, plus Tuscany, Cottages, Pinehurst, The Palms named across sources). CONFLICT on home count: 1,471 (Pulte figure) vs 1,526 (FirstService/guide site) - record both.

## Lifestyle & Location
- **Drive to Strip:** ~25-35 min (guide site: 28 min; other sources 30-35 min at peak); ~20 miles.
- **Drive to Airport (Harry Reid Intl):** ~32 min (35-40 min peak).
- **Drive to Downtown Las Vegas:** ~22-25 min; Downtown Summerlin ~18 min. RTC CX Centennial Express connects Centennial Hills Transit Center to downtown/Strip.
- **Walk Score:** Not found (no published neighborhood-specific score located). Area is car-dependent - NeighborhoodScout notes 88.1% of residents drive alone; most commutes 15-30 min one-way.
- **Nearest Hospital:** Centennial Hills Hospital Medical Center (226-bed) nearby; also MountainView Hospital (~20 min south), VA NW Primary Care Clinic, Elite Medical Center Centennial (freestanding ER).
- **Safety:** No Silverstone-Ranch-specific police stats found. Surrounding Centennial Hills is one of the safer city areas - ~79th percentile for safety, crime ~30-40% below citywide; violent-crime odds ~1 in 239, property-crime ~1 in 27 (CrimeGrade/AreaVibes). City-data lists an 89131 neighborhood crime index of 278.9 (2024) vs US avg 189.5 - note city-data's index runs opposite in scale; treat as ZIP-level and directional only. Use official sources for any published claim.
- **Pet Friendly:** Yes - Nextdoor residents cite "dog friendly"; walking trails, greenbelts, adjacent Floyd Lamb Park.
- **Retirement Friendly:** All-ages (not 55+); single-story options exist; quiet/low-crime, but note the shuttered golf course and car-dependence. Appears in NW Las Vegas retirement-community roundups.
- **Notable:** Community identity is tied to the now-closed Robert Cupp golf course and its decade-long legal saga; active resident advocacy ("Saving Silverstone Ranch" Facebook group). Base of the Sheep Mountains, next to Floyd Lamb Park and Tule Springs Fossil Beds. English (23.4%) is the most common cited ancestry; among the top ~15% highest-income neighborhoods in the U.S. 89131 ZIP: pop 50,731; median HH income $116,468 (2024); median age 42.
