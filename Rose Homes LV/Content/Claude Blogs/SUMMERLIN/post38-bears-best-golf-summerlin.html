## Bear's Best Golf Club Summerlin: Nicklaus Design Bear's Best Las Vegas was a Jack Nicklaus designed public course in Summerlin that recreated 18 of his favorite holes from courses around the world. It closed to public play on June 28, 2025 and is being rebuilt as Amara Golf Club at The Ridges, a private members club. ## What Was the Concept Behind Bear's Best? Bear's Best Las Vegas took a completely original approach to course design. Jack Nicklaus selected 18 of his most iconic holes from courses he designed across the globe and rebuilt them in the foothills of Summerlin. Each hole was a faithful reproduction of the original, giving golfers the chance to play legendary layouts from courses they may never visit otherwise. The course opened in 2001 at 11111 West Flamingo Road, played to a par 72 at 7,194 yards, and worked as a "greatest hits" collection from one of golf's most celebrated architects, all within a single round. ## Is Bear's Best Still Open to the Public? No. The last day of public play at Bear's Best was June 28, 2025. The course no longer takes public tee times, and there is no daily fee option there anymore. If you are looking for public golf in Summerlin, Angel Park Golf Club and Siena Golf Club are the courses to look at instead. Anyone who still sees old tee time listings for Bear's Best is looking at outdated information. ## What Is Replacing Bear's Best? Mulligan Holdings Inc., led by Andrew Pascal and Mike Mixer, bought the property for $30.5 million and is spending up to $300 million to turn it into Amara Golf Club at The Ridges. Amara is planned as a private members only club with club villas and custom homes on the site. Reported timing puts the club reopening in October 2026, with the residences following in 2027. The setting stays the same, near The Ridges with sweeping views of Red Rock Canyon and the Las Vegas skyline, but the access model changes from public to private. ## How Does the Site Compare to Other Summerlin Courses? With Bear's Best off the public list, Angel Park Golf Club carries the everyday public golf load in Summerlin with its two Arnold Palmer courses and a friendlier price point. TPC Summerlin, The Summit Club, and Red Rock Country Club remain the private names in the community. Amara is set to join that private group rather than compete for public rounds, which puts the old Bear's Best land in a different category than it held for the last two decades. ## Local Market Insight According to Ryan Rose of Rose Homes LV, homes near the former Bear's Best and The Ridges attract buyers who care about both golf and high end surroundings.
