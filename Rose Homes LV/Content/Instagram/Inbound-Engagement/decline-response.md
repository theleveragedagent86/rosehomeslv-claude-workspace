# Decline Response — the referral close

Set by Ryan 2026-09-22.

## When to use it

Send this when someone replies to a DM and tells you they are NOT in the market. Typical triggers:

- "No", "Nope", "Not right now", "No plans to move"
- "I just like your content" / "I only liked the post"
- "I already bought" / "Just moved here"
- "I have a realtor already" / "I have my own license"
- Any polite decline, soft or blunt

The point: a no is not a dead end. It is the moment to ask for referrals, because they have nothing to lose by saying yes and people who decline often know somebody who is looking.

## When NOT to use it

- They are a realtor, broker, or agent. Skip entirely, do not pitch referrals to a competitor.
- They said something hostile or annoyed. Thank them once and leave it alone.
- They asked a real question. Answer the question first. This is not a substitute for a real reply.
- They already got this message before. Check dm-history.md. Never send it twice to the same person.
- Do not stack it onto a live lead conversation. It is a closer for a no, not a nudge for a maybe.

## Canonical message

> I am available if you have any questions or anything that has to do with real estate. As a side note, if you do know anyone who is looking to buy or sell a home, give them my info and make sure they tell me you sent them! I like to reward my referral people with a great gift to show my appreciation. The least I can do 🙏👍 Have a great rest of your week!

## Style rules (same as everything else)

- No em-dashes. Use commas, periods, or ellipses.
- One or two emoji max, and only the warm ones already used above.
- Do not add links or hashtags.
- Do not say "as a realtor".
- Keep it as one message, not a chain.

## Logging

After it sends, append to `dm-history.md` with the handle, the date, and which variant was used, so the person is never re-sent a decline response.
