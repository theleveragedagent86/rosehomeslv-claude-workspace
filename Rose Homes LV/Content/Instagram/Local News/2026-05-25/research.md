- **Summary:** The Henderson City Council approved a fiscal year 2026-2027 budget of $1,014,610,205 in spending on May 20, 2026, with projected revenue of $950,683,401. About 60 percent of expenditures, roughly $253 million, are directed toward public safety. The budget adds 14 new patrol officers, a jail administrator, and permanently funds the Crisis Response Team.
- **Why It Matters:** Henderson is running a slight deficit in its general fund while still expanding public safety services, reflecting the difficult fiscal balancing act facing Southern Nevada municipalities.
