## Story 25: Las Vegas Launches a Plan to Move Beyond Tourism

Las Vegas wants to stop leaning so hard on tourism, and there is now a plan to do it.

The Las Vegas Global Economic Alliance, or LVGEA, launched a three-year plan.

The goal is to diversify our economy.

The plan targets five industries.

Those are advanced manufacturing, creative work, business and finance, health and life sciences, and technology.

The aim is to reduce how much we rely on tourism.

There is a clear measuring stick. The plan wants to raise the Hachman index from its current level of 74.

A higher score means a more balanced and diverse economy.

To fund the work, the budget is growing. It rises from $3.8 million to $4.5 million.

Here is the bigger meaning. Tourism is great until it is not.

That sounds harsh. But we learned it the hard way during the pandemic.

When visitors stopped coming, our economy took a brutal hit. A more diverse economy would soften the next downturn.

New industries also bring stable, high-paying jobs. Those jobs help families buy homes and put down roots.

I want to see Vegas grow into more than a tourist town. This plan is a step in that direction.
