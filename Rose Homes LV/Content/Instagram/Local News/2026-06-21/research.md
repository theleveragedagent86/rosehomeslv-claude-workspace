### 2. New Federal Study Shows Lake Mead Headed Nearly 29 Feet Below All-Time Low
- Category: Government and Development | Area: Boulder City / Clark County | Viral: Red
- Source: Las Vegas Review-Journal | URL: https://www.reviewjournal.com/news/environment/lake-meads-slow-demise-just-sped-up-in-latest-federal-study-3838754/ | Date: June 15, 2026
- Summary: Bureau of Reclamation study projects Lake Mead at 1,015.77 ft by July 2027 and a record-low 1,011.74 ft by May 2028, nearly 29 ft below the 2022 record. Worst snow season on record, plus federal decision to prop up Lake Powell. ~29% full at ~1,047 ft mid-June.
