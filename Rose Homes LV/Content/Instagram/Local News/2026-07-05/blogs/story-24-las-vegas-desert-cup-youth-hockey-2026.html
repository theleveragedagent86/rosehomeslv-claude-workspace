12th Annual Las Vegas Desert Cup Youth Hockey Tournament Set for July 2-5 at America First Center

The 12th Annual Las Vegas Desert Cup is coming to Henderson. This is a youth hockey tournament. It is set for July 2 through 5, 2026. The games take place at America First Center in Henderson. That building is the home rink of the Henderson Silver Knights. The tournament runs right over the Independence Day weekend.

The Desert Cup brings youth hockey teams to the valley. Many of these teams travel from out of town. They come to play, to compete, and to have fun on the ice. Young players from many places will lace up their skates here. Their families come with them. That means hotel stays, meals out, and shopping in Henderson.

This is the 12th time the event has been held. That long run shows how much the tournament has grown. It also shows how much youth hockey has grown in Southern Nevada. Not long ago, hockey was rare in the desert. Today it is a real part of life in the valley.

Background and History

Let me give some background on how we got here. For most of its history, Las Vegas was not a hockey town. The desert and ice do not seem to go together. Hockey was rare in Southern Nevada for a long time. There were few rinks and few youth teams. That has changed in a big way.

The turning point came in 2017. That year, the Vegas Golden Knights began play. They were the first major pro sports team in the valley. They won right away and drew huge crowds. The whole city fell in love with hockey. That love did not stop with the pro team. It spread to kids and families all over the valley.

After the Golden Knights arrived, youth hockey took off. More kids wanted to play. More families signed up. New rinks and programs followed to meet the demand. The Henderson Silver Knights added to this growth. As the top minor league partner of the Golden Knights, they gave the area another pro team to cheer for. Their home, America First Center, became a hub for hockey in Henderson.

The Desert Cup fits right into this story. This is the 12th year of the tournament. That means it started well before the Golden Knights arrived. In a way, an event like this helped lay the groundwork for the hockey boom. It gave young players a place to compete year after year. As interest in hockey grew, a tournament like this had room to grow too.
