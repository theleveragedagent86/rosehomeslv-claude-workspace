Golden Knights to Host Battle-Worn Authentics Equipment Sale at City National Arena

The Vegas Golden Knights will hold their annual Battle-Worn Authentics equipment sale on July 22 at City National Arena in Summerlin, and fans can walk out with real game-used team gear. This is the one day each year when jerseys, sticks, gloves, and other pieces that actually saw ice time go straight to the people who cheer the loudest.

If you have ever wanted to own something a Golden Knights player wore or used during a season, this is the event to circle. It happens right here in the northwest valley, at the team's own practice home, and it draws hockey fans from all over Clark County.

What Happened

According to FOX5, the Vegas Golden Knights announced their yearly Battle-Worn Authentics equipment sale for Tuesday, July 22, 2026. The sale takes place at City National Arena, the team's practice facility in the Downtown Summerlin area of the northwest valley. The event lets fans buy authentic gear that players actually used, not replica items made for a store shelf.

The Battle-Worn label is the key detail here. These are pieces that came off the ice after real games and practices. Think game-used sticks, worn gloves, helmets, pads, and other equipment tied to the team. Each item carries the wear and tear of an NHL season, which is exactly what makes it special to a collector or a die-hard fan.

This is an annual tradition for the club, not a one-time thing. Every summer the Golden Knights clear out gear from the season that just ended and put it in front of the fan base. The sale gives supporters a rare chance to own a real piece of the team's history at a set price rather than fighting through an online auction.

The range of items at a sale like this can be wide. Fans often find game-used sticks, gloves, helmets, skates, pads, jerseys, and practice gear, along with other team pieces. Prices vary depending on the item and any player connection tied to it. A stick used by a star can carry a higher price than a piece of practice equipment, but there are usually options at different price points so a range of fans can walk away with something. Part of the fun is not knowing exactly what you will find until you get there and start looking.

City National Arena sits just off the 215 Beltway near Downtown Summerlin, which makes it easy to reach from Summerlin, Centennial Hills, Spring Valley, and the rest of the west side. The building is where the team practices during the season, so fans who show up are stepping into the same space the players use. That connection is part of the draw. You are not buying gear from a warehouse. You are buying it from the team's own house.

The appeal of game-used gear comes down to authenticity. A store-bought jersey is nice, but a Battle-Worn piece carries a story. It might have scuff marks from the boards, tape still wrapped on a stick handle, or the small dents and scratches that only come from real NHL action. Collectors value that history, and casual fans love the idea of owning something that was part of the team's season. For many people who attend, the goal is not just to buy something. It is to bring home a piece of the Golden Knights they can keep for years.

Events like this also tend to have a friendly, low-pressure feel. Fans wander the floor, chat with other supporters, and take their time deciding what to grab. It is the kind of gathering where you might bump into a neighbor from Summerlin or run into a coworker who is just as into hockey as you are. That community vibe is a big part of why the sale keeps drawing crowds each summer.

You can bring the kids, walk the floor, and look at real gear up close. For young hockey fans in Summerlin and the northwest, seeing and holding equipment a pro used can be a genuine thrill. It turns the team from something on TV into something you can touch.

Background and History

City National Arena opened in 2017 as the practice home of the Vegas Golden Knights. It was one of the first big pieces of the team's arrival in the valley, built in the Downtown Summerlin district before the franchise even played its first NHL game. The facility features two ice sheets, public skating, youth and adult hockey programs, team stores, and a restaurant, so it stays busy year round and not just on game days.

The Golden Knights themselves have a short but loud history. They stunned the sports world in their very first season by reaching the Stanley Cup Final, and they later brought home a championship. That instant success created one of the most passionate fan bases in the league. Vegas embraced the team fast, and the connection between the club and the community has only grown since then.

Battle-Worn Authentics is the team's own program for selling and verifying game-used items. The idea is simple. Instead of tossing or storing gear at the end of a season, the team makes it available to fans who want a real piece of the action. The annual in-person sale at City National Arena has become a favorite summer stop for collectors and families alike. It is a tradition that fits the do-it-big spirit of Vegas hockey.

The timing lines up with a busy hockey summer in the valley. The Golden Knights have been active in the offseason, including a recent trade that sent longtime forward Keegan Kolesar to the Detroit Red Wings for draft picks. The Henderson Silver Knights, the team's American Hockey League affiliate, just dropped their 2026-27 schedule. And youth hockey is in full swing, with the Vegas Summer Showdown tournament bringing teams to City National Arena and other local rinks. The equipment sale is one more sign that hockey never really goes quiet here.
