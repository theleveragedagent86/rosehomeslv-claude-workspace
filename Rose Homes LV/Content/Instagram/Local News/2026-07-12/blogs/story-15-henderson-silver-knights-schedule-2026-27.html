Henderson Silver Knights Announce Their 2026-27 Regular Season Schedule

The Henderson Silver Knights just dropped their 2026-27 regular season schedule, and the big date to circle is October 3. That is opening night at Lee's Family Forum in Henderson, the start of a full 72-game American Hockey League slate. If you are a hockey family in the valley, you can now start planning your fall and winter around home games.

The Silver Knights are the top minor-league affiliate of the Vegas Golden Knights. That means the players skating in Henderson this season are the ones fighting for a spot on the NHL roster. For fans, it is pro hockey close to home, at a price that is a lot easier on the family budget than a night on the Strip.

What Happened

On July 9, 2026, the Henderson Silver Knights released their full 2026-27 regular season schedule. The season runs the standard American Hockey League length of 72 games. That breaks down into 36 home games and 36 road games across the fall, winter, and early spring. The home opener is set for Saturday, October 3, at Lee's Family Forum in Henderson.

Lee's Family Forum is the team's home arena in Henderson, just off the 215 Beltway near the Green Valley area. The building holds around 6,000 fans for hockey. That smaller size is part of the charm. There is not a bad seat in the house, and you are close enough to the ice to hear the hits and the skates carving the surface. It is a very different feel from the giant NHL barns.

The Silver Knights play in the AHL's Pacific Division, which is packed with West Coast affiliate teams. That includes clubs tied to NHL organizations across California, Arizona, and the Pacific Northwest. Those shorter travel distances mean more regional rivalries and more weeknight and weekend games that line up well for local families who do not want a super late night.

The team has not confirmed every promotional night, giveaway, or theme game for the full season yet. Those details usually roll out closer to the start of the year. What fans have right now is the game-by-game calendar, which is enough to start picking the dates that work best. Season ticket plans, partial plans, and single-game tickets are the three main ways to lock in seats. A 72-game schedule is a marathon, not a sprint. Games are spread across roughly six months, from early October through the middle of April. Most weeks bring a mix of home and away dates, with clusters of Friday and Saturday home games that are made for families. Those weekend nights are the ones that fill up fastest, so fans who want the best seats tend to plan around them early. Weeknight games can be a quieter, easier ticket if you do not mind a school-night outing.

The AHL regular season leads into the Calder Cup Playoffs in the spring for teams that qualify. That means a strong Silver Knights season could stretch well past the final April date on the regular schedule. For fans, that is a bonus. A deep playoff run keeps hockey in Henderson alive when the Golden Knights are chasing their own postseason at T-Mobile Arena. It is possible to follow both teams through the spring without ever leaving the valley.

For a lot of Henderson and Las Vegas families, this schedule drop is the real start of hockey season planning. Golden Knights tickets at T-Mobile Arena can get expensive fast, especially for a family of four with parking and snacks. For families in Inspirada, Cadence, Seven Hills, and Whitney Ranch, this is a short drive, not a trek across town. Live pro sports in your own backyard is one of those quality-of-life perks that people do not always think about until they have it.

Many young players study the pros up close, then head to their own rinks with new moves to try. A local team gives that whole community a shared home base and a season to look forward to together.

Background and History

The Henderson Silver Knights joined the American Hockey League in 2020 as the top farm team of the Vegas Golden Knights. The idea was simple. Keep the Golden Knights' best young prospects close to Las Vegas so the big club could call players up quickly and keep a close eye on their growth. Having the affiliate a short drive away instead of across the country is a real edge for player development.

In their early years, the Silver Knights played home games at the Orleans Arena while their permanent home was being built. That changed when the team moved into its own arena in Henderson. The building has since taken on the Lee's Family Forum name, giving the franchise a true home base built for hockey and for families. Having a dedicated arena helped the team plant deeper roots in the community.

The American Hockey League is the main stepping stone to the NHL. Nearly every player in the NHL spent time in the AHL first. That is what makes these games fun to watch. You are seeing tomorrow's Golden Knights today. A player who lights it up in Henderson this winter could be scoring at T-Mobile Arena by spring. Fans who follow both teams get to watch that whole journey unfold.

The Golden Knights organization has built one of the most successful young franchises in pro sports, including a Stanley Cup. That winning culture flows down to Henderson. The Silver Knights are coached and managed with the same standards, and the pipeline between the two clubs stays busy all season long. When Vegas needs a fresh body due to injury, Henderson is the first phone call.

What Happens Next

The next step for fans is picking a ticket plan. Season plans give you every home game and usually the best per-game price. Partial plans and flex packs work well for families who cannot make all 36 dates but still want a handful of nights locked in. Single-game tickets go on sale ahead of the season for people who just want to grab a game or two here and there.

Between now and the October 3 opener, expect the team to announce its promotional schedule. That usually includes giveaway nights, theme games, and special events like youth hockey days and community nights. Those dates tend to sell fastest, so fans who care about a specific giveaway or theme should watch the team's channels once those details drop closer to the season.

On the ice, training camp and the preseason will set the roster. Some names on the opening-night lineup will be familiar returners. Others will be fresh draft picks and signings trying to prove they belong. By the time the puck drops in early October, the Silver Knights will have a clearer picture of which prospects are ready to push for a call-up to Vegas. That competition is part of the fun of following a development team.

Fans should also keep an eye on how the schedule sets up the season's rhythm. Long road trips can test a young roster, while a run of home games can build momentum in front of the Henderson crowd. Coaches often talk about the importance of a strong start, and opening the season at home on October 3 gives the Silver Knights an early chance to set the tone. A good October can carry a team a long way through the winter grind.

It is worth watching the Golden Knights schedule alongside the Silver Knights one, too. When Vegas is on a long road trip, some fans fill those hockey-free nights with a trip to Henderson. The two calendars often complement each other, giving the valley live pro hockey on more nights than either team could offer alone. For a household that loves the sport, that overlap is a real perk of living here.

What You Can Do

If you want in on the season, start at the team's official website to browse the full 2026-27 schedule and pick your dates. From there you can compare season plans, partial plans, and single-game options to see what fits your family and your budget. Mark October 3 first, since opening night at Lee's Family Forum is always one of the best atmospheres of the year.

For families new to hockey, do not overthink it. Grab tickets to one game, get there a little early, and let the kids soak up the energy of a live arena. Watch how the fans around you follow the action, and you will pick up the flow quickly. A single night can turn a curious kid into a lifelong fan, and Silver Knights games are a friendly, affordable place to test the waters.

It also helps to plan the logistics ahead of time. Lee's Family Forum sits near the 215 Beltway, so arriving a little early on weekend nights saves you the parking crunch. Bring layers, since arenas run cold, and check the team's app or website for the latest on gates, bag rules, and mobile tickets. A few minutes of planning turns a good night into an easy one, especially with kids in tow.

Season ticket holders often get the best perks, from early access to playoff seats to member-only events and discounts. If your family knows it will go to more than a handful of games, running the math on a plan usually pays off. For everyone else, flex packs let you split games with friends and neighbors. Sharing a plan across two or three households is a smart way to keep the cost down while still catching plenty of hockey.
