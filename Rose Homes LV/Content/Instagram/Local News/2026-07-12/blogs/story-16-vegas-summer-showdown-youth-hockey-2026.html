Vegas Summer Showdown Youth Hockey Tournament Comes to City National Arena and America First Center

Youth hockey takes over Las Vegas from July 16 to July 20, 2026, when the Vegas Summer Showdown brings teams of young players to two of the valley's best rinks. Games run for five straight days at City National Arena in Summerlin and America First Center in Henderson, and the whole thing is free to watch.

This is a family event first and a hockey event second. Parents, siblings, grandparents, and neighbors are all welcome to pack the stands, cheer on the kids, and see what makes Las Vegas one of the fastest growing hockey towns in the country. If you have never set foot inside a local ice rink, this is the perfect weekend to change that.

What Is the Vegas Summer Showdown

The Vegas Summer Showdown is a youth hockey tournament that runs from July 16 through July 20, 2026. It brings youth teams from around the region to the Las Vegas valley for several days of games. The tournament is organized through TCS Hockey, a group that runs travel and tournament hockey events across the country.

Games are split between two rinks that Vegas hockey fans already know well. The first is City National Arena in Summerlin, which is the official practice home of the Vegas Golden Knights. The second is America First Center in Henderson, a newer sheet of ice on the south side of the valley. Splitting the tournament across both rinks lets more teams play more games in a short window.

Summer tournaments like this one usually group teams by age and skill level. You will see squads made up of players in different birth-year brackets, from younger kids just learning the game to older teens who play at a competitive travel level. Each team is guaranteed a set number of games, and many tournaments end with a playoff round and a championship game on the final day.

Because this runs in the middle of July, the timing is smart. Summer is the offseason for the pro teams, so the rinks have open ice. Families who travel in for the event get to turn a hockey weekend into a Las Vegas trip, and local families get a low cost way to spend a hot afternoon indoors on the cool side of the glass.

Why It Matters to Las Vegas Residents

Hockey is a bigger deal in Las Vegas than a lot of people outside the valley realize. Since the Vegas Golden Knights arrived in 2017 and won the Stanley Cup in 2023, the sport has taken deep root here. Youth hockey registration has climbed, new rinks have opened, and more local kids are lacing up skates than ever before. A tournament like the Vegas Summer Showdown is proof of how far that growth has come.

For families, this event is a chance to spend real time together without spending a lot of money. Watching youth games is free, the arenas are air conditioned, and there is something happening on the ice all day long. In a summer where afternoon highs push past 105 degrees, a cool rink is one of the best places in town to be. You can drop in for an hour or stay for the whole afternoon.

The tournament also puts a spotlight on two neighborhoods that a lot of homebuyers are watching. Summerlin, home to City National Arena, is one of the most sought after master planned communities in the valley. Henderson, home to America First Center, keeps landing on national lists of the best places to live in America. Both areas have built family friendly amenities, and youth sports are a big part of that story.

There is also a quiet economic side to this. Out of town teams book hotel rooms, eat at local restaurants, and shop while they are here. Youth sports tourism brings real dollars into Clark County, and events like this help fill the summer calendar between the big conventions and the fall sports season. When families travel here for a tournament, they often start picturing what it would be like to live here too.

Background and History of Vegas Hockey

Las Vegas was not always a hockey town. For decades the valley had only a handful of small rinks, and the sport lived on the edges of the local sports scene. That changed fast when the Golden Knights joined the National Hockey League as an expansion team in 2017. The team's surprise run to the Stanley Cup Final in its very first season turned casual fans into diehards almost overnight.

City National Arena opened in 2017 in Downtown Summerlin as the Golden Knights practice facility. It holds two full sheets of ice and quickly became a hub for youth and adult leagues, learn to skate programs, and public skating. Families across the valley have watched their kids take first steps on the ice at that very building, and many of the teams in this tournament trace their roots to programs that grew there.

Henderson joined the hockey map in a big way when the Henderson Silver Knights, the Golden Knights minor league affiliate, set up in the city. America First Center added another quality sheet of ice on the south end of the valley, giving families in Green Valley, Inspirada, Cadence, and Anthem a closer place to play. More rinks means more open ice, and more open ice means events like the Summer Showdown can actually happen here.

The valley has hosted a growing list of youth and amateur tournaments over the past few years. Each one builds on the last. Coaches, referees, rink staff, and volunteer parents have all gained experience running these events. That local know how is a big reason travel organizations feel comfortable bringing their tournaments to Las Vegas instead of a bigger, older hockey market.

What Happens Next

The tournament opens on Thursday, July 16, and runs through Monday, July 20. Expect the first games each day to start in the morning, with games running through the afternoon and into the evening across both rinks. The exact schedule, brackets, and game times get posted by the organizer, so check the TCS Hockey page for the Vegas Summer Showdown for the latest details before you head out.

If you plan to attend, it helps to pick your rink ahead of time. City National Arena sits inside Downtown Summerlin near the 215 Beltway on the west side of the valley. America First Center is in Henderson on the southeast side. The two are a solid drive apart, so most families pick the rink where their team or their neighborhood favorite is playing rather than trying to bounce between both in one day.

Beyond this single event, the summer hockey calendar in Las Vegas keeps filling up. The Golden Knights host their annual Battle-Worn Authentics equipment sale on July 22 at City National Arena, right after the tournament wraps. The Henderson Silver Knights just released their 2026-27 regular season schedule, with opening night set for October 3. Youth hockey families have a busy few months ahead.

What You Can Do

If you want to catch the action, the Vegas Summer Showdown runs July 16 through July 20 at City National Arena in Summerlin and America First Center in Henderson. Watching youth games is free, so you can just show up, find a seat, and enjoy. Bring a light jacket, because the rinks stay cool, and plan to grab a snack from the concession stand while you are there.

Check the TCS Hockey website for the Vegas Summer Showdown to find the game schedule, the bracket, and start times before you go. If you know a family with a player in the tournament, ask which rink and which time slot they play, then plan your visit around their games. Cheering on a kid you know makes the whole day more fun.

If your own child wants to try hockey, this is a great weekend to spark that interest. Both arenas run learn to skate and youth hockey programs during the year, and watching real games up close is a low pressure way to see if it clicks. Talk to rink staff while you are there, and they can point you to the right starter program for your child's age.
