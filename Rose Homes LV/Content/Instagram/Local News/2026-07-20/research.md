## 2. A Federal Shortcut Approved a Boulder City Data Center and Shut the Public Out
- **Category:** Government and Development | **Story Type:** Local | **Viral Score:** Red | **Extremely Important:** No
- **County/Area:** Boulder City
- **Summary:** In late June, the Bureau of Land Management granted Townsite Solar 2, a subsidiary of a Texas-based hedge fund, an amendment to a 2023 solar farm permit that lets it build a data center on federal land instead. Federal officials used a "determination of NEPA adequacy," ruling the old solar environmental review covered the data center too, which shut out public comment. Boulder City loses an estimated $2.3 million in annual revenue it would have earned from a city land lease. - **Why It Matters:** If this maneuver holds up, developers anywhere in Clark County could bypass local government by building on nearby federal land.
- **Related context (not a separate selected story):** On July 14 the Boulder City Council voted unanimously to appeal the approval to the Interior Department's Board of Land Appeals and requested a stay. City Attorney Brittany Walker said, "We were not involved in a collaborative way in the process of looking at the application at all."
