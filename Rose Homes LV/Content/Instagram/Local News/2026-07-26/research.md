### 1. CCSD Reaches $1.5M Settlement Over Dyslexia and Foster Care Support
- **Category:** School Board and Education | **Story Type:** Local | **Area:** Clark County
- **Viral Score:** Red | **Extremely Important:** Yes | **Flag:** CONTROVERSIAL
- **Date:** July 22, 2026 (follow-up coverage through July 25)
- **Source:** FOX5 Vegas (also Las Vegas Sun, News3LV, KTNV) | https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/07/22/legal-aid-center-ccsd-reach-settlement-expand-dyslexia-screening-foster-care-support/
- **Summary:** CCSD and the Legal Aid Center of Southern Nevada announced a settlement July 22 resolving more than 20 due process complaints alleging the district failed to give required reading instruction to dyslexic students, many in foster care. Separate funds totaling $1.5 million go over four years to 23 named students in grades 3 through 12. Districtwide, CCSD agreed to screen any student who scores below benchmark on interim reading assessments for dyslexia indicators regardless of age, and to audit academic plans for all foster students before the 2026-27 school year.
- **Why It Matters:** New dyslexia screening rules could touch tens of thousands of struggling readers across the district, and the case exposes how CCSD handled its most vulnerable students.

### 3. NV Energy Rolls Out Daily Demand Charge and $63M in Refunds
- **Category:** Government and Development | **Story Type:** Local | **Area:** Southern Nevada (Clark County utility service area)
- **Viral Score:** Red | **Extremely Important:** No
- **Date:** July 24, 2026
- **Source:** News3LV (also The Nevada Independent, KTNV) | https://news3lv.com/news/local/as-energy-costs-remain-a-concern-nv-energy-explains-new-billing-change
- **Summary:** NV Energy is moving ahead with a new daily demand charge that takes effect January 1, 2027, after community opposition pushed the start date back from earlier this year. Customers will be billed based on a 15-minute peak of their highest daily usage, at about 14 cents per kilowatt, which the utility estimates at roughly $15 to $20 per month, offset by lower basic and volumetric rates. The company is also issuing about $63 million in refunds tied to past mischarges on some multifamily accounts.
- **Why It Matters:** This changes how nearly every Southern Nevada household is billed for power, so families will want to understand it before it starts.
