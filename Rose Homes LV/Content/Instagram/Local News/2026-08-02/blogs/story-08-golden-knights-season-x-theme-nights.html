Golden Knights Unveil Season X Theme Nights for Their 10th Anniversary

The Vegas Golden Knights just unveiled their Season X theme nights, a full slate of special games built to celebrate the team's 10th anniversary at T-Mobile Arena. This is the milestone season fans have been waiting for, and the team is marking it with five Anniversary Knights, a Nevada Day tribute, a Military Night, a Kids Day, and a first-ever crossover night with PWHL Las Vegas.

If you have followed this team since the 2017 expansion draft, this season is your season. The Golden Knights are turning a full year of games into a decade-long thank-you to the city that adopted them from day one. Here is the full breakdown of what is coming and why it matters to Las Vegas.

What Happened

On July 30, 2026, the Vegas Golden Knights announced their Season X theme nights, a year-long campaign to honor the team's first decade. The team calls this "Season X" because X is the Roman numeral for ten. The whole plan centers on T-Mobile Arena, the team's home on the Las Vegas Strip since the very first puck dropped.

The headline piece is a group of five Anniversary Knights. Each one pays tribute to a different era of the franchise. The lineup runs like this. "Welcome to Impossible Knight" celebrates Season I on Saturday, October 10 against the Los Angeles Kings. "Vegas Born Knight" honors Seasons II through V on Wednesday, December 16 against the San Jose Sharks. "Misfits to Champions Knight" marks the Season VI Stanley Cup run on Saturday, February 13 against the Calgary Flames. "The Golden Age Knight" covers Seasons VII through IX on Monday, March 15 against the Winnipeg Jets. And "Fan Appreciation Knight" closes out Season X on Saturday, April 10 against the Los Angeles Kings.

The team is not stopping at the anniversary games. The Golden Knights plan to host 14 theme nights in total this season, plus a set of Collaboration Knights that team up with other clubs, leagues, and big sporting events. Fan-favorite nights are back on the calendar. Nevada Day lands on October 30. Military Night is set for November 11, which is Veterans Day. Hockey Fights Cancer runs November 13. Kids Day is November 28. And a VGK x PWHL Las Vegas night is scheduled for December 5.

That December 5 crossover is a big deal. It ties the Golden Knights to PWHL Las Vegas, the new professional women's hockey team that will also call T-Mobile Arena home. It is a sign that hockey in this town is growing into something bigger than one franchise.

Why It Matters to Las Vegas Residents

The Golden Knights are more than a hockey team here. They are woven into the identity of the city. When the team arrived in 2017, it gave Las Vegas its first major pro sports franchise. For a lot of families in Summerlin, Henderson, and Spring Valley, going to a game at T-Mobile Arena became a shared tradition. This anniversary season turns that shared history into a full calendar of reasons to show up.

Theme nights are also a smart way to make games feel special and personal. Nevada Day on October 30 celebrates the state itself, and it lands right around the holiday that marks Nevada joining the Union. Military Night on November 11 honors the huge number of veterans and active service members who live across the valley, from Nellis Air Force Base families to retirees in Boulder City. Kids Day on November 28 gives parents a low-key reason to bring the whole family downtown during the holiday stretch.

There is a real economic angle too. When T-Mobile Arena fills up, the whole surrounding area feels it. Restaurants, bars, rideshare drivers, and hotels on the south end of the Strip all benefit from packed game nights. A year built around special events means more of those nights, and that ripples out to the people who work in and around the entertainment corridor.

For homeowners, the Golden Knights are part of what makes Las Vegas a place people want to live, not just visit. A strong sports scene helps neighborhoods hold their appeal. When buyers ask me what there is to do here, the Knights come up almost every time. A team celebrating ten years of roots in the community is exactly the kind of thing that makes a city feel like home.

Background and History

The Vegas Golden Knights began play in the 2017 to 2018 season as an NHL expansion team. Almost nobody expected much. Expansion teams are supposed to struggle for years. Instead, the Golden Knights stunned the hockey world by reaching the Stanley Cup Final in their very first season. That "Misfits" run turned casual fans into diehards overnight and gave the whole city something to rally around.

The team kept building from there. Over the next several seasons, the Golden Knights became a regular playoff club and one of the more consistent franchises in the league. Then came the payoff. The team won the Stanley Cup in 2023, its Season VI, giving Las Vegas its first major pro sports championship. That title is exactly what the "Misfits to Champions Knight" on February 13 is built to celebrate.

T-Mobile Arena has been the backdrop for all of it. The arena opened in 2016 near the south end of the Strip, and it became the Golden Knights' home from the start. Over ten years it has hosted playoff runs, a Cup celebration, and countless sold-out crowds. Naming the whole season after the arena's decade of memories makes sense, because so much of the team's story happened inside those walls.

The addition of PWHL Las Vegas adds a fresh chapter. Pro women's hockey coming to the same building shows how far the sport has come in this market in just ten years. The December 5 crossover night is the team acknowledging that the next decade of Vegas hockey is going to be bigger and broader than the first.

What Happens Next

The season builds through the fall and winter, with the theme nights spread across the calendar so there is almost always something coming up. The first Anniversary Knight, "Welcome to Impossible Knight," tips off October 10 against the Kings. From there the special nights roll out month by month, closing with Fan Appreciation Knight on April 10.

Fans who want to attend a specific theme night should plan early. Games against rivals like the Kings and the Sharks tend to draw bigger crowds, and the anniversary framing will only add to demand. Tickets for the marquee nights, especially the Cup-celebrating game in February and the fan appreciation finale in April, are likely to move fast. If a certain night matters to your family, it is worth locking in seats before the schedule fills out.

Expect the team to keep adding details as the season nears. The Golden Knights said the campaign will include unique fan experiences, community-driven programs, and Collaboration Knights alongside the theme nights already announced. Watch the team's official channels for giveaways, special jerseys, and promotions tied to each night. Those extras are usually where the anniversary theme really comes to life.

Ryan's Take

As someone who lives and works in this valley, I see the Golden Knights as one of the best things that has happened to Las Vegas in the last decade. When people talk about what has changed here, they mention the stadiums, the teams, and the sense that Las Vegas finally has its own identity beyond the casinos. The Knights kicked that off, and this anniversary season is a reminder of how quickly they became part of who we are.

From a real estate view, a mature sports scene is a quiet selling point that never shows up on a listing sheet. Buyers moving here from other cities want to know the place has a real community, not just tourists. A hometown team celebrating ten years, with theme nights for veterans, kids, and the state itself, tells that story better than any brochure. It is the kind of thing that keeps neighborhoods desirable and helps this city feel like a place worth putting down roots.

What You Can Do

If you want to be part of the anniversary season, start by checking the Golden Knights' official schedule and picking the theme nights that fit your family. Nevada Day on October 30, Military Night on November 11, and Kids Day on November 28 are great low-pressure options if you are bringing kids or first-time fans. The five Anniversary Knights are the ones to circle if you want the full 10-year celebration experience.

Buy tickets early for the games you care about, and keep an eye on the team's social channels for special promotions, giveaways, and jersey drops tied to each night. If you have never been to a game, this is a perfect season to start. The energy at T-Mobile Arena during a theme night is hard to beat, and the anniversary framing makes even a regular-season game feel like an event.

You can also support the growth of hockey in the valley by checking out the VGK x PWHL Las Vegas crossover on December 5. Pro women's hockey is new to this city, and turning out for that night helps show there is an audience for it. Ten years in, the best way to celebrate the Knights is the same way it started, by filling the building and being loud.
