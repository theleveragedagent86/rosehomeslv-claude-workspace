Las Vegas Ice Center Fall Youth House League Signups Close September 1

Families in Las Vegas have a hard deadline coming up fast. Registration for the Las Vegas Ice Center Fall and Winter 2026 Youth House League closes on Tuesday, September 1, 2026. If your child wants to play organized hockey this season, you need to sign up before that date.

This is a full season of real hockey, not a quick clinic. The league runs from September through January. Kids get a personalized jersey, team socks, coaching from local pros, and a spot on a real team with games, practices, and playoffs. The clock is ticking, so here is everything a Vegas parent needs to know.

What Happened

The Las Vegas Ice Center opened registration for its Fall and Winter 2026 House League, and the sign-up window closes on Tuesday, September 1. The rink is located at 9295 W Flamingo Rd, Unit 130, in the west valley near Spring Valley. You can reach the front desk at (702) 320-7777.

Once registration closes, the season moves quickly. Evaluation skates happen on September 10 and September 13. These sessions help the coaches sort players into balanced teams by skill. First practices start the week of September 17. Preseason games kick off on September 20. The regular season begins September 27.

From there, the schedule stays busy all winter. Each player gets 16 games plus 14 practices and evaluation sessions. Playoffs begin January 10, 2027, and the championship and final games are set for January 14, 2027. The playoff format includes championship games, consolation games, and placement games, so every team keeps playing meaningful hockey deep into January.

The coaching is a real selling point. Teams are led by the Las Vegas Storm and Bishop Gorman coaching staff. USA Hockey officials work every level, so kids learn the game the right way from day one. Little touches make it feel like the big leagues too. Players get spotlight lineup introductions, a player-of-the-game honor, and online stats and standings so families can follow along all season.

Why It Matters to Las Vegas Residents

Youth hockey is booming in Southern Nevada, and this league is one of the easiest ways for a family to get in the game. The Vegas Golden Knights put hockey on the map here in 2017, and kids all over the valley have picked up sticks ever since. A house league gives those kids a place to play that is local, structured, and welcoming to beginners.

For parents in Spring Valley, Summerlin, and the southwest, the location is a big plus. The Las Vegas Ice Center sits right off West Flamingo Road, so the drive is short for a huge chunk of the valley. That matters when you are hauling gear to Thursday or Friday practices and Sunday games all winter long.

The typical weekly schedule is built around family life. The 10U, 12U, and 14U divisions usually practice on Thursdays. The 8U and High School divisions usually practice on Fridays. Games are played on Sundays. That rhythm makes it easier to plan around school, work, and everything else a Vegas family juggles.

There is also real value packed into the fee. A personalized jersey and team socks come included. So do the game-day extras like player introductions and online stats. For a lot of families, a house league season becomes the highlight of the winter and a way to meet other local parents who spend every Sunday at the rink.

Background and History

The Las Vegas Ice Center has been a home base for the valley's hockey community for years. As interest in the sport exploded after the Golden Knights arrived, local rinks became busy hubs for youth programs, adult leagues, learn-to-skate classes, and public skating. The house league is the backbone of the youth side, giving new and returning players a full season on the ice.

House league hockey is different from travel hockey. Travel teams compete against clubs in other cities and demand a bigger time and money commitment. House league keeps everything local. Kids play against other teams inside the same program at the same rink. It is the classic starting point for most young players, and it stays fun for families who want organized hockey without long road trips.

Divisions are set by USA Hockey age using the 2026-27 season guidelines. The 8U or Mite group is for players born in 2018 through 2020. The 10U or Squirt group covers 2016 and 2017. The 12U or Peewee group is 2014 and 2015. The 14U or Bantam group is 2012 and 2013. The Midget or High School group covers 2008 through 2011. That structure keeps kids playing with and against others close to their own age and size.

USA Hockey age brackets are the national standard, so a child who plays here is learning the same framework used across the country. That consistency helps if a family ever moves or if a player later wants to try travel hockey or a higher level of competition.

What Happens Next

Before anything else, families need to hit the September 1 deadline. Registration is handled online. You can sign up at the league's registration page at lvic.finnlyconnect.com. If you have questions, you can email Sean Stephenson at sean@lasvegasice.com or call the rink at (702) 320-7777.

There are a few requirements to check before you register. Players must pass all three levels of the Lil' Knights program first. A new 2026-27 USA Hockey number is also required, so budget a little time to get that squared away. Travel players from 8U through 18U are eligible for the house league at the A level or below, which gives those families another option to keep skating locally.

After sign-ups close, the calendar takes over. Watch for the evaluation skates on September 10 and 13, then first practices the week of September 17. Preseason games on September 20 lead into the regular season opener on September 27. Mark January 10, 2027, for the start of playoffs and January 14 for the championship and final games.

What You Can Do

If your child wants to play this season, act before Tuesday, September 1. Register online through the league's page at lvic.finnlyconnect.com. Make sure your player has passed all three Lil' Knights levels and has a current 2026-27 USA Hockey number before you sign up.

Pricing is straightforward. The 8U division is $760. Every division from 10U through High School is $915. Goalies pay the same price as skaters, which is a nice break for families raising a future netminder. You can choose to pay in full or split the cost into three payments, so there is some flexibility to fit your budget.
