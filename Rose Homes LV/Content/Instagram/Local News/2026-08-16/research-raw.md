### GD1. Lake Mead Hits Lowest Water Level Ever Recorded
- **Area:** Clark County, Lake Mead National Recreation Area, Boulder City area
- **Summary:** Lake Mead dropped to a surface elevation of about 1,040.5 feet, falling below the previous record low of 1,040.58 feet set in July 2022. The reservoir is now at roughly 27 percent of capacity. Federal forecasters project Lake Mead will set a new record low nearly every month through 2028, and that projection does not yet account for future shortage cuts imposed on states.

- **Date:** August 7, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Lake Mead supplies about 90 percent of Southern Nevada's drinking water, so a record low touches every household bill, landscaping rule, and new-construction water allocation in the valley.
