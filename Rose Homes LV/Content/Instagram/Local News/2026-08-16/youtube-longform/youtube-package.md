THE NUMBERS IN THIS VIDEO
Lake Mead surface elevation, about 1,040.5 feet, roughly 27% of capacity, new record lows projected nearly every month through 2028.
Airbnb listings in the Clark County area, more than 16,000. Licenses issued, 228. Proposed platform fines, $500 first violation, $1,000 after.
Las Vegas Valley Water District class action, more than $96 million collected since 2023, $9 per 1,000 gallons over a monthly limit that changes month to month.
Nevada home insurance, $1,407 average in 2023, $1,672 two years later, projected $1,720 in 2026. Nevada is still the sixth cheapest state.
Redfin, four weeks ending August 9: US median sales price up 2.2% year over year, Las Vegas down 1.6%, one of the five biggest declines of any major metro.
Cash buyers, 26% nationally, 23.9% in Clark County, versus 23.8% a year ago and 59.5% back in February 2013.
Days on market, national median 57 days in July. Clark County, 80% of single family homes sold inside 60 days, up from 78.8%. Condos and townhomes, 67.6%, down from 73.5%.
Luxury entry point, top 10% of sales, $753,000 in December 2019 to about $1.1 million in December 2025 to near $1.2 million as of May 2026. Valley median list price, $319,000 to about $470,000, a 45.6% gain against a 33.4% national increase.
Rent gap, median about $1,300 to $1,400 multifamily versus about $2,100 for a house, a $729 monthly difference and a 51.5% average premium since 2014.
CCSD, more than 280,000 students eating free under the Community Eligibility Provision. 19 extra minutes at 18 schools, about 10 more instructional days a year. Up to $1,000 a year, $500 a semester, to leave a one-star school.
RTC, 35 of 39 routes rewritten August 23. RED LINE on Maryland Parkway, 12.5 miles, 7.2 miles of dedicated transit lanes, 15 hydrogen fuel cell buses, 12 minute daytime frequency, $150 million federal.
Howard Hughes, 354 units, five stories, over 6,500 square feet of commercial on just under 4 acres on Spruce Goose Street. Commission hearing September 2.
Hockey, Shane Smith 33 goals and 68 points in 61 games. Silver Knights group tickets from $35, 36 home games, opener October 3 vs Bakersfield. Golden Knights open Season 10 at home September 29 vs Chicago.
