### Story 1: Las Vegas Realtors Reports July Median Home Price Pulled Back to $480,000 From Record High
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Clark County / Southern Nevada
- **Summary:** Las Vegas Realtors reported the median price of an existing single-family home sold in July was $480,000, down 1.0 percent from July 2025 and down 2.0 percent from the all-time high of $490,000 set in May and June 2026. The median price for condos and townhomes was $290,000, flat year over year and well under the record $315,000 from October 2024. A total of 2,587 homes, condos and townhomes sold in July, with home sales up 1.2 percent and condo/townhome sales down 1.1 percent from a year earlier, leaving Southern Nevada with close to a four month housing supply. At the end of July there were 7,442 single-family homes listed without offers, up 4.1 percent year over year, and 2,719 condos and townhomes without offers, up 3.7 percent.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal (Resale News, verbatim LVR press release)
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/homes/resale-news/lvr-reports-local-home-prices-pull-back-from-record-high-3864239/
- **Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** This is the official local scorecard, and it tells sellers that record pricing has cooled slightly while buyers now have more homes to choose from than a year ago.

**Additional verified stats from the same release:**
- 80 percent of homes and 67.6 percent of condos/townhomes sold within 60 days, versus 78.8 percent and 73.5 percent a year ago.
- Cash sales were 23.9 percent of all local property sales, versus 23.8 percent a year ago. The February 2013 peak was 59.5 percent.
- Short sales and foreclosures together were 0.7 percent of all sales, down from 0.9 percent a year ago.
- Total MLS transaction value in July was nearly $1.3 billion for homes and more than $169 million for condos, high-rise condos and townhomes, up 2.1 percent and 0.4 percent year over year.
- LVR President George Kypreos: "Despite the slight decline in prices during July, we're seeing a steady demand for homes here in Southern Nevada, and that continues to drive home sales and keep prices near record levels... That demand has stayed strong even though mortgage rates have remained elevated."
- **Note:** LVR does not publish a median days on market figure in this release. Do not convert the "sold within 60 days" percentages into a days-on-market number.

### Story 2: Mortgage Rates Fall a Second Straight Week to 6.65 Percent
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Clark County (national rate, direct local impact)
- **Summary:** Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey put the 30-year fixed-rate mortgage at 6.65 percent as of August 20, 2026, down from 6.67 percent the prior week and up from 6.58 percent a year earlier. The 15-year fixed averaged 5.95 percent, down from 5.96 percent the week before and up from 5.69 percent a year ago. Freddie Mac Chief Economist Sam Khater said the dip provides "modest relief for homebuyers" and noted borrowers can save thousands of dollars by shopping multiple lenders. Rates were 6.69 percent on August 6 and 6.67 percent on August 13.
- **Source:** Freddie Mac (via GlobeNewswire)
- **URL:** https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/20/3348688/0/en/mortgage-rates-decline-for-second-consecutive-week.html
- **Date:** August 20, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Rates drive what a Las Vegas buyer can actually afford each month, and two straight weeks of small declines is the kind of window that gets a stalled buyer off the fence.

### Story 6: Clark County Commissioners Approve Short-Term Rental Rule Changes
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Unincorporated Clark County
- **Summary:** Clark County commissioners voted unanimously on Tuesday, August 18, 2026 to approve amendments to the county's short-term rental ordinance, with commissioners Michael Naft and Jim Gibson absent. The changes target booking platforms such as Airbnb and Vrbo: business-license confirmation will only be required once a booking is made, platforms will no longer have to remove listings from unlicensed properties, and the county will implement an electronic verification system. Platforms must also retain data for three years and produce it on request, and must give homeowners copies of county-issued subpoenas within seven days. County Deputy District Attorney Jeff Rogan said some amendments were needed to comply with federal law by removing any suggestion that government requires platforms to police bookings.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/news/politics-and-government/clark-county/clark-county-approves-changes-to-its-short-term-rentals-regulations-3865834/
- **Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Anyone in Clark County who owns, rents or is thinking about buying a short-term rental now has a new compliance picture to plan around.
- **Corroborating source:** Las Vegas Sun, August 18, 2026 — https://lasvegassun.com/news/2026/aug/18/clark-county-commission-targets-booking-sites-in-5/
- **Conflicting detail [NOT VERIFIED]:** A secondary read of the Sun coverage described the ordinance as requiring platforms to verify a license before accepting payment and deactivate non-compliant listings, with a $500 first-violation penalty, $1,000 for each additional violation, and an effective date of September 2. The Review-Journal account describes the platform-removal requirement as being lifted. These two descriptions conflict. Confirm the final ordinance language with Clark County before stating penalties or effective dates on camera.

- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Winchester, North Las Vegas, Spring Valley, Henderson, Enterprise, Paradise, Las Vegas
- **Summary:** A new Zumper report shows one-bedroom rents fell year over year in July in every Las Vegas Valley submarket it tracks. ### Story 8: Las Vegas Leads the Country in Rental Concessions at 51.7 Percent of Listings
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas Valley (southwest valley, Spring Valley, North Las Vegas)
- **Summary:** Zillow's June rental report found 51.7 percent of Las Vegas-area apartment listings offered concessions, far above the 39.7 percent national average and the highest share among the nation's 50 largest metros. The share offering concessions rose 15.5 percentage points from a year earlier, the largest jump of any top-50 metro. Zillow senior economist Orphe Divounguy said "Property managers are competing for renters, and renters are in the driver's seat."

### Story 9: Canadian Interest in Las Vegas Homes Drops 25 Percent
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas / Clark County
- **Summary:** Year-over-year data from Redfin's search database shows Canadian searches for Las Vegas homes fell 25.2 percent from June of last year, well above the 15.3 percent national drop in Canadian interest in U.S. homes. Redfin head of economic research Chen Zhao attributed the pullback to a struggling Canadian economy, trade uncertainty and Canada's own housing problems, including a building slowdown and elevated mortgage rates. Canadians accounted for an average of 44 percent of all global air travelers into Las Vegas between 2019 and 2024, but Canadian visitation was down 20 percent in 2025 and airline seat capacity from Canada was down 30 percent.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/canadian-interest-in-las-vegas-homes-falls-25-outpacing-national-decline-3865196/
- **Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Canadian buyers have long been a real slice of demand for Las Vegas second homes and condos, so a 25 percent drop in interest takes pressure off certain price points.

### Story 12: Nevada Home Insurance Rates Are Up 22 Percent Since 2023
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Nevada statewide, with Clark County detail
- **Summary:** Data from insurance comparison platform Insurify shows the average Nevada home insurance price was $1,407 in 2023, rose to $1,672 two years later, and is projected at $1,720 in 2026 across all home types, an increase of 22.3 percent since 2023. Nevada still ranks sixth cheapest in the country. Insurify economic analyst Julia Taliesin said "In Nevada, rebuilding costs and growing wildfire risk are pushing premiums up, particularly in northern parts of the state." A June presentation by the Nevada Division of Insurance showed Clark County ranked second in the state for homeowners insurance cancellations and nonrenewals due to wildfire risk, with 357 Clark County homeowners canceled or nonrenewed in 2024 out of 772,861 policyholders.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/home-insurance-rates-in-nevada-have-shot-up-22-since-2023-report-says-3862827/
- **Date:** August 12, 2026 (modified August 21, 2026)
- **Why It Matters:** Rising insurance premiums quietly raise the monthly payment on every Clark County home purchase, and buyers should quote insurance before they write an offer.
- **Recency flag:** Published August 12, modified August 21.
