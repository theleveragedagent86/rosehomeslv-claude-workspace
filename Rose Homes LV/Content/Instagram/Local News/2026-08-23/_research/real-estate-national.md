In Clark County, Las Vegas REALTORS reported that short sales and foreclosures were only 0.7% of all local sales in July 2026, down from 0.9% a year earlier. Filings rising is not the same thing as distressed homes flooding the local market.

- **Story Type:** National-to-Local
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas / Clark County
- **National Figure:** The NAHB/Wells Fargo Housing Market Index rose one point to 35 in August 2026, the 16th straight month below 40. 35% of builders cut prices in August, down from 37% in July, with an average price reduction of 6%. Sales incentives were used by 63% of builders, unchanged from July (NAHB, released Aug. 17, 2026).
- **Local Vegas Figure:** Southern Nevada builders logged 4,284 net new-home sales through June 2026, down 15% from the first half of 2025, and pulled 4,156 new-home permits, down 25% from the same stretch in 2025. Closed new-home sales totaled 4,044, down 22%. June alone brought 573 net sales, the lowest monthly tally of the year and down 11% from June 2025 (Home Builders Research via Las Vegas Review-Journal).
- **The Local Angle:** National builder confidence ticked up a point. Las Vegas builders pulled 25% fewer permits in the first half of 2026 than in 2025, so the local new-construction slowdown is much sharper than the flat national reading.
- **Summary:** NAHB reported its August builder confidence index at 35, up one point but below 40 for a 16th straight month, with 35% of builders cutting prices. In Southern Nevada, builders recorded 4,284 net sales through June, down 15% year over year, and 4,156 permits, down 25%. Local buyers are seeing heavier incentives because local builder activity fell further than the national average.
- **National Source:** NAHB — https://www.nahb.org/news-and-economics/press-releases/2026/08/affordability-pressures-keep-builder-confidence-low
- **Local Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal — https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/las-vegas-homebuilders-sales-keep-tumbling-this-year-3854606/
- **Date:** Aug. 17, 2026 (national)
- **Why It Matters:** A Las Vegas buyer shopping new construction has more negotiating room than the flat national builder number implies, because local permits and sales fell far harder.

**Stories dropped for lack of a verifiable local counterpart:**
1. **S&P Cotality Case-Shiller national home price index.** The June 2026 data release falls on Tuesday, Aug. 25, 2026, which is after this research window. No verified current reading was available, so no story was built on it. Do not use any Case-Shiller number this week.
2. **Insurify 2026 home insurance projections** (average U.S. premium projected to rise 4% to $3,057 by December 2026). The national release is from March 2026, well outside the 10-day window. There is a strong local counterpart available (Las Vegas Review-Journal, Aug. 12, 2026: Nevada home insurance rates up 22.3% since 2023 — https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/home-insurance-rates-in-nevada-have-shot-up-22-since-2023-report-says-3862827/), so this could be revived if a fresh national insurance release drops.

4. **First-time buyer share** (29% of U.S. July sales, down from 33% in June, per NAR). No Clark County first-time buyer share was findable from GLVAR/LVR or UNLV within the window. Marked NOT FOUND and excluded.

**Recency flags:**
- Story 4 (ATTOM foreclosure) uses a July 16, 2026 national release, older than 10 days. The local counterpart is current (Aug. 6). Use only if the foreclosure angle is a priority.
- Stories 5, 6, 7 use the Realtor.com July report released Aug. 3, 2026, which is 20 days old. Data itself is the most recent monthly available.
- Stories 3, 8, 9 are the freshest, all from Aug. 17-20, 2026.

**Conflicting values noted:**
- Las Vegas median price: Las Vegas REALTORS reports $480,000 median *sold* price for existing single-family homes in July 2026. Realtor.com reports $469,900 median *list* price for the Las Vegas-Henderson-North Las Vegas metro in the same month. These measure different things (sold vs. asking, MLS area vs. MSA) and are not in conflict, but do not mix them in one sentence.
- Nevada foreclosure rate appears three ways in the ATTOM mid-year release: 0.22% of housing units for the first half of 2026 (one in 452), one in every 871 units in Q2 2026, and one in every 2,508 units in June 2026 alone. Use the matching time period to whatever national figure is quoted alongside it.
