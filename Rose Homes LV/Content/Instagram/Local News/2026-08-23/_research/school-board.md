### Story 10: CCSD trustees pick two bill draft requests for the 2027 Nevada legislative session
- **Category:** School Board and Education
- **County/Area:** Clark County
- **Summary:** Trustees selected two bill draft requests for the 2027 legislative session at the Aug. 13 meeting: increased special education funding, approved 6-1, and changes to state budgeting processes for education funding, approved 7-0. The budgeting proposal would base funding on the prior year's Oct. 1 enrollment instead of current-year quarterly fluctuations and would establish a firm statutory minimum funding level for public education.
