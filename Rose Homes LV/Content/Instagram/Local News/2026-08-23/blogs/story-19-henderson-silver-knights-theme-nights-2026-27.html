Henderson Silver Knights Release 14 Theme Nights and 24 Giveaway Dates for 2026-27

The Henderson Silver Knights announced their full Theme and Promotional Knights schedule for the 2026-27 season on August 18, 2026. The list includes 14 themed games and 24 separate dates with free giveaways, all at Lee's Family Forum in Henderson.

If you have kids, this is the single most useful calendar drop of the Henderson fall. Theme nights and giveaway games are the cheapest way to get a family of four out of the house for a night, and the good ones sell out. Fans who plan around this list get the seats they want. Fans who wait get whatever is left.

Returning favorites on the schedule include Lucky Launch, Star Wars Knight, and Wands and Wizards Knight. New additions this season include HSK-Pop Knight and Vintage Vegas Knight. That mix of familiar and fresh is exactly what a minor league team does well, and it is a big part of why Lee's Family Forum has become a real gathering spot for this side of the valley.

What Happened

On August 18, 2026, the Henderson Silver Knights published their Theme and Promotional Knights schedule for the upcoming American Hockey League season. The team confirmed 14 theme nights and 24 dates that include a free giveaway item for fans. Every one of those games is at Lee's Family Forum, the team's home arena in Henderson.

Theme nights and giveaway nights are not always the same thing. A theme night is built around a concept. The team leans into it with special jerseys, arena decor, music, activities on the concourse, and staff who play along. A giveaway night means a set number of fans through the doors receive a free item. Some games are both. Some are one or the other. That is why the counts are different, 14 versus 24, and why reading the actual calendar matters more than skimming the headline.

Lucky Launch returns for another season. It has been one of the more popular promotions on the Silver Knights calendar because it turns a normal intermission into something the whole building watches. Star Wars Knight also comes back. Star Wars nights are a staple across minor league hockey for a simple reason. They pull in families who are not necessarily hockey fans, and a good chunk of those families come back later in the season for a regular game.

Wands and Wizards Knight is on the list again too. That one has been a consistent draw for the elementary and middle school crowd in Henderson, and it usually brings out costumes in the stands. The two brand new nights are HSK-Pop Knight and Vintage Vegas Knight. HSK-Pop leans into the K-pop wave that has been enormous with teenagers and young adults. Vintage Vegas Knight goes the other direction and plays off classic Las Vegas style, the neon and lounge era look that this city trades on.

The Silver Knights are the AHL affiliate of the Vegas Golden Knights. That means the players on the ice at Lee's Family Forum are one call away from T-Mobile Arena. Some of the names you watch in Henderson this winter will be playing on the Strip within a season or two. That connection is part of the appeal for local hockey families, and it is a real reason to pay attention to the roster and not just the promotions.

The announcement came out on the team's official site, which is the only place the full grid of dates lives in one piece. Schedules like this get screenshotted and reposted in neighborhood groups within an hour, and by the third repost half the details are missing. If you want the real list, pull it up yourself.

Why It Matters to Las Vegas Residents

Start with the money, because that is what most Henderson families are actually weighing. A night out on the Strip for a family of four gets expensive fast. Dinner, parking, tickets, and a couple of souvenirs will run past what a lot of households want to spend on a random Friday. An AHL game with a giveaway is a different math problem. Minor league hockey has always priced itself as community entertainment, and Lee's Family Forum sits inside Henderson instead of a 30 minute drive plus resort parking away.

Then there is the time. Parents in Green Valley, Anthem, Inspirada, Cadence, and Seven Hills all know the drill. Getting to a Strip event on a weeknight means committing an entire evening. Getting to Lee's Family Forum from most of Henderson is a short drive. Kids can be home and in bed at a reasonable hour. That difference alone is why a lot of families choose the Silver Knights over a bigger ticket downtown.

There is also a community piece here that people underrate. Henderson has grown very fast. Whole neighborhoods have filled in over the last decade with families who moved here from California, Arizona, Utah, and the Midwest. When you move somewhere new, you look for the places that make it feel like a town instead of just an address. A local arena where you recognize the same season ticket holders in your section does that. A theme night where a few hundred kids show up in wizard robes does that even better.

For anyone shopping for a home in Henderson right now, this is worth understanding as a quality of life factor. Buyers ask me constantly about what there is to do. Schools, parks, and trails come up first, and then people want to know whether there is anything happening at night that does not require driving to the Strip. Lee's Family Forum is a legitimate answer to that question, and a 14 night theme schedule gives it real substance.

It matters for renters and newer residents too. A hockey game is a low commitment way to meet people and get a feel for the city. You do not need to know anybody, and you do not need to know the rules.

There is a practical scheduling angle as well. AHL hockey runs from fall through spring, which covers the exact stretch when Southern Nevada weather turns and outdoor plans get less reliable. Winter is when the valley moves indoors, and an arena calendar fills that gap.

How to Read a Theme and Giveaway Schedule

A lot of people look at a promotional calendar, see a fun name, and buy tickets. Then they get to the game and are confused about why they did not get the item, or why the night felt different than they pictured. A little bit of literacy about how these calendars work saves you that.

First, separate the two lists in your head. The 14 theme nights are about atmosphere. That is where you get the special look, the music, the concourse activity, and the fans who dress up. The 24 giveaway dates are about the item at the door. If your kid mainly wants the free thing, you are shopping the giveaway list. If your kid wants to wear a costume and see the arena transformed, you are shopping the theme list. The overlap dates are the ones that do both, and those are usually the fastest to fill.

Second, understand that giveaway items are almost always limited in number and distributed at the entrance. Teams publish the specifics for each promotion, and those specifics vary from night to night. If a giveaway is the reason you are going, plan to arrive early rather than rolling in at puck drop. That is standard across minor league sports, and it is the single most common reason families walk away disappointed.

Third, look at the day of the week. A Saturday theme night with a giveaway is the busiest possible combination. A midweek game without either is the quietest and usually the easiest ticket. If you have young kids and want a calmer first experience, the quiet nights are your friend. If you want the full spectacle, take the weekend date and accept the crowd that comes with it.

Background and History

The Silver Knights arrived in Henderson as part of the Vegas Golden Knights organization bringing its development team in house. Before that, the Golden Knights sent prospects to an affiliate far from Nevada. Moving the AHL club to Henderson meant coaching, training, and player development all happened in the same metro area as the NHL club. It also meant Southern Nevada got a second professional hockey team to follow.

For the first stretch, the team played its home games at the Orleans Arena while a permanent Henderson home was built out. That arrangement worked, but it was not the long term plan. The point of a Henderson team was to be in Henderson. Once the team moved into its own arena in the Henderson event center, the identity finally matched the name on the jersey.

Lee's Family Forum is the current name on that building. It is a mid sized arena, which is exactly right for AHL hockey. You are close to the glass in almost every seat. The sound carries. A crowd that would look thin in a 17,000 seat NHL building feels loud and full in a venue this size. That is a big reason theme nights land better here than they would in a larger room.

Minor league hockey has leaned on promotional nights for decades. It is not a gimmick, it is the business model. AHL teams do not have national television money, so they build revenue on people walking through the doors. That means giving families a reason to pick a Tuesday in November. Specialty jerseys, giveaway items, and themed nights are how that gets done, and the teams that do it best build multi generational fan bases.

What has changed for Henderson specifically is scale. The city has added tens of thousands of residents in the last decade, with major master planned growth in Inspirada, Cadence, and the Anthem and Seven Hills areas. A promotional schedule that might have been niche in 2005 now has a very large local audience within a short drive. That is why announcements like this one get shared so widely in neighborhood groups.

Hockey itself has changed here over the same stretch. Before the Golden Knights arrived, this was not a hockey market. Now there are kids in Henderson who have never known this city without an NHL team, and a meaningful number of them play. A themed AHL calendar lands on much softer ground today than it would have a decade ago.

The two new nights say something about where the team is aiming. HSK-Pop Knight is a direct play for teenagers and young adults, a group minor league teams historically struggle to reach. Vintage Vegas Knight leans the other way, toward adults who have a soft spot for the classic look of this city. Taken with the returning favorites, the 14 nights cover a wide age range on purpose.

What Happens Next

The next thing on the Silver Knights calendar is the preseason. The team announced separately that it will play two preseason exhibition games at the first Southwest Showdown in Tucson, hosted by the Tucson Roadrunners at Tucson Arena from September 25 to 27. The Roadrunners, Colorado Eagles, and Texas Stars round out that field, and there is a fan event scheduled for Friday, September 25. If you want a road trip to kick off the season, that is the one on the board.

After that comes the regular season home schedule at Lee's Family Forum, which is when the theme and giveaway calendar actually starts paying off. Individual game tickets and any promotion specific details, including which giveaway items go to which fans and how many are available, come through the team's official channels. Those specifics can and do change during the season, so the team site is the place to confirm rather than a screenshot passed around a group chat.

The practical move right now is simple. Pull up the schedule, pick the three or four nights your family actually cares about, and get those dates on the calendar before the fall gets away from you. Popular theme nights and the best giveaway games tend to fill in faster than a regular Wednesday, especially the ones that appeal to kids. If you are eyeing a specific promotion, earlier is better.

Also keep an eye on the individual game pages once the season begins. That is where the giveaway item for each date and any theme specific activities usually get posted. If you buy tickets months ahead, set a reminder to check that page again in the week leading up, because that is when the details are most reliable.
