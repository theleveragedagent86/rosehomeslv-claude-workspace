Water: Nevada loses about 50,000 acre feet a year, lower basin cuts 1.5 million, Lake Mead near 1,039 feet at 27% full
Robotaxis: Tesla up to 5,000 cars in year one, Waymo and Aviari 1,000 each, operations within 120 days
CCSD: $127,170,000 in capital contracts approved 6 to 1 on August 13
Ethics: Nevada State High School CEO, $179,000 salary, $2,500 fine
Canada: Las Vegas home searches down 25.2%, national drop only 15.3%
Fleury: $25 million, 9,857 square feet, lot bought for $3.9 million in 2021, never listed
Prices: median condo and townhome now $290,000, more than doubled since July 2016
Rates: 30-year fixed 6.65%, starter home costs about $2,131 a month, down 4.4%

Rents: Winchester down 18.8%, North Las Vegas down 14.5%, Spring Valley down 9.7%, Henderson down 6.5%
