BY THE NUMBERS
Water: Nevada's 300,000 acre-foot allocation could be cut by up to 213,556, leaving under 86,500, and the valley used just under 212,500 in 2024
CCSD money: $1 million settlement, plus more than $90 million in construction contracts approved 7 to 0
Flock cameras: valleywide access started August 24, paused hours after the memo went public, in use since August 2024
Property tax: the 3% annual cap, and three reform options already vetted and sitting on the shelf
Vaccines: national kindergarten exemptions up to 4.2% from 3.6%, about 155,000 children
Medical tower: 207,000 square feet on 4 acres, 1,000 parking spaces against an 852 cap
Casino proposal: 313 rooms, about 275,000 square feet, Las Vegas Boulevard and Ford, September 2 zoning agenda
Hylo Park: 73 acres at Rancho and Lake Mead, 200,000 square foot sports facility, $1 million ice rebuild, reopening summer 2027
Reflection Ridge: from about $1.2 million, 3,300 to 3,800 square feet, sales open in September
Apartments: 5,000 units needed a year, 350,000 valley renters, $26.9 billion contributed, 118,100 jobs, 38th most rent burdened
Hockey: 15 national games, 69 on free over-the-air, opener September 29 against Chicago
Ballpark: $2 billion, 33,000 fans, a 290 foot high point
