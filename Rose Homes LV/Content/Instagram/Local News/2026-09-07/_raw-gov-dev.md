### Story 1: Developers Buy 940 Acres for Monument Hills, Las Vegas' Biggest New Community in Years
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** Northwest Las Vegas (City of Las Vegas)
- **Summary:** Olympia Companies and Bruin Capital Partners closed the week of Aug. 24 on roughly 940 acres for $94 million, land the federal Bureau of Land Management sold through the city of Las Vegas. The master plan, called Monument Hills, is penciled for up to 6,000 homes plus two school sites, commercial land, and about 90 acres of parks and trails. Olympia executive vice president Chris Armstrong said lots will be sold to homebuilders over several years and the first homes should be delivered in spring 2028.

### Story 3: North Las Vegas Sells 16 Acres of Old Civic Land to a Developer for $1,575
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** North Las Vegas
- **Summary:** Agora Realty and Management closed in July on roughly 15.8 acres along Civic Center Drive just north of Lake Mead Boulevard for $1,575, part of a City Council-approved deal for about 19 acres at a combined $1,938.80. The token price reflects brownfield cleanup costs after EPA-funded assessments found asbestos, lead paint, and mercury across five aging municipal buildings, including the former City Hall at 2200 Civic Center Drive that sat vacant for 14 years. Agora's One Civic Center plan calls for at least 15,000 square feet of commercial space, an apartment complex of at least 100 units, and a civic building of at least 30,000 square feet, with construction starting late 2026 or early 2027 on a roughly 60-month schedule.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/developer-buys-16-acres-for-less-than-1600-for-big-project-in-north-las-vegas-3881877/
- **Date:** September 3, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** North Las Vegas is handing over publicly owned downtown land at a token price to finally build a walkable city center, a bet that will shape Civic Center corridor property values for a decade.

### Story 4: Henderson City Council Votes 4-1 to End the Retiree Health Insurance Subsidy
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** Henderson
- **Summary:** On Sept. 1 the council voted 4-1 to amend agreements with the Henderson Police Officers Association, Henderson Police Supervisors Association, and Henderson Professional Firefighters Local 1883, ending the Retiree Health Insurance Premium Assistance program and shifting workers to enhanced Retirement Health Savings accounts. The program pays retirees $200 to $500 a month based on years of service, has about $2 million left, and was projected to go insolvent by 2037 with costs potentially reaching $7.5 million a year. Ten speakers asked for a delay, and current recipients get a three-month grace period for final payments.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/local/henderson/henderson-city-council-votes-to-eliminate-retirement-subsidy-program-3873648/
- **Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Henderson taxpayers avoid a growing unfunded liability, but hundreds of current and retired city workers lose a benefit they built their retirement plans around.

### Story 5: Clark County Approves a $40 Million Recreation Center at Echo Trail Park

### Story 6: Haas Automation Holds Job Fairs Ahead of Its Henderson Factory Opening
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** West Henderson
- **Summary:** Haas Automation is running career fairs Sept. 19, Oct. 3, and Oct. 17 at Palace Station, hiring operators, machinists, engineers, and assemblers, with the first date already full. ### Story 7: Stadium Authority Approves $75 Million in Public Money for a $158 Million Allegiant Stadium Upgrade
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas Strip corridor, unincorporated Clark County
- **Summary:** The Las Vegas Stadium Authority board voted unanimously on Sept. 2 to fund a $158 million redo of Allegiant Stadium's north entrance, with $75 million coming from the public stadium fund and $83 million from the Raiders. The plan builds a plaza with event decks plus improved transportation drop-off points, the first major improvement since the $1.9 billion, 65,000-seat stadium opened in 2021. Construction starts after the Raiders season ends in January and targets completion in late 2028, ahead of the 2029 Super Bowl.

- **Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** The public share comes from hotel room taxes, and locking in a 2029 Super Bowl bid protects the event calendar that supports Strip-adjacent property values.

### Story 8: Federal Judge Halts the Townsite Solar II Data Center Outside Boulder City
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** Boulder City area, Clark County (BLM land)
- **Summary:** A Department of the Interior administrative judge issued a stay blocking construction of the Townsite Solar II data center on Bureau of Land Management land near Boulder City. The Skylar Capital Management project was first proposed on an 88.5-acre site inside city limits, withdrawn after the Boulder City Planning Commission recommended denial, then refiled on federal land where a 2023 solar farm approval had already cleared environmental review. Boulder City, the Sierra Club, and the Center for Biological Diversity appealed, and the judge found that a solar farm and a data center are not substantially the same for permitting purposes. The proposal called for a 167 megawatt data center with 70 megawatts of battery storage.
- **Source:** NVBEX, reporting sourced to The Nevada Independent
- **URL:** https://nevbex.com/2026/09/05/townsite-solar-ii-data-center-stay/
- **Date:** September 5, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** This is the clearest sign yet that Southern Nevada communities can slow data center projects, which affects power costs, land use, and what gets built near homes.
- **Note:** The original federal approval and the appeal were covered in prior weeks. The judge's ruling is the new development.

### Story 9: Work Begins on a 155-Mile Southern Nevada Transmission Upgrade
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** Clark and Nye counties, groundbreaking at Jean Airport
- **Summary:** GridLiance West, a NextEra Energy Transmission subsidiary, broke ground Sept. 7 on the Core Upgrades Project, rebuilding 155 miles of 230 kV and 500 kV lines and upgrading substations. ### Story 11: Clark County Holds a 348-Unit Veterans Housing Project for October Review
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** Sunrise Manor, unincorporated Clark County
- **Summary:** At the Sept. 2 zoning meeting, veterans and union members packed the room to support Patriot Housing LV's proposed $90 million affordable housing community on 15 acres near Vegas Valley Drive and Tree Line Drive, west of Hollywood Boulevard. The plan calls for 348 units from studios to three bedrooms, buildings designated for women residents, and wraparound services for veterans and people with disabilities, financed through tax credits, bonds, and about $15 million in donations. County staff had recommended denial in June over the industrial surroundings, and commissioners deferred the item to Oct. 7 pending deed review, financing detail, an affordable housing department analysis, and a City of Las Vegas zoning agreement.
- **Source:** FOX5 Vegas
- **URL:** https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/09/03/veterans-call-housing-las-vegas/
- **Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** More than 110,000 veterans live in the Las Vegas area and advocates say many cannot afford local rents, so this is one of the largest veteran-specific housing decisions the county has faced.

### Story 13: Las Vegas City Council Approves To-Go Alcohol Sales for Restaurants
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** City of Las Vegas
- **Summary:** The City Council unanimously adopted Bill No. 2026-29 / Ordinance No. 6967 on Sept. 2, amending city code so restaurants already licensed for on-premise alcohol sales can apply for an endorsement to sell alcohol to go with a meal in sealed, tamper-evident containers.
