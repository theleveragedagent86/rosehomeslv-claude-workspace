### Story 1: Golden Knights Open Training Camp September 16 With a Shorter Camp and Only Four Preseason Games
- **Category:** Hockey
- **County/Area:** Summerlin (City National Arena) and T-Mobile Arena
- **Summary:** New Vegas Golden Knights head coach Ryan Craig said the team opens training camp on Sept. 16 with 12 days of camp instead of the usual 19, and four exhibition games instead of seven. ### Story 3: Golden Knights Single-Game Tickets Go on Sale With 42 Home Games in the NHL's First 84-Game Season

- **Summary:** The Vegas Golden Knights put single-game tickets for the 2026-27 regular season on sale Sept. 1. ### Story 7: PWHL Las Vegas Will Split Home Games Between T-Mobile Arena and Lee's Family Forum
- **Category:** Hockey
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas Strip and Henderson
- **Summary:** PWHL Las Vegas announced it will play home games at both T-Mobile Arena and Lee's Family Forum in Henderson during its inaugural season. The team said splitting venues lets it reach residential communities across the valley as well as visitors on the Strip. Henderson will be the club's home base, and the team will practice at America First Center, the Henderson Silver Knights facility. Season ticket members can now select seats, and the league schedule is expected in the fall. If the PWHL keeps a 30-game schedule, that would mean about 15 home games split between the two buildings.
