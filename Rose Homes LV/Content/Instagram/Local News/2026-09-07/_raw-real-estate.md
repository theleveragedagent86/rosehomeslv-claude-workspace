### Story 1: Developers Buy 940 Acres in Northwest Las Vegas for Monument Hills, Up to 6,000 Homes
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Upper northwest Las Vegas Valley (city of Las Vegas), Clark County
- **Summary:** Olympia Companies and Bruin Capital Partners paid about $94 million for roughly 940 acres in the upper northwest valley, land the city of Las Vegas had just acquired from the Bureau of Land Management. The site is planned for up to 6,000 homes in a master plan called Monument Hills, ranging from attainable housing to executive homes, plus dedicated off-base housing for personnel at Nellis and Creech Air Force bases. It is the largest federal land transaction in the Las Vegas Valley in more than two decades, and the developer expects the first homes in spring 2028.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/developers-buy-900-plus-acres-for-las-vegas-biggest-new-community-in-years-3873678/
- **Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Land supply is the single biggest reason Las Vegas home prices stay high, so a 6,000-home pipeline is the first real relief valve buyers have seen in years.

**Corroborating sources:**

### Story 2: A Las Vegas Household Now Needs $116,563 a Year to Afford a Home
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas Valley, Clark County
- **Summary:** Redfin's latest affordability report says a Las Vegas Valley household needs to earn about $116,563 to comfortably afford a home, down 2 percent from a year earlier. The estimated median valley household income is about $82,975, meaning a typical household would have to spend 42.1 percent of its income on housing, well above Redfin's 30 percent guideline. Only 18.5 percent of valley listings on Redfin are affordable to the median household. Nationally, buyers need about $109,796, down 0.5 percent from the all-time high of $110,382.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/how-much-do-you-need-to-make-to-afford-a-home-in-the-las-vegas-valley-3872567/
- **Date:** August 31, 2026 (updated September 2, 2026)
- **Why It Matters:** It puts a hard number on the income gap local buyers face, and shows the gap is finally shrinking instead of widening.

### Story 3: Mortgage Rates Rose to 6.71 Percent, a Second Straight Weekly Increase
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** National rate, direct impact on Clark County buyers
- **Summary:** Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey put the 30-year fixed rate at 6.71 percent for the week ending September 3, 2026, up from 6.66 percent the prior week. The 15-year fixed averaged 6.04 percent, up from 5.98 percent. A year ago the 30-year averaged 6.50 percent and the 15-year averaged 5.60 percent.
- **Source:** Freddie Mac (via GlobeNewswire press release)
- **URL:** https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/03/3356148/0/en/mortgage-rates-average-6-71.html
- **Date:** September 3, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** On a $480,000 Las Vegas median-priced home, every quarter point of rate movement changes the monthly payment by roughly $60 to $70, so this is the number that decides whether local buyers can qualify.

**Corroborating source:** Freddie Mac PMMS — https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms

### Story 4: Million-Dollar New-Home Closings Hold Steady While Overall Builder Closings Fall Over 20 Percent
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Summerlin, Henderson (MacDonald Highlands, Lake Las Vegas), Clark County
- **Summary:** Home Builders Research reports Las Vegas builders closed 325 new homes priced at $1 million and up in the first half of 2026, nearly matching the 334 closed in the first half of 2025 and far ahead of the 227 in the first half of 2024. That steadiness comes even though total new-home closings are down more than 20 percent in 2026. Toll Brothers led with 65 closings and Pulte had 54, both concentrated in Ascension in Summerlin, where top prices hit $3 million and $3.6 million. Blue Heron closed a $13 million home in MacDonald Highlands, and the priciest 2026 new home in public records was a $22.5 million Robert Elliott Custom Homes property in The Summit Club.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal (RJNewHomes.Vegas, Buck Wargo)
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/homes/new-homes/1m-plus-home-sales-hold-steady-3882295/
- **Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** The high end of the Las Vegas new-home market is holding up while everything below it slows, which tells move-up sellers exactly where the buyers still are.

### Story 7: Mesquite ZIP Code 89034 Named the Fastest-Growing in Nevada
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Mesquite, Clark County
- **Summary:** A MovingPlace report that analyzed 5 million moves found ZIP code 89034, covering Mesquite north of Interstate 15, added about 73.3 residents per 1,000 from January through May 2026, the fastest per-capita growth of any Nevada ZIP code. The report credits the warm climate, golf and outdoor recreation, newer residential development, and a lower-density alternative to Las Vegas. Nevada's top five also included 89113 in southwest Las Vegas and 89501 in Reno, though no Nevada ZIP cracked the national top 50.
- **Source:** Las Vegas Review-Journal
- **URL:** https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/mesquite-zip-code-89034-named-fastest-growing-in-nevada-report-finds-3869731/
- **Date:** August 26, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Mesquite is still Clark County, and it shows retirees and price-sensitive buyers are willing to drive 80 miles to get away from Las Vegas Valley pricing.

### Story 8: Nearly 57 Percent of Las Vegas Renters Are Millennials, Sixth Highest in the Country
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas Valley, Clark County
- **Summary:** A RentCafe study covering demographic trends from 2018 to 2023 found 56.8 percent of all Las Vegas Valley renters are millennials, born between 1981 and 1996. That ranks Las Vegas sixth in the nation behind Los Angeles, San Jose, San Diego, New York and San Francisco. The report ties the concentration to rising cost of living and comparatively lower local wages.

- **Date:** August 31, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Millennials are the biggest block of would-be first-time buyers in the valley, and this shows how many of them are still stuck renting.

### Story 9: Las Vegas Single-Family Inventory Climbs to 5,840 Homes, With the Biggest Gains Under $500,000
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas Valley, Clark County
- **Summary:** Active single-family listings in the Las Vegas Valley rose by 64 homes to 5,840 in the week ending September 3, 2026, up 1,028 homes or 23 percent since January 1, when the count was 4,748. A year ago the same week showed 5,675 homes. Broken out by price, there are 2,536 active listings under $500,000, 1,677 between $500,000 and $699,999, and 1,627 at $700,000 and above, with the sub-$500,000 tier posting the biggest weekly increase.
- **Source:** Very Vintage Vegas Market Watch (Jack LeVine), using Las Vegas REALTORS MLS data
- **URL:** https://veryvintagevegas.com/2026/09/03/very-vintage-vegas-market-watch-9-3-2026/
- **Date:** September 3, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Entry-level buyers finally have real choice for the first time in years, and sellers in that range can no longer count on multiple offers.
- **NOTE:** This is the same weekly inventory series covered in a prior week at 5,711 homes and 19 percent. The numbers and the price-tier breakdown are new, but treat this as a lower-priority pick to avoid feeling repetitive.

### Story 10: July Resale Volume Hit 2,587 Properties as Condo and Townhome Sales Slipped
- **Category:** Real Estate Market
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas Valley, Clark County
- **Summary:** Las Vegas REALTORS data shows 2,587 existing local homes, condos and townhomes sold in July 2026. Compared with July 2025, house sales were up 1.2 percent while condo and townhome sales were down 1.1 percent. The July sales pace equates to nearly a four-month housing supply, similar to a year earlier. The median price of existing single-family homes was $480,000 in July, down from $490,000.
- **Source:** Very Vintage Vegas Market Watch (Jack LeVine), using Las Vegas REALTORS MLS data
- **URL:** https://veryvintagevegas.com/2026/09/03/very-vintage-vegas-market-watch-9-3-2026/
- **Date:** September 3, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Houses and condos are moving in opposite directions right now, which changes the advice for anyone deciding what type of property to buy or sell.
- **NOTE:** Conflicting figure recorded. A Review-Journal report cites 2,046 previously owned single-family houses sold in July. The 2,587 figure includes condos and townhomes; the 2,046 figure is single-family only. Both are consistent with each other. RJ source: https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/las-vegas-home-sales-inch-up-prices-dip-amid-headwinds-nationwide-3860052/
