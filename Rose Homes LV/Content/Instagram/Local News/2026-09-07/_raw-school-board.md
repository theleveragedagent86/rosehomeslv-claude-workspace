### Story 2: CCSD Police Traffic Stops Near Schools Are Up More Than 40 Percent Under the New Chief
- **Category:** School Board and Education
- **County/Area:** Clark County
- **Summary:** New CCSD Police Chief Jeff Clark said that in the first three weeks of the school year, traffic stops near schools were up more than 40 percent and citations were up just under 40 percent compared to the same stretch last year, while traffic accidents were down almost 15 percent. The department has also been towing vehicles as part of the push. The crackdown follows Nevada's School Zone Bill, which took effect July 1 and doubles fines for violations in school zones, and a year in which more than 420 students were struck by cars on the way to or from school.
- **Source:** FOX5 Vegas (KVVU)
- **URL:** https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/09/05/ccsd-police-increase-enforcement-near-schools-traffic-safety-becomes-top-priority/
- **Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Why It Matters:** Drivers dropping kids off are far more likely to get pulled over this year, and the early numbers suggest fewer crashes near campuses.
- **Flag:** CONTROVERSIAL
- **Note:** This is a new development in an ongoing story. The chief's swearing-in was covered in a prior week. The new element is the first three weeks of enforcement data.
