- Raw ID: RE11

- Area: Southern Nevada valley-wide; new construction concentrated in Henderson, North Las Vegas, and the northwest valley
- Viral Score: Orange | Extremely Important: No
- Source: Las Vegas Review-Journal
- Article Title: Homebuilders landed big jump in monthly sales in Las Vegas

- Secondary Source: Las Vegas Review-Journal, "Home sales are slowing in Southern Nevada, report says"
- Secondary URL: https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/housing/home-sales-are-slowing-in-southern-nevada-report-says-3883938/
- Secondary Source: VEGAS INC, "Las Vegas builders shrink homes, slash prices as affordability crisis squeezes buyers"
- Secondary URL: https://vegasinc.lasvegassun.com/business/2026/may/12/las-vegas-builders-shrink-homes-slash-prices-as-af/
- Date: August 24, 2026 (July builder data); September 9, 2026 (August resale data); May 12, 2026 (builder strategy context)
- Verified Numbers: New single-family median closing price $581,930 in July 2026, up 2.1% year over year (Home Builders Research via Review-Journal). Median for all newly built homes $535,114 in July, up 2.9%. Existing single-family median $475,000 in August 2026, down 1% year over year and $15,000 below the $490,000 record set in May and June (Las Vegas REALTORS via Review-Journal). Gap between the two: $106,930. Builders pulled 620 permits in July, up about 1.5% from June and down 23% year over year, and logged 735 net sales, up 28% from June and down 7% year over year, with 706 closings, down 12%. Year to date through July: 4,776 permits, down 25%, and 4,750 closings, down 20%. In 2025 builders pulled 9,734 permits, down 20% and the lowest total since 2016, and closed 9,990 sales, also down 20% (Home Builders Research via VEGAS INC). Nationally the median new single-family home shrank to 2,176 square feet in Q3 2025 and has generally been falling since 2015, and NAHB reported in January that 40% of builders cut prices and 65% used sales incentives. Southern Nevada had roughly 7,590 single-family homes listed without an offer at the end of August, up 5.3% year over year, with supply just over 4.5 months.
- Detail: Home Builders Research President Andrew Smith called the 2026 opening "a positive sign moving into 2026 after the lowest annual total in a decade." Las Vegas REALTORS President George Kypreos said local prices "have been pretty stable this year." Nevada's Assembly Bill 540, the Nevada Housing Access and Attainability Act, created the Nevada Attainable Housing Account with a $133 million appropriation aimed at households earning up to 150% of area median income. Southern Nevada Home Builders Association CEO Tina Frias called it "a step in the right direction, but it's a piece of a much larger supply challenge."
- Homeowner/Buyer Angle: The new-construction premium is now about the price of a Las Vegas condo, and builders are shrinking floor plans rather than cutting sticker prices. Fewer permits in 2026 means fewer finished homes in 2027, which supports resale values and narrows buyer choice.
- Source Note: The previously selected version of this story sourced its incentive and permit-concentration figures to a competing Las Vegas brokerage. Those figures could not be independently verified and were dropped. Every number above traces to the Review-Journal, VEGAS INC, Home Builders Research, Las Vegas REALTORS, or NAHB.
