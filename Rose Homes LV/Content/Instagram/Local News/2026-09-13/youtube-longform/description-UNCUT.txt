BY THE NUMBERS
CCSD budget: $51.6 million cut, $29.1 million from schools, $22.5 million from the central office, after losing 10,400+ students
School zones: 45 students hit in 22 school days, 23 on e-scooters, drivers at fault in about 60%
New vs resale: new single-family median $581,930 in July vs $475,000 resale in August, a $106,930 gap, and permits down 25%
Resale: $475,000 median, down 1% year over year, $15,000 under the $490,000 peak, total sales down 11.9% from July
National: record $434,100 national median in July, up 1.4%, while Las Vegas slid
Condos: $299,900 median, up 0.6%, with 2,714 units sitting without an offer, up 6%
Rates: 6.76% national 30-year (Freddie Mac) vs about 7.125% quoted in Nevada, 15-year near 6.375%
Watering: 3 assigned days a week, no Sundays, September 1 through October 31
Sandstone: 1,500+ homes on nearly 300 acres in North Las Vegas, from the $300,000s
Lake Mead Blvd: $37.7 million, Simmons Street to Losee Road, through the end of 2026
Schools: Skye Canyon elementary $92.9 million, opens August 2027, Cashman pre-K through 8 campus $150 million
VGK: Mark Stone 2 years at $9 million a year, Braeden Bowman 6 years at $3.4 million a year
PWHL: camp opens November 18, preseason in Henderson November 22 and 24
