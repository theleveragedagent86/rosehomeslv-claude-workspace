### Story 6: Boulder City Voters Will Decide in November Whether to Open 14,000 More Acres to Data Centers
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** Boulder City
- **Summary:** Boulder City's Question 1 asks voters whether data center facilities should be an approved land use within the Eldorado Valley Transfer Area, outside the Multi-species Habitat Conservation Easement. The city charter bars development on the 100,000-acre tract without voter approval, and more than 86,000 acres are protected by conservation easement. If it passes, the City Council could lease up to 14,000 additional acres to data center developers, with each project still going through the city's normal land-use and leasing review. Supporters say the city could charge roughly 10 times more rent per acre for data center leases than for current solar leases.
- **Source:** Nevada Current

### Story 14: Legal Aid Challenges Who Decides Las Vegas Eviction Cases
- **Category:** Government and Development
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas Justice Court, Clark County
- **Summary:** In a petition filed September 4, attorneys with the Legal Aid Center of Southern Nevada challenged the Las Vegas Justice Court's exclusive use of unelected hearing masters to rule on summary eviction cases without a judge's oversight, arguing the practice is unconstitutional.
