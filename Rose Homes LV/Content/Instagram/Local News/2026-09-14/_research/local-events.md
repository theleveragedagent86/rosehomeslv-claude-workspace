### 6. Nevada DMV's Registration Spotter Tool Tops 50,000 Reports and Splits Drivers

- **Category:** Local News and Events
- **Story Type:** Public Safety
- **County/Area:** Valley-wide, with interviews at the Henderson DMV office
- **Summary:** Nevada drivers have filed more than 50,000 reports through the DMV's Nevada Registration Spotter, an online tool that lets people report vehicles with expired tags or no license plates.
