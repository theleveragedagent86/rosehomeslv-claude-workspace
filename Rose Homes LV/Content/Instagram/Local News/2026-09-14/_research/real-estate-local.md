## MOST RECENT LAS VEGAS REALTORS / GLVAR MONTHLY STATS

The most recent monthly report from Las Vegas REALTORS (formerly GLVAR) covers **AUGUST 2026**. It was released Wednesday, September 9, 2026. Key figures, direct from the LVR press release:

- Median price, existing single-family homes sold through the MLS in August 2026: **$475,000**, down 1.0% from August 2025, and below the all-time high of $490,000 set in May and June 2026.
- Median price, condos and townhomes: **$299,900**, up 0.6% year over year, below the record $315,000 set in October 2024.
- Total existing homes, condos and townhomes sold in August: **2,252**. Home sales down 1.7% year over year, condo and townhome sales down 7.4%.
- Single-family homes listed without an offer at the end of August: **7,590**, up 5.3% year over year. Condos and townhomes listed without an offer: **2,714**, up 6.0%.
- Supply: just over **four and a half months**.
- 74.8% of existing homes and 68.9% of condos and townhomes sold within 60 days, versus 77.5% and 72.2% a year earlier.
- Cash transactions: **21.9%** of all sales, down from 22.9% a year earlier.
- Distressed sales (short sales plus foreclosures): **1.0%** of sales, versus 0.5% a year earlier.
- Total MLS transaction value in August: nearly $1.2 billion for homes, more than $136 million for condos, high-rise condos and townhomes.

Primary source for these numbers: Las Vegas REALTORS press release, carried in full by Nevada Business Magazine, "LVR Reports Fewer Homes Selling, and at Slightly Lower Prices," https://nevadabusiness.com/2026/09/lvr-reports-fewer-homes-selling-and-at-slightly-lower-prices/ (post dated September 8, 2026; the release itself says the report was issued Wednesday, September 9, 2026). Verified: loads with full content.

**This August LVR release is NOT returned as a story below.** The weekly run dated 2026-09-13 already covered it in at least three separate stories (median price, condo vs house split, and the national comparison). ### Conflicting figures recorded
- **Las Vegas median home value.** Las Vegas REALTORS reports a median SALE price of $475,000 for August. Zillow reports a typical HOME VALUE of $423,354 and Realtor.com reports a median LIST price of $469,000. These measure three different things (closed MLS sales, an index of all homes, and active asking prices). All three are recorded above with their own sources. Do not mix them in a single sentence without saying which is which.
- **Median days on market.** Realtor.com puts the metro at two days longer than a year ago. Redfin's city-level page has shown roughly 53 to 55 days.
