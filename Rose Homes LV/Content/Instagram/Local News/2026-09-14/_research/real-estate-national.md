- **Story Type:** National-to-Local
- **County/Area:** Las Vegas / Clark County
- **National Figure:** The 30-year fixed-rate mortgage averaged 6.76% as of September 10, 2026, up from 6.71% the week before. A year earlier it averaged 6.35%. The 15-year averaged 6.09%, up from 6.04%, versus 5.50% a year ago. (Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, September 10, 2026)

- **Summary:** Freddie Mac's weekly survey put the 30-year fixed at 6.76% on September 10, the second straight weekly increase and higher than the 6.35% average from a year ago. Over that same twelve months, the Las Vegas median single-family price fell 1.0% to $475,000. Rising borrowing costs slowed local sales but did not break local pricing.

- **Local Article Title:** Report: Las Vegas home prices dip again in August, sales slow
- **Date:** September 10, 2026 (national); September 9, 2026 (local)
- **Why It Matters:** A Las Vegas owner reading "rates are rising" should know the local price response to a full year of higher rates has been about 1 percent, not a crash.
- **Dedup note:** Prior runs covered 6.58%, 6.66% and "stuck in the mid-6s." What is new here is a brand new September 10 release, a new number, a second straight weekly increase, and the year-over-year crossover that means rates are now higher than they were last September.
