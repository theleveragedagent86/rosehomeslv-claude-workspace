### 9. Nevada Logged 933 Testing Irregularities Out of 1.5 Million Test Sessions Last Year
- **Category:** School Board and Education
- **Story Type:** Local
- **County/Area:** Clark County and statewide Nevada
- **Cut Reason:** Score
- **Summary:** The Nevada State Board of Education received the annual test security summary for 2025-26 on September 9. Out of roughly 1,500,000 test sessions statewide, there were 933 reported testing irregularities, about 0.06 percent. Improper test administration accounted for 38 percent of the irregularities, student cheating or misconduct 24 percent, and testing interruptions 20 percent. Recommended fixes include better web-based tracking software, revised training materials for testing staff, more frequent webinars for district staff and on-site monitoring by state staff. The board also heard an update on the new Nevada District Performance Framework and set a goal of raising the share of high school students graduating with a College and Career Readiness or advanced diploma from 36 percent to 50 percent by the end of 2030-31.
- **Why It Matters:** Test scores drive school star ratings and school choice decisions, so parents have a stake in whether the tests are administered cleanly.
