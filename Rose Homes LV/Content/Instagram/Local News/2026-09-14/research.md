- **Summary:** The Clark County Education Association unveiled a plan on September 9 that would end the abatement of the Local School Support Tax for data centers and revisit the 75 percent personal property tax abatement. ## Reference Data, Most Recent Las Vegas REALTORS Monthly Report

The most recent Las Vegas REALTORS monthly report covers AUGUST 2026 and was released Wednesday, September 9, 2026. The August sales report itself was fully covered by the 2026-09-13 run and is not a selected story. These figures are on the record here because several selected stories use them as the local counterpart.

- Median price, existing single-family homes sold through the MLS in August 2026: $475,000, down 1.0 percent from August 2025, below the all-time high of $490,000 set in May and June 2026.
- Median price, condos and townhomes: $299,900, up 0.6 percent year over year, below the record $315,000 set in October 2024.
- Total existing homes, condos and townhomes sold in August: 2,252. Home sales down 1.7 percent year over year, condo and townhome sales down 7.4 percent.
- Single-family homes listed without an offer at the end of August: 7,590, up 5.3 percent year over year. Condos and townhomes listed without an offer: 2,714, up 6.0 percent.
- Supply: just over four and a half months.
- 74.8 percent of existing homes and 68.9 percent of condos and townhomes sold within 60 days, versus 77.5 percent and 72.2 percent a year earlier.
- Cash transactions: 21.9 percent of all sales, down from 22.9 percent a year earlier.
- Distressed sales, short sales plus foreclosures: 1.0 percent of sales, versus 0.5 percent a year earlier.
- Total MLS transaction value in August: nearly $1.2 billion for homes, more than $136 million for condos, high-rise condos and townhomes.
- Primary source: Las Vegas REALTORS press release, carried in full by Nevada Business Magazine, "LVR Reports Fewer Homes Selling, and at Slightly Lower Prices," https://nevadabusiness.com/2026/09/lvr-reports-fewer-homes-selling-and-at-slightly-lower-prices/

## Conflicting Figures to Watch

- **Las Vegas median home value.** Las Vegas REALTORS reports a median SALE price of $475,000 for August. Zillow reports a typical HOME VALUE of $423,354. Realtor.com reports a median LIST price of $469,000. These measure three different things. Do not mix them in a single sentence without saying which is which.
- **Median days on market.** Realtor.com puts the metro at two days longer than a year ago. Redfin's city-level page has shown roughly 53 to 55 days. Las Vegas REALTORS reports the share selling within 60 days, 74.8 percent of homes. Use the LVR share-within-60-days number for local claims.
- **Las Vegas August sales direction.** Las Vegas REALTORS, working from MLS closings, reported local home sales down 1.7 percent year over year. Zillow's sales-count nowcast showed sales up 2.1 percent. No selected story is built on the Zillow sales figure.
