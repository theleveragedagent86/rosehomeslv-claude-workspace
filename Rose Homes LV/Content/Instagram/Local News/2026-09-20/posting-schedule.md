- **S24, viral rank 24, now P01.** Last quarter of the viral list, first post of the week. Henderson Silver Knights camp opens Monday at noon. Nothing else in the set expires that fast.

- **S22, viral rank 22, now P26 in Overflow.** Nostalgia listing with an unverified price and no date. It is the right story to sacrifice, and it doubles as the cleanest trial reel test in the set.
