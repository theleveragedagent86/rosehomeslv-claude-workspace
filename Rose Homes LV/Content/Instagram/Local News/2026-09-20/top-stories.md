- **Article Title:** "Nearly 43% of Clark County homes are not owner-occupied, records show"

- **Summary:** County tax filing data through 2025 obtained by the Review-Journal shows 332,040 of Clark County's 775,199 residential parcels, about 43 percent, are not occupied by their owner.
