# Research brief: new construction area pages (geo spokes)

Rose Homes LV (Ryan Rose, Real Broker LLC, Las Vegas). Each area gets a page at
`rosehomeslv.com/<area>-new-construction` that sits under the valley hub at
`/las-vegas-new-construction`. Today is September 2026. Your job is RESEARCH ONLY: write
`research.md` into your area's folder. Do not write HTML.

## Hard rules
- **Factual only.** Every number, name, and price carries a source URL and the source's date.
  If you cannot find it, write `NOT FOUND`. Never estimate, never fill from memory.
- **Freshness.** Prefer 2026 sources. Builder sites and the master-plan developer's own site beat
  third-party agent blogs. Treat competitor agent blogs (nevadarealestategroup.com etc.) as leads
  to verify, not as sources, and say so if one is the only source.
- **Clark County, Nevada only.**
- **No em-dashes** anywhere in the file.
- Budget: be thorough but stop at roughly 25 fetches/searches.

## What to collect (use these exact headings)

1. **Area snapshot**: what/where it is, city jurisdiction (City of Las Vegas, Henderson, North
   Las Vegas, or unincorporated Clark County), master plan developer if any, size, ZIP codes.
2. **Where building is happening now**: master plans / villages / districts with active new
   construction, and anything closing out or opening soon.
3. **Active neighborhoods table** (the most important section): one row per currently selling
   neighborhood. Columns: neighborhood | builder | village/master plan | product (SFR,
   townhome, condo, single-story, 55+) | sq ft range | beds | starting price as published |
   source URL | source date. Aim for every active neighborhood you can verify. Mark sold-out
   ones as sold out rather than omitting silently.
4. **Builders active in the area**: list. Use these names where they apply: Beazer, Blue Heron,
   Century Communities, Christopher Homes, D.R. Horton, Harmony Homes, KB Home, Lennar, LGI
   Homes, Meritage, Pulte / Del Webb, Richmond American, Shea, Signature Homes, Storybook
   Homes, Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers, Touchstone, Tri Pointe, Woodside. Note any builder
   active there that is NOT on this list.
5. **Price picture**: lowest and highest published starting prices, what drives the spread
   (lot premiums, views, product type). Median new-build price if a dated source exists.
6. **HOA, SID/LID, taxes**: master HOA and sub-HOA amounts with source; whether SID/LID
   assessments apply and to which areas; anything Clark County Assessor/Treasurer confirms.
7. **Schools**: CCSD zoned schools for the main new-construction pockets (elementary, middle,
   high) with source (CCSD zoning search or school pages). Planned new schools. Do NOT include
   ratings unless from a named dated source.
8. **Location and commute**: key freeways, drive distance/time to the Strip and Harry Reid
   Airport (from a named source or state as approximate miles via freeway).
9. **Amenities that matter to buyers**: parks, trails, community centers, shopping, hospitals.
   Only what's relevant to new-construction pockets.
10. **Buyer watch-outs specific to this area**: e.g. lot premiums, SID/LID, flight paths,
    wind, construction traffic, builder direct sales not on MLS, age-restricted rules.
11. **FAQ candidates**: 8 questions real buyers search about new construction in this area,
    each with a 2-3 sentence factual answer and its source.
12. **Existing Rose Homes content to link**: search
    `/Users/ryanrose/Downloads/Claude/Rose Homes LV/Content/Claude Blogs/` for folders/posts
    about this area, especially new construction, HOA/SID, schools, and village guides. List up
   to 12 candidates as: local file path | likely slug (the post's canonical URL if present,
   else filename minus the postNN-/batchN-postN- prefix). Do NOT fetch rosehomeslv.com, it is
   a JS app that returns nothing useful to fetchers. The lead verifies these against the live
   sitemap.
13. **Sources**: numbered list of every URL used with its date.

Keep the file tight: tables and bullets, no prose padding.
