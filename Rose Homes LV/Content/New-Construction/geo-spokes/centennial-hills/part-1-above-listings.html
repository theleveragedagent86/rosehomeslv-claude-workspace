Centennial Hills is an established area in northwest Las Vegas, centered on the US-95 and 215 Beltway interchange. It has no single master plan or master HOA. As of September 16, 2026, the builders' own sites show 9 neighborhoods selling new homes here. Most are in Sunstone, a separate master plan next to Skye Canyon, where Lennar, Shea's Trilogy, and Woodside are building. The rest are stand-alone neighborhoods on bigger lots by Toll Brothers and Lennar in 89131 and 89149. Starting prices run from $383,990 for Lennar's Axel at Sunstone to $1,094,990 at Lennar's The Estates. The tip: every neighborhood has its own HOA, and builders do not publish the amounts, so ask for them before you fall in love with a model.

What to know first

  1. Sunstone is where most of the building is. Seven of the 9 selling neighborhoods are there, at US-95 and Skye Canyon Park Drive.
  2. Sunstone is not Skye Canyon. They sit side by side off US-95, but they are different master plans with different HOAs, so confirm which one a lot is in.
  3. There is no master HOA for Centennial Hills. Each neighborhood sets its own fee, and none of the builder pages we checked publish it.
  4. Trilogy Sunstone by Shea is a gated 55+ community, with homes from $399,990 and a 15,000 square foot club.
  5. Want RV parking or a big single-story lot? Look at Toll Brothers' Elkhorn Grove in 89131 and Lennar's The Estates in 89149.
  6. Skye Summit is coming, not selling. KB Home plans to open Vertice at Skye Summit for sale in early 2027.
