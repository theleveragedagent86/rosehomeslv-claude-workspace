Where is Centennial Hills, and what is close by?

Centennial Hills is in the northwest corner of Las Vegas, built around the interchange of US-95 and the 215 Beltway. Its ZIP codes are 89131, 89149, 89143, and 89166.

  • Getting around: US-95, also signed I-11, and the 215 Beltway meet in the middle of the area. Sunstone sits right off US-95 at Skye Canyon Park Drive.
  • Town center: a 110 acre town center with Centennial Centre shopping, Centennial Hills Hospital, the Centennial Hills Library, the YMCA, and a park.
  • Parks and open space: Centennial Hills Park, Floyd Lamb Park at Tule Springs, and Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument.
  • Groceries: Woodside notes a Smith's Marketplace near Sunstone.
  • For 55+ buyers: Trilogy Sunstone's Cabochon Club has a pool, fitness, a culinary studio, a restaurant and bar, pickleball, and bocce.

Most of Centennial Hills is inside the City of Las Vegas, with some pockets of unincorporated Clark County. It is worth checking which one covers the exact lot.

What should you know before buying new in Centennial Hills?

Centennial Hills is a patchwork of neighborhoods, not one master plan, and that creates a few surprises for new buyers.

  • Sunstone or Skye Canyon? They sit side by side off US-95 but are different master plans with different HOAs. Confirm which one the lot is in.
  • Watch the ZIP code. Sunstone builder addresses read 89143, but some sites list Sunstone as 89166.
  • Get the HOA numbers in writing. None of the builders we checked publish them, and there is no master HOA to fall back on.
  • Read the 55+ rules. Trilogy Sunstone is age qualified and gated. Get the governing documents before you commit.
  • Closing out means fewer choices. Capella at Sunstone is marked Final Opportunity, so expect limited lots and plans.
  • Skye Summit is not open yet. The first sales are planned for early 2027, and a master plan of about 3,500 homes means years of grading and construction traffic near Tropical Parkway and the 215. Residents raised water concerns when it was approved.
  • Look past the MLS. Many new homes here are sold directly by the builder and may not show up on listing sites.
