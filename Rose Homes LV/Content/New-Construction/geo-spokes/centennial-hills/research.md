# Centennial Hills new construction: research

Researched 2026-09-16. "Accessed" = date the page was read live for this file (builder pages carry no publish date). Page target: `rosehomeslv.com/centennial-hills-new-construction`.

**Scope boundary:** Skye Canyon master plan (Skyeview, Sierra/Terra/Mesa/Arroyo by Century, etc.) has its own page and is excluded. Sunstone sits right next to Skye Canyon (US-95 at Skye Canyon Park Dr) but is a separate master plan, so it is included here. Skye Summit (Olympia) is also separate from Skye Canyon and is included as "coming".

**Fetch limits:** Zillow, Redfin, Homes.com, NewHomeSource and reviewjournal.com returned 403. Richmond American pages are JS-rendered and returned no data. Anything taken only from a search snippet is marked (snippet).

## 2. Where building is happening now

- **Sunstone master plan** (~630 acres, ~3,000+ homes planned, US-95 at Skye Canyon Park Dr, bounded roughly by Centennial Pkwy north and Grand Teton Dr south). Most active new-build pocket in the area. Builder addresses use ZIP 89143; one agent site calls it 89166 (conflict, verify). Sources [3], [6], [7], [8], [9].
  - Lennar: 5 active collections (Asher, Axel, Alia Pointe, Alia Village, Alia Estates) [3]
  - Shea: Trilogy Sunstone (55+, gated) [8]
  - Woodside: Capella at Sunstone, labeled "Final Opportunity" (closing out) [9]
  - Richmond American: Solstice at Sunstone [10] (snippet)
- **Infill estate lots, 89131 / 89149** (larger lots, RV parking, single-story):
  - Toll Brothers Elkhorn Grove, gated, 5819 Kings Bluff Ave 89131 [11]
  - Lennar The Estates, 9556 W Hammer Ln 89149 [12]
  - Lennar Braydon, Deer Springs & Eula 89149 (snippet, status NOT verified) [13]
  - Lennar Erhardt Estates, Elkhorn & Sisk 89131: SOLD OUT [13]
- **Skye Summit (coming, not selling yet).** Olympia Companies, ~505 acres, ~3,500 homes, north side of Tropical Pkwy west of the 215, west of Providence where Centennial Pkwy ends. Includes 20-acre La Madre Foothills Park. KB Home bought first tract Jan 2026 (~38.6 acres, $46.4M) for Vertice at Skye Summit, 299 homesites, land development summer 2026, **sales open early 2027**. Also reported: Century Communities 77-lot tract, D.R. Horton 175-lot subdivision; Pulte, Taylor Morrison, Tri Pointe named as builders. Sources [5], [14], [15] (RJ blocked; builder names from search snippet of RJ/other coverage).

## 8. Location and commute

- Freeways: US-95 (I-11) and CC-215 Beltway interchange at the area's center [1]. Sunstone sits at US-95 and Skye Canyon Park Dr [7].
- Strip: approx. 20 to 25 min off-peak via US-95 south (competitor blog, lead only) [19]; existing Rose Homes research says ~18 mi.
- Harry Reid Airport: approx. 25 mi (40 km) road distance per Rome2Rio [20]; 25 to 30 min via US-95 and I-15 (competitor blog) [19].
- Peak southbound US-95 congestion is the main commute risk (competitor blog, lead only) [19].

## 10. Buyer watch-outs

- Skye Canyon vs Sunstone confusion: side by side off US-95, different master plans, different HOAs. Confirm which one a lot is in.
- ZIP confusion: Sunstone builder addresses read 89143; some agent sites say 89166.
- 55+ rules at Trilogy Sunstone (age-qualified, gated) [8].
- Closing-out stock: Woodside Capella is "Final Opportunity", limited lot choice [9].
- Skye Summit is years of active grading and construction (buildout projected 2034 to 2035, per existing Rose Homes post, source not re-verified); residents raised water concerns at approval [5].
- Construction traffic and dust around Sunstone and Tropical Pkwy/215 while Skye Summit develops.
- HOA and SID amounts not published on builder pages; request disclosures before contract.
- Estate lots: RV garage and lot size push prices past $1M at The Estates [12].
- Wind: NOT FOUND in a source. Flight paths: NOT FOUND.

## 11. FAQ candidates

1. **Who is building new homes in Centennial Hills right now?** Lennar, Shea (Trilogy), Toll Brothers and Woodside have active communities on their own sites as of Sept 2026. Richmond American and KB Home show listings in the area too. Sources [3], [8], [9], [11].
2. **What is the cheapest new construction in Centennial Hills?** Lennar's Axel at Sunstone starts at $383,990 for an attached NextGen home. The lowest detached start found was Lennar Asher at $440,990. Source [3].
3. **Is there a 55+ new home community in Centennial Hills?** Yes. Trilogy Sunstone by Shea Homes is a gated 55+ community from $399,990 with a 15,000 sq ft club. Source [8].
4. **Is Sunstone part of Skye Canyon?** No. Sunstone is its own master plan at US-95 and Skye Canyon Park Drive, built by Lennar, Shea, Woodside and Richmond American. Sources [6], [7].
5. **What is Skye Summit and when can I buy there?** It is a 505-acre Olympia Companies master plan approved for about 3,500 homes near Tropical Pkwy and the 215. KB Home plans to open Vertice at Skye Summit for sale in early 2027. Source [14].
6. **Are there new homes with RV parking in Centennial Hills?** Yes. Toll Brothers Elkhorn Grove offers RV garage and parking options, and Lennar The Estates has RV-height garages on large lots. Sources [11], [12].
7. **Does Centennial Hills have one master HOA?** No. Each subdivision or master plan has its own HOA, so fees differ block to block. Published HOA amounts for current new builds were NOT FOUND. Source [1].
8. **What schools serve new homes in Sunstone?** Lennar lists Bilbray and Scherkenbach elementary, Cadwallader middle, and Arbor View High. Confirm with CCSD zoning before buying. Source [3].
