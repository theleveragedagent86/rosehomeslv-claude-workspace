Most new homes in Henderson are being built in four master plans: Cadence in east Henderson off Lake Mead Parkway, Inspirada in southwest Henderson, KB Home's Meriden, which opened April 10, 2026, and Lennar's Black Mountain Ranch. Cadence is the biggest, with 2,200 acres and up to 12,250 homes planned. Eleven builders are active, led by KB Home, Lennar, D.R. Horton, and Woodside. Published 2026 starting prices run from $326,990 for a KB Home townhome at Meriden to $724,990 at D.R. Horton's Symmetry Summit II in Cadence. Inspirada is closing out, so choices there are shrinking. One tip: the Cadence FAQ says Cadence has no SID or LID fees, but that is not true of every Henderson home, so check the exact parcel.

What to know first

  1. Meriden is the newest place to look. KB Home opened it on April 10, 2026, with five neighborhoods and townhomes from $326,990.
  2. Cadence has the most builders. Its site lists Beazer, Century Communities, D.R. Horton, Lennar, Richmond American, Taylor Morrison, and Woodside.
  3. Inspirada is in its last stretch. KB Home marks its two neighborhoods there Last Chance and Final Opportunity, so fewer homes are left.
  4. Cadence's official FAQ says there are no SID or LID fees. Some Cadence townhome and condo neighborhoods can still have other assessment fees, so ask.
  5. Several Cadence builder pages were last updated in 2024 or 2025. For those neighborhoods, get a current price sheet from the builder before you compare.
  6. Some builder sites label Lake Las Vegas homes as Henderson. Those are on my separate Lake Las Vegas page, not in this table.

Where in Henderson are new homes going up?

Four master plans carry most of the building: Cadence in the east, Inspirada in the southwest, KB Home's Meriden, and Lennar's Black Mountain Ranch. A few luxury custom homes are also going up in MacDonald Highlands.

The Cadence FAQ is clear that Cadence has no SID or LID fees. You may still read blogs that quote SID or LID ranges for Cadence, but those do not match the master plan's own answer. Other parts of Henderson can be a different story. The City of Henderson bills its LIDs through Assessment Management Group, and a balance can pass to the buyer when a home sells. Before you sign, ask for the LID status on the exact parcel.

Cadence's FAQ also says Henderson has the lowest tax rate in the Las Vegas Valley. That is the developer's claim, so check the rate on your parcel with the county.

Where are the new Henderson neighborhoods, and what is close by?

All of the neighborhoods on this page are in the City of Henderson. Cadence is on the east side, Inspirada is in the southwest, and Meriden sits near I-11 and I-215.

  • Cadence: off Lake Mead Parkway. Cadence says it is minutes from Lake Mead National Recreation Area, Harry Reid International Airport, and the Strip.
  • Cadence parks: a Central Park of nearly 50 acres with two amphitheaters, splash pads, and pickleball, a 100-acre sports park, 10 neighborhood parks, and nearly 450 acres of open space. The Central Park Pool is open May 1 to October 31.
  • Near Cadence: the Water Street District, Galleria at Sunset, Cowabunga Bay, America First Center, and the Henderson Bird Viewing Preserve.
  • Meriden: KB Home points to quick access to Interstates 11 and 215.
  • Black Mountain Ranch: Lennar says Las Vegas is 15 miles away.
  • Inspirada: the Solista Community Center, the Toll Brothers Clubhouse, and weekly community events.

Drive times change a lot by time of day. If a commute matters, test it at rush hour before you pick a lot.

What should you know before buying new in Henderson?

Henderson has good choices at many price points, but a few things catch new buyers off guard.

  • Check the price date. Several Cadence builder pages were last updated in 2024 or 2025. Ask the builder for a dated price sheet.
  • Closeouts move fast. Inspirada neighborhoods carry Last Chance and Final Opportunity badges, Lennar marks Black Mountain Ranch Last Chance, and three Lennar Cadence neighborhoods are already sold out.
  • Ask about stairs. Avenues and Peaks at Meriden are three-story plans, and Midtown in Cadence has three-story plans too.
  • Budget for the lot. KB Home says a homesite premium may apply at every Henderson neighborhood.
  • Get every fee. Cadence has sub-associations, and some townhome and condo neighborhoods can add non-HOA assessments.
  • Plan the pool. In Cadence, a backyard pool needs Design Review Committee approval.
  • Know your neighbors. Cadence includes build-to-rent operators such as American Homes 4 Rent and Elysian Homes.
  • Check the address. Builder sites label some Lake Las Vegas homes as Henderson. Those are on my separate Lake Las Vegas page.

Questions about new construction in Henderson

What is the cheapest new construction in Henderson right now?

Meadows at Meriden by KB Home, with two-story townhomes from $326,990 as of September 16, 2026. The lowest detached homes start in the $350,000s, like Liberty at Meriden from $358,990.

Does Cadence have SID or LID fees?

The Cadence master plan FAQ says there are no SID or LID fees. Some townhome and condo neighborhoods in Cadence can have non-HOA assessment fees, so check each neighborhood.

Is Inspirada still building new homes?

Yes, but it is closing out. KB Home marks its Inspirada neighborhoods Last Chance and Final Opportunity, and Lennar's Lucere Place starts from $578,990.

Who builds in Cadence?

Cadence lists Beazer, Century Communities, D.R. Horton, Lennar, Richmond American, Taylor Morrison, and Woodside. Homes range from 1,300 to 4,425 square feet, including townhomes, duplexes, and one, two, and three-story homes.

What is Meriden in Henderson?

Meriden is a KB Home master plan that opened April 10, 2026, near Galleria Drive and Stephanie Street with access to I-11 and I-215. It has five neighborhoods: Avenues, Glades, Liberty, Meadows, and Peaks.

How big is Cadence?

Cadence covers 2,200 acres and plans up to 12,250 homes plus 1.1 million square feet of commercial space. It has nearly 450 acres of parks and open space, including a Central Park of nearly 50 acres.

What schools serve Inspirada new homes?

Builders list Robert and Sandy Ellis Elementary, Del E. Webb Middle, and Liberty High School for Inspirada. Always confirm the exact address with the CCSD zoning search.

Are there 55 and better new homes in Henderson?

Heritage at Cadence by Lennar is an age-qualified neighborhood inside Cadence, so ask for current availability. Del Webb's Henderson 55 and better community is in Lake Las Vegas, covered on my Lake Las Vegas page.
