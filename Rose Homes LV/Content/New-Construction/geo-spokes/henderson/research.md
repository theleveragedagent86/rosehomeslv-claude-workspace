## 2. Where building is happening now

- **Cadence** (east Henderson, off Lake Mead Pkwy). Builders listed on cadencenv.com: Beazer, Century, D.R. Horton, Lennar, Richmond American, Taylor Morrison, Woodside. Sales began Dec 2014. Official price range "$300,000s to the $700,000s". Lennar shows Cadence "4 Communities", $390,990 to $598,550. [1][3][9]
- **Inspirada** (southwest Henderson, 89044). Closeout. KB Home badges "Last Chance" (Gardens) and "Final Opportunity To Own" (Landings); Lennar "2 Communities" (Lucere Place/Square); Toll Brothers Amiata townhomes; Tri Pointe Highview townhomes. [9][10][12][13]
- **Meriden** (KB Home, W Galleria Dr / N Stephanie St area, near I-11 and I-215). Opened 2026-04-10 with 5 neighborhoods from the mid $300,000s. [11][10]
  - Note: competitor blog calls Meriden "West Henderson" with ~940 homes and $475K-$850K. Those numbers do NOT match KB's own site (from $326,990). Treat as unverified. [17]
- **KB Home Caspian Bay** (Landings and Reserves; 676 Lunar Lake Way, Henderson 89002). Master plan affiliation NOT FOUND. [10]
- **Black Mountain Ranch** (Lennar MPC, badge "Last Chance!", $355,990 to $843,990). Only one community (Highpointe) was individually visible in Lennar data. [9]
- **Lennar The Presley** (gated townhomes, from $363,990). Location/master plan NOT FOUND. [9]
- **MacDonald Highlands / Ascaya (custom luxury)**. Christopher Homes SkyVu sales office at 1797 MacDonald Ranch Dr [16]. Blue Heron listed 3 MacDonald Highlands spec homes, $13.5M to $20M, completions through April 2027 (per search snippet of Review-Journal article; full article returned 403, unverified) [18]. Ascaya active product/prices: NOT FOUND from an official source.
- **Coming / other**: Inspirada Station casino and Centurion mixed-use at Inspirada are mentioned only by a competitor source (lead, unverified) [17].
- **Overlap with Lake Las Vegas page (excluded)**: Toll Brothers Incanta Lago ($939,995, 427 Terra Alta) is inside Lake Las Vegas [5][15]; Lennar Lake Las Vegas ($474,990 to $1,169,990: Lago Del Sol, Piazza Paradiso) [9]; Del Webb Henderson 55+ is Lake Las Vegas only [8].

## 5. Price picture

- **Lowest published starting price**: $326,990, Meadows at Meriden townhomes, KB Home [10].
- **Lowest SFR**: $350,990 is a D.R. Horton townhome; lowest detached SFR is Woodside Meridian $355,950 (Cadence) [19] or Lennar Highpointe $355,990 (multi-family product) [9]. KB Liberty at Meriden $358,990 [10].
- **Highest production starting price**: D.R. Horton Symmetry Summit II $724,990 [6]; Lennar Lucere Square $656,990 (range to $728,395) [9].
- **Luxury/custom**: Blue Heron MacDonald Highlands specs $13.5M-$20M (unverified) [18].
- **Drivers of spread**: product type (townhome vs 3,000+ sq ft SFR), master plan (Inspirada closeout inventory is priced above Cadence and Meriden), lot premiums (KB: "Homesite premium may apply" on every listing [10]), view/hillside custom lots (MacDonald Highlands).
- **Median new-build price**: No official dated source. Competitor lead only: median new-build list $606,450 and 90-day median sold $509,495 as of 2026-07-12 [17]; another competitor snippet: Henderson new-home median list $558,988 (April 2026) [21]. Both unverified.

## 10. Buyer watch-outs specific to this area

- **SID/LID confusion**: Cadence officially has none [1], but competitor blogs publish SID/LID dollar ranges for Cadence [17]. Inspirada LIDs reported but unverified [23]. Always pull the parcel's AMG/Treasurer balance [24].
- **Lot premiums**: KB lists "Homesite premium may apply" on every Henderson community [10].
- **Closeout inventory**: Inspirada neighborhoods carry "Last Chance" / "Final Opportunity" badges; Lennar Black Mountain Ranch "Last Chance!"; Lennar Preston Terrace, Preston Pointe, Serenity Place sold out [3][9][10].
- **Three-story product**: Meriden Avenues (townhomes) and Peaks (SFR) are three-story; Lennar Midtown has three-story plans [3][10].
- **Sub-HOAs and non-HOA assessments** in Cadence townhome/condo neighborhoods and Heritage at Cadence [1].
- **Pools need Design Review Committee approval** in Cadence [1].
- **Build-to-rent neighbors**: Cadence includes rental operators (AMH, Elysian, Ascend Symmetry, Adler, Element 12) [20].
- **Lake Las Vegas overlap**: builder sites label Incanta Lago and Lennar Lago Del Sol / Piazza Paradiso as "Henderson". Route those to the LLV page [5][9][15].
- **Stale price data on master plan site**: several cadencenv.com builder pages were last modified 2024-2025; confirm with builder before quoting.
- **Local content errors found**: Rose Homes posts say Cadence has 13,250 homes (official 12,250) and Henderson LIDs $500-$3,000+ "on many newer communities" (unsourced). Fix when linking.

## 11. FAQ candidates

1. **Does Cadence have SID or LID fees?** The Cadence master plan FAQ states there are no SID/LID fees. Some townhome or condo neighborhoods can have non-HOA assessment fees, so check each neighborhood. Source [1].
2. **What is the cheapest new construction in Henderson right now?** KB Home's Meadows at Meriden two-story townhomes start from $326,990 as of Sept 16, 2026. The lowest single-family options start in the mid $350,000s at Cadence and Meriden. Sources [10][19].
3. **Is Inspirada still building new homes?** Yes, but it is closing out. KB Home marks its Inspirada neighborhoods "Last Chance" and "Final Opportunity," and Lennar lists two remaining Inspirada communities from $578,990. Sources [9][10].
4. **Who builds in Cadence?** Cadence lists Beazer, Century Communities, D.R. Horton, Lennar, Richmond American, Taylor Morrison, and Woodside. Homes range from 1,300 to 4,425 sq ft, including townhomes, duplexes, and one-, two-, and three-story homes. Sources [1][20].
5. **What is Meriden in Henderson?** Meriden is a KB Home master plan that opened April 10, 2026, near Galleria Drive and Stephanie Street with access to I-11 and I-215. It has five neighborhoods: Avenues, Glades, Liberty, Meadows, and Peaks. Sources [10][11].
6. **What schools serve Inspirada new homes?** Builders list Robert and Sandy Ellis Elementary, Del E. Webb Middle, and Liberty High School for Inspirada. Always confirm the exact address with CCSD zoning. Sources [10][13].
7. **How big is Cadence?** Cadence covers 2,200 acres and plans up to 12,250 homes plus 1.1 million sq ft of commercial space. It has nearly 450 acres of parks and open space, including a nearly 50-acre Central Park. Source [1].
8. **Are there 55+ new homes in Henderson?** Heritage at Cadence by Lennar is an age-qualified sub-association inside Cadence. Del Webb's Henderson 55+ community is in Lake Las Vegas. Current Heritage at Cadence inventory: NOT FOUND. Sources [1][8].
