Lake Las Vegas is a master-planned community of about 3,600 acres around a 320 acre man-made lake in east Henderson, ZIP 89011. Thirteen new home neighborhoods are selling or taking reservations: the 12 on the Lake Las Vegas new homes page, updated August 21, 2026, plus Blue Heron's new Liora. Eight builders are active: Beazer, Blue Heron, Del Webb, Lennar, Richmond American, Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers, and Tri Pointe. Starting prices run from $434,990 at Del Webb, a 55 and better community, and $490,990 at Taylor Morrison's Verona for all ages, up to $1,756,000 at Blue Heron's Arvada on The Island. The one tip: get every HOA fee in writing, because the master, neighborhood, and SouthShore sub-master associations stack up.

What to know first

  1. Thirteen neighborhoods and eight builders are active, with starting prices from $434,990 for 55 and better and $490,990 for all ages.
  2. Most of the lower priced all-ages homes are on the North Shore side, at Verona, Lago Del Sol, and Piazza Paradiso. Most of the million dollar homes sit on The Island, in SouthShore, and on the new Liora peninsulas.
  3. You can pay more than one HOA. Homes belong to the Lake Las Vegas Master Association, SouthShore adds a sub-master, and some neighborhoods may add their own. Get every amount from the builder.
  4. Henderson calls its assessment districts LIDs. Whether a lot carries one depends on the parcel, so look it up before you sign.
  5. A private dock is an option, not a given, at Velaris, Arvada, and The Island. Ask what it costs and what is included.
  6. Register your own agent on your first visit to a Lake Las Vegas model. The sales team works for the builder.

What will you pay each month on top of the mortgage?

At Lake Las Vegas you are looking at more than one HOA, property tax, and on some parcels a City of Henderson LID payment. Here is how each one works.

Builder websites show HOA figures for a few neighborhoods, $262 at Taylor Morrison's Verona, $351 at Tri Pointe's Lakeview Ridge, and $259 and $267 at Lennar's Lago Del Sol, checked September 16, 2026. None of them says what period that covers or whether it includes every association, so ask for the full total in writing.

The layers are what catch people. Ask the builder for the master, sub-master, and neighborhood amounts together, and read the association documents before you close. I cover how to compare these in HOA fees on Las Vegas new construction.

The City of Henderson calls its assessment districts LIDs, and the bills are handled by Assessment Management Group. Whether a Lake Las Vegas lot carries one depends on the parcel, so look it up by parcel number and ask the builder before you sign. I explain how these work in SID, LID, and taxes explained.

Where is Lake Las Vegas, and what is close by?

Lake Las Vegas is on the east edge of the valley in the City of Henderson. To get there, take the 215 east toward Henderson until the freeway ends, keep going east on Lake Mead Parkway, and turn left on Lake Las Vegas Parkway.

  • Getting around: an older developer page puts the Strip about 25 minutes away. Your real drive depends on the 215 and Lake Mead Parkway at your time of day.
  • The lake: 320 acres, with electric Duffy boat rentals, paddleboards, kayaks, pedal boats, and a wakeboard cable.
  • The Village at MonteLago: waterfront dining, Seasons Grocery, and seasonal concerts and events.
  • Golf and the Sports Club: Reflection Bay Golf Club and SouthShore Country Club, plus the private Lake Las Vegas Sports Club for residents, with pools, tennis, pickleball, a fitness center, and a spa.
  • Trails: the paved 34 mile River Mountains Loop Trail, and the Historic Railroad Trail, with 5 tunnels toward Hoover Dam. Lake Mead access is close by.
  • Hospitals: St. Rose Dominican Rose de Lima on Lake Mead Parkway, Henderson Hospital, and St. Rose Siena.

Lake Las Vegas was first planned in the 1960s, went through bankruptcy and legal delays, and took off again in the mid-2010s with new investors. Today it has roughly 4,500 homes, and construction is still going. I compare it with another Henderson favorite in Lake Las Vegas vs Seven Hills.

What should you know before buying new at Lake Las Vegas?

Lake Las Vegas has a lot going for it, but a few things catch new buyers off guard.

  • Ask for a dated price sheet. The Lake Las Vegas site and builder sites show different prices for the same neighborhood.
  • Get every HOA number. Master, sub-master in SouthShore, and neighborhood fees can all apply. Builder sites show a single number without saying what it covers.
  • Check the LID on the exact lot. Henderson assessments depend on the parcel.
  • Price the dock separately. At Velaris, Arvada, and The Island, a private dock is an option, not standard.
  • Know the Del Webb lawsuit. In December 2025 the Del Webb at Lake Las Vegas Community Association sued Pulte Homes of Nevada, alleging nearly 90 homes are sinking and that retaining and rockery walls have problems. Pulte disputes how serious it is. It says repairs are done or underway with 43 homeowners, under 5% of homes. That is from the Las Vegas Review-Journal, May 4, 2026. If you look at Del Webb, ask about it and read the disclosures.
  • Check age rules in writing. Del Webb at Lake Las Vegas is age restricted, 55 and better.
  • Liora pricing is early. Blue Heron took reservations at founders pricing before the official release, so get final pricing in writing.
  • Know what is next door. Aries by Pulte is a separate community, not part of Lake Las Vegas. It covers more than 1,100 acres on the south side of Lake Mead Parkway, at the former Three Kids Mine. It has a grand opening in September 2026 and 5 subdivisions from $300,000 to $500,000 (Review-Journal, September 11, 2026). Expect building activity across the parkway for a while.
  • Look past the MLS. Many new homes here are sold directly by the builder and may not show up on listing sites.
