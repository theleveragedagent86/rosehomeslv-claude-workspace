## 2. Where building is happening now

- **Developer site lists 13 new-home options** (12 builder neighborhoods + The Island custom lots), page modified 2026-08-21 [1].
- **North Shore / Lago Del Sol area (production):** Lennar Lago Del Sol (Ridges, Hills, Falls collections) and Piazza Paradiso (Vita, Serena); Taylor Morrison Verona; Beazer Verona (56 two-story homes off Strada Veneto) [1][7][8].
- **Reflection Bay area:** Taylor Morrison Portofino (single-story, 83 acres / 274 lots per RJ 2022 via search snippet) [1][10]; Marbella (TM, 34 lots) SOLD OUT [10].
- **Hillside/view:** Tri Pointe Lakeview Ridge (53 elevated homesites off Highland Shores Dr and Lakestone Pl) [1][3]; Toll Brothers Incanta Lago (gated) [1][12].
- **SouthShore (guard-gated):** Richmond American Salerno Summit [1][13].
- **The Island / lakefront luxury:** Blue Heron Shoreline, Velaris, Arvada; The Island custom half-acre lots from $900,000+ [1][4].
- **Opening now:** Blue Heron **Liora**, 60 lots on two north-shore peninsulas (about 15.6 and 11 acres) bought March 2026 for about $33.3M; groundbreaking spring 2026; site in "pre-grand opening release" ahead of "official August release"; called "the last remaining lakefront parcels" [9][14][15].
- **55+:** Del Webb at Lake Las Vegas still selling; RJ 2026-06 (via search) says over 50% built out [6][16].
- **Closed out:** Marbella (TM) sold out [10]; Riviera Vista (Lennar) "Sold out!" [8].
- **Adjacent, NOT inside the master plan:** Pulte **Aries**, 1,100+ acres on the south side of Lake Mead Pkwy at the former Three Kids Mine, grand opening Sept 2026, 5 subdivisions from $300,000 to $500,000; Del Webb expected to sell there next year [17]. Do not label it Lake Las Vegas.

## 9. Amenities that matter to buyers

- **The lake:** 320 acres; electric Duffy boat rental, paddleboard, kayak, pedal boat, flyboard, wakeboard cable [1][2 village page mod 2026-03-13].
- **The Village (MonteLago):** waterfront dining, Seasons Grocery, seasonal concerts and events [2].
- **Golf:** Reflection Bay Golf Club, SouthShore Country Club [1].
- **Lake Las Vegas Sports Club** (private, residents): pools, tennis, pickleball, fitness center, spa, teen space; home of the UNLV Sports Science Center; 101 Via Vin Santo [2 sports club page mod 2025-07-15].
- **Trails:** Terrazza Park trail connector; River Mountains Loop Trail (34 miles paved); Lake Mead Historic Railroad Trail (5 tunnels toward Hoover Dam) [2 trails page mod 2022-01-04].
- **Neighborhood amenities:** Liora: trails, dog park, pickleball, beach cove, private boat dock [9]. Shoreline: walking paths, pet areas, fitness stations, lake access [4]. The Island: guard-gated, park, private beach, dock option [1].
- **Hospitals (per LLV, mod 2025-08-25):** St. Rose Dominican Rose de Lima (102 E Lake Mead Pkwy), Henderson Hospital (US-95 and Galleria Dr), St. Rose Siena (3001 St. Rose Pkwy) [2].

## 10. Buyer watch-outs

- **Del Webb construction-defect lawsuit:** Del Webb at Lake Las Vegas Community Association sued Pulte Homes of Nevada (filed Dec 2025); alleges nearly 90 homes sinking, retaining/rockery wall issues. Pulte disputes severity; says repairs done or underway with 43 homeowners, under 5% of homes [5, RJ 2026-05-04].
- **Layered HOAs:** master + SouthShore sub-master + neighborhood sub-HOA; online portals often miss tiers. Read CIC resale certificate [19].
- **LID:** Henderson LIDs billed via AMG; confirm per APN, some builders paid off at land purchase (agent claim) [18][19].
- **Transfer fees/capital contributions** vary widely by HOA [19].
- **Waterfront premiums and docks:** dock is an "option," not standard, at Velaris, Arvada, The Island [1].
- **Prices differ by source:** developer page vs builder page disagree (Lakeview Ridge $944,000 vs $899,500; Shoreline "High $900,000s" vs $1,031,000). Use builder page on publish date [1][3].
- **Distance:** east edge of the valley; commute depends on I-215/Lake Mead Pkwy [2].
- **Construction nearby:** Pulte Aries (1,100+ acres) building out across Lake Mead Pkwy [17].
- **Wildlife:** TM markets Verona "amidst a serene lake and bighorn sheep" [1]; wildlife-rule specifics NOT FOUND.
- **55+ rules:** Del Webb is age-restricted 55+ [1].
- **Liora pre-sale:** founders pricing on reservations before official release; final pricing still pending at access [9].

## 11. FAQ candidates

1. **Who is building new homes at Lake Las Vegas in 2026?** Beazer, Blue Heron, Del Webb, Lennar, Richmond American, Taylor Morrison, Toll Brothers and Tri Pointe all list active neighborhoods. The developer site shows 13 new-home options including custom lots on The Island. Source [1], 2026-08-21.
2. **What is the cheapest new home at Lake Las Vegas?** Del Webb, a 55+ community, starts at $434,990. For all ages, Taylor Morrison's Verona starts at $490,990 and Beazer's Verona at $499,990. Source [1][6].
3. **Are there waterfront new homes at Lake Las Vegas?** Yes. Blue Heron's Velaris and Arvada on The Island offer waterfront homesites with dock options, and Liora offers 60 lakefront lots from $1,677,000. Source [1][9].
4. **Is Lake Las Vegas in Henderson or Las Vegas?** It is in the City of Henderson, ZIP 89011, on the east side of the valley. Source [5][3].
5. **What schools serve new homes at Lake Las Vegas?** Builders list Josh Stevens Elementary, B. Mahlon Brown Academy of International Studies and Basic Academy of International Studies. Confirm your address with CCSD zoning. Source [3][7].
6. **How much are HOA fees at Lake Las Vegas?** Every home pays the master association, reported at $459 per quarter in 2024, plus a neighborhood sub-HOA. SouthShore adds a sub-master fee of about $1,017 per quarter. Source [19] (agent source, 2024; verify in CIC documents).
7. **Do Lake Las Vegas homes have LID assessments?** Henderson uses Local Improvement Districts, billed through Assessment Management Group. Whether a lot carries one depends on the parcel, so look it up by APN. Source [18][19].
8. **Is there a 55+ community at Lake Las Vegas?** Yes, Del Webb at Lake Las Vegas offers single-story homes from 1,285 to 2,736 sq ft starting at $434,990. Source [6].
(Alt) **How far is Lake Las Vegas from the Strip?** The developer says about 25 minutes via I-215 and Lake Mead Pkwy. Source [2].
