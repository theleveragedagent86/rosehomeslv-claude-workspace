North Las Vegas is its own city on the north side of the Las Vegas Valley, and most of its new building sits along and north of the 215 Beltway. As of September 16, 2026, builder websites show 18 new home collections selling from three builders: D.R. Horton, KB Home, and Lennar. Most of the detached homes are near Tule Springs, in D.R. Horton's gated Heartland at Tule Springs and KB Home's brand new Sandstone at Tule Springs. Starting prices run from $313,490 for a Lennar townhome at Heritage Square to the $721s for a six bedroom home at Heartland Summit. The one tip: builder pages do not clearly publish HOA or SID amounts, so get both in writing before you sign.

What to know first

  1. The lowest prices are townhomes. Lennar's gated Heritage Square starts at $313,490, and most new homes under $400,000 here are two-story townhomes.
  2. Most detached building is near Tule Springs. D.R. Horton has nine phases selling in Heartland at Tule Springs, and KB Home opened Sandstone at Tule Springs on September 4, 2026.
  3. Single-story costs more. Heartland Grove single-story homes start in the $520s, while a similar size two-story at Heartland Cottages tops out in the $461s.
  4. Clear HOA and SID amounts are not on the builder pages I checked. Ask for the HOA budget in writing and look up the parcel with the Clark County Treasurer.
  5. Phases sell and disappear fast. Two Heartland phase pages have already dropped off D.R. Horton's site, and another is marked Final Opportunities.
  6. The schools a builder lists as nearby are not your zoned schools. Check the exact address with CCSD.
