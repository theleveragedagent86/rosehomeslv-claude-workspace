Where is North Las Vegas, and what is close by?

North Las Vegas covers the north side of the valley. The newest building runs along and north of the 215 Beltway, from the Tule Springs area east toward I-15.

  • Getting around: the 215 Beltway, I-15, and I-11 all serve the city. Sandstone sits right at North 5th Street and the 215. Lennar places Heritage Square close to the 215 and Solara close to I-15.
  • Commute: drive your route at the hour you would actually drive it before you commit, especially if you work on the south end of the valley.
  • Nellis Air Force Base: Lennar markets Meridian, on the east side, for its closeness to the base.
  • Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument: Heartland markets trail access and views of the monument, and Sandstone plans a new trailhead to it.
  • Parks: Sandstone plans more than 12 acres of parks, Heartland has community parks and dog parks, and Tropical Breeze Park near Grandview has a splash pad and walking trails.
  • Golf: Aliante Golf Club, which D.R. Horton places 1 mile from Heartland Trails.

Every community on this page lists its city as North Las Vegas. That is its own city, separate from the City of Las Vegas, so check the city on any address you find online before you assume it belongs here.

What should you know before buying new in North Las Vegas?

The starting prices here are easy to like. A few things are worth checking before you fall for a model.

  • Know what the low price buys. Most new homes under $400,000 here are gated two-story townhomes. Single-story detached homes at Heartland start in the $520s.
  • Get the HOA and SID in writing. The builder pages do not clearly publish either one. Ask for the HOA budget and look up the parcel with the Clark County Treasurer.
  • Ask about every association. Some Tule Springs area homes pay a master and a sub-master HOA.
  • Move fast, but not blind. Two Heartland phases have already dropped off D.R. Horton's site, and Heartland Falls 3 Car is marked Final Opportunities.
  • Expect construction for years. Sandstone opened in September 2026 with more than 1,500 homes still to come, and two of its neighborhoods are not open yet.
  • Visit near Nellis at different times. If you are looking at the east side, like Meridian, drive by in the morning and evening to hear the area for yourself.
  • Nearby is not zoned. The schools on a builder's page are nearby, not always the ones your address is zoned for.
