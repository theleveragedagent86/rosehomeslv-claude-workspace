## 8. Location and commute

- **Freeways**: CC-215 Beltway, I-15, I-11. Sandstone sits at North 5th St and CC-215 [S1]. Heritage Square "convenient proximity to I-215"; Solara "proximity to I-15" [S7] [S6]. Meridian near Nellis Air Force Base [S8].
- **To the Strip**: approximately 15 miles / 30 minutes from Aliante (existing local research, agent source, undated). Competitor blog says 20 to 30 min via I-15 or US-95 [LEAD].
- **To Harry Reid Airport**: approximately 15 to 16 miles / 25 to 28 minutes from Aliante (existing local research, undated). KB release notes "proximity to Harry Reid International Airport," no number [S1].
- **Job anchors**: Nellis AFB (Lennar Meridian copy [S8]); Apex Industrial Park and VA Southern Nevada Healthcare System on N Pecos Rd [LEAD S21].

## 9. Amenities that matter to buyers

- **Tule Springs Fossil Beds National Monument** (22,650 acres per search result [LEAD]). Heartland pages market trail access and views of the monument [S11] [S12].
- **Sandstone**: 12+ acres of planned parks, trail system, new trailhead to the Fossil Beds monument, pickleball/basketball courts, horseshoe pits, ramadas, playgrounds, dog park, BBQ/picnic areas [S1].
- **Heartland**: community parks, dog parks, walking trails; Aliante Golf Club "just 1 mile away" from Heartland Trails [S9].
- **Tropical Breeze Park** near Grandview: splash pad, walking trails, open grass fields [S4].
- **Aliante** (near Tule Springs pockets): Aliante Nature Discovery Park, Aliante Casino + Hotel + Spa, Aliante Marketplace (Smith's), ER at Aliante (MountainView freestanding ER), 24-mile trail system (aliante-research.md).

## 10. Buyer watch-outs specific to this area

- **Townhome-heavy entry level.** Most sub-$400K new product is gated two-story townhomes (Lennar Heritage Square, Solara, Grandview; D.R. Horton Heartland Trails) [S4-S9]. Single-story SFR starts in the $520s at Heartland [S11].
- **Lot premiums.** KB: "Homesite premium may apply" [S3].
- **SID exposure in Tule Springs area is claimed but unverified.** Pull the parcel on Clark County Treasurer and read the public offering statement [S21] [S23].
- **Layered HOAs.** Villages at Tule Springs uses master plus sub-master associations [S16] [S18].
- **Phases sell and disappear fast.** Two Heartland phase URLs already redirect to generic pages; D.R. Horton labels others "Final Opportunities" [S9] [S13].
- **Brand-new master plan = construction for years.** Sandstone opened Sept 2026 with 1,500+ homes to come; Meadows and Gardens still under construction [S1].
- **Nellis AFB proximity** (jet noise, east side pockets like Meridian): Lennar markets closeness to Nellis [S8]. No noise contour data pulled: NOT FOUND.
- **"Nearby" schools on builder sites are not zoning.** Heartland lists a middle school 4 to 5 miles away [S10].
- **Sunstone confusion.** Some agent blogs lump Sunstone into NLV. It is a northwest valley plan [S21].

## 11. FAQ candidates

1. **What is the cheapest new construction in North Las Vegas right now?**
   Lennar's Heritage Square townhomes start at $313,490 for a 2-bed, 1,014 sq ft plan. KB Home's upcoming Meadows at Sandstone is listed at "low $300s." Source: [S7] [S3], accessed 2026-09-16.
2. **Are there new single-story homes in North Las Vegas?**
   Yes. D.R. Horton's Heartland Grove has single-story homes from 1,839 sq ft starting in the $520s, and Heartland Manor Phase IV has single-story 2,300 to 2,754 sq ft homes from the $601s. Source: [S11] [S12].
3. **What is Sandstone at Tule Springs?**
   KB Home's new North Las Vegas master plan at North 5th Street and the 215 Beltway, opened September 4, 2026, with more than 1,500 homes planned across four neighborhoods. Homes start in the $300,000s. Source: [S1].
4. **Which builders are building in North Las Vegas?**
   D.R. Horton, KB Home, and Lennar all have active North Las Vegas communities on their own websites. Aggregator listings also show Tri Pointe, Richmond American, and Touchstone. Source: [S2-S14] [S19].
5. **Do new homes in Tule Springs have SID fees?**
   Some Tule Springs-area parcels are reported to carry special assessments, but no official amount was confirmed. Check the parcel on the Clark County Treasurer site before you sign. Source: [S21] [LEAD], [S23].
6. **How much are HOA fees for new homes in North Las Vegas?**
   It depends on the community. Lennar page data shows HOA figures of 149 to 284 for its NLV townhome communities, and one Villages at Tule Springs parcel reports $64/month total. Source: [S4-S8], [S18] [LEAD]. (Confirm billing period with Lennar before publishing.)
7. **What schools serve Heartland at Tule Springs?**
   D.R. Horton lists Triggs Elementary, Saville Middle, and Legacy High as nearby schools. Confirm your exact address on the CCSD zoning search. Source: [S9-S12].
8. **Is there new 55+ construction in North Las Vegas?**
   Del Webb at North Ranch is reported as built out in 2024, so it is resale now. No other active 55+ new-build community was verified in the City of North Las Vegas. Source: [S22] [LEAD].
