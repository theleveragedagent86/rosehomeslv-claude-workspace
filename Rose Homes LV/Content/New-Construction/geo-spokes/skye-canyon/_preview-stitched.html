Skye Canyon is a 1,700 acre master plan by Olympia Companies in northwest Las Vegas, off US-95 at Skye Canyon Park Drive. As of September 16, 2026, the developer lists three builders: Century Communities, LGI Homes, and Toll Brothers. Century sells four neighborhoods at Skyeview, which is inside Skye Canyon: Arroyo, Sierra, Terra, and Mesa. LGI sells Topaz townhomes, and Toll Brothers' gated Paloma is in its final phase. Prices run from a $376,990 base plan at Arroyo at Skyeview to $800,000 at Paloma. The one tip: part of Skye Canyon sits in a City of Las Vegas special improvement district, SID 610, so check the exact parcel before you sign.

What to know first

  1. Only three builders are active, per the developer: Century Communities, LGI Homes, and Toll Brothers. Many websites still list Lennar, Pulte, and Beazer as building here.
  2. Skyeview is part of Skye Canyon. Century's Mesa, Terra, Sierra, and Arroyo at Skyeview are four of the six neighborhoods selling.
  3. Century shows two different starting prices for some collections. The Century prices on this page are the base plan price for the smallest plan.
  4. Mesa at Skyeview is the single-story choice, with base plans from $488,990.
  5. Toll Brothers' Paloma is in its final phase, with only a limited number of move-in ready homes left.
  6. Part of Skye Canyon is in a City of Las Vegas SID. Ask whether your exact lot is in one, and what it costs each year.

A few things move the price. First is the type of home, since a townhome and a detached home are different products. Second is size, from 1,256 square feet at Topaz to 2,900 at Paloma. Third is the builder. Century and LGI sell entry and move-up homes, and Toll Brothers sells larger homes. Single-story costs more per foot, too. Mesa's smallest plan is about the same price as Terra's smallest, with 157 fewer square feet. Builder pages do not publish lot premiums, so ask for them.

Prices also move between releases. Arroyo's Beverly plan was $372,990 and Sierra's 1602 plan was $433,990 in earlier price checks, and both went up. Sierra's 1965 plan went down, from $478,990 to $471,990. Paloma shows two numbers as well: $800,000 on Toll's site and "from the $600s" on the developer's site. Always work from a dated price sheet.

What will you pay each month on top of the mortgage?

Plan for an HOA, property tax, and on some lots a special improvement district payment. The builders do not publish HOA dues, so get the exact numbers in writing.

CostWhat it isAmount
Master HOASkye Canyon's community associationNot published by the developer or builders, ask for the exact figure
Neighborhood HOAYour own neighborhood's association, if it has oneNot published, ask the builder
SID paymentRepays bonds that built roads, sewer, drainage, and water linesVaries by parcel, check the lot
Property taxClark County, with a 3% yearly cap on increases for a primary homeBased on assessed value

Skye Canyon HOA phone number, from the developer's site: (702) 786-0207. HOA figures quoted on other websites could not be traced to a primary source, so they are not used here.

The SID is the one people miss. The City of Las Vegas set up Special Improvement District No. 610 for Skye Canyon Phase 3. It sold $12.5 million in bonds for streets, storm and sanitary sewer, drainage, and water lines on a 267 acre site planned for 1,282 homes. Which Skyeview, Topaz, or Paloma lots sit inside SID 610 is not published, and neither is the yearly amount. Before you sign, check the parcel with the Clark County Treasurer or ask the title company for the preliminary report.

You may read online that Skye Canyon has no SID. The federal highway agency's profile of SID 610 shows otherwise for Phase 3, so check your lot.

Where is Skye Canyon, and what is close by?

Skye Canyon is in far northwest Las Vegas, off US-95 at Skye Canyon Park Drive. It is inside the City of Las Vegas, and the active builder addresses are all in ZIP 89166.

  • Getting around: US-95 at Skye Canyon Park Drive. The developer lists the Strip at 18 miles and downtown Las Vegas at 16 miles.
  • Mountains: Mt. Charleston Resort is 17 miles away.
  • Shopping: Skye Canyon Marketplace, anchored by Smith's Marketplace, is half a mile away.
  • Community centers: Skye Center is about 8,000 square feet, and Skye Fitness is about 10,000 square feet with a pool and sports facilities.
  • Parks: Skye Canyon Park and several parks with splash pads and sports courts. Officer Alyn Beck Memorial Park is 1.2 miles away, and Floyd Lamb Park at Tule Springs is 2 miles away.
  • Medical: the developer lists a hospital and medical center 3 miles away.

What should you know before buying new in Skye Canyon?

Skye Canyon has good choices under $500,000, but a few things catch new buyers off guard.

  • Check who is really building. Many websites still list Lennar, Pulte, and Beazer in Skye Canyon. The developer lists only Century Communities, LGI Homes, and Toll Brothers.
  • Ask which price you are looking at. Century shows a header price and a lower base plan price for Arroyo, Sierra, and Mesa.
  • Get the HOA numbers in writing. Builders do not publish master or neighborhood dues. Ask for the HOA documents with the exact amounts.
  • Check the SID on the exact lot. SID 610 covers Skye Canyon Phase 3, and the yearly amount depends on the parcel.
  • Move fast on Paloma, or plan on resale. Toll Brothers is in its final phase with limited move-in ready homes.
  • Know the lot and home type. Sierra homes sit on cul-de-sac lots, and Arroyo and Topaz are townhomes.
  • Expect construction nearby. Skye Summit is planned for about 3,500 homes, with KB Home's first homes expected in early 2027.

Who is building new homes in Skye Canyon in 2026?

What is the cheapest new home in Skye Canyon?

Arroyo at Skyeview by Century Communities, with townhomes on a base plan from $376,990 as of September 16, 2026. LGI's Topaz townhomes start at $399,900.

Are there single-story new homes in Skye Canyon?

Yes. Mesa at Skyeview by Century Communities is single-story, with plans from 1,635 to 1,816 square feet and base plans from $488,990.

Is Skyeview part of Skye Canyon?

Yes. The Skye Canyon developer lists Mesa, Terra, Sierra, and Arroyo at Skyeview as Century Communities neighborhoods in Skye Canyon.

Does Skye Canyon have a SID?

Part of it does. City of Las Vegas SID No. 610 sold $12.5 million in bonds for infrastructure on the 267 acre Skye Canyon Phase 3. Check your exact parcel with the Clark County Treasurer or your title company.

Which schools serve the Skyeview homes?

Century lists Divich Elementary, Escobedo Middle, and Arbor View High for Mesa, Terra, and Arroyo. Confirm with CCSD zoning, since boundaries change.

How far is Skye Canyon from the Strip?

The developer lists the Strip at 18 miles and downtown Las Vegas at 16 miles. Mt. Charleston Resort is 17 miles away.

Is more new construction coming near Skye Canyon?

Yes. Skye Summit, a separate 505 acre Olympia plan approved for about 3,500 homes, sold its first land to KB Home. Homes there are expected on the market in early 2027.
