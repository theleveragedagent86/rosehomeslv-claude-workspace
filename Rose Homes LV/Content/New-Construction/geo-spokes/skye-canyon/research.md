## 2. Where building is happening now

- **Skyeview (Century Communities, Vanhoy Creek St.)**: four active collections (Mesa, Terra, Sierra, Arroyo). Move-in ready and under-construction homes with Sept to Dec completions listed [S5-S8].
- **Topaz (LGI Homes, 10693 Tibbetts Ave.)**: townhomes, "Now Selling" [S9].
- **Paloma (Toll Brothers)**: developer says it "has reached its final phase. A limited number of move-in ready homes remain" [S3]. Toll shows 1 quick move-in home [S4]. **Closing out.**
- **Vista Rossa (Toll Brothers)**: sold out per search listings (Redfin/Livabl aggregators, not Toll directly) [S13]. Treat as sold out, verify.
- **Not active per developer site:** Lennar, Pulte, Beazer, Pardee. Developer builder page lists only Century, Toll, LGI [S2].
- **Coming nearby (outside Skye Canyon):** Skye Summit, a separate Olympia plan of 505 acres approved for ~3,500 homes. KB Home bought 38.6 acres (Vertice at Skye Summit, 299 homesites), north side of Tropical Pkwy west of the 215; homes to market early 2027 [S16].

## 6. HOA, SID/LID, taxes

- **Master HOA:** Skye Canyon has an HOA [S1]. Dollar amount **NOT FOUND** on developer or builder pages. Existing local reports and blog use $83/month, source not cited; agent sites say $75 to $200/month total; one listing site shows $133 total for Skye Mesa [S19]. Treat all as unverified leads.
- **Sub-HOA amounts:** NOT FOUND for Arroyo, Sierra, Terra, Mesa, Topaz, Paloma.
- **SID:** City of Las Vegas **Special Improvement District No. 610 (Skye Canyon Phase 3)**. $12.5 million in SID bonds for streets, storm sewer, sanitary sewer, drainage and water on a 267-acre site planned for 1,282 units; financial close expected first half 2019 [S10]. Also listed in the City's FY2022-2026 Capital Improvement Plan [S11].
- **Which lots pay:** SID 610 covers Phase 3 only. Whether Skyeview, Topaz or Paloma lots fall in SID 610: NOT FOUND. Annual per-lot amounts: NOT FOUND.
- **Conflicting claims to ignore:** one competitor blog says Skye Canyon has "no additional SID/LID line item"; the FHWA profile contradicts that for Phase 3 [S10]. The local reports' "$2,400 to $4,800/year" and "$138/year" figures have no primary source.
- **How to verify a lot:** Clark County Treasurer / title prelim for the parcel; Clark County SID info page [S20].
- **Property tax:** NOT FOUND (no Assessor/Treasurer data pulled).

## 10. Buyer watch-outs specific to this area

- **SID on Phase 3 lots** (SID 610). Ask which SID, if any, applies to the exact lot and the annual amount before contract [S10].
- **HOA stacking:** master + sub-HOA amounts are not published by builders. Get the resale/public offering package with exact dues [S5-S9].
- **"From" price vs. base plan:** Century shows two different starting numbers per collection [S5][S7][S8].
- **Lender-tied incentives:** prior local reports note Century incentives require its affiliated lender (Inspire Home Loans). Not re-verified this pass.
- **Toll closeout:** Paloma is in final phase with limited move-in ready homes, so choice is limited [S3][S4].
- **Outdated info online:** many sites (including our own May 2026 post) still list Lennar, Pulte, Beazer as active in Skye Canyon. Only Century, Toll, LGI are listed by the developer [S2].
- **Skyeview is dense product:** Sierra is cul-de-sac cluster lots; Arroyo and Topaz are townhomes [S7][S8][S9].
- **Future construction nearby:** Skye Summit land development starting 2026, homes 2027; expect construction traffic in the NW [S16].

## 11. FAQ candidates

1. **Who is building new homes in Skye Canyon in 2026?** The developer lists three builders: Century Communities, Toll Brothers and LGI Homes. Toll's Paloma is in its final phase. [S2][S3]
2. **What is the cheapest new home in Skye Canyon?** Century's Arroyo at Skyeview townhomes have a base plan from $376,990 (Beverly, 1,531 sq ft). LGI's Topaz townhomes start at $399,900. [S8][S9]
3. **Are there single-story new homes in Skye Canyon?** Yes. Century's Mesa at Skyeview is single-story, 1,635 to 1,816 sq ft, base plans from $488,990. [S5]
4. **Is Skyeview part of Skye Canyon?** Yes. The Skye Canyon developer site lists Mesa, Terra, Sierra and Arroyo at Skyeview as Century neighborhoods in Skye Canyon. [S2]
5. **Does Skye Canyon have a SID?** Part of it does. City of Las Vegas SID No. 610 financed $12.5 million of infrastructure for the 267-acre Phase 3. Confirm your exact lot with title. [S10]
6. **Which schools serve the Skyeview homes?** Century lists Divich Elementary, Escobedo Middle and Arbor View High for Mesa, Terra and Arroyo. Confirm with CCSD zoning, since boundaries change. [S5][S6][S8]
7. **How far is Skye Canyon from the Strip?** The developer lists the Strip at 18 miles and downtown at 16 miles. Mt. Charleston Resort is 17 miles. [S21]
8. **Is more new construction coming near Skye Canyon?** Yes. Skye Summit, a separate 505-acre Olympia plan approved for about 3,500 homes, sold its first land to KB Home. Homes are expected to hit the market in early 2027. [S16]
