Southwest Las Vegas covers Enterprise and western Spring Valley, including Mountain's Edge, Rhodes Ranch, and Southern Highlands. Most of it is unincorporated Clark County, not the City of Las Vegas, even with a Las Vegas address. As of September 16, 2026, we verified 42 neighborhoods selling new homes here from 12 builders, led by Lennar, Pulte, KB Home, and Richmond American. The busiest pocket is ZIPs 89139 and 89141, near Cactus Avenue and Rainbow Boulevard. Published starting prices run from $324,990 at KB Home's Chelsea Commons to $1,499,950 at Richmond American's Galway Grove. One tip: SID payments depend on the parcel, so check the exact lot before you sign.

What to know first

  1. Enterprise south, mostly ZIPs 89139 and 89141, is where most of the building is. Twenty two of the 42 neighborhoods in our table are there.
  2. A Las Vegas mailing address does not mean the City of Las Vegas. Enterprise and Spring Valley are unincorporated towns in Clark County, so the county handles services.
  3. Richmond American says "No SID fee" for most of its southwest neighborhoods. We found no SID statement for the rest, so check the exact parcel before you sign.
  4. Seven neighborhoods here from Pulte, AmericanWest Homes, and KB Home have three-story plans. If stairs are a problem, ask for the single-story plans first.
  5. Seven more neighborhoods are coming soon, including Lennar in Southern Highlands and a gated Lennar neighborhood called Aurora Estates.
  6. Register your own agent on your first visit to any model. The sales team works for the builder.
