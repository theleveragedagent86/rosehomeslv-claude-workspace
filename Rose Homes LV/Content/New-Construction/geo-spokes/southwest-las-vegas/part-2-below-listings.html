Where in southwest Las Vegas are new homes going up?

Most new building is in Enterprise south, in ZIPs 89139 and 89141. Smaller pockets sit along Durango and Warm Springs, in western Spring Valley, around Mountain's Edge, and on the west side of Southern Highlands.

City or county? It matters

New homes here often have a Las Vegas mailing address, but that does not mean they are in the City of Las Vegas. Enterprise, which includes Mountain's Edge and Southern Highlands, and Spring Valley, which includes Rhodes Ranch, are unincorporated towns in Clark County. That means Clark County, not the city, provides local services. We found no City of Las Vegas area in these new home pockets, but you can confirm any parcel on the Clark County Assessor site.

Where is southwest Las Vegas, and what is close by?

The southwest runs from Spring Valley south through Enterprise toward Sloan, west of I-15. Most pockets reach the south end of the Strip by way of the 215 Beltway and I-15.

  • Getting around: I-15, the 215 Beltway, Blue Diamond Road (SR 160), and St. Rose Parkway (SR 146). Main streets include Durango, Rainbow, Buffalo, Fort Apache, Cactus, Warm Springs, and Blue Diamond.
  • Mountain's Edge trails: Richmond American's Archer Park page points to trails nearby.
  • Southern Highlands: 24/7 roving patrol by Securitas, and private gated enclaves by Blue Heron.
  • Hospital: St. Rose Dominican San Martín Campus, 147 beds, at 8280 W Warm Springs Road in 89113.
  • Builder extras: Beazer includes solar in all its homes, Signature's Royal Ridge Estates has solar, and Archer Park offers Richmond American's UltraGarage.

Drive times change a lot by time of day. If a commute matters, test it at rush hour before you pick a lot.

What should you know before buying new in southwest Las Vegas?

The southwest has a lot of choices at many price points, but a few things catch new buyers off guard.

  • Check who governs the address. A Las Vegas address here is almost always unincorporated Clark County. Confirm the parcel on the Clark County Assessor site.
  • Check the SID on the exact lot. It depends on the parcel. Richmond American says "No SID fee" for most of its neighborhoods here, but we found no SID statement from the other builders.
  • Get every HOA number. Southern Highlands has a master HOA plus a sub-association, and official dollar amounts are not published.
  • Budget for the lot. KB Home says a homesite premium may apply.
  • Ask about stairs. Paldona, Cordora, Delamar, Hayford Collection, Rainbow Crossing Luxury, Quinn Canyon, and Chelsea Commons all have three-story plans.
  • Know what "home" means. Caliterra sells Duo (paired) homes, and Beazer's Aster Point and Granite Point are townhomes.
  • Do not assume a gate. Hayford Collection, The Bluffs at Southern Highlands, and Aurora Estates (coming soon) are gated. Most other neighborhoods here are not.
  • Watch the brand name. Pulte now sells Quinn Canyon and Wesley Park under its AmericanWest Homes brand.
  • Coming soon means no real price yet. Montclair, Aurora Estates, and Lennar's Southern Highlands show rounded "From" prices only.
  • Look past the MLS. Some new homes are sold directly by the builder and may not show up on listing sites.
