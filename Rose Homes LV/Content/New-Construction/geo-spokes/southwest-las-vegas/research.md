# Southwest Las Vegas new construction: research

Compiled 2026-09-16. Builder pages are undated live pages, so "source date" = date accessed (2026-09-16) unless noted. Source numbers [n] refer to section 13.

## 1. Area snapshot

- **What it is:** the southwest valley. Includes Enterprise, western Spring Valley, Mountain's Edge, Rhodes Ranch, the west side of Southern Highlands, and the I-215 / Durango / Blue Diamond / Cactus / Warm Springs corridors running south toward Sloan.
- **Jurisdiction:**

| Pocket | Jurisdiction | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise (incl. Mountain's Edge, Southern Highlands, Cactus/Blue Diamond/Warm Springs south corridors) | Unincorporated Clark County (unincorporated town of Enterprise) | [1], [3] |
| Spring Valley (incl. Rhodes Ranch, Durango/Warm Springs north of 215 area) | Unincorporated Clark County (unincorporated town of Spring Valley) | [1], [2] |
| Sloan / Blue Diamond (south and west edges) | Unincorporated Clark County | [1] |
| City of Las Vegas | No City of Las Vegas jurisdiction found in the core new-construction pockets. "Las Vegas, NV" mailing address does NOT mean City of Las Vegas. Verify by parcel on Clark County Assessor. | [1], [2] |
| Henderson | Henderson annexed about 1,400 acres of former Enterprise land in 1999 (east edge, not the SW core) | [1] |

- **Enterprise:** 66 sq mi, 2020 population 221,831, town formed 1996. ZIPs 89113, 89118, 89119, 89123, 89139, 89141, 89183. Borders Spring Valley (N), Paradise (E), Henderson (SE), Sloan (S), Blue Diamond (W) [1].
- **Spring Valley:** unincorporated town/CDP, 2020 population 215,597, formed 1981 [2]. Rhodes Ranch was placed in Spring Valley town in 1997 [1].
- **Master plans:**
  - Mountain's Edge: Focus Property Group, started Feb 2004, about 14,500 homes planned, about 12,500 built by end of 2025, ZIPs 89178/89139 [3]. Master association: Mountain's Edge Master Association, 8015 Blue Diamond Rd Ste 120, 89178 [4]. Acreage: 3,500 acres per nevadarealestategroup.com, LEAD ONLY, not verified [5].
  - Southern Highlands: developer Olympia Companies, managed by Olympia Management Services [6][7]. 2,200 acres per competitor blog, LEAD ONLY [8].
  - Rhodes Ranch: guard-gated golf community, Spring Valley. Local Rose Homes research (undated, not re-verified) [9].
- **SW ZIPs used in this file:** 89113, 89118, 89139, 89141, 89147, 89148, 89178, 89179. Borderline east of I-15: 89123, 89183 (listed separately).

## 10. Buyer watch-outs specific to this area

- **Jurisdiction confusion:** "Las Vegas" address, but almost all SW pockets are unincorporated Clark County (Enterprise or Spring Valley). County services, not City [1][2].
- **SID varies by parcel:** some Mountain's Edge parcels reported to carry a SID (lead) [35]. Check APN on amgnv.com / Clark County Treasurer before contract [36]. Richmond American publishes "No SID fee" on its SW pages [16].
- **Two-tier HOA:** Southern Highlands has master plus sub-association; amounts not published officially [6][7].
- **Lot / homesite premiums:** KB "Homesite premium may apply" [13]; half-acre homesites at Richmond American Eldorado Valley [16].
- **Three-story plans:** Pulte Paldona, Cordora, Delamar, Hayford, Rainbow Crossing, AmericanWest Quinn Canyon, KB Chelsea Commons [10][12][13].
- **Attached product called "homes":** Richmond American Duo (paired) plans at Caliterra and Vandalia; Beazer townhomes [16][19].
- **Coming soon without prices:** Aurora Estates, Montclair, Southern Highlands Westridge publish "From" rounded figures only; Cashmere Meadows, Silver Bow, Caliterra, Marigold have no price [14][16][19].
- **Build-out and construction traffic:** new BLM land near Blue Diamond Rd and I-15 auctioned April 2026 means more building nearby [28][29].
- **Builder brand shifts:** some Pulte communities (Quinn Canyon, Wesley Park) now sell under AmericanWest Homes [12].
- **Gated vs not:** Hayford Collection and Aurora Estates gated; most others not [10][14].

## 11. FAQ candidates

1. **Is southwest Las Vegas in the City of Las Vegas?** Mostly no. Enterprise and Spring Valley are unincorporated towns in Clark County, even with a Las Vegas mailing address. Mountain's Edge and Southern Highlands sit in Enterprise; Rhodes Ranch is in Spring Valley. Sources: [1], [2], [3].
2. **What is the cheapest new construction in southwest Las Vegas right now?** As of 2026-09-16, KB Home Chelsea Commons (89148) lists from $324,990 for 3-story homes. The lowest detached home found is Lennar The Richardson (89139) from $419,990. Sources: [13], [14].
3. **Which builders are building in Mountain's Edge?** Richmond American sells Archer Park at Mountain's Edge (89178) from $732,950, ranch plans 2,390-2,830 sq ft. Lennar Triad Springs, Century Southwind and Storybook Ivyleaf Square are also in 89178. Sources: [16], [14], [20], [21].
4. **Do new homes in southwest Las Vegas have SID fees?** It depends on the parcel. Richmond American states "No SID fee" on its southwest pages, while parts of Mountain's Edge are reported to carry a SID. Check the parcel on amgnv.com or with the Clark County Treasurer. Sources: [16], [35], [36].
5. **What is the median price of a new home in 89141 / 89139?** Redfin on 2026-09-16 showed 46 new homes in 89141 at a $579K median listing price and 36 in 89139 at $531K. Source: [32].
6. **Are there single-story new builds in the southwest?** Yes. Beazer Wisteria (from $599,990), Richmond American Archer Park, Eldorado Valley, Camden and Galway Grove (ranch plans), and Pulte Daylight (from $965,990). Sources: [19], [16], [10].
7. **What schools serve new homes near Cactus and Rainbow?** Builder school data lists Fine ES, Canarelli MS and Desert Oasis HS for Pulte Cordora, Rainbow Crossing Luxury and Delamar. Confirm each address with CCSD zoning search. Source: [10].
8. **Is a new high school coming to Mountain's Edge?** Wikipedia reports a magnet high school planned for 2027-2028. CCSD confirmation NOT FOUND, so verify with CCSD. Source: [3].
