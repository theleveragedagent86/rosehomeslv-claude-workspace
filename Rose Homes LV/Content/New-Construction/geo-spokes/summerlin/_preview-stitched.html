Summerlin is a 22,500 acre master plan on the west edge of the Las Vegas Valley, built by Howard Hughes. As of September 10, 2026, the Summerlin home search lists 26 neighborhoods selling new homes across 7 villages, and most of them sit west of the 215 Beltway in Grand Park, Kestrel, Kestrel Commons, La Madre Peaks, and Redpoint Square. Nine builders are active, led by Toll Brothers and Tri Pointe. Starting prices run from $397,990 for a KB Home townhome in Grand Park to $1,998,995 in Toll Brothers' guard gated Ascension. On top of the price, plan for a $69 a month Summerlin West master HOA, a neighborhood HOA, and in many villages a City of Las Vegas SID payment on your tax bill.

What to know first

  1. Grand Park is where most of the building is. Eleven of the 26 selling neighborhoods are there, west of the 215 and north of Far Hills Avenue.
  2. Starting prices change with every release. The Summerlin home search and Summerlin's own July article already disagree by tens of thousands on some neighborhoods, so ask the builder for a dated price sheet.
  3. You pay two HOAs. The Summerlin West master assessment is $69 a month in 2026, and each neighborhood adds its own fee on top.
  4. Many Summerlin West villages sit inside a City of Las Vegas special improvement district. That payment is on your property tax bill, not in your HOA, so ask for the balance on the exact lot.
  5. School zones can split inside one village. Kestrel Commons has addresses zoned to two different elementary schools, so check the exact address with CCSD.
  6. Register your own agent on your first visit to a Summerlin model. The sales team works for the builder.

What will you pay each month on top of the mortgage?

In Summerlin West you are looking at two HOA fees, property tax, and in many villages a special improvement district payment. Here is how each one works.

CostWhat it isAmount
Master HOASummerlin West Community Association$69 a month in 2026, up from $60
Neighborhood HOAYour own neighborhood's association, on top of the master feeSet by each neighborhood, ask the builder
SID paymentPays back bonds for public improvements, billed apart from your HOAVaries by parcel, check the lot
Property taxClark County, with a 3% yearly cap on increases for a primary homeBased on assessed value

Master HOA amounts are the 2026 assessments reported from the Summerlin Council's newsletter. Summerlin North is $74 and Summerlin South is $76.

The SID is the one people miss. Summerlin West was built with City of Las Vegas special improvement districts, including SID 814, which covers Summerlin Villages 21 and 24A. The payment shows up as its own line on your property tax bill, not in your HOA dues, and the amount depends on the parcel. Before you sign, ask for the SID balance and yearly payment on the exact lot. I explain how these work in SID and LID fees in Summerlin.

Where is Summerlin, and what is close by?

Summerlin is on the west edge of the valley, with Red Rock Canyon behind La Madre Peaks. The new villages sit west of the 215 Beltway.

  • Getting around: the 215 Beltway runs along the east edge of the new villages. Summerlin Parkway is the other main freeway.
  • Downtown Summerlin: the main shopping and dining center for the master plan. The Loughton condos sit right in that core.
  • Parks and trails: Summerlin has more than 200 miles of trails. Grand Park, at more than 90 acres, anchors the busiest new village.
  • Golf: ten golf courses inside Summerlin.
  • Red Rock Canyon: the backdrop to La Madre Peaks.

Summerlin West is inside the City of Las Vegas. The Peaks, where Ascension sits, is part of Summerlin South, which is unincorporated Clark County. That changes which government bills your SID and handles services, so it is worth knowing before you buy.

What should you know before buying new in Summerlin?

Summerlin is a well run master plan, but a few things catch new buyers off guard.

  • Ask for a dated price sheet. Published prices for the same neighborhood differ by tens of thousands of dollars between Summerlin's own sources.
  • Get both HOA numbers. The $69 master fee is only part of it. The neighborhood fee is set separately.
  • Check the SID on the exact lot. It is billed with your property tax, and the amount depends on the parcel.
  • Do not assume a gate. Ascension in The Peaks is guard gated. For any other neighborhood, confirm gate status with the builder before you count on it.
  • Check age rules in writing. Summerlin describes Esplanade at Red Rock as an all ages community, but some sites list it as 55 and better. Read the governing documents.
  • Expect construction. Summerlin planned 11 new neighborhoods for 2026, so building traffic west of the 215 will be around for a while.
  • Look past the MLS. Some quick move-in homes are sold directly by the builder and may not show up in the Summerlin home search or on listing sites.

Questions about new construction in Summerlin

What is the cheapest new construction in Summerlin right now?

Caldwell Park by KB Home in Grand Park, with townhomes from $397,990 as of September 10, 2026. The Loughton condos by Toll Brothers in Summerlin Centre start at $449,995.

How many new home neighborhoods are selling in Summerlin?

The Summerlin home search lists 26 neighborhoods selling across 7 villages as of September 10, 2026. Eleven of them are in Grand Park.

What are the HOA fees for new homes in Summerlin West?

The Summerlin West master assessment is $69 a month in 2026, up from $60. Each neighborhood adds its own HOA fee on top, so ask the builder for the exact total.

Do new Summerlin homes have SID payments?

Many do. Summerlin West villages were built with City of Las Vegas special improvement districts, such as SID 814. The payment is on your property tax bill, so check the balance for the exact parcel before you sign.

Which schools serve the new Summerlin West neighborhoods?

For 2026 to 2027, most of Grand Park, Redpoint Square, and La Madre Peaks is zoned to Givens Elementary, Becker Middle, and Palo Verde High. Kestrel addresses, and some in Kestrel Commons, go to Lummis Elementary instead. Always check your exact address with CCSD.

Is Summerlin in the City of Las Vegas or Clark County?

Most of Summerlin, including the Summerlin West villages where most new homes are built, is inside the City of Las Vegas. Summerlin South, which includes The Peaks, is unincorporated Clark County.

Are there single-story new homes in Summerlin?

Yes. Esplanade at Red Rock by Taylor Morrison in La Madre Peaks starts at $698,990, and Fairview by Lennar in Grand Park offers single-story homes with a Next Gen suite from $1,898,990.

Are there guard gated new homes in Summerlin?

Ascension by Toll Brothers in The Peaks is guard gated, with homes from $1,854,995. For any other neighborhood, confirm gate status with the builder.
