## 2. Where building is happening now

Villages with active listings in the home search filter [1]: **Grand Park, Kestrel, Kestrel Commons, La Madre Peaks, Redpoint Square, Summerlin Centre, The Peaks.** Reverence and Redpoint (the older district) return **0 active listings** [1].

- **Grand Park** (biggest pocket, 11 neighborhoods): west of the 215 Beltway, north of Far Hills Ave. Grand Park itself (the park) is more than 90 acres [4].
- **La Madre Peaks** (5 neighborhoods plus Astra custom homesites): Astra has 167 custom homesites on 171 acres [3]. Esplanade at Red Rock sits at the base of the La Madre mountains [9].
- **Kestrel / Kestrel Commons** (5): Mockingbird, Sandpiper and Raven Crest opened in 2025 [3].
- **Redpoint Square** (2): Cordillera (Toll), Vertex (Tri Pointe) townhomes [1].
- **Summerlin Centre** (1): The Loughton condos by Toll [1][2].
- **The Peaks** (1): Ascension by Toll, guard-gated [1][10].
- **Opened in 2025:** Raven Crest, Mockingbird, Sandpiper, Ashland, SHAWOOD at Arcadia, Glenrock, Caldwell Park, Carlisle, Primrose Park, Brantley, and Astra homesites [3].
- **Coming:** 11 new neighborhoods planned for 2026 [3]. Names: NOT FOUND.
- **Closing out or gone from the home search** (were selling earlier): see the bottom of the section 3 table.

## 10. Buyer watch-outs specific to this area

- **Reverence gate claim, do not repeat:** do NOT say all of Reverence is guard-gated.
  - Rose Homes' own `post109` and `Reverence/batch1-post1` say "Reverence is a 300 acre guard gated village". That is the known-wrong claim.
  - summerlin.com's Monument articles [10][11] never call Monument gated.
  - "Heights at Reverence is guard-gated" appears only on third-party agent sites (search results). **Not verified from Pulte or summerlin.com.**
  - Reverence has 0 active listings in the home search [1].
- **Ask for the dated price sheet:** summerlin.com's article and home search prices differ by tens of thousands on the same neighborhood ([1] vs [2]).
- **Stale "opens" pages:** some summerlin.com launch articles (Carmel Cliff, Crystal Canyon) show a 2026 page date but describe older launches. Do not cite them as current.
- **Two HOA layers:** $69/mo Summerlin West master [18] plus a neighborhood sub-HOA (amounts NOT FOUND).
- **SID assessments:** City of Las Vegas SIDs cover Summerlin West villages [14][15]. They show on the tax bill, not in HOA dues. Pull the per-APN balance.
- **Jurisdiction mix-up:** The Peaks/Ascension = unincorporated Clark County. Summerlin West = City of Las Vegas [7]. A competitor blog gets this backwards.
- **Age rules:** Esplanade at Red Rock is described by summerlin.com as an "all-ages community" [9]. Some third-party sites (55places) list it as 55+. Confirm in the governing documents; Taylor Morrison says to check them [23].
- **School zoning splits** inside villages (section 7). New schools: none announced.
- **Builder-direct inventory:** quick move-ins (e.g. Edgewood, Vireo, Mira Villa in [6]) may not show in the home search. Several neighborhoods show "Price upon request" (Reflection Ridge [1]).
- **Construction:** 11 more neighborhoods are planned for 2026 [3], so expect ongoing build-out traffic west of the 215. Flight paths and wind: NOT FOUND.

## 11. FAQ candidates

1. **What is the cheapest new construction in Summerlin right now?** Caldwell Park Groves by KB Home in Grand Park starts at $397,990 for a townhome. The Loughton condos by Toll Brothers in Summerlin Centre start at $449,995. [1] (2026-09-10)
2. **How many new home neighborhoods are selling in Summerlin?** The summerlin.com home search lists 26 neighborhoods across 7 villages as of Sept 10, 2026. Summerlin's July 2026 article said more than 110 floorplans in 20 neighborhoods. [1], [2]
3. **What are the HOA fees for new homes in Summerlin West?** The Summerlin West master assessment is $69 a month in 2026, up from $60. Most neighborhoods add their own sub-HOA fee on top, so ask the builder for the exact number. [18] (2025-10-09)
4. **Do new Summerlin homes have SID payments?** Yes, many do. Summerlin West villages are covered by City of Las Vegas special improvement districts such as SID 814 (Villages 21 and 24A). The payment shows on the property tax bill, so check the balance for the exact parcel. [14], [20]
5. **Which schools serve the new Summerlin West neighborhoods?** For 2026-27, most of Grand Park, Redpoint Square and La Madre Peaks are zoned Givens Elementary, Becker Middle and Palo Verde High. Kestrel and Reverence addresses are zoned Lummis Elementary instead, and southern Grand Park goes to Rogich Middle. Always check your exact address. [21]
6. **Is Summerlin in the City of Las Vegas or Clark County?** Most of Summerlin, including the Summerlin West villages where most new homes are built, is inside the City of Las Vegas. Summerlin South, which includes The Peaks, is unincorporated Clark County. [7]
7. **Are there single-story new homes in Summerlin?** Yes. Esplanade at Red Rock by Taylor Morrison offers 12 single-story plans from 1,569 to 3,406 sq ft. Fairview by Lennar in Grand Park is single-story with a Next Gen suite. [9], [2]
8. **Are there guard-gated new homes in Summerlin?** Ascension by Toll Brothers in The Peaks is a guard-gated neighborhood, with plans starting at $1,854,995. Gate status for other neighborhoods varies, so confirm with each builder. [10], [1]
