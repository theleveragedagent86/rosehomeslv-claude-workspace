## Index check

**Google has already crawled the AEO posts.** A `site:rosehomeslv.com` search
returns Listing Agent in North Las Vegas, Listing Agent in Henderson, Listing
Agent in Summerlin, Buyer's Agent in Henderson and Who Pays the Buyer's Agent
in Las Vegas, one of them crawled 22 hours before this test. DuckDuckGo shows
none of these slugs, and Perplexity cited none of them, so the gap is those
engines' own indexes, not Google's.

That makes the zero score more meaningful than a pure "too new" result. The
pages exist in Google's index and still did not get picked as a source.

## What the baseline actually shows

1. **Directories decide the "best realtor" answers, not blogs.** Every naming
   answer traced back to HomeLight, U.S. News, Realtor.com or 55places. None of
   them traced to anybody's own blog post. Writing more posts will not move
   these ten queries. Being in those directories will.
2. **Nevada Real Estate Group appears in four of ten answers.** They are the
   same source several Rose Homes LV posts cite for days on market. They are the
   competitor to watch.
3. **A Real Broker colleague, Sandy Margolin, took the Aliante 55 plus answer.**
   Same brokerage, same niche, and their profile got picked over Ryan's
   dedicated post on that exact topic.
4. **The informational questions cite no agent at all**, which is the opening.
   Those answers pull from calculators and government pages, so a clearly
   sourced, dated post is the kind of thing that can earn a citation there.
