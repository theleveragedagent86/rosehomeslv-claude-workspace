## Week 2: Mon Oct 12. Spring Valley vs Summerlin

**Button:** Learn more
**Link:** https://www.rosehomeslv.com/blog/spring-valley-vs-summerlin
**Photo:** Red Rock Canyon view from Summerlin, or a Downtown Summerlin street shot.

**Post text:**

Spring Valley or Summerlin? They sit right next to each other, but they live very differently.

Spring Valley is about 2 miles west of the Strip. It is an unincorporated town in Clark County, home to Las Vegas Chinatown on Spring Mountain Road and Desert Breeze Park.

Summerlin is about 9 miles west of the Strip, right next to Red Rock Canyon. It is one master plan with more than 300 parks, over 200 miles of trails, and Downtown Summerlin at its center.

Spring Valley tends to fit people who work on the Strip or in the middle of the valley. Summerlin tends to fit people who want trails and Red Rock close to home.

One tip for both: check the exact school zone and the HOA dues for each home. They can change from one street to the next.

## Week 3: Mon Oct 19. Mountain's Edge

**Button:** Learn more
**Link:** https://www.rosehomeslv.com/blog/buyers-agent-mountains-edge
**Photo:** Exploration Peak, or a Mountain's Edge street or park shot.

**Post text:**

Thinking about Mountain's Edge? Here are three things buyers often don't know.

1. It is not in the City of Las Vegas. Mountain's Edge sits in Enterprise, an unincorporated part of Clark County, mostly in the 89178 ZIP code. The county handles permits and code questions.

2. Most homes pay two HOA fees. There is a master association fee plus a sub association fee. Add them together to see what you really pay each month.

3. Homes vary a lot. Building started in 2004, so a 2005 home and a 2019 home can sit a mile apart with very different floor plans, roof ages and finishes.

The parks are a big reason people love it here. Exploration Peak Park covers nearly 80 acres, with trails to the summit, a splash pad and an amphitheater.

## Week 4: Mon Oct 26. Southern Highlands

**Button:** Learn more
**Link:** https://www.rosehomeslv.com/blog/buyers-agent-southern-highlands
**Photo:** A Southern Highlands entry gate or foothills view.

**Post text:**

Buying in Southern Highlands? A few things to know before you tour.

Southern Highlands is in the south foothills of the valley, ZIP code 89141. Like Mountain's Edge, it sits in Enterprise, so Clark County handles permits. That matters the day you want to add a pool, a casita or an RV gate.

Most homes have two layers of HOA. The master association pays for the 24 hour roving security patrol, parks and common areas. Most homes also have a sub association with its own dues.

Buying a home here does not come with a golf membership. Southern Highlands Golf Club is private and has its own cost.

Some enclaves have manned gates, so showings need to be set up ahead of time. I plan tour days around the gates so you are not stuck waiting at a call box.
