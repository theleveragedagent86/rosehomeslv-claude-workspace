## The punch list

### 1. No HomeLight profile. Start here.

HomeLight was the source Perplexity leaned on for almost every "best realtor"
answer in the baseline, including all three city level ones. A site search found
no Ryan Rose profile at all.

Chris Nevada / Nevada Real Estate Group shows 5.0 from 46 reviews there, which
is exactly why the engines keep naming them.

This is the single highest value item on the list. It is free and it is the
front door AI is walking through.

### 2. Google Business Profile is thin

It has the basics and nothing else:

- One category only, "Real estate agency"
- No visible business description
- No services listed
- No service areas set
- 5 reviews

Fill in the description, add services (buyer's agent, listing agent, new
construction, relocation, luxury), add secondary categories, and set the service
areas to the southwest and Summerlin. Then work reviews. Five is thin for a
profile this otherwise clean, and review count is what the directories rank on.

### 3. Zillow points away from the niche

- Service areas: 24 listed, but **Summerlin North, Summerlin South and Sun City
  Summerlin sit at positions 19 through 22**, below Nellis AFB, Whitney and
  Winchester.
- **Mountains Edge, Southern Highlands and Spring Valley are missing entirely.**
  Enterprise is the only southwest area on there.
- Specialties include **Senior Communities**, which is the niche Ryan explicitly
  does not want.
- 2 reviews, versus 5 on Google. The Google reviews are not being mirrored.

### 4. Homes.com contradicts Zillow

- Bio says "a local agent on a **Top Producing Las Vegas Real Estate Team**".
  Ryan is Real Broker. If the team reference is stale it reads as someone else's
  brand on his profile.
- Specialties are Luxury listings, Listing Specialist, First Time Home Buyers.
  Zillow lists ten specialties. They do not match.
- "Areas Ryan is Experienced In" is computed from closed sales: Henderson 3,
  Paradise 2, Paradise Valley East 1, Aliante 1, Valley View 1, Tule Springs 1.
  **Zero Summerlin. Zero southwest.**

That last one is not a profile problem, it is a production problem. Directories
derive specialty from where deals actually close, so the niche gets built by
closings, not by bios.

### 5. Agent Pronto is publishing weak numbers on page one

Agent Pronto ranks on page one of Google for his name and shows "3 sales past 3
years, $461K, 4.8 out of 5 across 4 reviews". Lower than his own Google rating
and a thin sales figure, on a page he does not control. Worth claiming the
profile to correct it, or at least knowing it is what a searcher sees.

### 6. Realtor.com profile exists but could not be audited

Listed at 9580 W. Sahara Ave Ste 200, 89117. The browser pane is blocked from
realtor.com, so Ryan needs to open it himself and check the specialties, service
areas and review count against this list.

### 7. Years of experience is inconsistent

Zillow, Homes.com and Instagram all say 8 years. The AEO blog posts say
"licensed more than five years", which was the approved conservative line. Both
are true, but the blog is under claiming against his own profiles. Worth
standardizing on the real number.
