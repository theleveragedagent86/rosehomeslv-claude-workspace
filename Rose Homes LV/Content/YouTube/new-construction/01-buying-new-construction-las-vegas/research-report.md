# Research Report: Buying New Construction in Las Vegas, The Full Process

## Overview
This is an evergreen, start-to-finish how-to for someone who has decided they want a brand-new builder home in the Las Vegas valley but does not know how the process works. It walks through registering a buyer agent before the first model-home visit, choosing communities and builders, the design center and upgrade selections, the builder contract and timeline, inspections and walkthroughs, the Nevada warranty and construction-defect framework, and how closing on a new build differs from a resale in Clark County. The angle is practical and local-first: real timelines, real master-planned communities and their developers, and the Nevada legal protections specific to new homes.

## Conflicts and Open Questions
- **[Conflict] Nevada statute of repose length.** Several law-firm sources describe NRS 11.202 as a 6-year statute of repose after substantial completion (e.g. lewisbrisbois.com summarizing AB 125), while others describe the post-2019 AB 421 framework as effectively a 10-year outer limit (grsm.com, "10-Year Statute of Repose"; nevadalaw.com). The earlier search-summary text also said "increased the time from six years to ten years." Both the 6-year and 10-year figures appear in reputable secondary sources discussing the same statute. QA should pull the current text of NRS 11.202 directly to state the correct number; do not assert one in the script until resolved. The script should likely frame this generally ("Nevada law gives homeowners a multi-year window to bring construction-defect claims under NRS Chapter 40") rather than cite a hard number until confirmed.
- **NOT FOUND: NRS 11.2055 primary text.** The Justia and (separately) some statute pages returned HTTP 403, so the substantial-completion definition (final building inspection / notice of completion / certificate of occupancy) rests on a search-result summary rather than a directly fetched primary page. QA should confirm against the official Nevada Legislature site (leg.state.nv.us).
- **NOT FOUND: Current median price specifically for NEW construction this month.** The RJ March 2026 median closing price of $502,990 is an all-product new-home figure; a clean new-build-only median for the current month should be pulled from the local MLS (GLVAR / Las Vegas Realtors) or Home Builders Research before quoting it as "the new construction median."
- **NOT FOUND: Exact statewide-standard builder warranty terms.** The "1-2-10" structure is the 2-10 HBW industry model, widely used, but each Las Vegas builder's actual warranty terms vary. The script should say "many builders offer a 1-2-10 style warranty" rather than imply it is uniform or legally mandated. Nevada law (NRS 40.607) defines and references a builder's warranty but does not itself set the 1/2/10 periods.
- **NOT FOUND: Hard, current count of "actively selling neighborhoods" per community for May 2026.** Earlier 2025 sources said Summerlin had 100+ floorplans across 20 neighborhoods and planned 11 new neighborhoods for 2026; treat as approximate and flag as potentially stale. A current figure should come from summerlin.com or the developer.
- **Date staleness flags:** The 2-10 HBW warranty pages, builder-options pages, and several agent blogs are undated (n/d); treat their general process descriptions as stable/evergreen but do not attach specific years. The Skye Canyon developer and acreage facts trace to 2015-2020 reporting and the Century Communities acquisition (2020); they describe the plan's structure, which is stable, but current buildout status should be confirmed.
