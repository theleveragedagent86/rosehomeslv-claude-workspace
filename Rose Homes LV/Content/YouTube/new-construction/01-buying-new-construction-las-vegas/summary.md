QA's own check leaned to a current 10 years, but it is not stated on camera until confirmed against live statute text at leg.state.nv.us. Optional to add; safe to leave as is.

If you want a new-build-only median on screen, pull it from GLVAR/MLS or Home Builders Research and the HyperFrames editor can swap it into the placeholder stat row.
3. **Builder warranty** is framed as the industry "1-2-10 style" model, not a Nevada legal mandate, on purpose.
