### Body (after "Show More")

New construction or resale? It's the question I get almost every week from buyers all over the Las Vegas valley. In this video I lay both sides out fair, no sales pitch, and walk you through the four trade-offs that decide it for most people: price and what's actually included, location and your move-in timeline, repairs and warranties, and the monthly costs nobody warns you about.

We start with the money. The median new home in the valley is over $500,000 right now, while the median existing single-family home sits around $475,000. But the sticker price on a new build is rarely the real price. Builders here often leave out window coverings, and the backyard is usually just dirt. I break down what those extras really cost so you can compare new construction and resale on equal footing.

Then we get into location. New construction lives on the rim of the valley, places like Skye Canyon up north and newer parts of Henderson, with young streets and skinny trees. Resale fills the built-out core, neighborhoods like Green Valley and Spring Valley with grown trees and real shade. We cover builder warranties versus the home inspection, why our brutal Las Vegas summer heat matters for repairs, and how HOA fees across the valley can flip the whole decision once you look at the monthly cost.
