# Research Report: New Construction vs. Resale in Las Vegas: How to Choose (Video 2 of 3)

## Overview
This is a balanced, head-to-head decision guide for a Las Vegas valley buyer who has not yet picked between a brand-new build and an existing resale home. The video weighs the real trade-offs across seven areas: price per square foot, move-in timing, location and lot maturity, landscaping and yard finishing costs, warranties and repairs, HOA fees and amenities, and how negotiation differs. The angle stays neutral and does not push either path. All numbers are sourced and current to 2026 Las Vegas / Clark County data where possible.

## Local Context

- **Edge vs. core geography:** New construction concentrates on the valley rim (Skye Canyon to the north, far southwest, parts of Henderson like Cadence and Inspirada). Resale stock fills the built-out core: Green Valley, Spring Valley, Centennial Hills, and older Summerlin villages. This drives the lot-maturity trade-off: new buyers get a blank, sun-exposed lot and a young streetscape; resale buyers inherit grown trees and shade but older systems. Source: facts #34, #35.
- **HOA stacking is a real Las Vegas quirk:** Newer master plans often layer a master association, a village/sub-association, and sometimes a LID or SID bond. Older neighborhoods may carry only a light HOA or none. Communities like Cadence advertise "no LID/SID" as a selling point. Sources: #27, #28, #31, #32, #33.
- **Heat stress matters for the repair comparison:** Las Vegas heat is hard on HVAC, water heaters, and plumbing, which raises the value of an active builder/HBW warranty on a new home and the inspection/repair risk on an older home. Source: https://www.rosehomeslv.com/blog/builder-warranty-vs-home-warranty-las-vegas

## Conflicts and Open Questions

- **[Conflict] Nevada warranty statute and minimum timelines.** The Ryan Rose blog frames the one-year minimum under NRS 624.602 with the common 1-2-10 builder structure. Railtor.ai frames it under NRS 116 and lists different statutory minimums (structural 1 year, mechanical 2 years, "other components / fixtures and finishes" 6 months). These describe overlapping but not identical legal frameworks (NRS 624 governs contractors/builders; NRS 116 governs common-interest communities and the construction-defect-related provisions). QA should confirm the correct statute and the exact statutory minimums before any number is spoken on camera. Sources: https://www.rosehomeslv.com/blog/builder-warranty-vs-home-warranty-las-vegas ; https://railtor.ai/blog/las-vegas-new-construction-warranty-guide
