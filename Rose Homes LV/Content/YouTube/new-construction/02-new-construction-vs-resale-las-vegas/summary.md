## NOT FOUND gaps (need a human)

2. **Nevada legal-minimum warranty number.** Sources split (NRS 624 vs NRS 116, with different minimums). The script speaks about builder-warranty protection generally and states no number on camera. Confirm against live statute text at leg.state.nv.us only if you want a number stated.
3. **Skye Canyon ~$83/mo is the master fee only;** sub-association dues vary. The hook's "about thirty thousand dollars more" is a rounded, directional figure (softened with "about"). Both are advisory, no change needed.
