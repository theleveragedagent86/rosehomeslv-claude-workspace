### Body (after "Show More")

Buying new construction in Las Vegas can be a great move, but the deal is built around the builder, not you. In this video, Ryan Rose walks through the four places buyers get burned on a new build in the valley, and exactly how to protect yourself on each one.

First, who actually represents you. The sales rep in the model home is licensed and friendly, but legally they represent the builder. The fix is simple. Bring your own agent on your very first visit. In Las Vegas your agent has to be registered on that first model-home visit, and your representation costs you nothing because the builder already set that money aside.

Next, the money traps. Lot premiums for a view, a corner, a cul-de-sac, or no back neighbor get baked into the price without a line item. The design center is where it gets wild, with flooring and upgrades commonly marked up well over what the same work costs after closing. Ryan shares an easy rule for what to do now and what to wait on.

Then the rate buydown. That super low builder rate can be real, but it is often tied to a price held artificially high to protect appraisals on the builder's other homes. You will learn how to compare the builder lender's loan estimate against an outside lender, and why federal law means no builder can force you to use theirs.

Finally, the fine print. Builder contracts often drop the appraisal contingency, weaken inspection and financing terms, and slip in a binding arbitration clause. Read it with your own agent before you sign.

If you want a second set of eyes on a builder contract or a quote, reach out anytime. No pressure, just real answers.
