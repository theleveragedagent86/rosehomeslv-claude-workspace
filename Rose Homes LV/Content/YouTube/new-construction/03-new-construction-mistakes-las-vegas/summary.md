## NOT FOUND gaps and accuracy guardrails (handled in the script)

1. **Lot premiums:** No defensible Las Vegas dollar figure or percentage exists, so the script never states one. It explains the drivers (view, corner, cul-de-sac, no back neighbor) and tells the buyer to ask how much of the price is the lot premium. Safe as is.
2. **Upgrade markups:** Only the verified local 50% to 100% flooring/upgrade figure (rosehomeslv.com) is spoken. Out-of-market category percentages (e.g. paint 200-400%) are NOT presented as Las Vegas numbers; the script says only "some finishes get marked up even more."
3. **Mortgage-shopping savings:** The unsourced $600/$1,200 figures are NOT quoted. The script keeps it directional ("shopping your loan can save you real money").
4. **Buydown example:** The $20K-vs-$25K baked-in example and Dallas design-center figures are NOT presented as local facts. The mechanism is described generically.
5. **Preferred-lender legal point:** Safe framing only. The script says a builder cannot force you to use their lender but can legally offer an incentive for using their in-house lender with disclosure. It stops short of the contested "can be conditioned on the lender" point, so the logged source conflict never surfaces on camera.
