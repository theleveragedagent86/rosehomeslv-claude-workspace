# Batch Plan: New Construction (3 standalone videos)

## Overview
This batch covers buying brand-new builder homes in the Las Vegas valley. These are 3 independent, standalone videos, not a connected series, so each one stands fully on its own and can be watched in any order. The framing is local-first throughout: real Las Vegas and Henderson master-planned communities like Summerlin, Skye Canyon, Cadence, and Inspirada, told from the point of view of a local agent who walks buyers through new builds every week.

## Video 1: Buying New Construction in Las Vegas: The Full Process
- **Slug:** buying-new-construction-las-vegas
- **Target length:** 13 minutes
- **One-line promise:** By the end, a buyer knows every step of buying a brand-new home in the Las Vegas valley, from picking a community to closing day, and what to expect at each stage.
- **Angle / what it covers:** This is the complete, start-to-finish walkthrough for someone who has decided they want a new build but does not know how the process works. It covers how to choose between communities and builders, how the model home and design center visits work, what the contract and timeline look like, the inspection and walkthrough steps, and what closing on a new build is like. It is the foundational, evergreen "how it all works" video.
- **Why it's distinct:** This is the broad how-to for people who already want new construction and just need the roadmap. It does not compare new to resale (Video 2) and it does not focus on traps or money tactics (Video 3). Its viewer intent is "explain the whole process to me."
- **Research questions (4-6):**
  1. What are the main steps and rough timeline for buying a new construction home in the Las Vegas valley, from first model home visit to closing?
  2. Which large master-planned communities are actively selling new homes around Las Vegas and Henderson right now, for example Summerlin, Skye Canyon, Cadence, and Inspirada, and what stage of buildout is each in?
  3. How does the design center and upgrade selection process work with builders in this market, and when in the timeline does it happen?
  4. What inspections and walkthroughs happen on a new build here, and what is a builder warranty typically expected to cover?
  5. How long does it usually take to build a home in the Las Vegas area right now, and what factors push that timeline longer or shorter?
  6. What does closing on a new construction home look like compared to closing on a resale in Clark County?

## Video 2: New Construction vs. Resale in Las Vegas: How to Choose
- **Slug:** new-construction-vs-resale-las-vegas
- **Target length:** 8 minutes
- **One-line promise:** By the end, a buyer can decide whether a brand-new home or an existing resale home is the better fit for their budget, timeline, and lifestyle in the Las Vegas valley.
- **Angle / what it covers:** This is a head-to-head decision guide for the buyer who has not chosen a path yet. It weighs the real trade-offs: price per square foot, move-in timing, location and lot maturity, landscaping and yard costs, warranties and repairs, HOA and community amenities, and how negotiation differs. It stays balanced and honest rather than pushing one side.
- **Why it's distinct:** This is for someone still deciding new versus existing, a different moment than Video 1 (which assumes the buyer already chose new) and Video 3 (which assumes new and digs into money tactics). Its viewer intent is "help me decide which type of home to buy."
- **Research questions (4-6):**
  1. How does price per square foot compare between new construction and resale homes in Las Vegas and Henderson neighborhoods right now?
  2. What are the typical extra upfront costs of a new build that a resale buyer may not face, for example landscaping, window coverings, and backyard finishing?
  3. How do location and lot maturity differ, since new construction tends to sit on the valley edges in places like Skye Canyon while resale spreads through established areas like Green Valley and Spring Valley?
  4. How does negotiation differ between builders and resale sellers in this market, including what is usually negotiable on each side?
  5. How do warranties, repairs, and maintenance expectations compare for a new build versus an older Las Vegas home?
  6. How do HOA fees and community amenities typically compare between newer master-planned communities and established Clark County neighborhoods?

## Video 3: What Builders Won't Tell You: New Construction Mistakes in Las Vegas
- **Slug:** new-construction-mistakes-las-vegas
- **One-line promise:** By the end, a buyer knows the common traps in new construction, including using the builder's sales rep as their agent, lot premiums, upgrade markups, and builder-lender incentives, and how to protect themselves.
- **Target length:** 6 minutes
- **Angle / what it covers:** This is a tight, punchy "watch out for this" video focused on the money and representation side. It explains why the friendly person in the model home works for the builder, why bringing your own agent matters and usually costs the buyer nothing, how lot premiums and upgrades can quietly inflate the price, and how builder incentives and rate buydowns through the builder's preferred lender actually work and what to compare them against.
- **Why it's distinct:** This is the cautionary, money-focused angle for a buyer who is leaning toward new and wants the insider warnings. It does not teach the full process (Video 1) or compare new to resale (Video 2). Its viewer intent is "tell me what to watch out for so I don't get burned."
- **Research questions (4-6):**
  1. In the Las Vegas market, what is the role of the on-site builder sales agent, and why is it valuable for a buyer to bring their own independent agent to the first visit?
  2. How do lot premiums work in local master-planned communities, and what tends to drive a higher premium?
  3. How do design center upgrades get priced, and where do buyers commonly overspend or overpay versus doing work later?
  4. How do builder incentives and interest rate buydowns through a builder's preferred lender typically work, and what should a buyer compare them against?
  5. Are builder incentives usually tied to using the builder's in-house or preferred lender and title company, and what are the trade-offs of that?
  6. What protections or contingencies do buyers sometimes give up in a builder contract that they would normally have in a resale purchase?

## How the three fit together
The three videos hit three different moments in a buyer's journey with no overlap: Video 2 serves the person still deciding new versus resale, Video 1 serves the person who chose new and needs the full roadmap, and Video 3 serves the person leaning new who wants the insider warnings about representation and money. Because each targets a separate question, "which type should I buy," "how does the process work," and "what should I watch out for," they support each other without cannibalizing search traffic or repeating content.
