## How they fit together

- **Video 2 (New vs. Resale)** serves the buyer still deciding which type of home to buy.
- **Video 1 (Full Process)** serves the buyer who chose new and needs the full roadmap.
- **Video 3 (What Builders Won't Tell You)** serves the buyer leaning new who wants the insider warnings on representation and money.

Because each targets a separate question, they cross-link and support each other without competing for the same search traffic. End-screens point across the set.

## Shared accuracy notes carried across the batch

- **Nevada construction-defect / warranty statute number** is never stated on camera in any video (sources conflict on the legal minimum). Confirm against live statute text at leg.state.nv.us before quoting a number.
- **New-build-only median and new-build $/sqft** for the 2026 valley were not cleanly sourceable; figures stay directional. Pull from GLVAR/MLS if a precise on-screen number is wanted.
- **Lot-premium dollar figures** were not sourceable locally; Video 3 explains drivers only.
