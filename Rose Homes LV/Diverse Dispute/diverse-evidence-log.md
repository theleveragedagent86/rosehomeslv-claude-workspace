## Contract terms that govern

Source: `contract/Agent Service Agreement - 2025.pdf` (blank template, emailed 2025-11-28).
The executed copy `Agent Service Agreement - Ryan Rose.pdf` is attached to the DigiSigner
email of 2025-12-11 (msg 19b0c851ba574e6f) and has **not yet been downloaded.** Save it into
`contract/` and confirm the Effective Date and the Coverage Area zip codes.

- **Refund right:** fewer than 12 prospects within 365 days of program start = full refund of
  deposit upon request. "A missed prospect call by the Agent is considered a prospect."
  A refund request cannot be processed before the 365-day mark. Any commissions already paid
  from Diverse prospects are subtracted from the refund.
- **Termination without cause:** either party, 30 days written notice. The refund is mandatory
  only if **Diverse** terminates before 365 days with fewer than 12 prospects. The clause is
  silent on Agent-initiated termination, so terminating that way risks forfeiting the $12,000.
  Do not send a bare without-cause notice.
- **Termination for cause:** immediate, on material breach left uncured 7 days after written
  notice detailing the breach. Also on violation of any US state or local law or advertising
  regulation. Fast exit, but it does not automatically return the deposit.
- **Referral fees survive termination:** 33% of gross commission on any prospect they
  provided, including if referred out or if Ryan changes brokerages.
- **Liability cap:** total fees paid ($12,000).
- **Claim bar:** claims must be made within one year of the incident they relate to.
- **Integration / no-reliance clause:** no representation or inducement was relied upon. Aimed
  at killing claims based on pre-sale statements.
- **Governing law:** Florida, sole and exclusive Florida jurisdiction.
- **Digital assets:** Diverse retains ownership of everything they created.

## Strategy

Hold the line to **2026-12-19**, then request the refund in writing. Rationale and risks:

**Why waiting works.** The refund right is triggered by two facts and nothing else: fewer than
12 prospects in 365 days, plus Ryan's request. No notice is required beforehand, and there is
no waiver clause that punishes patience. Terminating early is the one move that clearly risks
the money, because the mandatory-refund language only fires when Diverse terminates.

**The risks, and what to do about each.**

1. *They control the scorecard.* They could later produce a list of "missed prospect calls."
   Countermeasure: monthly portal captures, answer every call to 702-747-5921, and put the
   prospect count in writing to them now, while the count is zero and nobody is arguing.
2. *A long cheerful silence could read as satisfaction.* Already handled. Ryan complained in
   writing on 6/5, and the last substantive word in the file is Diverse admitting failure and
   promising a fix that never arrived. Keep sending neutral check-ins so the record stays
   current.
3. *The one-year claim bar.* By 2026-12-19 the fallback misrepresentation and FDUTPA theories
   built on pre-sale statements are largely time-barred. The 12-prospect refund right is a
   contract right and is the real play, but this is a reason not to slide past December.
4. *No leverage.* Florida small claims caps around $8,000, so $12,000 goes to county court,
   there is no prevailing-party fee clause, and Florida is the exclusive forum. Out-of-state
   litigation is roughly break-even at this dollar amount. The leverage is the paper trail,
   not the courthouse.
5. *Counterparty risk.* If Diverse folds before December, there is nothing to collect from.
   Nothing to do about it except not extend the timeline further.

**Now through December:** stay an ordinary, engaged client. Ask for the lead list in writing,
capture the portal monthly, log every call. Do not mention refunds, the contract, or the
365-day clock until 2026-12-19.
