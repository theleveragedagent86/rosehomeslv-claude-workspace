Written for the person, not the sale.

Read it in ten minutes. Keep it for the move.

This is a plain look at moving to the Las Vegas valley: where people live, what it costs, how schools work, and the order things happen in. Nobody paid for a page of it.

What is in here, and what is not?

A relocation guide, not a sales brochure.

It covers Las Vegas, Henderson, and the rest of the valley. It is not legal, tax, or lending advice, and it is not a price list for any one home.

How should I read the numbers?

Every figure carries a source and a date.

Costs move. Each number here names where it came from and when it was checked, so you can look it up yourself later instead of trusting a headline.

Do I have to read all of it?

No. Start with the part you are stuck on.

If you are choosing an area, page 4 is your page. If you are running the numbers, page 7. If you already have a date on the calendar, jump to page 9 and work backward.

When you see Not found it means I looked, could not verify a number, and would rather hand you the lookup than a guess.
