North Las Vegas, Green Valley, Inspirada Where to live

North valley

North Las Vegas

Growing, and usually the best value. Its own city on the north end, with a mix of established neighborhoods and a lot of brand new construction. It is often where the same budget goes furthest.

Good fit for first time buyers and anyone who wants more home for the money.

Inside Henderson

Green Valley

Established, leafy, convenient. One of the older parts of Henderson, so the trees are grown in and the shopping, dining, and parks are already there rather than coming later.

Good fit if you would rather have a mature neighborhood than a brand new one.

South Henderson

Inspirada

Newer, parks first, social. A newer master plan laid out around a web of parks, sports fields, and trails, with a younger feel and a real community calendar.

Good fit for young families who want a newer home and easy ways to meet neighbors.

How do I actually narrow this down?

Answer three questions, in this order.

  1. 1Where will you drive most days? Work, school, family. Start there and the map shrinks fast.
  2. 2Newer home or established street? This one splits the valley almost in half.
  3. 3What is the real budget? Not the pre approval ceiling. The payment you want to live with.

Seven is not all of them

These are the areas people ask about first, not the whole valley. Mountain's Edge, Southern Highlands, Cadence, Aliante, Providence, and the Lakes all have real followings, and a few of them are where I end up sending people once I hear the commute. If you have heard a name that is not on this list, ask. It is probably worth a look.

Here is where I stop

I am not going to tell you which area is best.

Any guide that ranks neighborhoods is ranking them for somebody else. The commute that makes Henderson obvious for one family makes it a bad idea for the next one.

What I can do is put your three answers next to real listings and show you where they line up and where they fight each other.

Send me the three answers. I will send back a short list, not a pitch.
