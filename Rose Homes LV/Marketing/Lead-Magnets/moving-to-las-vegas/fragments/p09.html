Nine days or nine months The practical part

Same nine steps, whatever your timeline is.

Some people plan a move for a year. Others get an offer on Friday and need keys next week. Both work. The order does not change, only the speed. Read down the left column first.

  1. 1Start your research. Read up on the valley, look at areas, and get a rough budget in your head. This guide is the start. Mark the areas that catch your eye.
  2. 2Set the budget, then talk to a lender. Get pre approved so you know the real range. It keeps the search focused and it makes your offer stronger when it counts.
  3. 3Pick a short list of areas. Two or three, chosen off your commute, your budget, and the pace you want. I will put them side by side for you.
  4. 4Visit, or tour online. Come see them if you can. If you cannot travel yet, I will walk homes and streets with you on video so you still get a real feel for it.
  5. 5Find the home. Tour what fits and pick the one that feels right. New construction and resale each have tradeoffs, and I will lay both out.
  1. 6Make the offer. We write it clean and competitive and negotiate the terms. You see every line before it goes out, and nothing gets signed that you do not understand.
  2. 7Inspections and appraisal. We line up the inspection and the lender orders the appraisal. This is where we confirm the home is solid and priced right, while you still have room to walk.
  3. 8Close. You sign, you fund, you get keys. Nevada closes through a neutral escrow company rather than an attorney settlement, which surprises a lot of people moving in from the east.
  4. 9Your first week. Utilities on, routes learned, coffee spot found. The checklist on page 10 covers the rest so nothing slips.

The one step people skip

Step 2. It is boring, and it is the one that decides whether steps 5 and 6 are calm or frantic. Do it before you fall in love with a house.
