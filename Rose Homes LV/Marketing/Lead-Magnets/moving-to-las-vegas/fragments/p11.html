Legal

Nothing here is legal, tax, or lending advice. It is public information and a description of process. For advice about a contract, your taxes, or a loan, talk to a Nevada attorney, a tax professional, or a licensed lender. Nevada advertising rules are why the brokerage name and license number appear on this page and on the cover.

Verify before you rely on it

Prices, utility rates, school programs, and residency requirements all change. Every figure in this guide was verified in July 2026 and each one names its source on the page it appears. Look up anything that matters to your decision before you act on it. Where verification failed, the guide prints Not found rather than a guess.

Sources

Home prices come from Las Vegas REALTORS. Energy figures come from the U.S. Energy Information Administration. Tax information comes from the Nevada Department of Taxation. School structure comes from the Clark County School District. Utility figures come from published rate schedules and 2026 valley averages. The cost pages are rebuilt each quarter.

Fair housing

This guide describes areas by geography, housing stock, and amenities only. It does not describe who lives anywhere, and it does not rank schools. That is a legal line and it is also just the honest way to do it.

What does working with me cost you?

We agree on it in writing, before you tour.

Since 2024, a buyer and their agent sign a written agreement before touring a home, and it spells out how the agent gets paid. Nothing about that is a surprise or a handshake, and you will read it before you sign it.
