Why Las Vegas

WHY PEOPLE
MOVE TO
LAS VEGAS

People move here from all over, and most of them tell me the same thing. They came for one reason and stayed for a few more. The weather is warm, the homes give you more space, and your paycheck tends to stretch further.

Nevada has no state income tax. That's a real difference for a lot of families, and it's one of the first things folks ask me about. You also get sunshine almost every day, so weekends outside become normal.

If you're coming from Southern California, the drive is easy and the airport is close. You can keep your ties back home and still build a new life here. The guide breaks all of this down in plain language.

NO STATE INCOME TAX
Nevada doesn't charge a state income tax. For many movers, that's the first thing that gets their attention.
SUNNY LIFESTYLE
Hiking, golf, pools, and patios. The weather here makes it easy to spend time outside almost year-round.
WARM WEATHER
Long, sunny summers and mild winters. You can leave the snow shovel behind and enjoy the open sky.
CLOSE TO SOCAL
An easy drive to Southern California and a busy airport nearby. Staying connected to family is simple.
