**Value Email A - Choosing a neighborhood**
Subject: `Picking the right Las Vegas neighborhood`

```
Hey #lead_first_name#,

One of the first questions people ask me when they move here is simple: where should I live? It is a big decision, so here is the short version to get you started.

- Henderson is known for being clean, safe, and family friendly, with easy access to parks and good schools.
- Summerlin sits on the west side near Red Rock, with newer homes and lots of trails and shopping.
- Spring Valley is more central, so you are close to almost everything, often at a friendlier price.
- Centennial Hills and Skye Canyon are up north, newer and a bit more room for your money.

Every area has a different feel, and the right pick depends on your commute, your budget, and your day to day life. Want me to send a short list of neighborhoods that match what you are looking for?

#signature#
```

**Value Email B - Cost of living and no state income tax**
Subject: `What it really costs to live in Las Vegas`

```
Hey #lead_first_name#,

Cost of living is usually the next thing on people's minds, so let me give you the honest picture.

- Nevada has no state income tax. That is a real perk, and for a lot of people moving from higher tax states, it makes a noticeable difference in their monthly budget.
- Housing here often stretches further than it does in California or the Pacific Northwest, though it depends on the area you choose.
- Everyday costs like groceries and gas are pretty normal for a big metro. Summer cooling bills are the one thing newcomers tend to forget to plan for.

I am happy to walk you through a rough monthly budget for the kind of home you have in mind, so there are no surprises after you move. Want me to put one together for you?

#signature#
```

## Lofty CRM Setup Instructions

### Step 1: Create the Action Plans
Create 3 separate action plans in Lofty:
1. Moving to LV - Hot
2. Moving to LV - Warm
3. Moving to LV - Nurture

### Step 2: Set the Trigger
For each plan, set the trigger to: **Tag Applied = `moving-to-las-vegas`**
Track assignment is based on the lead's answer to the form custom field "When do you want to move? 3, 6, or 12+ months" (see Track Assignment table above). If the field is blank, assign to Warm by default.

### Step 3: Add Actions
Add each touch from the per-track schedule above as an action in the matching plan:
- **Text Message actions:** Set the delay, paste the copy, enable business hours (9:00 AM to 7:00 PM PT)
- **Email actions:** Set the delay, paste the subject and body, set sender as Ryan Rose
- **Call Task action (Hot only):** Set the 2 hour delay, paste the task description
- **Day 0 lead email:** Attach the relocation guide PDF (`relocation-guide.pdf`), or include the hosted link if the guide lives online
- **Internal Notification Email (Day 0):** Set the recipient to **ryan@rosehomeslv.com** (NOT the lead)
- **Day 35 Handoff:** Use "Start Another Action Plan" action, target = "Long Term Nurture - Buyer"

### Step 4: Configure Settings
- Enable **Stop on Reply** for all 3 plans
- Set business hours for text messages: **9:00 AM to 7:00 PM PT**
- Email sender: **Ryan Rose (ryan@rosehomeslv.com)**

### Step 5: Test
- Create a test contact in Lofty
- Apply the tag `moving-to-las-vegas` to the test contact
- Verify the action plan triggers and the Day 0 actions fire correctly
