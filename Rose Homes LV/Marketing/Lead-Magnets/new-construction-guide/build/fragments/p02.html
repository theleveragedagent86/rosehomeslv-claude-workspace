Written from your side of the table.

No builder paid for a page of it.

This is the buyer's side of a new construction purchase in Clark County. It plays no favorites among builders.

What is this, and what is it not?

A buyer's guide, not a brochure.

It covers Las Vegas, Henderson, and the rest of Clark County. It is not legal advice and it is not a price list.

How do I read the numbers?

Every fact carries a source and a date.

The small mark after a fact points to the source list that ships with this guide. Each entry gives you the organization, the web address, and the day I checked it.

Where do I go next?

The guide splits three ways on page 5.

Read the four pages that sound like your situation and skip the other eight. The three paths rejoin on page 18. Pages 6 through 17 carry a tab on the outer edge, so you can flip straight to yours.

Two pages go out of date on purpose. See pages 36 and 37.

When you see Not found it means I looked, could not verify the number, and would rather hand you the lookup than a guess.
