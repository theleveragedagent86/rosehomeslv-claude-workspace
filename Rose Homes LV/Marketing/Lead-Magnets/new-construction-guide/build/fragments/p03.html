Who works for whom Start here

Who is the person at that desk?

The rep works for the builder. That is the job.

The on-site sales rep is paid by the builder and represents the builder. One national builder puts it in its own buyer materials in writing: the new home consultant is not your broker or agent.*

That is a role, not a character flaw. Nobody here is a villain. The rep is good at the job, and the job is selling that builder's homes at that builder's price.

Nevada keeps the money trail visible. A licensee has to tell each party, as soon as practicable, every source they get paid from,* and hand every party the state's Duties Owed form.* Ask for that form. Read the box that says who the licensee represents.

What does my own agent owe me?

Duties Nevada will not let anyone sign away.

An agent who represents you owes reasonable care, confidentiality for a year after the agreement ends, presenting every offer, and disclosing known material facts.* Those duties cannot be waived.*

Since August 17, 2024, you and your agent sign a written agreement before you tour a home, and it spells out how the agent gets paid.** Read that page. It is short, and it is where the cost of representation is written down.
