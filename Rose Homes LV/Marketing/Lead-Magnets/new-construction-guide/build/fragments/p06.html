Path A  ·  Moving here from out of state

You are buying in a city you do not live in yet.

One afternoon inside the house. Maybe none.

That is the real situation, so this section is about the four things that change when you buy new construction from a distance. It does not try to sell you the valley.

Where is the part about neighborhoods?

In the other guide, on purpose.

Neighborhoods, cost of living, schools, and the general case for living here are covered in my relocation guide at rosehomeslv.com/moving-to-las-vegas. I am not going to repeat it in smaller type. Read that first if you are still choosing a city.

These four pages assume you have chosen. Also worth a read: moving from California into a new build and the buying process start to finish.

What will feel unfamiliar first?

Nobody's attorney runs your closing.

Nevada closes through a neutral escrow company, not an attorney settlement, and escrow is licensed under state law.* The Division of Insurance licenses the title and escrow people who handle your money.* How a Nevada closing runs is on page 27.

And do not book the movers off the builder's date.

Two builders publish that the closing date is an estimate,* and that it is subject to change and cannot be guaranteed.* The date that is actually enforceable is the outside completion date in your contract, which at one builder is two years from signing.* Build timing is on page 23.
