Why does cash cost me money?

Because the incentive is built around a loan.

Nearly a quarter of local sales in June 2026 were cash.* A cash buyer can walk past the biggest concession in the deal without being told it existed.

Here is the split, where a builder publishes it. One states that up to $15,000 of an advertised up to $50,000 in flex cash is contingent on closing with its own lender,* and caps any lender tied incentive at 3 percent of the price.* Another says you can buy without its affiliate, but not get the advertised rate.* A third requires its own mortgage company.*

None of it is a trick. A builder may price a discount around its own lender, as long as you can still buy without that lender and the discount is real.* When a builder sends you to a company it owns, federal law requires a written Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure with a range of charges,* at the referral.* Incentives, page 22.

One builder warns that combined incentives may exceed your loan program's limits.*

Pro tip

Get the affiliated lender's Loan Estimate anyway.

Even if you never intend to borrow a dollar. It prices the concession you are giving up. Then ask in writing what the builder will do for cash instead. Page 16.

The five day right, and its condition

Walking the house can spend it.

If your new home sits in a common interest community with an HOA, you may cancel in writing until midnight of the fifth calendar day after signing,* with no penalty and a prompt full refund.*

The catch almost nobody reads: the right is conditioned on not having personally inspected the unit.* Fly in, walk the home, sign that afternoon, and you may have signed it away.
