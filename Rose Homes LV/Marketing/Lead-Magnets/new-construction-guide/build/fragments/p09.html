Do the taxes compare to home?

Not line for line. Different starting number.

Nevada does not tax market value. It taxes 35 percent of taxable value,* built from replacement cost, minus wear and tear, plus the land.* If your state taxes market value, do not compare percentages.

Your main home is capped at 3 percent a year,* everything else at up to 8 percent.* Only one property per person statewide counts as a primary residence.* A home in your old state is fine. A second Nevada one is not.

A brand new home gets no cap in year one.* Page 21 has the whole picture.

Can I use the state's worker program?

Not yet. Six months of residency first.

Worker Advantage requires six months of Nevada residency,* even if your job qualifies: health care, education, public safety, or construction trades.*
