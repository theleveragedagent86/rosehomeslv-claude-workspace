Path B  ·  Moving up inside the valley

You already own. That changes the schedule.

Two transactions, two clocks, one of them yours.

You know the valley and you know what your house is worth. The hard part is the calendar, not the city.

What will a builder commit to?

Less than you would expect, in writing.

Eleven of the twelve volume builders publish no contract to close time for Las Vegas at all.* One publishes an average build of four to five months,* plus a separate 30 to 50 day window before construction starts for the loan, the selections, and the permit.* Together that is roughly five to six and a half months.* Another publishes an average of 6 to 12 months.*

Your selections have to be final before the build starts, at the one builder that publishes the rule.** So the design appointment sits at the front of the schedule.

So when will it be done?

Wrong question. Ask for the outside date.

The estimate at the sales desk and the deadline in the contract are different things. Only one is enforceable. One builder's published completion clause commits to finishing within two years of signing unless the agreement says otherwise, with extensions for recognized delays.** That is an outside deadline, not a delivery promise.

Closing dates are published as estimates,* subject to change and not guaranteed.*

Ask what happens to you on the outside date.
