List early, or list late?

Both cost something. Neither one is a rule.

List early and you risk carrying two payments. List late and you risk the build slot. There is no rule, because the answer sits inside your contract and your budget.

Two honest gaps first.

Not found No Nevada statute gives a new home buyer a remedy for late delivery, and no current builder purchase agreement was obtained for this guide. So I cannot tell you what your contract says about a home sale contingency. Find that clause and read it out loud before you sign.

Not found There is also no Nevada law setting how much earnest money a builder can require, or whether it is refundable. The builder's contract sets all of it.* Ask the number and the refund terms in one sentence.

Your build's closing date is published as an estimate.* Anything you sign on the sale side that treats it as firm is a bet. Cancelling is its own subject: what happens if you cancel.
