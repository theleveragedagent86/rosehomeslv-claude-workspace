Does a bigger down payment buy anything besides a smaller loan?

Yes, and almost nobody explains this one.

How much a builder may pay toward your costs scales with your down payment on a conventional loan. More equity down, more the builder may contribute.

Builder paid rate buydowns count against that cap,* so nobody stacks an unlimited buydown on unlimited closing cost help. Builder money can never fund your down payment, reserves, or minimum contribution.* It may cover up to 12 months of HOA assessments.*

One builder warns in writing that combined incentives may exceed your loan program's limits.* Ask your lender to check the cap before you accept the package. Full treatment, with the diagram, on page 22.

And if I sell again in year three?

You compete with the builder, in your own neighborhood.

Nobody names this at the design center. Your resale sits down the street from the same builder's new inventory, with whatever incentive is running that month.

Two measurements of the same gap

In June 2026 the new home median was $525,000* and the existing single family median was $490,000,* a gap of about $35,000.* A national study published June 22, 2026 measured the same gap at $79,460.* It ranked Las Vegas 45th in the country, against a national average gap of $52,565.*

Both are right. The first compares single family homes sold through the local MLS in one month. The second uses a broader mix. They answer different questions, so I print both instead of the flattering one. See pages 36 and 37.
