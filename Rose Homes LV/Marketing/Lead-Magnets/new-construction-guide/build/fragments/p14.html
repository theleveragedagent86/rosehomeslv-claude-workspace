Path C  ·  Your first new build

"Included" means three different things.

Depending on who you are standing in front of.

Builders here run three different business models. The word included changes meaning in each one.

What are the three?

Fixed package, included features, or a design center.

One builder has no design center and no upgrades at all, just a set package.* A second uses included features instead of a buyer facing design center, and says features vary by community and homesite.* Others compete on what is standard: owned solar, an EV outlet, smart home, tankless water heater.* One tags nearly every Clark County community solar included.*

Then the studios. A Home Expressions Studio,* a valley design studio,* Home Gallery showrooms,* and nine designer appointed collections instead of item by item.* The three models are drawn on page 15.

What are the limits nobody puts on a flyer?

Two, and then two blanks.

Your selections must be final before the build starts, at the one builder that publishes the rule.** Another states on every community page that buyers may be limited in the structural changes, options, and upgrades they can make.*

Not found Not one of the twelve volume builders publishes a design center spend requirement, a credit amount,* or whether upgrades can be financed.* Ask both in writing. Pre drywall confirms the options you ordered got installed,* page 26. The lot is on page 24.

Longer: the design center, upgrade costs, what you cannot change.
