How do I tell if a loan offer is good?

One document, page 3, two lenders, same day.

A lender must hand you a Loan Estimate within three business days of your application.** Ask for that. Not a worksheet or screenshot.

Page 3 carries a Comparisons box: what you will have paid in five years, the APR, and the total interest percentage.* That is where two offers get compared.

It only works on the same loan type, same day.* Rates move weekly. In July 2026 the national 30 year average moved every week, from 6.43 to 6.58 percent.* That is a survey average, not a quote. Your rate depends on your credit, your down payment, and the day you lock.* Your rate during a long build is on page 23.

And points?

One point is 1 percent of the loan.

On a $100,000 loan, one point is $1,000.* There is no fixed rule for how much a point lowers your rate.* So ask the total cost with and without points, over the shortest, longest, and most likely time you would keep the loan.*

If you are a veteran

A Certificate of Eligibility showing $36,000 basic entitlement means you have full entitlement.* Entitlement is not approval. The lender still checks credit, income, debts, and savings.*
