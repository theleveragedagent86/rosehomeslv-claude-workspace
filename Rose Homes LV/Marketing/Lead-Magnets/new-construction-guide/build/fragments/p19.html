The bill outside your mortgage The shared core

So where did the money go?

Into the ground, before your house was there.

Las Vegas builds new neighborhoods on empty desert. Somebody has to pay for the streets, the sidewalks, the curbs, the streetlights, and the sewer and water lines that go in before the first house does.

Often that somebody is you.

A Special Improvement District, usually shortened to SID, is a charge on the properties inside the district. It pays for exactly that kind of public work.* In Henderson the same thing is called a Local Improvement District, or LID. Different letter. Same idea.

Here is the part that surprises people. The district spreads that cost over ten to thirty years at a low interest rate.* You do not clear it at closing. You pay a piece of it, twice a year, for decades.

Clark County Treasurer and Clark County Public Works, verified July 2026

What should I know before I sign?

Five things, in plain English.

  1. 1It is billed twice a year. The bill goes out about sixty days before it is due.*
  2. 2It is not part of your property tax bill. It is billed separately, and it is usually not inside your mortgage payment.*
  3. 3It is a lien on the home until it is paid off.
  4. 4Missing a payment is serious. In unincorporated Clark County a late payment adds 2 percent a month plus interest,* and the county can begin foreclosure within sixty days.*
  5. 5Two houses on one street can owe different amounts. The cost is split by how much each lot benefits, on a front foot, area, zone, or other fair basis.*
