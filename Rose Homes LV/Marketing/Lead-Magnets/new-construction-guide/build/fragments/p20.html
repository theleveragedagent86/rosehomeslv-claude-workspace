Can I pay it off early?

In unincorporated Clark County, yes, any time, with a 3 percent prepayment penalty on the unpaid balance.* Not found for city district terms. Ask for a written payoff quote.

Run this before you sign. About five minutes.

  • 1. Get the parcel number for the exact lot. Not the community. The lot.
  • 2. Run it at the administrator's free parcel search,* which covers the county and all three cities.*
  • 3. Ask in writing for four numbers. Amount each year, balance left, year it ends, early payoff penalty.* Billing, (702) 796-0082.*
  • 4. Watch your closing date. Inside about fifteen days of a billing date, have escrow pay it.*
