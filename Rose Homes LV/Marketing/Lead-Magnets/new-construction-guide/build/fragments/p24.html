How do I pick the lot?

Almost everybody compares premiums. Almost nobody compares lots.

The premium is a number on a page. One builder notes on every price that a homesite premium may apply,* and another that prices may exclude options and lot premiums.* Fine. Now go stand on it.

Six things to walk before you pick

  • Which way the backyard faces, and how much glass carries the afternoon in a 110 degree summer.
  • What is behind the wall. An arterial road and a sound wall are not the same neighbor as a backyard.
  • How close the retention basin or wash is, and which way water runs when it finally rains.
  • Whether a power line easement crosses it. Ask to see it drawn, not described.
  • Whether you sit downhill from the street. Desert drainage finds the low lot.
  • Which flood zone the parcel is in. A standard policy excludes flood,* and insurers price zones into your premium.*

Two numbers I do not have

Not found for what lot orientation costs here in utility bills or comfort. Judgment calls, so I am giving you a walk instead.

Not found for any Las Vegas builder phase pricing schedule. Lots release in phases and the price commonly moves between them. Ask for the release calendar.

Living in one still being built

Ask what is built. Not what is coming.

One builder's published terms say amenities are subject to change, substitution, or deletion without notice, with no guarantee the community gets built as planned.* The same terms allow swapping materials and brands for whatever the builder judges equal,* and prices change without notice.*

It happens here. One North Las Vegas association still lists a school and a grocery store as planned* after announcing completion in January 2026.* Henderson settled a dispute with a developer over a missed park deadline.*
