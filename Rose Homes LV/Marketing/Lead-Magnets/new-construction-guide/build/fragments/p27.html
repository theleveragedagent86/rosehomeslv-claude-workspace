What actually happens at the end?

A neutral escrow company, and bills nobody mentioned.

Nevada closes through a neutral escrow company, not an attorney settlement, licensed by the state* and regulated by the Division of Insurance.* Nobody there represents you. That is the point.

Transfer tax

Clark County charges $2.55 per $500 of value, or part of it, at recording,* and both sides are liable if more is owed.* Who pays it on a builder contract is Not found.

Title

One general Nevada custom, per a State Bar brochure: the seller pays your title policy, you pay the lender's.* A builder contract overrides it. Whether any builder follows it is Not found.

Insurance

Bound before closing, priced on rebuild cost, not what you paid.* Insurers price distance to a fire house, alarms, flood zones, and age.* Flood is excluded.* Nevada runs a free Homeowner Coverage Tool.* A verified 2026 county average is Not found. Get three quotes.

Closing near an assessment billing date? Have escrow pay it, or the bill follows the builder's old address.**
