Chapter six  ·  Clark County only

Six areas, and three rules that apply to all of them.

No school rating. No drive time. No invented HOA fee.

These four pages describe what is built and what is documented. Where a number is not published, I say so.

The three shared rules

  • Assessments are per parcel. The lookup on page 19 works in every area here.*
  • HOA dollars are rarely published. I will not repeat an uncited figure. Nevada law does entitle you to the association budget and its assessment history.*
  • Schools change by address. This guide rates no school and prints no assignment. Check the address at the district's own tool, or call 702-799-7678.***
