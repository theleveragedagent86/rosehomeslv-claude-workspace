Northwest and southwest Six areas

Skye Canyon, the northwest, and the southwest.

Skye Canyon sits in northwest Las Vegas off US 95 at Skye Canyon Park Drive.* The master plan names three builders on its own site: Century Communities, Toll Brothers, and LGI Homes.* Century's four Skyeview communities ran $379,000 to $520,000, Toll Brothers started at $665,000, and the master plan advertises from the $400s.***

What is open today is the point here. Skye Canyon Park, Skye Center with a coffee house, Skye Fitness with a junior Olympic pool, plus trails and bike lanes.* No coming soon amenities are named. No vacant lots either, homes and townhomes only.*

The southwest

Enterprise is the unincorporated township covering most of the southwest valley, roughly 66 square miles, south of the 215 from near Fort Apache and Warm Springs to I-15 and St. Rose Parkway.** It grew almost 60 percent between 2010 and 2023 against 20 percent valleywide, to 245,243 residents at the end of 2023.** Two of its tax districts ran 2.7081 and 2.9328 per $100 for fiscal 2026 to 2027.**

Mountain's Edge started in 2004.* The master association lists Exploration Peak Park, Paiute Park, Nathaniel Jones Park, plus paseos and walking trails.* Mountain's Edge Regional Park is a Clark County park and is not part of the association.* The association does not publish dues, so call 702-457-6362.* Acreage, home count and buildout: Not found
