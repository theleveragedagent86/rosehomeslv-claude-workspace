Henderson Six areas

Henderson: Cadence and Inspirada.

Cadence sits in east Henderson, ZIP 89011, inside the city's Eastside redevelopment area near the Sunset Industrial Corridor.* It covers 2,200 acres, is planned for up to 12,250 homes and 1.1 million square feet of commercial space, broke ground in August 2013, and began selling in December 2014.****

Builders on the developer's own FAQ: Beazer, Century Communities, D.R. Horton, Lennar, Richmond American, Taylor Morrison, and Woodside.* The developer states a range of $300,000s to $700,000s.* Central Park, about 50 acres, is the largest named amenity,* and Fire Station 87 opened in January 2023.* Which amenities are open versus planned, and the buildout date: Not found

Cadence has the best published carrying cost story in this guide. The developer states its homes carry no SID or LID fees,* and its new homes page shows $40 monthly HOA, which is most likely the master fee only and not an all in number.** Absence from a district list is still not proof for your lot, so run the parcel anyway.*

Inspirada sits in the far southwest corner of Henderson, ZIP 89044, at the base of the Black Mountain foothills.* Its master developer is a partnership of KB Home, Toll Brothers and Tri Pointe.* A September 2024 report put buildout at 7,500 homes and 18,000 residents at that time.* There are seven parks, and the seventh was the last one planned.* Whether Sentiero Park opened: Not found

The comparison that earns the page

Same city, different carrying cost. Inspirada carries Henderson district T-18, first payment June 1, 2008 and final payment June 1, 2035.** Cadence publishes none.* That is not the whole picture, but it is a real line in a budget for another nine years. Henderson tax district 505 ran 2.9613 per $100 for fiscal 2026 to 2027.*
