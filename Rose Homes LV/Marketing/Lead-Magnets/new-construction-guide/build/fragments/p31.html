North Las Vegas, Union Village, and the six areas.

North Las Vegas is its own city. Its improvement districts, permits, police and fire are city services.* Tax district 250 ran 3.3544 per $100 for fiscal 2026 to 2027, the highest of the four rates on page 21.*

Valley Vista began in 2017. The association says the master plan completed in January 2026, with 3,469 homes planned.*** Built: Galaxy Park at 9 acres, an emergency room, a detention basin, multifamily.* Still listed as planned after that announcement: an elementary school and a grocery store.*

Tule Springs is where D.R. Horton concentrates: eleven of its 22 Clark County communities are phases of it.* A Village 2 is planned off North 5th Street,* and KB Home was approved for 700 more homes.* Tule Springs acreage and home count from a developer or city: Not found

City districts: 64 at Valley Vista runs to March 1, 2049, 66 at Tule Springs Village 1 to March 1, 2052, and Aliante district 60 has matured.***

Union Village, briefly

Henderson Hospital opened in November 2016 and anchors it.* Built: the hospital, a patient tower, an 85,000 square foot surgery center, a medical office building, a 55 plus rental community.* Still on paper: a dialysis clinic, memory care village, hotel, condominiums, promenade, retail, apartments, an athletic club.* The city and the developer settled a dispute over a missed park deadline.* Active for-sale communities: zero.*
