A list of facts, not a scorecard.

Builders appear A to Z. That is the only order used. Every box holds a published fact or NOT FOUND.

About the information in this table

Every item in this table is a record published by an outside group, not an opinion of Ryan Rose or Rose Homes LV. We did not rate these builders. We did not rank them. We looked up what three kinds of outside sources say, and we copied it down.

The sources are the Better Business Bureau, the Nevada State Contractors Board, and named research studies that publish their methods. Each entry shows the source and the date we looked it up. Where we could not find a record, the entry says NOT FOUND. That means we did not find it, not that nothing exists.

These records change. A license status, a letter rating, and a complaint count can all change in a week. Everything here was checked on July 25, 2026. Before you rely on any of it, look it up yourself. The links are free and take about a minute:

Better Business Bureau business profiles: https://www.bbb.org/search

Nevada State Contractors Board license lookup: https://app.nvcontractorsboard.com/Clients/NVSCB/Public/ContractorLicenseSearch/ContractorLicenseSearch.aspx

Nevada State Contractors Board disciplinary actions: https://app.nvcontractorsboard.com/Clients/NVSCB/Public/DisciplineSearch/DisciplineSearchPage.aspx

A few things to keep in mind while you read it. A Better Business Bureau letter rating is that group's own measure and is not a government rating. A count of complaints is a count of complaints filed, not a count of findings against the builder, and a builder who closes more homes will usually show a larger number. A builder can choose to pay for Better Business Bureau accreditation or choose not to, and either choice is normal. Many builders hold their state license under a company name that is different from the name on the sales office, so search the company name shown here, not the brand.

Nothing in this table is a recommendation to use or avoid any builder. If a record raises a question for you, ask the builder about it directly, and ask us to help you read the answer.

Nothing in this table is legal advice. It is a record of public information. For advice about a contract, a warranty claim, or a dispute with a builder, talk to a Nevada attorney.
