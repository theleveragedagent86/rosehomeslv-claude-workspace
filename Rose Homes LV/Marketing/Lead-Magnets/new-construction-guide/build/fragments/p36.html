Builder activity, first half of 2026: net sales 4,284 down 15 percent, closings 4,044 down 22 percent, permits 4,156 down 25 percent.*** June alone was the slowest month of the year for net sales.* Full year 2025, for comparison: 9,734 permits down 20 percent and the fewest since 2016, and 9,990 closings down 20 percent.*
