Before you sign

Ask these before you sign.

Or photograph it.

Registration and representation

  • Are you registering my agent today, at this first contact? (p4)
  • Does an appointment booked on your website register my agent? (p4)
  • Must my agent walk in with me, and how long does registration last? (p4)
  • Who do you represent in this transaction? (p3)

The lot and the assessment

  • What is the annual assessment on this exact lot, the balance, the payoff year, and the prepayment penalty?* (p19)
  • Is there a homesite premium on this lot, and how much? (p17)
  • Which flood zone is this parcel in? (p24)
  • When does the next phase release, and how do I get on the list? (p24)

The money

  • Lender: may I see page 3 of the Loan Estimate, the Comparisons box?* (p16)
  • Lender: at my down payment, what is the cap on builder contributions?* (p22)
  • Lender: are you escrowing on the completed home or on the land?* (p21)
  • What is the design center spend requirement, and what credit comes with it?* (p14)
  • Can upgrades be financed?* (p14)
  • What are the master and sub association monthly amounts, in writing?* (p17)

The contract

  • May I have the warranty document, with its term lengths, plus the rights sheet?* (p26)
  • What is the outside completion date in this contract, and what happens on it? (p10)
  • How much earnest money, and when does it stop being refundable? (p11)
  • If you own the lender or the title company, where is the disclosure? (p8)

The build

  • May I bring my own inspector, and at which stages? (p26)
  • Who attends pre-drywall if I am out of state? (p7)
  • Will the punch list be written, and what date completes it? (p26)
  • How does the rate lock work, and what does an extension cost? (p23)
