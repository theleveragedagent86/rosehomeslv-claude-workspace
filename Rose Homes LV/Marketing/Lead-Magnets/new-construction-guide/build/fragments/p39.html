Legal

Nothing here is legal advice. It is a record of public information and a description of process. For advice about a contract, a warranty claim, or a dispute with a builder, talk to a Nevada attorney. Assume an arbitration clause in a builder contract is enforceable, because federal arbitration law overrides the Nevada statute that says otherwise.** Nevada advertising rules are why the brokerage name and license number appear here.*
