Before You Walk Into the Model Home, six design directions

Every direction renders the identical copy: the cover, the SID and LID spread on pages 19 and 20, and the landing page hero. Differences you see are design, never content. Previews are live and scaled to 30 percent. Open one full size to judge type at reading distance, which is the only fair way to judge it.

1Desert Bloom

.** The Pozek energy translated to the Mojave: sunbaked, saturated, cheerful the whole way through. Prickly pear magenta, sunset coral, spring water turquoise and sunshine yellow sit on warm sand paper as solid panels, not tints of a serious neutral. Fraunces carries display, Nunito Sans carries reading, and a flat vector valley map with the 215 as a ribbon is the signature device.
