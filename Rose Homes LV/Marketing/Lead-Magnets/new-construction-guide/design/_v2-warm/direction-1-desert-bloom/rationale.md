# Desert Bloom, rationale

**Thesis.** The Pozek energy translated to the Mojave: sunbaked, saturated, cheerful the whole way through. Prickly pear magenta, sunset coral, spring water turquoise and sunshine yellow sit on warm sand paper as solid panels, not tints of a serious neutral. Fraunces carries display, Nunito Sans carries reading, and a flat vector valley map with the 215 as a ribbon is the signature device.

**Why it warms protective content.** The rejected round made the sourcing apparatus into the design. Desert Bloom makes the *question* into the design. Every hard block opens with a friendly question in colored caps, so the reader is answered rather than warned: "What is this bill nobody mentioned?", "So why did nobody ask me?", "Can I pay it off early?", "Can I check my own lot?". The facts underneath are untouched.

The NOT FOUND callout has to prove that. It is a solid prickly pear panel, the largest object on page 19, with a yellow ribbon reading HERE IS WHERE I STOP over "I am not going to hand you a number." It is the loudest thing on the spread, so it cannot read as a gap or an apology. The load-bearing line, "A wrong number here would steer your budget for thirty years", is pulled out in pale yellow at 5.00 contrast.

The same refusal runs through the data. The twenty row table gets a magenta header band and reads as a magazine sidebar, NOT FOUND cells are coral pills with a heavy left rule, and retired districts are turquoise pills reading Matured and Paid off.

**Wins and loses.** It wins the actual audience: someone who has driven past a model home and is not a finance reader. This looks like a place guide, not a disclosure. It loses anyone who wants the guide to look expensive, and the number-only skimmer, because color blocking gives every element equal confidence and leaves no gray zone to skim past.

**Ink.** The expensive direction. Sand floods all 44 pages at about 15 percent TAC and a typical spread carries another 20 to 25 percent of its area in solids at 146 to 186 percent TAC. Budget two and a half to four times the click cost of a text weight duplex book. No press risk: every solid is under 190 percent TAC, so no set-off and no fifth ink. The economy override is one rule, set `--sand` and `--sand-2` to `#FFFFFF`, stripping the flood for roughly 40 percent less coverage. Every ratio still passes on white.

**Grayscale.** The tint cards survive: yellow 238, turquoise 236, coral 232 and magenta 230 read as near whites with dark type. The two solid saturated fills did not. Magenta drops to gray 101 and turquoise to 112, which is no difference on a home laser. Fixed by giving every solid turquoise card a 3.5pt sunshine yellow left edge, gray 205, so it separates by shape and tone rather than hue. Nothing here carries meaning by color alone.

**Mobile hero.** One markup block, one container query at 760px. At 1280 the copy holds the left column and the photo the right, with the valley map overlapping up into the photo. At 375 the grid collapses and the art block goes `display:contents`, letting the photo reorder above the copy while the map falls below: photo band at 110px, headline at 345px, map at 1276px. Button goes full width, nav stacks, and the brokerage keeps its own pill so `Real Broker, LLC` never wraps. No form in the embed, no `position: fixed` anywhere.

**Three reader paths.** Every page opens with the same four color band across the full bleed head. The band is the path indicator: the active path's segment runs wide and the others compress, so a reader flipping through watches a stripe move across the top edge. The same hues key the question subheads. Yellow is never a path. Yellow is always the author speaking directly: the credibility band, the kicker, the NOT FOUND ribbon, the soft close. Pages 19 and 20 mirror the band order, and the folios meet at the fold, 19 in a magenta tile, 20 in turquoise.

**What did not fit, flagged rather than cut.** The copy runs about 900 words against an 840 budget, plus artwork. To hold both pages at exactly 11 inches: SVG-04 is 2.4 inches tall rather than 45 percent of column height, and the NOT FOUND callout outranks it in size, because the callout is the argument, the diagram support. Body sits at 8.5pt and the table at 7.6pt, small but legible at 15.63 contrast. The valley map is a 1.1 inch locator here rather than the divider spread it becomes in the book. Headings were rewritten as questions. No fact, number, date, statute, phone number or source changed.
