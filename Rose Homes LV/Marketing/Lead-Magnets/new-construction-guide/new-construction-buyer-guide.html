Who works for whom Start here

Who is the person at that desk?

The rep works for the builder. That is the job.

The on-site sales rep is paid by the builder and represents the builder. One national builder puts it in its own buyer materials in writing: the new home consultant is not your broker or agent.1

That is a role, not a character flaw. Nobody here is a villain. The rep is good at the job, and the job is selling that builder's homes at that builder's price.

Nevada keeps the money trail visible. A licensee has to tell each party, as soon as practicable, every source they get paid from,2 and hand every party the state's Duties Owed form.3 Ask for that form. Read the box that says who the licensee represents.

What does my own agent owe me?

Duties Nevada will not let anyone sign away.

An agent who represents you owes reasonable care, confidentiality for a year after the agreement ends, presenting every offer, and disclosing known material facts.4 Those duties cannot be waived.5

Since August 17, 2024, you and your agent sign a written agreement before you tour a home, and it spells out how the agent gets paid.67 Read that page. It is short, and it is where the cost of representation is written down.

Longer answer here: do you need a realtor for new construction.

Nobody's attorney runs your closing.

What actually has to be solved?

Four mechanics, in writing, before you fly home.

Remote signing. Who attends your inspections. Whether you need a power of attorney. Whether you can close by mail.

Being straight with you: I have no published source for how each builder handles a mail-away closing. So this page names no fees. It gives you the questions.

Can I do the walkthroughs on video?

No. Somebody local has to stand in the house.

Four points let a buyer catch problems: pre drywall, blue tape, the final walkthrough, and the eleven month warranty visit.20 Pre drywall is the only time anyone sees framing, straps and hangers, plumbing and electrical runs, duct routing, and waterproofing.21 After that it is behind the wall. Page 26.

Nevada certifies home inspectors through the Real Estate Division,22 and a certified inspector carries at least $100,000 in errors and omissions plus $100,000 general liability.23 Ask to see the certificate.

One thing to know: no Nevada statute gives you the right to bring your own inspector onto a builder's job site.24 It is a contract question, so it belongs on the list, not in a phone call later.

Get these in writing

Seven questions, before you get on the plane home.

  • 1. Signing. Electronic signatures on every document, or original ink?
  • 2. Inspections. May I bring my own certified inspector, and at which stages?
  • 3. Who stands in for me. Who may attend pre drywall and blue tape, and how much notice?
  • 4. Power of attorney. Do you and escrow accept one, and who approves the form?
  • 5. Mail-away closing. May I sign with a notary in my state, and who pays the overnight?
  • 6. If your date moves. What happens to my deposit and my rate?
  • 7. Progress. Who sends photos, how often? Get a name, not a department.

Nevada escrow is a neutral third party, not an attorney settlement.15

Why does cash cost me money?

Because the incentive is built around a loan.

Nearly a quarter of local sales in June 2026 were cash.25 A cash buyer can walk past the biggest concession in the deal without being told it existed.

Here is the split, where a builder publishes it. One states that up to $15,000 of an advertised up to $50,000 in flex cash is contingent on closing with its own lender,26 and caps any lender tied incentive at 3 percent of the price.27 Another says you can buy without its affiliate, but not get the advertised rate.28 A third requires its own mortgage company.29

None of it is a trick. A builder may price a discount around its own lender, as long as you can still buy without that lender and the discount is real.30 When a builder sends you to a company it owns, federal law requires a written Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure with a range of charges,31 at the referral.32 Incentives, page 22.

One builder warns that combined incentives may exceed your loan program's limits.33

Pro tip

Get the affiliated lender's Loan Estimate anyway.

Even if you never intend to borrow a dollar. It prices the concession you are giving up. Then ask in writing what the builder will do for cash instead. Page 16.

The five day right, and its condition

Walking the house can spend it.

If your new home sits in a common interest community with an HOA, you may cancel in writing until midnight of the fifth calendar day after signing,34 with no penalty and a prompt full refund.35

The catch almost nobody reads: the right is conditioned on not having personally inspected the unit.36 Fly in, walk the home, sign that afternoon, and you may have signed it away.

Do the taxes compare to home?

Not line for line. Different starting number.

Nevada does not tax market value. It taxes 35 percent of taxable value,37 built from replacement cost, minus wear and tear, plus the land.38 If your state taxes market value, do not compare percentages.

Your main home is capped at 3 percent a year,39 everything else at up to 8 percent.40 Only one property per person statewide counts as a primary residence.41 A home in your old state is fine. A second Nevada one is not.

A brand new home gets no cap in year one.42 Page 21 has the whole picture.

Your cap does not move Path B  ·  Moving up

I have had the 3 percent cap for years. Does it come with me?

No. A new house has nothing to cap against.

A brand new home gets no cap at all in its first fiscal year, and the cap starts the year after.42 The law only caps property that had a separate assessed value the year before, and it excludes the value added by new improvements.61 A house that did not exist last year has nothing to compare to.

Then there is the part people lose by mail.

Recording any ownership document knocks the cap off. You claim it back.62 The Assessor mails a postcard after July 1 to properties whose ownership changed.63 Sign it and send it back.64 It looks like junk mail. It is worth real money every year.

Only one property per person statewide can carry the 3 percent cap.4139 Keep the old house as a rental and it moves to the higher cap.40 Claiming the primary residence cap falsely carries a penalty of three times the tax deficiency, plus the tax and any other penalty.65 Pick one house and be right about it.

One document, page 3, two lenders, same day.

The bill outside your mortgage The shared core

So where did the money go?

Into the ground, before your house was there.

Las Vegas builds new neighborhoods on empty desert. Somebody has to pay for the streets, the sidewalks, the curbs, the streetlights, and the sewer and water lines that go in before the first house does.

Often that somebody is you.

A Special Improvement District, usually shortened to SID, is a charge on the properties inside the district. It pays for exactly that kind of public work.152 In Henderson the same thing is called a Local Improvement District, or LID. Different letter. Same idea.

Here is the part that surprises people. The district spreads that cost over ten to thirty years at a low interest rate.153 You do not clear it at closing. You pay a piece of it, twice a year, for decades.

There is no cap at all.

Almost everybody compares premiums. Almost nobody compares lots.
