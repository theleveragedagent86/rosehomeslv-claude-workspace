# Builder Universe, Clark County New Construction

**Prepared:** 2026-07-25
**Prepared by:** Internal Builder and Community Archivist
**Purpose:** Normalize the internal picture of the active Clark County new construction builder universe and hand the external researchers a ranked verification worklist.

---

## READ THIS FIRST: what this document is and is not

**This document contains ZERO externally verified facts. By design.**

Every number below was compiled from files on disk that were written around **May 1 and May 2, 2026**, with one file updated **May 24, 2026**. As of today, 2026-07-25, that makes the newest internal number roughly **two months old** and most of them roughly **three months old**.

None of it is citable. The citation format for this project is `value [source: <full URL> | verified: 2026-07-25]`, and nothing here qualifies, because nothing here was checked against a live source today. Instead every internal figure carries an internal provenance tag:

> `[internal: <file> | compiled: <date> | UNVERIFIED]`

**Rule for downstream agents:** treat every number in this file as a **verification lead**, never as a fact. Do not copy a price, a community count, an HOA figure, a SID amount, a school rating, or an incentive from this document into reader-facing prose. Go get it live, cite it properly, or write `NOT FOUND`.

**Why this matters more than usual here:** the internal files themselves record that the Las Vegas new construction market was softening. One report notes about 25 percent of new homes took price cuts in Q4 2025. Several communities have already changed since compile. One community lost an entire floor plan between May 1 and May 3, 2026 (the Madden at Rainbow Crossing Luxury). Another had its builder misattributed for weeks. Stale data here becomes a wrong number in a buyer's hands.

**Geography:** Clark County, Nevada only. Pahrump, Mesquite, and Boulder City rows were stripped. See Section 4.

## 3. Verification worklist

Ordered by how much each item moves a real buyer's decision or monthly payment. Item 1 matters most.

### Tier 1: changes what a buyer pays every month

1. **SID and LID, per named community: presence, current annual dollar amount, remaining balance, payoff year, and prepayment terms.** This is the largest hidden carrying cost in Clark County new construction and the internal data is wildly inconsistent. Inside Skye Canyon alone the internal figures run from about $137.66 per year (Arroyo townhomes) to $2,400 to $4,800 per year (single family), with a third file estimating $1,000 to $2,500. Lake Las Vegas is put at roughly $1,020 per year based on one Del Webb benchmark, with every community specific figure marked NOT FOUND. Multiple reports say Cadence and Tuscany Village have none, while one report says Cadence "has its own LID structure." Get parcel level numbers or write NOT FOUND.

2. **HOA, per named community: current monthly master fee, current monthly sub-association fee, combined total, what each tier covers, and how many tiers exist.** Internal anchors, all unverified: Skye Canyon master $83, Cadence master $75, Lake Las Vegas master about $153, Mountain's Edge master $60 to $70, Tuscany Village $175, Arroyo sub $161.67, The Bluffs sub $244, Southwind $69, Delamar $150, Watercolor $188, Arcadia $129 to $139. Nine of the internal community reports mark the HOA figure NOT FOUND outright.

3. **Current base prices and current quick move-in prices for every community that will appear in the guide.** Every price in this document is from early May 2026 in a market the internal files describe as softening, with about 25 percent of new homes taking price cuts in Q4 2025 averaging about 5 percent. Nothing here survives as a citable price.

4. **Current builder incentives.** **Every single incentive captured internally has an expiration date that has already passed.** The internal record includes offers expiring 01/31/2026, 03/17/2026, 03/31/2026, 04/15/2026, 04/30/2026, and a Toll Brothers promotion that ran May 2 to 17, 2026. Rate buydowns quoted internally include 3.375, 3.75, 3.875, 3.99, 4.75, and 4.99 percent. **Do not reuse any of these figures.** Either re-pull live or drop the incentive section to a general explanation of how builder incentives work.

5. **Current mortgage rates and 2026 loan limits.** Internal reference cites roughly 5.9 to 6.15 percent, an FHA limit of $541,287, and a conforming limit of $832,750, all as of early 2026. Re-verify all three.

### Tier 2: changes which community a buyer chooses

6. **Selling status for every community.** Roughly 20 internal rows are marked "Coming Soon" as of May 1, 2026. By July 2026 some are open, some slipped, some may be shelved. Separately, confirm the Sold Out and Final Opportunity rows: Peace Landing (Beazer), Havenwood (Taylor Morrison), Arcadia (Lennar), Spectrum Bay (D.R. Horton), Rosa (Beazer), Marbella at Lake Las Vegas (Taylor Morrison, final phase), Emerson Estates (Beazer, final lots), and both StoryBook "Final Opportunity" rows.

7. **Communities per builder, re-counted from each builder's own website.** Every count in Section 1 is a May 2026 row count from a third party aggregator, and four of the 24 files have header counts that do not match their own tables.

8. **School assignments and current ratings for every named community.** Internal reports disagree with each other about Skye Canyon assignments: one assigns Arbor View High, another assigns Shadow Ridge High, for neighborhoods on the same street. Several ratings are marked NOT FOUND. CCSD rezones. Verify at the district zone lookup, not from a listing site.

9. **Whether Centennial Hills has any active new construction at all.** Zero internal coverage. This is a from scratch research task.

10. **Whether Meritage Homes is active in Clark County.** One internal file says zero active communities and that the local sales office shows as closed. Another internal report names Meritage as one of six active Mountain's Edge builders. Settle it.

11. **Skye Summit lead builder and sales timeline.** Internal reference says KB Home is first builder with sales in 2027. The Century file says Century bought 17.6 acres for about $22.9M and is breaking ground on a 77 lot tract in 2026. Both cannot be the headline.

12. **Verona at Lake Las Vegas builder attribution.** One internal report lists "Verona at Lake Las Vegas by Taylor Morrison" from $490,990 and, separately on the same page, "Verona by Beazer Homes" from the high $400,000s. The Beazer builder list has a Henderson "Verona" marked Coming Soon. The Taylor Morrison builder list has no Verona at all.

13. **Windsor Heights builder attribution.** Listed as an active Las Vegas community by **both** Richmond American and Taylor Morrison.

14. **Whether Del Webb, Centex, American West, DiVosta, and Shea Homes belong on the roster.** Del Webb in particular is described internally as an active Lake Las Vegas and North Las Vegas builder with roughly 1,400 planned homes at Lake Las Vegas alone, and it has no builder file.

15. **Product type per community.** The builder lists have no product column. Single family, townhome, condo, cluster home, and 55 plus age restricted have to be recovered community by community. This matters because a townhome buyer and a single family buyer need different sections of the guide.

### Tier 3: matters for accuracy and credibility, less for the buying decision

16. **Master plan totals and buildout dates** for Cadence, Inspirada, Summerlin, Skye Canyon, Mountain's Edge, Union Village, and Lake Las Vegas. Internal figures conflict for Cadence and Inspirada. See Section 5.

17. **Builder ranking and closings table.** Internal figures (Lennar 1,796, D.R.
