### This Weekend in Las Vegas

**[Wurst Fest](https://bcsr.org/wurst-festival)** · Sat, Sept 26, 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. · Boulder City
Boulder City's Rotary festival turns 30 at Bicentennial Park, with a car show, live music, a kids area, brats, and a [live auction from 4 to 6 p.m.](https://bouldercityreview.com/news/wurst-fest-celebrating-30th-anniversary-131594/) that includes a 1929 Ford Model A pickup.

**[Golden Knights camp practice](https://www.nhl.com/goldenknights/news/golden-knights-announce-2026-training-camp-roster-schedule)** · Fri, Sept 25, 11:30 a.m. · Summerlin
Practices at City National Arena are free and open to the public, and the team hosts the Sharks at T-Mobile Arena Saturday at 7 p.m.

**[Las Vegas Greek Food Festival](https://lasvegasgreekfestival.com/)** · Fri, Sept 25 to Sun, Sept 27 · Southwest Las Vegas
Greek food, pastries, music, and dancing at St. John the Baptist Greek Orthodox Church on El Camino Road, open Friday 3 to 11 p.m., Saturday noon to 11 p.m., and Sunday noon to 10 p.m.

**[Free Nevada State Parks day](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/09/21/nevada-state-parks-offer-free-entry-national-public-lands-day/)** · Sat, Sept 26, all day · Valley-wide
Entry, camping, and boating are free at every Nevada state park for National Public Lands Day, and Spring Mountain Ranch hosts a volunteer cleanup from 9 to 10 a.m.

**[One Piece Cafe, last chance](https://neon.reviewjournal.com/dining-out/restaurant-inspired-by-hit-anime-franchise-to-close-in-chinatown-vegas-3342398/)** · Open through Fri, Oct 2 · Chinatown
The only official One Piece restaurant in the U.S. closes its 5600 Spring Mountain Road spot after October 2 and becomes a traveling pop-up.
