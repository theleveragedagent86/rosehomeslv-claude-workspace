### Rates Hit 6.95 Percent. That Is About $173 More a Month Here.

[Freddie Mac says the 30-year fixed rate averaged 6.95 percent](https://freddiemac.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/mortgage-rates-average-695) last week. A year ago it was 6.26 percent. It was the fourth straight weekly jump.

So I ran the math on a real local home. The [Southern Nevada median single-family price was $475,000 in August](https://news3lv.com/news/local/las-vegas-home-prices-august-2026-sales-real-estate-housing-mortgage-interest-rates-condos-townhomes-southern-nevada). With 20 percent down, that is a $380,000 loan. At today's rate the payment is about $2,515 a month in principal and interest. At last year's rate it was about $2,342. That is roughly $173 more a month on the same house. (These are my own estimates, not published figures.)

Here is the good news for buyers. The [typical Las Vegas home sold for 98.0 percent of its list price](https://www.redfin.com/city/10201/NV/Las-Vegas/housing-market) in August, so there is still room to negotiate.

**If you're selling:** Buyers are watching every dollar of that payment, so price it right from day one.
**If you're buying:** Ask about seller credits to buy down your rate, because that $173 can shrink.
