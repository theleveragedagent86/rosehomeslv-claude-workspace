⚡ [E]VERYTHING ELSE

- **Slower school zones:** In the first 30 days of school, Clark County crews [replaced 1,601 traffic signs and repainted or replaced 108 crosswalks](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/09/16/clark-county-ccsd-boost-school-zone-safety-following-student-pedestrian-crashes/). School-zone flashers now run 40 minutes before and after school, so plan on slower drives past campuses valley-wide.
- **Data center tax breaks:** Gov. Joe Lombardo [signed an executive order](https://www.reviewjournal.com/news/politics-and-government/nevada/lombardo-sets-new-conditions-for-nevada-data-center-tax-breaks-3887842/) making data center builders pay the full local school support sales tax to get a tax break. The Clark County Education Association says these breaks have cost K-12 schools $357 million since 2015.
- **Ice Palace comeback:** Clark County commissioners [set aside $10 million for Insomniac](https://www.reviewjournal.com/news/politics-and-government/clark-county/10m-earmarked-as-edc-owner-eyes-event-center-redevelopment-project-3887595/), the company behind EDC, to redevelop the old Sahara Event Center at 800 Liberace Ave. Neighbors in Las Vegas Country Club Estates, about 150 feet away, asked commissioners to table it.
- **Goodbye, Girl Scout house:** The [Downtown Henderson Girl Scout house](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/09/17/historic-henderson-girl-scout-house-closing-demolition/) at Pacific and Van Wagenen, open since 1943, closed Sept. 17 ahead of demolition for the city's redevelopment plans. More than 100 girls in 13 troops used it, and it will not be rebuilt.
- **Reavo out:** Former Golden Knights enforcer Ryan Reaves [retired after 16 NHL seasons](https://www.fox5vegas.com/2026/09/16/reavo-out-former-golden-knights-fan-favorite-ryan-reaves-retires-after-16-nhl-seasons/), finishing with 1,137 penalty minutes in 962 games. The good news for fans: GM Kelly McCrimmon says Reaves still makes Las Vegas his home.
