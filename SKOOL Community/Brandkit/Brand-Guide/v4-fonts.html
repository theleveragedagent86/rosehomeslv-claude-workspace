Font pairings, all on the Vector palette
Color is held constant so you are only judging type. Every card uses the same headline, the same 13px paragraph, and the same two thumbnails, because display faces all look good big and the decision actually gets made at caption size and at 320px.

Space Grotesk, Fraunces and Bodoni Moda are out at your call, so the surviving faces are Poppins, Inter, Archivo, Inter Tight, Barlow and Raleway. That removes every serif, which means this is now a decision between grotesks and the hierarchy has to come from weight, size and width instead of from a contrast of face.

Raleway appears in two different roles here. As a display face it is P12. As subtext, meaning the eyebrow, the subhead and the small label but never a paragraph, it is P02, P05, P08 and P09. The second role is the one that works, and the reason is at the bottom of the page.

Tracking is not constant. Each face carries the value that is actually correct for it, and the spec block on every card lists it.

Combinations to skip, and why

These come up naturally from the same six faces, so they are worth ruling out on the record rather than rediscovering later. None of them are broken. They just cost you either legibility or hierarchy.

Raleway as running body

This is the distinction that matters for your question. Raleway has a low x-height and tight apertures. At 15px and up on a single short line it looks refined, which is why it works as subtext in P02, P05, P08 and P09. At 13px across a full paragraph it goes mushy, and 13px across a full paragraph is what a Skool post actually is. Read the block below and then read the one under P08.

Module 03 walks you through the exact follow-up sequence, then hands you the automation so it runs without you. Set it once and stop thinking about it. An inline link in here is where it gets worst.

Raleway head + Raleway subtext

Two lines of the same low x-height face stacked on top of each other. The subhead stops reading as a separate level and the whole block flattens. If Raleway is your display face, the subtext has to be something else.

Poppins + Poppins

Poppins is geometric and wide. It is built for headlines. At 13px in a paragraph the round shapes and low x-height slow reading down noticeably, and long copy gets tiring.

Inter Tight + Inter, at similar sizes

Included above as P11 because it is a legitimate cost-saving choice, but be aware: if the headline is not much larger and much heavier, the two are indistinguishable and the page reads flat. Now that every serif is gone, this failure mode applies to Archivo + Barlow and to Barlow + Barlow as well. Buy your hierarchy with weight and size.
