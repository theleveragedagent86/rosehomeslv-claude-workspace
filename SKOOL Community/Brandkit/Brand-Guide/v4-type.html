Type experiments: same palette, six display faces
Nothing here is adopted. Every section below is byte-identical markup on the Ultramarine palette. The only variable is the display face and its tracking. Body copy stays Inter in all six, because finding 4 of the research is that these brands run exactly two fonts and the body font is never the interesting one. Judge the headline, the stat numbers, the card titles and the wordmark line at the bottom of each section. Palette options are on v4-preview.html. Spec: V4-PALETTES.md.
Poppins 800 geometric sans · the current system
What you already run, and what the carousels are built in. Geometric, friendly, round. Reads modern and approachable. The risk is that it is everywhere in coaching and course design, so it does not signal pedigree on its own.

Archivo 800 neo-grotesque · closest to the pedigree set
Closest to what Serhant and Compass actually run. Tighter, squarer, more editorial than Poppins. Same weight of voice, more authority, less warmth. If you want to look like the researched brands without going serif, this is the one.

Bodoni Moda 700 high-contrast serif · the luxury convention
Camp A from the research: the Sotheby's and Elliman convention. Very high thick and thin contrast. Expensive at large size, fragile at small size. Tracking is dialed back to -0.01em because a didone breaks if you tighten it the way you tighten Poppins.
