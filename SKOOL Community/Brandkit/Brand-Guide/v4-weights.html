Barlow 600 to 900, on P08
Everything else is held at P08: Raleway 600 subtext, Inter 400/500 body, Vector palette. Only the headline weight moves.

Tracking is not constant, on purpose. Heavier weights need more negative tracking, because the letterforms get wider and the space between them looks proportionally larger. Locking tracking across all four would make 900 look loose and 600 look cramped, and you would be judging the tracking instead of the weight. The values used here are the ones you would actually ship: 600 at -0.020em, 700 at -0.024em, 800 at -0.028em, 900 at -0.032em.

Judge the thumbnails first. The headline differences are obvious at 41px and mostly cosmetic. The 320px thumbnail is where weight actually changes whether someone stops scrolling.

All four, stacked

Adjacent comparison at headline size. Look at the counters, the enclosed white space inside a, e, o and g. That is what closes up as weight increases, and it is the thing that decides whether a weight survives at small sizes.
