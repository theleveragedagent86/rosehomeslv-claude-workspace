#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

🗺️ **See the Whole Ladder Before You Climb It** by walking Level 1 through Level 10 in under three minutes, so you always know where you are and what is coming next.

🧠 **Understand the One Thing You're Actually Building** by meeting the Business Brain, the single folder every level of this course adds a layer to, so nothing you do here is a throwaway exercise.

🧰 **Know Exactly What You Need Before Level 1** by getting the short list of accounts and tools up front, so you are not stopping mid-setup to sign up for something.

#### In this video, Ryan covers:

Most agents who try to use AI quit in the first week, and it is almost never because the technology was too hard. It is because they opened a chat box, got a generic answer that sounded nothing like them, and concluded it does not work for real estate. That is a setup problem, not an AI problem, and this course exists to fix it in order.

The system is **the Business Brain**, one folder on your computer that knows your farm areas, your price points, your voice, your past clients and your brokerage rules. Every level of this course adds a layer to that same folder. Level 1 builds it. Level 4 gives it memory. Level 5 teaches it to launch a listing. By Level 10 it is running the administrative half of your business.

The practical steps here are short: get the map of the ladder, understand what carries through it, collect the two or three accounts you will need before Level 1, and learn the one troubleshooting loop that gets you unstuck without waiting on anybody.

#### Who this is for:

- **Agents who have tried AI and been disappointed** who got generic, obviously robotic output and assumed the tool was the problem rather than the setup.

- **Agents who have not started at all** who keep hearing they are falling behind and want one clear path instead of forty YouTube videos.

## Production notes (not for Skool)

**Keep this under five minutes.** It is a map, not a lesson. The temptation is to teach something here. Do not.

**No software touched on camera.** Not one terminal window. This lesson exists so that a nervous agent can watch it on their phone in the car and feel like the course is possible.

**The troubleshooting loop goes here, before anything can break.** This is the highest-value 40 seconds in the level:

> "Something is going to not work. It happens to me weekly. Screenshot it, paste the screenshot into Claude, tell it what you were trying to do, ask for step by step. That fixes it about eight times out of ten. The other two, post it in the community and tag me."

**Say the ladder out loud with the payoff, not the topic.** Not "Level 2 is landing pages." Instead: "Level 2, you'll have a live page on your own domain that you can text to a seller."

**The close names Level 1 and the honest cost:**

> "Level 1 is the long one. It's about forty-five minutes and at the end of it you will not have made a dollar. What you'll have is the foundation that makes every other level work. Set aside an hour, do it once, never do it again."

**No description-block launch date on this one.** Level 0 stays evergreen.
