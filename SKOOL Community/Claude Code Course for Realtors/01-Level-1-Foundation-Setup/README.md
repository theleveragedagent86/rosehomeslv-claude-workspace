**The BROKER file, in 200 words or less:**

```
## BUSINESS
Name, license #, brokerage, years in, team or solo.

## RANGE
Farm areas. Price bands. Property types. Who you actually serve.

## OUTPUT
Voice rules. 6th grade reading level. Short sentences. No jargon.
No em-dashes. Soft CTAs. Always end emails with my signature block.

## KEEP OUT
Never invent a price, square footage, school rating, HOA amount or
commission number. Never write anything that touches a protected class.
Mark anything you don't know as NOT FOUND.

## ESCALATION
Ask me before: sending anything to a client, posting publicly,
committing to a date, quoting a number.

## RESULTS
This quarter I'm trying to [goal]. Bias your suggestions toward that.
```

## Production notes (not for Skool)

**Be honest at minute one that this level makes them nothing.** Then immediately promise the three wins. That trade is what keeps them watching.

> "I'm gonna be straight with you. At the end of this level you will not have made a dollar and you will not have a listing description. What you'll have is the reason every other level in this course works. And I'm gonna give you three real wins along the way so you're not sitting here on faith."

**Delegate the install on camera.** Do not narrate fourteen clicks. Open the AI, paste the setup page, say "walk me through installing this on my machine, configure whatever needs configuring, and tell me anything else I need to know." This is the move that kills the "my screen looks different" support load forever.

**De-jargon the terminal with a disclaimer.**

> "The terminal. I know. It looks like the Matrix and it makes you feel like you're about to break something. All it is, is a place where you type instructions instead of clicking buttons. That's it. I've never taken a coding class in my life."

**The 5-8 folders for a real estate business.** Show these, then tell them to have the AI generate their own version:

```
Listings/        Buyers/        Transactions/     Marketing/
Sphere/          Market-Data/   Brokerage/
```

Then the prompt, read out loud: *"Based on everything you know about how a real estate business runs, organize my work into five to eight thematic folders. If you get past eight, I'm overcomplicating it, so simplify."*

**Cap the BROKER file at 200 words and say why.** A long file is a file the AI half-reads. Show the cap on screen.

**The three wins, spaced roughly 8 minutes apart:**
1. Minute 14: desktop cleanup by prompt. Fast, visual, zero risk.
2. Minute 31: a folder structure built entirely by prompt.
3. Minute 37: a real client email drafted in their voice, using the BROKER file. This is the one that lands, because it is the first time they hear themselves come out of the machine.

**Leave a failure in.** If a connector does not authenticate on the first try, keep it. If nothing breaks naturally, break one deliberately: delete a working connector and reconnect it live, so they see the recovery path.

**Homework at the close, small enough to actually do:**

> "Before you watch Level 2, build the folder and write the file. That's it. Twenty minutes. Do not try to make it perfect, you'll be editing it for the next six months anyway."

**Close on the Level 2 question, not the Level 2 topic:**

> "So you've got a brain now. Which brings up the obvious problem: how do you get it to build something a client can actually see? That's Level 2, and by the end of it you're gonna have a live page on your own domain."
