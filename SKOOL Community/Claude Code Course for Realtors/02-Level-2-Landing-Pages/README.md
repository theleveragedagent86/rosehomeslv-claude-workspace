#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

📄 **Own Five Pages Instead of One Website** by building single-purpose landing pages for a listing, a buyer guide, a neighborhood, an open house RSVP and a home valuation, so every campaign has a destination that is not your brokerage's template site.

🔍 **Find What Already Converts Before You Design Anything** by having Claude research the pages actually winning in your market and build its own scoring matrix, so your page is based on evidence instead of taste.

⚡ **Build Three Versions Simultaneously and Throw Two Away** by running parallel builds with different strategies, so you are choosing between real options instead of accepting the first thing that appeared.

🧐 **Make the AI Grade Its Own Work** using a critic pass in a fresh session that returns critical, high, medium and low issues, so problems get caught before a client sees them.

#### In this video, Ryan covers:

Most agents have one website, it came from their brokerage, it looks like every other agent's website, and it converts nothing because it is trying to do nine jobs at once. Meanwhile the thing that actually converts, a single page built for one listing or one offer with one call to action, is the thing they have been told costs two thousand dollars and takes three weeks.

The system is **the Three-Step Page System**: competitive intelligence first, then a written design brief, then a parallel build. You find what is already working in your market, you lock the findings into a `best-principles.md` file so the AI cannot drift, and then you build three versions at once and keep the best one.

The practical steps are researching converting pages in your market, generating a scoring matrix, writing the design brief, running three parallel builds, running a critic pass in a fresh session, adding real listing data, wiring lead capture to your inbox and CRM, deploying, and connecting a custom domain.

#### Who this is for:

- **Agents whose only web presence is a brokerage template** who need a destination for their ads, their QR codes and their door hangers that does not look like everyone else's.

- **Agents already paying a web person or a monthly page builder** who want to see what thirty minutes and no monthly fee actually produces before the next invoice.

## Production notes (not for Skool)

**This is the first visible win in the course and it needs to land hard.** The moment that sells the whole thing is buying a domain on camera and loading the page on a phone.

**The five pages, named early so they know the reuse:** listing page, buyer guide, neighborhood/relocation page, open house RSVP, home valuation. Build one on camera, hand them the other four as prompts.

**Do the parallel build for real.** Three at once, different strategies, evaluate out loud, delete two. This is the single most differentiating move available and it teaches parallel agents through use rather than explanation. Say why:

> "I'm not gonna show you the one that worked. I'm gonna build three and throw two away in front of you, because picking is the actual skill here. The AI can build. It can't have taste for you."

**Read the research prompt out loud, word for word,** then say "I'll put it down below."

**The critic pass has to be a fresh session.** Say why: a session that just built something will defend it.

**Leave the DNS wait in the cut.** It is the most common place a student panics.

> "This is gonna take anywhere from ten minutes to a couple hours to propagate. That's normal, you didn't break it, go get lunch."

**Compliance touch, briefly, without derailing:** any page with listing data needs the brokerage attribution and fair housing logo their state and MLS require. Flag it in one sentence and point forward to Level 9.

**Close on the Level 3 question:**

> "You just did all that by hand. Which is fine once. But you're gonna build a page for every listing you take for the rest of your career. So how do you make that a one-word command? Level 3."
