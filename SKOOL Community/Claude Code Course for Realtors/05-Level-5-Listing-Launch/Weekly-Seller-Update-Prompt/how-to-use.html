How to Use the Weekly Seller Update Builder

One paste. One interview. A seller update that runs itself every week.

This prompt builds a system that turns your listing's views, showings, feedback and marketing into a branded seller report and a ready-to-send email. It interviews you first, so every number is real and every word sounds like you. You set it up once. After that, it's one command a week.

What you'll need

Claude Code + your Business BrainOpen it inside the folder you built in Level 1.
Your listing basicsAddress, MLS number, list price and the seller's email.
Your logins open in ChromeZillow, Homes.com, your CRM, Meta Ads and your showing service.
About 20 minutesFor the first run only. Every week after that is about 5.

The five steps

  1. Open Claude Code in your Business Brain.

    Same folder as always. It reads your CLAUDE.md, voice and brand before it asks you anything.

  2. Switch to Plan Mode.

    Press Shift+Tab until the bottom of the screen says "plan mode." Nothing gets built until you say so.

  3. Paste the whole prompt file and hit Enter.

    Open "weekly-seller-update-plan-prompt.md," copy everything, and paste it in. Don't add anything else yet.

  4. Answer the interview.

    It asks in 10 short rounds: you, the listing, the seller, where your views live, showings, marketing, nearby listings and how you deliver. Don't know one? Say so. It marks it "NOT FOUND" and never guesses.

  5. Read the plan, approve it, and let it build.

    You'll see every file it will make and every step of the weekly run. Approve it and Claude builds the whole system.

/weekly-update 123 Main St
Every week after that. It asks a few quick questions, pulls the numbers, builds the report and drafts the email. You read it and hit send.

What the system gives you back

Five pieces, every week, fully yours

A
The Seller ReportA branded page with a short summary, total online views by site, a plain-talk showings recap and your marketing numbers.
B
A CRM-Ready CopyThe same report as one file you paste into your landing page. Same link for the seller every week.
C
The Seller Email DraftSummary, report link and nearby listings to watch. Drafted, never sent. You send it.
D
Running RecordsA view history, one showing log and one feedback file, so next week starts where this one ended.
E
Your /weekly-update CommandA skill saved in your Business Brain. Works on every listing you take from now on.

What the interview asks you

01
You + brandName, headshot, colors, sign-off
02
The listingAddress, MLS #, price, hero photo
03
The sellerName they go by, email, what stays private
04
Online viewsEach site's link and where its views live
05
ShowingsShowing service, feedback sources
06
MarketingMailers, Meta ads, agent emails
07
Competition3 to 5 nearby listings or solds
08
This weekTone, the one takeaway, what's next
09
DeliveryReport link, subject line, draft or send
10
AutopilotSkill, reminders, what happens at pending

Rounds 1 to 7 and 9 to 10 are one-time setup. Round 8, plus new feedback and mailer rounds, is all it asks you each week.

Three tips to get the best output

Log in before you startOpen every site in Chrome first. It pauses for a login, but it never types your passwords.
Paste your texts inFeedback from texts and calls is the best stuff. Paste it in raw. It keeps the agent's actual words.
Pick your tone on purposeMomentum, subtle repositioning or hold steady. It never tells your seller in writing to cut the price.
Why it works: it was built backward from a real listing, not a template. Ryan's 29 Amber Rock St ran five weekly updates through 30 days on market, 21 showings and 5,500+ online views. Every snag from those weeks is built in as a question: the seller who goes by a different name, feedback split across two files, a view count that dropped because a site only counts the last 30 days, and offer amounts that belong in a phone call, not a report.
