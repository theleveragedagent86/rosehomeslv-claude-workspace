#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

⏰ **Run Your Skills on a Schedule Instead of on Demand** by moving a Level 3 skill into a routine that fires without you, so the weekly work happens whether or not you remembered it.

☕ **Build a Monday Morning Brief** pulling your week's showings, your active transactions and anything that went quiet, so the week starts with a document instead of a scroll.

🌐 **Hand It the Browser** for the tasks that need a live site rather than a file, so the handful of workflows Claude Code cannot reach stop being manual.

🗓️ **Build the Three Routines Worth Having First** so you start with the ones that pay off weekly instead of the ones that are fun to build.

🛑 **Know What Not to Schedule** because an unattended routine that touches a client is a different risk than one that writes you a summary.

#### In this video, Ryan covers:

Everything in this course runs on Claude Code, and that is on purpose. But there are two jobs it is not the right tool for: work that should happen on a schedule without you starting it, and work that needs a live browser rather than files on your computer. That is where Cowork earns its place, and it is the only place it does.

The system is **scheduled routines**, taking a skill you already built in Level 3 and giving it a time instead of a trigger. The Monday morning brief, the transaction check, the quiet-listing alert.

The practical steps are connecting Cowork, converting an existing skill into a routine, building the Monday brief, running one browser-based task, and applying the Level 9 permission tiers to anything that runs unattended.

#### Who this is for:

- **Agents who finished Level 3** and have skills sitting there that should really be running on their own.

- **Agents who keep forgetting the weekly stuff** and want the follow-up to happen without depending on their memory.

## Production notes (not for Skool)

**Be honest about the split, in Ryan's own words.** This is the one place in the course where a second tool appears, and the credibility comes from not overselling it:

> "I'll be straight with you about how I actually work. Ninety percent of what I do is Claude Code. I use Cowork for two things: when I want something to run on a schedule without me, and when it needs to actually go use a website. That's it. That's the whole reason this lesson exists."

**The three routines worth building first:**
1. Monday morning brief: the week's showings, active transactions, anything that went quiet.
2. Transaction deadline check: what is due in the next seven days across every open deal.
3. Sphere touch reminder: who from the past client roster has not been contacted in ninety days.

**Apply Level 9 tiers explicitly.** An unattended routine drafting a summary for Ryan is tier one. An unattended routine emailing a client is tier three and should not run unattended at all. Say it directly.

> "Anything that runs while you're asleep should be reading and writing to you. Not sending. Ever."
