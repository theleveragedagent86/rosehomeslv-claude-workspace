#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

📣 **Generate the Full Promo Pack on One Command** covering the social posts, the story frames, the flyer and the neighbor invite, so the marketing is done days before the open house instead of the night before.

🚪 **Write Door Knock Invites That Get a Conversation** using your farm data, so the invite sounds like a neighbor and not a mailer.

🎯 **Run Geo-Targeted Ads Without a Big Budget** by tightening the radius and the audience to the people who could plausibly show up.

📞 **Circle Prospect Around the Listing** with a call and text framework built from the actual property and the actual street.

✍️ **Capture Sign-Ins That Are Worth Following Up** so you leave with something better than nine illegible names.

⚡ **Run Same-Day Outbound Before the Leads Go Cold** with follow-up drafted from what each visitor actually said, sent the same afternoon.

#### In this video, Ryan covers:

Most agents treat an open house as a seller obligation, run it for three hours, collect a handful of sign-ins and never touch them again. The listing gets marketed, the opportunity does not. The difference between an open house that produces a deal and one that produces a Saturday is almost entirely what happens in the seventy-two hours around it, not the three hours inside it.

The system is **the Open House OS**, a promo stage before, a capture stage during, and an outbound stage after, all generated from the listing folder you built in Level 5.

The practical steps are running the promo pack, producing door knock invites and circle prospecting scripts from your farm data, setting up geo-targeted ads, structuring the sign-in, and firing same-day outbound written from each visitor's actual situation.

#### Who this is for:

- **Agents holding open houses regularly** who know the follow-up is where the money is and rarely get to it before Monday.

- **Newer agents using open houses for lead generation** who need the whole system rather than a sign-in sheet.
