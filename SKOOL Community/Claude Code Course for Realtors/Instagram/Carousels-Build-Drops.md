# Carousel Build-Drops: 10 Ready Posts

Format reverse-engineered from the Kristi Jencks teardown (9 Sep 2026). The only carousel
shape that earns comments on an agent-facing account:

1. Slide 1 is a confession or a contradiction, first person, about a thing you already built.
2. 7 to 8 slides. Her 7-8 band medians 10 comments vs 4 for the 5-6 band.
3. Slides 2-6 are the build, in order, with one screenshot or one artifact per slide.
4. Slide 7 is the result with a number.
5. Slide 8 is the keyword. One word. Same word in the last line of the caption.
6. Workflow topics convert. Mindset topics with the same treatment do not.

Rule for every post below: the build has to exist before it posts. The comment is a delivery
promise, and the DM has to contain the actual file.

All sourced from real course levels, so nothing here needs to be invented.

## Posting notes

- One keyword per post, never two. Two splits the DM list and kills the delivery promise.
- Do not repeat a keyword within 30 days. Kristi reuses WINNING across posts and the comments
  drop on the second one.
- Reply to every keyword comment. That is what doubles the public comment count, and the
  doubled number is what makes the next post look worth engaging with.
- Reels bring strangers, carousels convert them. Run both. The carousel is where the list gets
  built.
- Her two highest-like posts of 2026 were family photos with no ask (614 and 546 likes vs
  20-60 on the builds). Personal posts buy affection, build posts buy DMs. Do not confuse the
  two or try to combine them.
