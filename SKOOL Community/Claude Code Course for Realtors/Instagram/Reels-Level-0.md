# Instagram Reels: Level 0, The Context Block

**12 reels.** Source: [YouTube/YT-Level-0-Context-Block.md](../YouTube/YT-Level-0-Context-Block.md) and [00-Level-0-Start-Here/SCRIPT.md](../00-Level-0-Start-Here/SCRIPT.md).

Each one is 130 to 160 spoken words, which lands between 55 and 65 seconds at a normal talking pace. All twelve stand alone. Nothing here requires you to have seen anything else, which is the point of Level 0 on social: it is the no-install entry point, so a stranger can act on it the same day.

## Format, and what is different from the older shorts

The five archetypes are the house set carried over from the `Scripts/Lesson-N-Instagram-Shorts.md` files: **Teaser, Quick Win, Before/After, Hot Take, Math.** Reels 6 through 12 are extensions, because twelve distinct angles is more volume than five and Level 0 has the material for it.

Two fields are new and were added deliberately:

- **Hook text.** The words burned into the first frame. Reels are decided in the first second and a half, before you have finished your first sentence. The older shorts had no on-screen hook and were leaning entirely on the audio.
- **Bio link.** Which destination that reel assumes. Level 0 reels mostly point at the free YouTube video, because it is the lowest-friction win in the whole funnel: no install, no account, usable that afternoon. A few point straight at the community. Set the bio link to match whatever you post that day.

**Caption style follows `Scripts/Instagram-Reel-Captions.md`, not `Lesson-4-Instagram-Shorts.md`.** The Lesson-4 generation is full of em-dashes, which the workspace rule now forbids. The Reel-Captions generation is the corrected style: no em-dashes, four hashtags, and the standing close, "link in bio. Community's called The Leveraged Agent."

## Rules for these specifically

- **Every number in a Math reel is yours to confirm before you post it.** I wrote the arithmetic so it works, but the inputs are claims about your own workflow, not facts I can verify. Change any of them that are not true for you.
- **Do not state Fair Housing law.** Reel 8 gets near it. It is written as your own practice with "ask your broker" attached, and it has to stay that way.
- **No plan names, no prices, no free-tier claims.** Every script says "whatever AI you already have open" on purpose. Tiers change and an evergreen reel with a stale price in it is a problem forever.
- **No em-dashes.** Commas, periods, or "and."

### REEL 12 · THE SPECIFICITY

**Hook text:** `Not "Las Vegas." Name the streets.`
**Bio link:** Level 0 YouTube video
**Shot:** Talking head. Put your real neighborhood names on screen.

**Script:**
This is the one section everybody rushes, and it is the section all of the quality comes out of. When you write down what you sell, do not write your metro. Do not write "Las Vegas and surrounding areas." That tells it nothing, because that describes forty thousand other agents. Name three to five actual neighborhoods, the ones you really work. Then your real price band, the low end and the high end. Then property types. Then who you usually work with, first time buyers, move up, relocation, investors. Mine says Skye Canyon, Centennial Hills, Summerlin. That is why what comes back sounds like it was written by somebody who lives here. If you write "surrounding areas," you are going to get a brochure back, and honestly that one is on you, not on the AI.

**Caption:**

Name 3 to 5 real neighborhoods you actually work. Your real price band. Property types. Who you actually sell to.
