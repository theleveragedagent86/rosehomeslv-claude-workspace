## How these differ from the Level 0 set

Level 0's reels sell a **paste block**. These sell a **folder**, which is a bigger claim and a bigger ask, so they run heavier on proof and lighter on "try this right now."

**The two sets are a ladder and they must not blur.** Level 0's reels never mention BROKER, Claude Code, or a folder. These do. If a Level 1 reel would work word for word as a Level 0 reel, it is the wrong reel, because the whole reason someone climbs is that the first thing they got for free ran out.

**The honest hinge, and use it out loud:** a pasted block dies when you close the tab. It also cannot read your sent folder, your listing files, or your transaction docs. That limitation is real, it is the actual reason the folder exists, and saying it plainly converts better than pretending the free version is crippled.

**Higher friction, so expect lower reach and better leads.** Reels 6 through 12 name a specific framework and a specific tool. Fewer people save them. The ones who do are the ones who join.
