## Read this before you shoot: this is not a cut of Level 0

**Level 0 cannot be ported to YouTube.** The Skool version is a course map. It says "what you signed up for," it walks the Level 1 to Level 10 ladder, and its whole payoff is "every level upgrades the same folder." A stranger has not signed up for anything, has no levels, and has no folder. Recording that on YouTube produces ten minutes of a man describing a product the viewer cannot see.

So this video keeps **Level 0's job** and throws away Level 0's structure. Level 0's real job is the diagnosis: *the reason AI writes garbage for you is that you never told it who you are.* That argument is the single most universal thing in the whole course, and it works perfectly on someone who has never heard of you.

**The win is a Context Block**, a reusable block of text about their business that they paste at the top of any AI chat. It needs no install, no Claude Code, no account, and it works in whatever they already have open. It is a real, complete, usable win on its own.

**It is also the honest 80/20 of the Business Brain**, which is why it funnels so cleanly: a paste block dies the moment you close the tab. The folder is what makes it permanent, and the folder is the Level 1 video and the course.

# Do not say

- **Do not name a plan, a price, or a free tier.** This video deliberately says "whatever AI you already have open" instead. Tiers and pricing change, and a stale number in an evergreen video is a support problem forever.
- Do not teach BROKER here. Four sections only. BROKER belongs to the Level 1 video.
- Do not say "Level 0," "the ladder," "what you signed up for," or anything else that assumes they bought something.
- Any specific commission number, MLS rule or statute
- "This replaces your agent" or anything that reads as advice to consumers
- Any client's real name, address or contact information, on screen or out loud
- Anything about other states' license rules. You are licensed in Nevada.
- Do not state Fair Housing law. The "never describe who should live somewhere" line is a guardrail you use, phrased as your own practice. Tell people to ask their broker, not you.
