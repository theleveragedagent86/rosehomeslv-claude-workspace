## PART 4, THE FILE THAT RUNS EVERYTHING, 6:35 to 9:15

[This is the core of the video. Slow down.]

Now the most important file you will make today.

Inside that folder we are going to make one file called CLAUDE.md. Think of it as the onboarding document you would hand a brand new assistant on their first day.

I use a framework for it. I call it BROKER, because I am a real estate guy and I cannot help myself.

[On screen, six lines, one at a time.]

**B, Business.** Who you are. Name, license, brokerage, how many years, solo or team.

**R, Range.** This is the one everybody skips and it is the one that matters most. Your farm areas, your price bands, your property types. Not "Las Vegas." Skye Canyon, Centennial Hills, Summerlin, three fifty to six fifty, single family.

**O, Output.** How you write. Short sentences. Sixth grade reading level. No jargon. Soft calls to action.

**K, Keep out.** Your guardrails. Never invent a price, a square footage, a school rating, an HOA amount or a commission. Never describe who should live somewhere. If it does not know something, it says so.

**E, Escalation.** When it stops and asks you. Before anything goes to a client, gets posted publicly, commits you to a date, or quotes a number.

**R, Results.** What you are working on this quarter, so everything it writes leans that direction.

[Type it live. Fill in B and R with your real information on camera. Do not fill in all six, it is boring to watch.]

Keep the whole thing under two hundred words. A long file is a file it half reads.

[Beat, look at camera.]

That K section is not optional. You are licensed. The AI is not. Everything it writes goes out under your name and your license number, so the guardrails are your job, not its job.

- [ ] Claude Code NOT installed yet, or a clean machine or fresh user account, so the install is real
- [ ] Current plan name and pricing confirmed that morning

# Do not say

- Any dollar figure you have not checked that day
- Any specific commission number, MLS rule or statute
- "This replaces your agent" or anything that reads as advice to consumers
- Any client's real name, address or contact information, on screen or out loud
- Anything about other states' license rules. You are licensed in Nevada.
