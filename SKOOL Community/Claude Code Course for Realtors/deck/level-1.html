The framework

BROKER

  • B

    Business

    Who you are. Name, license, brokerage, years in, solo or team.

  • R

    Range

    Farm areas, price bands, property types. The one people skip and the one that matters most. Not "Las Vegas." Skye Canyon, Centennial Hills, Summerlin, 350 to 650, single family.

  • O

    Output

    How you write. Short sentences, sixth grade reading level, no jargon, no em-dashes, soft calls to action.

  • K

    Keep Out

    Your guardrail block. Never invent a price, square footage, school rating, HOA amount or commission. Never describe who should live somewhere. Unknown means NOT FOUND.

  • E

    Escalation

    When it stops and asks you. Before sending to a client, posting publicly, committing to a date, quoting a number.

  • R

    Results

    What you're working on this quarter, so it biases toward that.

Under two hundred words. A long file is a file it half reads.
