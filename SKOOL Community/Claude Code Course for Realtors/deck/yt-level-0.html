The diagnosis

That's not a you problem.
It's a setup problem.

Almost every agent has opened an AI, asked for a listing description, gotten back something with the word "nestled" in it, and quietly decided this stuff doesn't really work for real estate.

It isn't writing badly. It's writing generically, because generic is all you gave it.

What actually happened

You asked for the average.
You got the average.

What you did

Asked a machine that read the whole internet to write a listing description

And gave it nothing else. No farm, no price band, no voice, no brokerage.

What it did

The only thing it could

Returned the average of every listing description ever posted. Which is why it sounds like a brochure from 2011.

If you feel behind on this, you're not behind. You're where almost everybody is, which is the beginning.

Think about it this way

A brilliant assistant
with no memory.

Day one, a new assistant is useless. Not because they aren't smart. Because they don't know your farm areas, your price points, your brokerage, or that you hate the word "stunning."

You don't fix that with better instructions. You fix it by telling them about your business once, and then never again, because they remember.

AI is that assistant, except every single time you open it, it's day one again.

The Context Block

Four parts

01

Who I am

Name, agent, brokerage, how long, solo or team, the state you're licensed in.

02

What I sell

Real neighborhoods, real price band, property types, who you actually work with. This is the one everybody skips.

03

How I write

Sentence length, reading level, and the words you never use.

04

Never do this

The guardrails. The one I would not skip.

Parts 1 and 2

Not "Las Vegas."
Name the streets.

Before you answer anything, here is my business. WHO I AM [Name], real estate agent, [brokerage], [years] years in, [solo or team], licensed in [state]. WHAT I SELL Areas: [3 to 5 actual neighborhoods, not the metro name] Price range: [low] to [high] Property types: [single family, condo, new construction] Who I usually work with: [first time buyers, move up, relocation]

Vague here means vague output. All of the quality comes out of this section.

Parts 3 and 4

Your voice,
and your guardrails.

HOW I WRITE Short sentences. [Grade] reading level. No jargon. Words I never use: nestled, boasts, stunning, oasis, must see, dream home. I never say "reach out." I say [what you actually say]. Soft calls to action. I invite, I don't push. NEVER DO THIS Never invent a price, square footage, school rating, HOA amount or commission number. Never describe what kind of person should live somewhere. If you don't know something, write NOT FOUND instead of guessing. Confirm you've got it, then wait for my request.

The fix

The block stops being
something you paste.

It becomes a file on your computer that the AI reads by itself, every time, without being asked. That folder is the next video, and it's free too.

Two more sections get added once it's a file. They do nothing while you're pasting, which is why they're not in the block I just gave you.

One last thing, the most useful thing here

Something is going to
not work.

It happens to me weekly.

Screenshot it. Paste the screenshot back in. Tell it what you were trying to do. Ask for step by step.

That fixes it about eight times out of ten, and it's the single habit that separates the people who stick with this from the ones who quit in week one.
