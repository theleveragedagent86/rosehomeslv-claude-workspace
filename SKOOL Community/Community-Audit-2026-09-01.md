## What is actually broken

### 1. The pinned welcome post sends people to three places that do not exist

This is the worst finding in the audit, because it is the first thing every new member reads.

| The post says | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Drop a post in **Welcome & Intros**" | no such category. Categories are Announcements, General discussion, Wins & Results, Ideas & Requests |
| "Post in **Q&A / Help**" | no such category. The Guidelines post also says "use the Q&A category" |
| "Check the **Calendar** for our next weekly Q&A" | **there is no Calendar tab.** Nav is Community, Classroom, Members, Leaderboards, About |
| "complete **Module 0: Start Here**" | exists, but is the curriculum being replaced by the Level 0-10 ladder |

A new member who follows the welcome post fails at step 2, step 3, and step 4. That alone
explains a large share of the zero member posts. It is not a copy problem, the copy is fine,
the destinations were never built.

### 2. The About page sells a weekly live call that is not happening

"Weekly live Q&A calls" is listed as an inclusion. There is no Calendar tab, no scheduled
event, and no call referenced in the feed since April. That is a promise on the sales page
being broken, and it is the kind of thing a member notices and quietly leaves over.

### 3. The flagship module is in Draft

Classroom holds three courses:

- **Module 0: Start Here**, published, 0%
- **Listing Launch Package**, described in its own copy as "the flagship module," **status: Draft.**
  Members cannot see it.
- **Open Houses**, published, "New course"

So the classroom a new member actually sees is Module 0 plus Open Houses. The thing the
community is named for is invisible.

### 4. Every announcement points at a curriculum being replaced

The feed sells Cowork setup, Module 0, Lesson 3, Lesson 5, Lesson 6, a listing-description
zip, and the browser-Claude path. The course being built now is **Claude Code only, Level 0
through 10, with Cowork demoted to bonus drop B1.** Every one of those eight posts is an
argument for the old ladder.

Two of them also name **Sonnet 4.6 and Opus 4.6** as the current models. In an AI community,
stale model names are a direct credibility hit, and they are the detail a skeptical agent
uses to decide the whole thing is out of date.

### 5. Em-dashes are live in the most public copy Ryan owns

The pinned post title is "Welcome to The Leveraged Agent — Start Here." More in the
Guidelines and Open Houses posts. Workspace rule, and this is the highest-visibility place
it is being broken.
