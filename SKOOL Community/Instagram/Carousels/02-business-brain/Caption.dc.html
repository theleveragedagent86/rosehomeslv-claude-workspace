Caption, paste as is

I stopped writing prompts for my real estate business. Claude runs it out of one folder now.

Every prompt you write starts from zero. It does not know your market, your brokerage, your fee, your voice, or the deal you were working on yesterday. So you re-explain all of it, every time, and you call that using AI.

The agents actually pulling ahead built a Business Brain instead. One folder Claude reads before it does anything.

Here is what is in mine.

A CLAUDE.md at the root, written on the BROKER framework. Business, Range, Output, Keep out, Escalation, Results. Six sections that tell Claude who I am, what market I work, what good output looks like, what it must never do, when to stop and ask me, and how I measure it. Written once.

A folder per client and per listing. The executed contract, the dates, the comps, the notes. Claude reads the folder, not my memory.

A skills folder. Every task I have done more than twice is a skill now. Listing description, CMA, escrow emails, weekly seller update. I type one word and it runs.

That is it. No prompt library. No 47 tabs. One folder that gets smarter every week because every deal adds to it.

Prompts are rented. A Business Brain compounds.
