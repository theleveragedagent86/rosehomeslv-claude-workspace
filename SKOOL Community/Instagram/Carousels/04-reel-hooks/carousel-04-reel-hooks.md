# Carousel 04, 20 Reel Hooks For Realtors Who Hate Cameras

The Leveraged Agent. Written 2026-09-11. Rewritten 2026-09-11, consumer-facing pass.
6 slides. Keyword CTA HOOKS added 2026-09-27 (was save/share), automation in ../automation/automation-04-HOOKS.md.

Pattern source: Whitney Bartlette teardown. Cover carries the number, the slides carry the
full list, nothing is gated. Her swipe-file carousel ran three times over eight months and
pulled 1,252 comments off one asset.

**Who each hook is aimed at, read this before editing.** The READER is an agent. The
TARGET of every hook is that agent's buyer or seller. A hook that only lands with another
realtor is a recruiting hook and does not belong here. First draft failed this on 13 of 20.

**The angle that makes it honest:** every hook is for a format where you never appear on
camera. Listing photos with voiceover (`listing-video`), a screen recording of consumer
data (`claude-shorts`), text on black, or green screen (`local-news`). All four are live
systems.

**Word-count note.** This is a list carousel, so the 20-word supporting-copy cap in
SKILL.md does not apply to slides 2 through 5. Each hook is held to 10 words or fewer.

## COVER HOOK, 3 options

1. **20 REEL HOOKS FOR REALTORS WHO HATE BEING ON CAMERA** / Not one of these needs your face.
2. **I HAVE NOT FILMED MY FACE IN WEEKS. 20 HOOKS THAT STILL WORK.** / Photos, screen recordings, text, green screen.
3. **20 REEL HOOKS YOU CAN SHOOT IN CLAUDE CODE WITH THE CAMERA OFF** / Steal the whole list.

Recommended: option 1. It names the objection out loud, which is the actual reason most
agents post nothing. Option 3 names the tool and is the better YouTube title.

## SLIDES 2 THROUGH 5

**Slide 2. OVER LISTING PHOTOS, VOICEOVER ONLY**
1. "The room that sells this house is not the kitchen."
2. "Here is what this price actually buys you."
3. "Nobody tours a house because of the front door."
4. "This one has been sitting. Here is why."
5. "The photos do not show you this part."

**Slide 3. OVER A SCREEN RECORDING**
Record the data, not your software. Price history, tax record, the map, the days-on-market
column. A buyer will watch a cursor move across a number they care about.
6. "Watch what happened to this home's price."
7. "This house sold twice. Look at the gap."
8. "Three houses on this street. Three different prices."
9. "Every home in this neighborhood under your number."
10. "This listing has changed price three times."

**Slide 4. TEXT ON BLACK, NO FOOTAGE AT ALL**
11. "Your offer was not too low. It was too slow."
12. "The inspection is not where you negotiate."
13. "Three reasons your house did not sell."
14. "You are not competing on price. You are competing on terms."
15. "Sellers, the first two weeks decide everything."

**Slide 5. GREEN SCREEN, NEWS DESK**
16. "Something changed in your neighborhood this week."
17. "One rule changed. Here is who it affects."
18. "This week's news, and what it means for your house."
19. "One change this month affects every listing here."
20. "Nobody in this market is talking about this yet."

## CAPTION

Most agents who say they hate being on camera do not actually hate the camera. They hate
sitting down with nothing to say and a red light on.

So here are twenty first lines, sorted by the format that needs the least from you.
Listing photos with a voiceover. A screen recording of a price history. Plain text on
black. Green screen at a desk. Your face appears in exactly none of them.

Every one of these is pointed at a buyer or a seller, not at another agent. That part
matters more than the format. Your feed is not a place to impress the people you compete
with.

I film one thing a week and everything else is a copy of that recording. That is not a
discipline hack, it is a file structure.

Pick one hook off this list, open your phone, and record it before you talk yourself out
of it. The hook is the hard part and I just did that part for you.

DM me HOOKS and I will send you all 20 as text, so you can copy them straight into your notes.
