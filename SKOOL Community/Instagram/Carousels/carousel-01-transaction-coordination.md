## DESIGN BRIEF (v3, built 2026-09-15)

Built, not a prompt: [render-v3.html](render-v3.html) renders all 9 slides at 1080x1350,
`node shoot-v3.mjs` writes them to `png-v3/` off the localhost:8091 server. The v1 brief that
used to live here specced the **retired** cream `#F5F2EB` / teal `#04404C` / gold `#C48424`
palette and Poppins. That system is dead. Do not rebuild from it.

**Palette v2.0 "Vector", the canonical brand.** White `#FFFFFF` base, `#F7F8FA` sunken fill,
true-black `#000000` dark surface, navy ink `#050E3D`, muted ink `#55627F`, hairline `#E4E7EF`.
One accent `#1768E5` on white, `#5B9BFF` on black, and never a second value. No warm colors,
no gradients, no shadows, no radius above 4px. Black is a surface, never a text color.

**Type.** Barlow 800 headlines at `-0.028em`, Raleway 600 for eyebrows and micro labels at
`0.15em` uppercase, Inter 400 body at line-height 1.58. The faces are self-hosted from
`Brandkit/Design-System/fonts/`, so a headless render needs no network.

**The frame.** 96px gutters, 120px top, 190px foot, so nothing is corner-pinned and every
slide survives Instagram's 1:1 profile-grid crop. `.slide` is a plain positioned block and an
inner `.stack` does the column layout: an abspos child of a flex container resolves against
its CONTENT box, which silently insets corner-pinned furniture by the gutter.

**Four elements carry the deck.**

1. **Structural black bands.** Slides 1, 4, 7 and 9 are full-bleed black butted against white.
   That contrast is the brand's pop, not color.
2. **A progress rail.** Full-bleed 12px bar along the bottom of every slide, filled to the
   reader's position. Ninth slide is full.
3. **A ghost numeral.** The slide number in Barlow 800 at 420px, bleeding off the right edge in
   `#F7F8FA` on white and 4.5% white on black. Builds a base, a raised and a floating plane out of value
   alone, since Vector has no shadow or gradient to spend.
4. **An eyebrow row.** Accent index, 56px accent rule, uppercase kicker. Gives each slide a
   named beat: The problem, The input, The extraction, The output, The detail, The switch,
   The first move.

**Illustration.** Nine inline SVG line drawings, 6px neutral `#C9CFDE` / 8px accent strokes,
round caps and joins, one accent pick per drawing, all bottom-aligned on a shared baseline.
Logo art stays out: every logo PNG is still v1, so the wordmark is set typographically.

## Why this works

The hook leads with a live number, three deals actually open on his board, which is the one
thing a course guru cannot copy. The slide order teaches the mechanism before the payoff, so slide 7
lands as a conclusion the reader already reached rather than a pitch. The CTA sends people to the DMs, which
starts a real conversation instead of a comment you have to chase. Today, screenshot slide 6 and check your own last three transaction emails
against it.
