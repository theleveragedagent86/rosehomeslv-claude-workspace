--- MESSAGE 2A | shortcut: tc2a | the deliverable, part 1 ---

THE SETUP

One folder per property, named by address. Inside it, one file called transaction.json. That file is the single source of truth. Nothing in the deal gets typed twice.

WHAT GOES IN THE FILE

Deal basics: address, type, status, contract date, COE date, purchase price, earnest money, contract number.

People, each with name, email and phone: buyer, seller, listing agent and their TC, buyer agent, escrow officer, lender, inspector, builder rep if it is new construction.

Dates, pulled straight off the executed contract: earnest money deadline, inspection contingency, due diligence, appraisal contingency, loan contingency, HOA review. My last new build had 17 dated items in this section.

Progress: completed steps, triggered events, next step, next step date.


--- MESSAGE 2B | shortcut: tc2b | the deliverable, part 2 ---

HOW IT RUNS

Drop the executed contract in the folder. The dates section gets filled from the contract, not from memory. Every email after that reads the file, so the escrow opening, the inspection notice, the appraisal update and the clear to close all go out with the right dates and the right people already copied.

WHERE TO START

Do not build this for a future deal. Build it for the file you are most behind on right now. The next deal inherits the whole thing for free.

That is yours, no strings. Quick question so I point you at the right thing: how many files do you have open right now, and are you coordinating them yourself or do you have a TC?
