# Peyson Robertson, the email side of the funnel

Teardown of **"IN THE TRENCHES with Peyson Robertson"**, his weekly agent newsletter.

**Source:** 8 consecutive issues, forwarded by Ryan from `ryanrose@hawaii.edu` to
`ryan@rosehomeslv.com` on 2026-09-01, one Gmail thread. Raw text saved at
`data/newsletter_2026-09-01_raw.txt` (76,078 characters).

**Capture caveats.** Plain text body only, so **every link and button URL is lost**, only the
anchor text survives ("GRAB YOUR OBSIDIAN BOOTCAMP TICKET", "APPLY TO JOIN THE TEAM"). Dates are
inferred from the Freddie Mac figures and event references inside each issue, not from headers.
This is 8 issues, not the full archive. Sent through **Constant Contact**.

---

## The headline finding

**The Instagram teardown was looking at half a funnel.** Instagram is acquisition. **The email
is where the selling happens**, and it sells three things at once, every single week, forever.

Nothing on his Instagram asks for money. Every newsletter asks three times.

```
IG carousel + keyword CTA  ->  DM  ->  free guide on peysonrobertson.com
                                            |
                                            v
                                   the email list
                                            |
                                            v
                       weekly newsletter, 3 stacked offers, every week
                       ├─ 7 Figure Agent Club   (paid mastermind, recurring)
                       ├─ The Obsidian Group    (team recruiting, his brokerage)
                       └─ Obsidian Bootcamp     (paid 3-day in-person event)
```

## The template, identical in 8 of 8 issues

He is not writing a newsletter each week. He is filling in nine slots.

| # | Block | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **THIS WEEK FROM ME** | personal cold open, then a table of contents for the issue |
| 2 | **TACTICAL TIP #1** | ~300 words, one tactic, ends with a do-this-this-week instruction |
| 3 | **TACTICAL TIP #2** | same, second tactic |
| 4 | **THIS WEEK'S VIDEO** | pitches the YouTube upload, always framed as free and practical |
| 5 | **THE 7 FIGURE AGENT CLUB** | recaps what the paid mastermind covered that week, then the link |
| 6 | **WHAT THE OBSIDIAN GROUP IS UP TO** | team news, ends "APPLY TO JOIN THE TEAM" |
| 7 | **THE OBSIDIAN BOOTCAMP** | paid event, with a live seat counter for scarcity |
| 8 | **FAST FORWARD MOVEMENT** | this week's Zoom schedule, password by reply |
| 9 | **IN THE MARKET THIS WEEK** | rate data plus "My take:", then sign-off and a forward ask |

Two of the eight swap the label to **SYSTEM TIP** or lead with a **NEW GUIDE** block above the
tips. Everything else is fixed. Same order, same headers, same sign-off, week after week.

**The tips always end with an assignment.** "Send ten video texts this week." "Pick one recent
sale and call 20 surrounding homes." "Block two hours a week to walk your farm." Never a summary,
always a next action.

## Block 9, the market report, is the retention device

Every issue, same shape:

1. The Freddie Mac 30-year fixed weekly average, with the exact date, versus last week.
2. Two or three sentences of context: Fed tone, inflation, oil, Fannie Mae and MBA forecasts.
3. **"My take:"** followed by the conversation the agent should be having with clients.

Eight weeks of it, tracked: 6.44% (Jul 4) → ~6.7% → 6.55% → 6.58% → ~6.6% → 6.69% (Aug 6, a
one-year high) → 6.67% (Aug 13) → 6.65% (Aug 20).

The "My take" is always a **script, not an opinion**: "The narrative of just wait for rates to
drop is officially dead, and you should be having that honest conversation with every buyer on
the fence." He is handing agents something to say on Monday. That is why the email gets opened
even in a week where the reader is not buying anything.

## What he does that Ryan does not

**1. The reply trigger, moved into email.**
> "Want the Zoom password? Just reply to this email and I will send it right over."

That is the IG comment keyword mechanic ported to the inbox. A reply is the highest value
deliverability signal there is, and it opens a one to one thread. He asks for one every week.

**2. Public scarcity with real numbers.**
> "We are now at 14 of 30 seats booked, which means we are nearly half full."

Then in a later issue, "we are down to the final seats." He publishes the counter and lets it
climb across weeks. No fake deadline, just the actual number.

**3. A free guide library as the standing offer.**
Every issue points at **peysonrobertson.com**: "dozens of free guides on there now and I add new
stuff every single week." Named example: **"100+ Buyer Leads a Week from Instagram."** Note that
the guide teaches the exact keyword-comment mechanic the IG teardown found, so the free guide
both delivers value and explains why his own posts look the way they do.

**4. Two domains, on purpose.**
`peysonrobertson.com` is the personal brand and the free library. `obsidianregroup.com` is the
team and the license. The coaching brand and the brokerage never blur.

**5. A running personal narrative.**
His son is due October 1st, and it is load-bearing across issues: "I am 76 days out from becoming
a father," "that single fact has reshaped how I run my entire business." He uses it to justify
the entire systems-and-leverage thesis. It makes the pitch feel like a confession.

**6. Volume, admitted openly.**
> "I am posting 10 trial reels a day on Instagram. Every single day."

That single line explains the 556 posts in the Instagram capture. The account is a test rig.

**7. The forward ask, every issue.**
> "If something in here helped you, forward it to an agent who needs it."

## Where he is still beatable, updated

The Instagram teardown said he teaches Claude at a surface level. **The email says otherwise,
inside the paid room.** He is teaching Claude Code, Cowork and ManyChat to a mastermind right
now. That is a real competitive fact and it moves faster than his public content does.

What is still open:

- **He teaches it live and it disappears.** Weekly Zoom sessions with no ladder and no carried
  artifact. Nothing compounds for the member between sessions. Ryan's Business Brain folder is
  exactly the thing Peyson's format cannot produce.
- **His public AI content stays surface level** ("4 prompts", "connect Claude to your MLS")
  because the real material is the paid hook. That gap is where Ryan can out-publish him: the
  8,689 accounts that commented on one of his AI posts are being taught the shallow version.
- **He is geographically anchored.** Palm Desert, the Inland Empire, an in-person bootcamp in
  the desert, team recruiting local to Southern California. Ryan's product has no venue.
- **His newsletter has no on-ramp for a cold reader.** Every issue assumes you already know what
  the 7 Figure Agent Club is. A cold subscriber gets three asks and no Level 0.

## What to copy, in priority order

1. **Build the weekly email.** It is the missing half. The Skool community currently has no
   outbound at all, and the Instagram funnel dead-ends in a DM.
2. **Steal the nine block template verbatim as a structure**, not as copy. It is the reason he
   can ship weekly without deciding anything.
3. **End every tip with an assignment**, never a summary.
4. **Run the "My take" market block.** Ryan already has better market data than Peyson does, he
   works an actual MLS every day. One Las Vegas number plus a script an agent can use Monday.
5. **Put the reply trigger in every issue.** One question, answer by reply. Same doctrine as
   `../../../Skool-Welcome-DM.md`: ask one question, hand the answer back.
6. **Recap the community publicly.** What got built in the Skool this week, in detail, with the
   join link under it. Report, do not pitch.
7. **Publish a free guide library.** `BUSINESS-BRAIN-STARTER.md` in
   `../../Carousels/02-business-brain/` is the first one. It needs a permanent home.
8. **Separate the brands in public the way he does.** Leveraged Agent for agents, Rose Homes LV
   for clients. That separation already exists in this workspace, it does not exist online yet.
