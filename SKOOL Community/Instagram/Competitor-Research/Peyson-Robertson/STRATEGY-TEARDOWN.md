## 2. Carousels beat reels for this audience, and it is not close

Carousels are 132 of 319 2026 posts but produce **44,131 of the comments (67%)**.

Median comment-to-like ratio: **carousels 0.72, reels 0.25.** Nearly one comment per like on a
carousel. That only happens when the format itself is doing persuasion work: a hook slide, 6-9
teaching slides, then an ask.

Reels are used for reach and for personality. Carousels are used for conversion.

## 4. Hook patterns that work, measured

Median comments on 2026 posts, sliced by what the first line of the caption does:

| Hook feature | n | median comments | median likes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Prove me wrong"** | 3 | **814** | 1,652 |
| Names Claude / AI | 20 | 227 | 274 |
| Starts "Most agents..." / "Most realtors..." | 22 | 137 | 157 |
| Contains a dollar amount | 33 | 112 | 254 |
| "Open house" in the hook | 39 | 113 | 298 |
| Contrarian word (not / never / wrong / stop) | 66 | 114 | 161 |
| Contains any number | 127 | 89 | 142 |
| First-person story ("I ...") | 43 | 94 | 190 |
| **Ends in a question mark** | 36 | **18** | 75 |
| **No real-estate noun at all (pure motivation)** | 157 | **21** | 112 |
| Baseline, all 2026 posts | 319 | 66 | 132 |

Two things jump out:

1. **"Prove me wrong" is his highest-leverage sentence on the account.** Three posts, 814 median
   comments. "One hour open houses are the best form of open houses right now. Prove me wrong."
   (1,899 comments) and "Weekday open houses from 4 to 6pm beat weekend open houses. Prove me
   wrong." (814). He states a specific, checkable, mildly heretical operational opinion and
   invites disagreement. Agents cannot scroll past it.
2. **Asking a question kills it (18 median).** Counterintuitive but consistent. A question invites
   an opinion; a claim plus "prove me wrong" invites a defence. The second is far stickier.

### Caption length is a dial, not noise

| Caption length | n | median comments |
|---|---|---|
| 0-80 chars | 45 | 6 |
| 80-300 | 76 | 35 |

The sweet spot is a **700-1,200 character caption**: a full teaching sequence in the caption
itself, then the keyword. Short captions get almost nothing.

## 5. The five hook formulas he reuses

1. **The specific-number proof story.**
   "36 homes in 6 months. Solo. No online leads." / "103 listings in 10 months. Solo agent, no
   assistant, every call herself." / "A two dollar sign in a buyer's front yard turned into over
   ten million in volume." (2,287 comments)
   Small, weird, concrete detail plus a large outcome. The weird detail is what makes it read as
   true.

2. **"Most agents X. Meanwhile the agents winning Y."**
   22 posts, 137 median comments. Sorts the reader into the losing group, then offers the exit.

3. **The stated heresy plus "prove me wrong."** See above. Highest performer on the account.

4. **The AI receipt post.**
   "I let Claude run my real estate Instagram for 30 days. 1 million views. 58,700 interactions.
   4,100 new followers. 216 pieces of content. All in 30 days. All built from 4 prompts."
   (3,778 comments, his #1 post ever). Repeated at 90 days for another 1,643. The mechanism is a
   **verifiable receipt with four numbers**, not a claim. "All built from 4 prompts" is the line
   that creates the keyword demand.

5. **The cheap-object referral story.**
   "A $100 coffee maker in a fire station can become the biggest referral source in your business."
   (1,064) / "40 people came through an open house on a Sunday and the agent paid for none of it"
   (750). Tiny input, disproportionate output, fully copyable this week.

## 7. The August regression, and the lesson in it

| Month | posts | % with a comment CTA | median comments (whole month) | median comments (CTA posts only) | total comments |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-05 | 43 | 88% | 180 | 197 | 15,989 |
| 2026-06 | 59 | 74% | 114 | 189 | 14,047 |
| 2026-07 | 61 | 57% | 39 | 108 | 11,886 |
| 2026-08 | 55 | 32% | 13 | **219** | 8,019 |

Total comments halved from May to August. **The CTA did not stop working, in August it was his
best month ever on CTA posts, 219 median.** He simply stopped using it, dropping from 88% to 32%
of posts, and replaced it with volume. On 2026-08-07 he posted "Day 5 update on posting 10 reels
a day for 100 days." Median reel plays fell from 7,354 (May) to 3,478 (August).

**Lesson for Ryan: volume without the mechanism destroys the mechanism.** 55 posts a month at 13
median comments is worse than 43 posts at 180. Do not chase a posting-count number.

## 9. What to steal, in priority order

1. **Ship a keyword CTA on every business post.** One word, in quotes, last line. This alone is
   the 11-12x.
2. **Reply to 100% of keyword comments.** That is the DM open and it is the only reason the funnel
   exists.
3. **Move to carousel-first for teaching.** Reels for reach, carousels for the ask. His carousel
   comment:like ratio is 3x his reel ratio.
4. **Lead with AI/Claude receipts, not AI promises.** "I let Claude run my Instagram for 30 days:
   1M views, 58.7K interactions, 4,100 followers, 216 pieces, 4 prompts" is the single best-
   performing structure on the account. Ryan has the same receipts and better ones.
5. **Write 700-1,200 character captions.** Full teaching sequence, then the keyword.
6. **Use "prove me wrong" on a specific operational opinion** about once a month. Highest
   comment yield of any sentence he has ever used.
7. **Cut motivational posts to near zero.** They are 30% of his output and 7% of his return.
8. **Never end a hook with a question mark.** Make a claim instead.
