IN THE MARKET THIS WEEK As we kick off July and the second half of the year, here is where the market stands and what it means for how you talk to your clients. The 30-year fixed is sitting right around 6.44% today and has settled into a stable mid 6% range that most forecasters expect to hold through the rest of the year. The bigger picture has become clear over the last month. After inflation spiked to 4.2% in May, the highest in over three years, the Federal Reserve has shifted its tone hard. Instead of the rate cuts everyone expected coming into the year, the majority of Fed policymakers now think a hike may be more likely than a cut later this year. Fannie Mae and the Mortgage Bankers Association are both projecting rates finish 2026 somewhere in the 6.3% to 6.5% range. Most economists do not expect rates to consistently drop below 6% until sometime in 2027 at the earliest. Here is the encouraging part for agents. Purchase applications are running ahead of last year's pace and have shown year over year growth for almost three straight months. Buyers are finding opportunities in markets with more inventory and easing price growth. People are adjusting to this rate environment and transacting anyway, because life does not pause for perfect conditions. My take: The narrative of just wait for rates to drop is officially dead, and you should be having that honest conversation with every buyer on the fence. The data now says waiting could mean paying more, not less, because if rates ever do fall meaningfully, the flood of buyers that returns will drive prices and competition right back up. The buyer who acts now with more inventory and more negotiating power is often in a stronger position than the one who waits. Be the agent who can explain that clearly and confidently.
