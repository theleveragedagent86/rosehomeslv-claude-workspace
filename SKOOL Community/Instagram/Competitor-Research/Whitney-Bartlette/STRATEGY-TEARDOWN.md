## 1. The one mechanic

**She runs a keyword-to-DM machine and nothing else is the business.**

She posts a short tip reel, buries "comment GUIDE" near the end of the caption, and a
Manychat automation delivers the asset in DM. She never replies by hand. Of 18,149 comment
rows on 228 posts, **17 are hers.** 0.09%. She has automated her entire funnel.

**58% of every human comment on this account is a person typing a keyword.** 10,508 of
18,132 rows are one of her trigger words. That is not engagement, that is an opt-in form
wearing a comment box.

### CTA lift table

| format | n with CTA | median comments | n without CTA | median comments | multiple |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| carousel | 4 | 333.0 | 8 | 64.5 | **5.2x** |
| reel | 58 | 130.5 | 97 | 27.0 | **4.8x** |
| image | 3 | 21.0 | 58 | 19.5 | 1.1x |

A keyword CTA on a reel earns 4.8x the comments. On a still image it earns nothing. The
mechanic only fires on motion.

### What Ryan should copy verbatim

- **One keyword per post, in caps, placed at ~82% through the caption.** Median CTA
  position across her 65 keyword posts is 82% of the way in. Only 9 of 65 put it in the
  first line. The hook sells the outcome, the ask comes after the reader is committed.
- **Automate the fulfilment.** 17 hand replies out of 18,149 rows. Whatever she is paying
  Manychat is the cheapest labour in her business.
- **Reuse the same keyword until it stops working.** SHIFT ran 17 times, GUIDE 16 times.
  She does not invent a new word per post. She invents a new post per word.

are the top of the funnel. The carousels are the conversion asset and she under-uses them.

## 4. Hooks

Her hooks are longer than her losers, not shorter. The shape is: reaction word, then the
deliverable, then a down arrow. No question marks. No curiosity gaps. She tells you exactly
what you are about to get.

- The ask sits at **82% of the way through the caption**. Only 9 of 65 put it in line one.

## 8. Month over month

She is **growing in volume and shrinking in conversion.**

Posts went 13 → 13 → 18 → 21 → 19 → 33 → 55 → 38 → 14 (Sept partial). Median comments per
post went 109 → 43 → 47.5 → 35 → 63 → 29 → 20 → 24.5 → 18. Median plays went 62,833 in
January to 22,037 in September, a **65% decline in reach per post** while output roughly
tripled.

July is the tell: 55 posts, 8,792 total comments, but a median of 20. She quadrupled output
and her per-post conversion fell to a fifth of January's. Total comments held up only
because two reels went viral that month.

Her CTA discipline also slipped: 46% of January posts carried a keyword, 12% in June, 16%
in August. **She is posting more and asking less.** That is the signature of a content
treadmill, not a funnel.

## 10. Where she is beatable

**She created enormous AI demand and sells no AI product.**

That is the opening, and it is not subtle. Her AI keywords pulled **2,720 opt-ins**
(AUDIT 1,286, PROMPT 624, MERLIN 569, OPUS 202, HIGGSFIELD 39) off a grand total of seven
asks. Her AI posts convert 4.4x her median topic. Her paid community keyword, SHIFT, pulled
220 off seventeen asks. **She is 12x better at generating AI demand than at generating
demand for the thing she actually sells, and she has not connected the two.**

The other openings:

- **No ladder past $29/mo.** Her Skool sits at 133 members at $29 (source: her own Skool
  page, checked 9 Sep 2026). Against 932,603 followers that is a **0.014% conversion** and
  roughly $3,857/mo. She has a firehose and a garden hose valve. There is no high-ticket
  tier, no done-for-you, no cohort.
- **She teaches the tool, never the workflow.** Every AI post is one prompt or one app.
  There is no system, no carried artifact, no "here is your business running on this in 30
  days." Ryan's Level 0-10 course structure is a category she has not entered.
- **Zero human reply.** 17 replies in 18,149 comments. Every one of her 28,461 engaged
  accounts has had exactly zero conversation with her. A person who actually answers is a
  differentiator against her, not a cost.
- **She is a generalist to business owners, not a specialist to realtors.** Only 1,089 of
  28,461 accounts (3.8%) carry a realtor or lender name signal. Her audience is creators,
  travel agents, artists, gift-wrappers. Ryan can be the only person in a realtor's feed
  who has ever closed a transaction.
- **Her per-post conversion is in decline.** Median comments per post fell 109 → 18 since
  January while volume tripled. She is out-running her own funnel.

**The play:** take her exact mechanic, point it at AI-for-realtors, put a real ladder behind
it, and answer the DMs like a human. She has already proved the demand exists and has
already declined to serve it.
