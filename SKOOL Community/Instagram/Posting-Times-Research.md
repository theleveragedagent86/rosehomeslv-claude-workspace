# When Realtors Are on Instagram: Posting Times for @the.leveraged.agent

Researched 2026-09-21. Audience = real estate agents (Leveraged Agent Skool prospects), not home buyers. All times Pacific (Ryan is in Las Vegas).

## Bottom line

- **Main slot: 5-6pm PT.** First push lands as the evening peak starts.
- **Optional 2nd slot: 5am PT** (East Coast morning scroll). Schedule the night before.
- **Best days: Sun, Mon, Sat.** Weakest: Thu, Fri.
- **Timing is the smaller lever.** A comment-keyword CTA moved comments ~15x more than any time slot.
- **After ~30 posts, switch to the account's own Insights > "Most active times."**

## Method

Source: the competitor harvests in `Competitor-Research/` (Peyson Robertson, Whitney Bartlette, Kimberly Prince). 57,920 third-party comments; 21,374 from accounts with a realtor name signal (realtor, realty, broker, homes, eXp, KW, Compass, etc. in handle or name).

Only comments left **24h+ after the post went up** were counted (n = 11,863). That removes the "people comment right after a post goes live" effect, so the curve shows when agents scroll, not when posts were published.

Post-time vs results: Peyson's 556 posts, split by whether the caption asks for a comment keyword (the keyword posts dominate his comment totals and cluster in the afternoon, so mixing them skews the result).

## When realtors are active (share of comments per hour, PT)

| Window | Share/hr | Read |
|---|---|---|
| 12-3am | ~1.5% | dead |
| 4-7am | ~5% | East Coast morning (7-10am ET) |
| 8am-4pm | ~3.8% | flat, working |
| 5-9pm | 5-7% | **peak**, 7-8pm busiest (6.8%) |
| 10-11pm | 3-4% | tapering |

Days: Sun 15.9%, Mon 15.4%, Tue 15.0%, Wed 14.7%, Sat 13.9%, Fri 12.7%, Thu 12.4%.

## General studies (for contrast)

- Buffer (9.6M posts, 2024-25): Wed best, Thu 9am top slot, Fri/Sat worst.
- Sprout Social (2B engagements, Nov 2025-Feb 2026): Tue/Wed midday to early evening, weekends lowest.
- Hootsuite: Mon-Fri late afternoon.
- Later: 5am local, Mon best.

These say weekends are weak. **That does not hold for a realtor audience**: agents scroll most Sun/Mon, and Peyson's weekend posts got the most plays.

- NAR 2025 Tech Survey: 62% of Realtors use Instagram; social named the top source of quality leads (39%).
- Mosseri (IG head): no single best time; post just before your followers' active peak. Top ranking signals are watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach. Posts keep getting pushed for ~48h, but the first 30-60 min decides the initial push.
- NOT FOUND: any published hour-by-hour study of when agents themselves scroll IG. The competitor data above is the closest thing.

## Limits

- Comments are a proxy. People who only like or watch may keep different hours.
- Commenter locations unknown. Peyson (Coachella Valley) likely skews West Coast, which fits Ryan.
- Kristi Jencks' data (no people lists) shows the same drift: evening posts got the most comments (median 8 vs 2 in the morning).
