## What this is

For each of the 286 West Coast target agents in [West-Coast-Realtor-Research.md](West-Coast-Realtor-Research.md), I opened their Instagram follower list and pulled out the followers who identify as realtors/agents/brokers in their name or handle. Everything is globally deduplicated, so a realtor who follows several of the target agents appears once (with a count of how many targets follow them). These are fresh Leveraged-Agent recruiting prospects, sourced from the audiences of agents already in the niche.

## How to read the priority shortlist

The `#` column = how many of the 286 target agents follow this person. A realtor followed by 8–12 of these mid-tier grinders is deeply embedded in the exact niche The Leveraged Agent recruits from — start here. The full 1,752 are in the TSV.

## ⚠️ Coverage & accuracy caveats (read before DMing)

- **Instagram follower privacy:** most accounts now hide their full follower list from outsiders ("Only [user] can see all followers"). For those "locked" accounts I could only read the ~10 *followers you know* panel (biased toward @rosehomeslv's own network), so those contributed few handles. Roughly half the target agents were fully searchable; the other half contributed only their visible panel. The list therefore under-samples locked accounts' audiences — it is not every realtor who follows every agent.
- **12 agents could not be opened at all** (private accounts / no follower link exposed): kendramillerrealtor, katirealestate, linayorkrealty, yourhomegirlsd, cindyplourde_, realestateagentorangecounty, susan_chase_, okolorealty, theryanrealestateteam, dreamdigspdx, heidi.kuker, realtroymacdonald.
- **Match rule:** inclusion was based on the visible name/handle saying realtor/realty/real estate/broker/homes/etc. I did not open each of the 1,752 profiles to read full bios — verify the handle resolves and is a producing agent before you DM.
- **Location not filtered:** these are realtors from all over (many are West Coast, but some are TX/ID/Toronto/etc. — whoever follows the target agents). The `name` column usually shows their market.
- **Nevada:** this harvest was not NV-filtered the way the batch-1 agent list was. If keeping NV agents out matters for followers too, screen the `name` column for Vegas/Henderson/NV before outreach.

**Compiled:** July 2026 · Logged in as @rosehomeslv via browser

## ⚠️ Coverage & accuracy caveats (read before DMing)

- **Instagram rate-limited the follower-search feature mid-run, twice.** After roughly 250–300 rapid searches the "search within followers" box started returning a false "No results found" for accounts that had real matches (confirmed by re-testing known-good accounts and by the empty state clearing after a ~20 minute pause, then again after a shorter ~10 minute pause). Every account that returned a suspicious zero during the two throttled windows was re-harvested once the limit cleared and overwritten with real data. A few isolated zero-results remain in the data; those were spot-checked against accounts harvested immediately before/after (which succeeded normally), so they are believed to be genuine small/no-match accounts, not throttle artifacts.
- **39 of 359 target accounts could not be opened at all** (no followers-list link found on the page, or a private/otherwise inaccessible profile): `_danieljresidential_`, `_ivanarjona_`, `achomegroup`, `allisonsrealestate`, `amanda.atx`, `ashtontheiss`, `becca_summers_ut_realtor`, `breortizrealtor`, `closingswithnickila`, `cm.sold`, `gisell.merizalde`, `gobealgroup`, `hannah_your_realtor`, `hannahtwelverivers`, `investinwisconsin`, `janaoman_`, `jennicarealtor`, `jpgrealtyteam`, `laurenmcmannrealtor`, `matthewlaymanhq`, `mbolt`, `midcoastmkerealty`, `r.coit54`, `rylanleopinarelli`, `teamlarkins1`, `thesethbrothersteam`, `tinahamblycreative`, `utahrealestate.karlee`, `valley_cole_the_realtor`, `wrg_mke`, plus `caleb_carlson_` (a page redirect during harvest attributed its data to the wrong account key, so it has no clean entry) and a handful of others. Same underlying cause as the original pass: Instagram hides full follower lists on many accounts.
- **Team/brand accounts were included this time**, per Ryan's direction. Their "followers" may include some non-agent fans/clients mixed with real prospects more than a personal account would; screen a bit more before DMing off a team-account source.
- **Match rule, location, and Nevada:** same as above — inclusion is based on the visible name/handle, location wasn't filtered, and this harvest is not NV-filtered. Screen the name column for Vegas/Henderson/NV before outreach.
- **Cross-follows are a feature, not noise:** 58 of the harvested handles are themselves agents already on the Part 2/3 target list (e.g. @jennyrojer, a Twin Cities Part 3 agent, follows several other target agents). That's a useful "these people already know each other" signal — no need to strip them out.
