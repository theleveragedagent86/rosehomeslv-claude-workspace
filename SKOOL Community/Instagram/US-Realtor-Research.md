# Part 1 — West Coast metros (Batch 1)

## Scope & rules

- **Metros (this batch):** Los Angeles, San Diego, San Francisco / Bay Area, Sacramento, Orange County, Seattle, Portland, Phoenix / Scottsdale, Denver (incl. Colorado Springs edge). Other regions come in future batches.
- **Tier:** roughly 3K–100K followers. Celebrity/TV mega-agents excluded (they aren't buying an $86/mo community). A few sub-3K verified grinders were kept where the metro pool was thin (mainly Portland/Seattle); treat those as second-tier outreach.
- **Hard exclusion — no Nevada:** any agent licensed or actively selling in NV/Las Vegas was excluded entirely, even if they also work an in-scope market. No NV ties were confirmed on any included agent; anything even ambiguous is called out in the per-metro flag notes.
- **Dedupe:** agents working multiple in-scope metros are listed once, under their primary market (noted in the entry).
- **Non-agents excluded:** lenders, coaches-only, stagers, and anonymous team/brand accounts with no identifiable producing agent.
