#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

🛠️ **Harness the Power of Claude Skills** by understanding how Claude reads markdown files (containing a name, description, and body) to automatically invoke the right script at the exact right moment.

🛒 **Navigate the Skill Marketplace** to find, clone, and leverage vetted, battle-tested community skills—saving you from writing integrations from scratch.

🪙 **Master Tokenomics to Slash Costs** by choosing the right AI model for the right job (like utilizing cheaper models for simple tasks), preventing you from "using a bazooka to open a door".

📉 **Prevent "Context Rot"** by keeping track of your token usage (using `/context`) and knowing when to run `/compact` or `/clear` to stop quiet context erosion from degrading your AI’s performance.

⚡ **One-Shot Your Prompts** by treating your first prompt as your most critical, ensuring you don't burn tokens on messy, mid-conversation refactoring.

🔄 **Automate with Local & Remote Routines** to schedule background tasks—like drafting Gmail replies or handling inbound API webhooks—acting as a direct, lightweight alternative to [Make.com](http://Make.com) or n8n.

🔌 **Demystify CLI vs. API vs. MCP** to understand how the Model Context Protocol (MCP) acts as a universal remote control, allowing Claude to interact with external tools locally and efficiently.

👥 **Deploy Sub-Agents & Critic Loops** by parallelizing tasks across multiple models (like Claude, Gemini, or Codex) to review, critique, and battle-test your output before it ever goes live.

#### In this video, Jack covers:

The main problem addressed is the massive waste of time, money, and context tokens when developers build sloppy AI integrations, overload their chat windows, or use overly expensive models for basic tasks.

The system taught is the **Power Features Ecosystem**, focusing on custom Claude Skills, scheduled Routines, local MCP setups, and multi-model Autonomous Critic Loops.

Practical implementation steps include utilizing `/context` to monitor token bloat, setting up a local Bitly or YouTube MCP, and using sub-agents to parallelize and critique complex tasks in real time.

#### Who this is for:

- **Advanced AI Automators** who want to move past basic prompting and start building highly optimized, cost-efficient local workflows.

- **BPO & Compliance Professionals** looking to secure their workflows by implementing the "principle of minimal access" when connecting AI agents to sensitive data.
