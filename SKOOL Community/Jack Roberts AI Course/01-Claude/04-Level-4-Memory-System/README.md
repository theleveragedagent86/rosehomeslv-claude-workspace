#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

🧠 **Understand the Three Levels of AI Memory** (short, medium, and long-term) to finally solve the context limitation problem and allow your AI to offer highly tailored advice and build superior software.

⚙️ **Hardwire Your Short-Term Rules** by configuring the local system rules and global instructions within your Claude Code settings or anti-gravity environments so the AI remembers who you are out of the gate.

📂 **Eliminate Context Rot with Mid-Term Buckets** by organizing your projects into 5 to 8 clean desktop folders (such as community, client strategies, or personal) so you can safely reset chat windows without losing your place.

📋 **Draft a Project Operating Manual (**`claud.md`**)** using custom templates that establish a clear "North Star," key goals, and historical decisions for each of your project folders.

🗺️ **Incrementalize Knowledge with Karpathy Obsidian RAG** by structuring a persistent, compounding wiki of markdown files that automatically identifies cross-references and flags contradictions.

🌐 **Build a Long-Term Vector Database with Pinecone** to run semantic searches (searching by meaning rather than literal file names) on dense, high-volume data.

🧬 **Create Cross-Environment Wrap-Up Skills** that automatically summarize and save the critical takeaways of your conversations to both your local Obsidian wiki and remote Pinecone database simultaneously.

🔍 **Unleash the Google NotebookLM Integration** by syncing your environment with the platform, building deep research notebooks, and using web extensions to clip entire YouTube channels directly into your memory ecosystem.

🎙️ **Connect Live Meeting Intelligence** using tools like Granola and custom MCP connections to capture meeting notes and automatically schedule routines that vectorize and store transcripts.

#### In this video, Jack covers:

The main problem addressed is the strict limitation of AI context windows and "context rot"—where starting fresh chat windows forces the AI to forget your business context, while keeping long chats active destroys output quality and model performance.

The system taught is the **Three-Tiered Memory Architecture**, bridging immediate user rules, structural mid-term project directories, and robust long-term vector/wiki environments (Obsidian, Pinecone, and NotebookLM).

Practical implementation steps include setting up your desktop project folders, drafting local `claud.md` operating files, constructing an Obsidian vault, and integrating a custom Pinecone API key to run persistent wrap-up skills.

#### Who this is for:

- **AI Developers and Business Automators** who need their agentic systems to reference consistent guidelines, branding constraints, and previous architectural decisions without wasting token budgets.

- **High-Performance Consultants** managing multiple clients and meetings who want their AI to securely recall and synthesize call transcripts and industry research on the fly.
