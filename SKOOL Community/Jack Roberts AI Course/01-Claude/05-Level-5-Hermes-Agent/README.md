#### In this video, Jack covers:

The main problem addressed is the disconnect between sitting at a desktop IDE (like Claude Code) and being away from your desk, which often means losing access to your connected business context and personal memory systems.

The system taught is the mobile agent bridge using Hermes Agent, routing a secure private Telegram line back to your local files, Obsidian vaults, and NotebookLM workspace.

Practical implementation steps include installing Hermes locally or via Docker, creating your custom Telegram bot, launching the Claude OS dashboard, and writing your core `soul.md` file.

#### Who this is for:

- AI Automators and developers who want a highly customized, secure, and always-on personal assistant that bridges their desktop workflows with mobile convenience.

- Busy professionals and creators who need to run deep academic or strategic research through NotebookLM and query their personal second brain while on the go.
