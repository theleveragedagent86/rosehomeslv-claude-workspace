#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

💼 Charge a setup fee to install Hermes agent for other individuals or teams (the "locksmith" business model)

🎯 Apply niche positioning (one vertical, not generalist) to increase closing rate, pricing power, and retention

📈 Use annual vs. monthly billing cycles to increase client retention (the "stickier billing" principle)

🏗️ Apply the "build it once, sell it many times" model — refine one Hermes system to product-market fit, then resell/reseller-tier it like GoHighLevel white-label agencies

#### In this video, Jack covers:

This video addresses how to monetize Hermes agent — both by using it to increase personal efficiency and by selling it as a product or service to other businesses.

The framework taught centers on two monetization paths: setting up and hosting Hermes agent for clients (a setup fee plus recurring "AI back office" retainer), and using Hermes internally to remove your own bottlenecks so you have more time to sell. Pricing is framed around outcome-based value rather than hours worked, with niching down as a way to boost pricing power and referrals.

Practical implementation steps include: installing and hosting Hermes for a client's team, charging a setup fee plus a monthly maintenance/retainer fee, structuring retainers around annual billing for stickiness, calculating price as roughly 10% of first-year value delivered, picking one niche/vertical to specialize in, refining a single repeatable Hermes setup system, and reselling that system at scale (referencing the GoHighLevel white-label agency reseller model as a comparison), plus using the "biggest bottleneck" exercise to prioritize which part of the business to fix first.

#### Who this is for:

- AI agency owners and automation builders who already use Hermes agent and want to turn it into a paid service or recurring revenue stream for clients.
