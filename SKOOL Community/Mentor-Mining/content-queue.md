## The packaging rule that applies to all 10

From [saraev/02-patterns.md](saraev/02-patterns.md) §4, age-controlled within the 2026 cohort: **titles naming a specific product or model do 1,165.2 median views/day versus 476.4 for those that do not.** A 2.4x lift from a factual proper noun.

That lever is free and legal for you, because naming a tool is a verifiable fact. Use "Claude Code", "Lofty", "Meta", "GLVAR", "Excalidraw" in titles.

**Do not use** the income-figure title formula. Two reasons, and the second is the one that matters: it exposes you under Nevada license advertising rules, and Saraev himself abandoned it. Zero listicle titles in his 2026 cohort, one income-figure title in 48, and in 2025 income-in-title actually underperformed its own cohort (56.6 vs 75.5 median views/day). You would be copying the part of his channel that stopped working.

## 1. The rebuttal video

**Angle:** "A guy with 488K subscribers says people like me are a bad business to sell AI to. He's right about three things and wrong about why."

**Why this is first:** [TxMAUMUn-is] is the single highest-leverage piece of content in the whole corpus for you, and nobody else in the realtor-AI space can make it. Saraev's argument is that local businesses have a small lead pool, low technical literacy, and thin margins. You answer from inside the industry:

- **Small pool:** doesn't apply. You *are* the local business, you're not selling into one.
- **Thin margins:** inverts. A realtor's marginal deal is a $5K to $15K commission, not 6% of a $2M widget business.
- **Low technical literacy:** this one genuinely lands. Don't dodge it. It's the honest reason most agents bounce off AI, and it's exactly what Module 0's day-one quick win exists to solve.

**The disclosure that makes it fair:** say plainly that Saraev's own revenue comes from Maker School, teaching people, not from selling AI services to local businesses. That is not a gotcha, it is the same model you run, and saying so out loud is what keeps the video honest instead of cheap.

**Structure to steal (from that same video):** contrarian open, three reasons previewed inside 90 seconds, then walked twice so skimmers and finishers both get served, whiteboard math, and a personal failure doing the persuading instead of a claim.

**Do not say:** that he is wrong about local businesses generally. He is not, and the comment section will have watched his video.
