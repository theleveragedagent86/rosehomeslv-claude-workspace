## System demoed
Not one build, a repeatable go-to-market template applied three times. Selection rules: digital, over $1,000 per deal, minimal regulation. Prospecting motion (identical across all three niches): find the target list (Apollo.io filtered searches, or Indeed and LinkedIn job postings for hiring signals, or YouTube and Skool profiles for coaches), scrape it with Apify, enrich decision-maker emails with Any Mail Finder, generate an AI personalization icebreaker and a free custom asset (a Google Doc of YouTube titles, a competitor analysis, an example funnel), then send cold email with a results guarantee and DM the same asset across every platform they are on. Sellable systems named: personalized cold email lead gen (10 domains, 30 mailboxes on Instantly or Smartlead, warmed, 4,000 leads), a proposal generator fed by a short post-call form, a staged hiring or coaching pipeline in ClickUp or Monday where a status change auto-triggers the next email and asset drop, a Google Sheets plus Looker Studio funnel dashboard, and a RAG chatbot trained on the client's own content.

## Who it's for

## Transferable to realtors?
- **Verdict:** ADAPTABLE
- **Why:** Split honestly. The niche-selection framework does **not** transfer, real estate fails two of his three rules outright since it is geographically locked and carries fair housing, TCPA, DNC, and MLS rules, and he specifically warns against regulated verticals. But three of the systems rebuild cleanly for a realtor: the status-triggered pipeline is a transaction coordination board where moving a deal to "inspection" fires the client email, the Google Sheets to Looker Studio dashboard is a seller-facing listing performance report, and the asset-first outreach motion is exactly how to approach expireds and FSBOs. The RAG "AI Nick" chatbot is also a direct blueprint for a Leveraged Agent community assistant, though that serves Ryan the coach, not the realtors he teaches.
