## System demoed
Two things. First, the Loom SOP, five steps: (1) start recording already on the prospect's own page, not a cover slide, and zoom or scroll in the first seconds so the animated thumbnail proves it is live; (2) spend the first 15 to 30 seconds entirely on them, framed as a small fixable thing costing them money; (3) explicitly disclaim the ask, "just want to let you know up front I'm not expecting you to work with me"; (4) drop social proof organically mid-explanation with a named client and a dollar result, never front-loaded; (5) close with a zero-friction reply CTA, "respond with a thumbs up" and he builds the whole action plan free. Second, a project management taxonomy: stage-based systems for linear high-volume queues (assigned, writing, editing, ready for submission, submitted) versus subtask-based systems for complex non-linear scopes where stages collapse to todo/working/complete and the real work lives in parallel subtasks, with stage changes or subtask completions firing webhooks into make.com.

## Who it's for
The solo AI automation freelancer roughly between $0 and $5,000 a month who is sending outreach and not getting replies, and who is asking whether to hire, whether to scale volume, or whether the market is saturated. Explicitly not the person building a large company: "We're just trying to earn a living. We're just trying to, you know, make significantly more money than the average person while working significantly fewer hours."

## Economic argument
The hiring math is the centerpiece. Verbatim: "let's say you do end up hiring them and then two months go by and you train them up and everything's good. Now you're making $5,000 a month. You pay them $3,000 a month. Now you're making $2,000 a month." The alternative: "instead of charging $5,000, you're charging $2,000 cuz it's a smaller, simpler project. But 2* 5 is 10, which is two times what you're making at $5,000... this is how freelancers and solo agencies are hitting, you know, 20, 30, and 40k months." Reply-rate math: "That's still one in 50 people responding to you... let's just say you send 10,000. Well, now you have 100 positive responses per day." Proof for the white-glove reframe: "he makes over a million dollars a year as a solo agency owner with like 90 whatever percent margins." Sample Loom lines carry made-up-on-the-spot figures: "make you, I think, 10, maybe $15,000 more per month" and "I just did this for XYZ, really big name, and we made them $43,000." Important: the title's $9K/month is never stated anywhere in the transcript, and no dollar figure is attached to the Loom strategy itself.

## Transferable to realtors?
- **Verdict:** ADAPTABLE
- **Why:** The Loom SOP is close to name-change-only for a realtor recording a personalized video for an expired listing, a FSBO, a past client, or a referral partner, and the two best tricks (open on their own page so the thumbnail proves it is real, and close with "reply with a thumbs up" instead of "book a call") transfer intact. What forces a rebuild is compliance: expired and FSBO video outreach is regulated solicitation in most states, and the hiring, cold-email-copy, and project-management segments are agency-specific filler.
