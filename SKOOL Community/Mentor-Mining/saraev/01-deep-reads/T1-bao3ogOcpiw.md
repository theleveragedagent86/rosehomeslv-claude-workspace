## System demoed
Sign up to Apify (uses the $5 free credit) and Apollo.io. Build an Apollo people-search filtered by industry, job title (owner, founder, CEO, partner), and country. Spot-check 10 results for fit. Copy the search URL into the "codec crafter Apollo scraper" [sic?] actor on Apify with no timeout and 512MB memory, which yields roughly 4,000 to 4,500 profiles for the free credit. Export CSV into a Google Sheet, delete non-predictive columns, filter to rows that have both an email and a website, and use that sheet as the CRM. Write one short template and build it in-sheet with a `CONCATENATE` formula that appends a Loom URL. Then for each lead, open their LinkedIn plus their company site, record a 5-minute Loom screen share that walks through a prebuilt ClickUp recruitment candidate pipeline (intake, stage 1-3, interview, presentation, with stage changes firing automated candidate emails and forms), scroll the page at the start so the thumbnail proves a human was there, paste the Loom URL into the sheet, then send the same message twice, once as a LinkedIn connection note and once by email. Quota: 15 per day, roughly 3 to 5 minutes each.
