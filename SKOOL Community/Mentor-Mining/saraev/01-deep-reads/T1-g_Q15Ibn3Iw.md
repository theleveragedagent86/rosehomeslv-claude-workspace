## Thesis
Pick your niche on unit economics, not on interest, because the money is in businesses with high engagement value, high customer acquisition cost, thin margins and repetitive volume where a small percentage improvement is worth a large fee.

## System demoed
None, commentary only. Well, with just one client, you're already producing a 2x ROI." Recruitment: "if they're placing a $200,000 role, right? They make 20% of the first year salary.
