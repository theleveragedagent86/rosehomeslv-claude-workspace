## System demoed
A five-pillar operating playbook plus a downloadable asset pack, not a build. Marketing: rank Upwork/platform sales, cold email, communities, and cold DMs on time, capital, and technical barrier, then start on platforms because they put you at the top of the buying ladder. Outreach copy follows Instantly's structure (personalization, value prop, niche, goal, case study, cliffhanger, CTA). Sales: a 30 to 45 minute six-step call, rapport, set the schedule, discovery, propose solution with a live demo from a system library, frame the investment last, send a proposal on Panda Doc or Better Proposals with auto follow-up. Onboarding: one kickoff call that does intro and framing, goals and timelines, communication expectations (one daily update Mon to Fri), then platform access and two-factor codes handled live on the call, with the client's own accounts so there is no later handoff. Fulfillment: a 60-second daily check-in template plus a Loom walkthrough and documentation at delivery. Retention: over-deliver by about 5%, then use reciprocity to pitch the follow-up project immediately after the win. Free assets promised in the description: Upwork CV and proposal video templates, cold email formula and scripts, community post skeletons, a cold DM pitch, the sales script, the kickoff SOP, daily check-in variations, a delivery email template, and his Gumroad build library.

## Transferable to realtors?
- **Verdict:** ADAPTABLE
- **Why:** Three of the five pillars port almost cleanly to a real estate practice: the kickoff call SOP becomes the listing or buyer consultation, the daily or weekly check-in template becomes seller updates, and the over-deliver-then-ask move becomes the referral and review ask right after close. The marketing pillar does not port, Upwork and cold email to businesses have no realtor equivalent, so that pillar needs a full rebuild around sphere, expireds, FSBOs, and paid leads. The bigger transferable asset for The Leveraged Agent is the video's own business model: free asset pack in the description as the bribe, five-pillar spine, guarantee-backed community at the end.
