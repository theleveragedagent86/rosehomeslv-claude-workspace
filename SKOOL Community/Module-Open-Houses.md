# Module: Open Houses, The Leveraged Agent

## Module Overview

AI workflows for every stage of the open house: before it happens (promotion), during it (sign-in), and after it (lead follow-up). No module number in the tile display (matches how "Listing Launch Package" is styled). Ryan's call.

**The core promise:** No sign-in sheet ever sits on your desk untouched again. Photograph it, hand it to Claude, get a CRM entry and a full outreach plan back.

**Who this is for:** Any agent who holds open houses and doesn't have a reliable system for working the leads afterward.

**Status:** Five lessons defined so far. One is fully produced (the follow-up outreach lesson); the four promotion-stage lessons are scripted with full content stacks (classroom post, YouTube long-form package, and 5 Instagram Shorts each) but not shot yet. Day-of sign-in lessons are the next batch. Lessons are added stage by stage rather than planning the entire lineup up front.
