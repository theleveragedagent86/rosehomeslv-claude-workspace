How to Use the Open House Lead Reconnection Prompt

One prompt turns a forgotten open house sign-in sheet into a full, ready-to-send follow-up plan. It interviews you first so every message sounds like you, in your market, then writes the whole buyer-first sequence for you to copy and paste.

What you'll need

Claude or ChatGPTEither one works. The prompt is written to run on both.
A photo of your sign-in sheetSnap the open house feedback form on your phone. Handwriting is fine.
Two minutes to answerIt asks a few quick questions about you and the lead, then builds the plan.

The five steps

  1. Copy the prompt.

    Open the "Open House Lead Reconnection Prompt" file and copy everything inside the box.

  2. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT.

    Start a new chat, paste the prompt, and send it. Don't add anything else yet.

  3. Upload your open house form when it asks.

    Attach the photo of your sign-in sheet. It reads the name, number, ratings, and even your handwritten notes in the margins.

  4. Answer the quick interview.

    It asks a few things in small batches: your name and brokerage, your market, your CRM, your texting voice, and what contact info you have for the lead. Skip anything you want and it fills in a smart default.

  5. Get your plan and put it to work.

    You get a ready-to-send outreach sequence plus a CRM follow-up process. Send the first message, then load the sequence into your CRM.

What the prompt gives you back

Five deliverables, fully personalized

A
Lead ProfileWho they are, what they want, and the single best angle to reconnect.
B
Channel MatrixExactly which channels to use based on whether you have their phone, email, both, or neither.
C
The Reconnection CadenceAbout six touches over 30 days: texts, voicemail, note, and video, all written in your voice.
D
CRM Follow-Up ProcessLoads into your CRM, with branches for reply, no reply, and opt-out.
E
Buyer-to-Seller TransitionThe exact lines to open the selling conversation later, once rapport is back.

Three tips to get the best output

Talk, don't typeIf you're on Claude Code, use the mic. Saying it out loud gets more detail out than typing does.
Be honest about prior contactTell it if you already texted them and they replied. It changes the tone of the first message.
Trust buyer-firstYou met at an open house, so it leads with buying. The selling ask comes later, on purpose.
Why it works: the prompt is trained on four proven playbooks so you don't have to be. It borrows from Alex Hormozi (give value first), Jimmy Mackin (short pattern-interrupt texts), Jason Pantana (personal, consistent touches), and Tom Ferry (value-first, permission-based asks). Every message it writes is built on one of these, so it reads like a real person, not a mass blast.
