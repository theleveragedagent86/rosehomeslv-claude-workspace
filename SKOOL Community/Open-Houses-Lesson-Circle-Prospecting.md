## Post Body (paste under the video)

Your next listing is not on Zillow. It is on your current listing's street.

Every time you take a listing, you have a reason to call the whole neighborhood, and it is not cold calling, because you actually have news: a home is coming to the market on their street. That reason is everything. It is the difference between "hi, do you know anyone selling" (click) and "I'm about to bring a home on your street to market, and I'm letting the neighbors know first."

This is circle prospecting, and the top listing agents build their pipeline on it. Jimmy Mackin teaches a sharp version: use the short Coming Soon window to create genuine urgency before the house is even live. Tom Ferry has agents personally call and invite the neighbors around every listing. The tactic works. The reason most agents skip it is they do not know what to say and they are afraid of sounding like a telemarketer.

In this lesson you have AI write every script, so the only thing left to do is pick up the phone.

**What you'll build in this one:**

- Why one listing is a reason to call the entire street, and why that makes the call warm instead of cold
- The Coming Soon urgency angle (Mackin's short-window play)
- The prompt that writes call scripts for four angles: Coming Soon, Just Listed, Just Sold, and Open House invite
- The two questions every call should reach: who do they know who might move in, and have they thought about selling
- Objection handling for "not interested," "how did you get my number," and "we're not moving"
- A voicemail script for the majority who will not pick up
- How to knock out 25 calls in one focused block

A note before you dial: scrub your list against the Do Not Call registry, stick to legal calling hours, and follow your state's rules. Many agents pair these calls with the door-knock invite from the last lesson to reach the same neighborhood two ways.

**The reusable prompt** (copy this, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, fill in the blanks):

> You are my real estate prospecting coach. I just took a new listing and I want to circle prospect the neighborhood around it, calling the surrounding homes with the listing as my reason for the call. Write me the scripts so it feels warm and natural, not like a random cold call.
>
> Here is the listing:
> - Address or street and neighborhood:
> - Price and one standout feature:
> - Status and timing (coming soon, just listed, open house date, or just sold):
> - My name, brokerage, and phone:
>
> Write me:
> 1. A Coming Soon call script that uses the short exclusivity window to create genuine urgency (the way Jimmy Mackin teaches), and gets to two questions: who do they know who has thought about moving into the neighborhood, and have they thought about selling their own home
> 2. A Just Listed version and a Just Sold version of the same call
> 3. An Open House invite version
> 4. Objection handling for "I'm not interested," "how did you get my number," and "we're not planning to move"
> 5. A short voicemail script for the calls that do not get answered
>
> Keep every script short enough to say naturally, warm, and respectful. No pushy language. If you need anything before you write, ask me first.

**Example output** (the Coming Soon call and the voicemail it wrote):

> **Coming Soon call:** Hi, is this [Neighbor]? Hi, my name is [Name] with [Brokerage]. I'm calling because I'm about to bring a home on your street, [address], to the market in the next few days, and before it goes live I'm letting the neighbors know. Homes like this on your street do not come up often, and it usually gets people thinking about their own place. Quick question, do you know anyone who has talked about moving into the neighborhood? And have you ever thought about what your own home might be worth in this market?
>
> **Voicemail:** Hi [Neighbor], this is [Name] with [Brokerage]. I'm about to list a home on your street, [address], and I wanted to give the neighbors a heads up before it hits the market. If you are at all curious what that means for your own home's value, give me a call back at [phone]. Again, this is [Name] at [phone]. Thanks.

**Your turn:** next time you take a listing, run the prompt and block out one hour to call the street. Post in Wins & Results how many people you talked to and whether anyone said they had thought about selling. That is where your next listing usually comes from.
