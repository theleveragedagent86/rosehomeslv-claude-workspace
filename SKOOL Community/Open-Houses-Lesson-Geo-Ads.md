## Post Body (paste under the video)

If you have ever boosted an open house post and gotten nothing, it is not because Facebook ads do not work. It is because you boosted a post instead of running an ad.

There is a real way to do this, and the marketing coaches teach it. Jason Pantana runs geo-targeted Meta and YouTube ads to become the agent people see everywhere locally. Jimmy Mackin's team at Curaytor points paid traffic at a single-property landing page and brings in leads at a real cost per lead. Both of them work inside one rule most agents have never heard of.

Real estate ads have to run under Meta's Housing Special Ad Category. It exists for Fair Housing reasons, and it strips out detailed and demographic targeting. You cannot pick your audience by age, interests, or "who should live here." You target by location and a radius, and that is it. Which means your creative, the words and the video, has to do the heavy lifting.

That is exactly the part AI is good at. In this lesson you have AI write the full campaign, then you spend ten minutes setting it up in Ads Manager.

**What you'll build in this one:**

- Why a small geo-targeted ad beats another organic post nobody sees
- The rule you must respect: the Housing Special Ad Category, and why it limits you to location plus a radius
- The prompt that writes three ad copy variations, headlines, and descriptions
- A geo-targeting plan you can copy straight into Ads Manager
- A short video-ad script you film walking the house (Pantana's video-first play)
- Single-property landing-page copy so your ad has somewhere real to send people (the Mackin / Curaytor move)
- What a lead should roughly cost, using Curaytor's reported benchmark, so you know if it is working

**The reusable prompt** (copy this, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, fill in the blanks):

> You are my real estate ads manager. I want to run a paid ad for an open house on Facebook and Instagram, and I need the whole thing written and planned so I can set it up myself in Meta Ads Manager.
>
> Important rule to respect: real estate ads must run under Meta's Housing Special Ad Category, which removes detailed and demographic targeting. I can only target by location and a radius. So the copy and the offer have to do the work.
>
> Here is the listing:
> - Address or area, and the open house date and time:
> - Price and three things that make the home stand out:
> - My name, brokerage, and the link people should click (single-property page or my site):
>
> Give me:
> 1. Three versions of the ad primary text, short and scroll-stopping, each with one clear call to action
> 2. Three headline options and two description options to pair with them
> 3. A geo-targeting plan I can copy into Ads Manager: the location to center on and a sensible radius, with a note that under the Housing category I target by location only, not by who people are
> 4. A 20 to 30 second video-ad script I can film walking the house, hook first
> 5. Single-property landing-page copy the ad sends people to, with a simple form ask (name, email, phone)
>
> Keep it honest and specific to this house. No Fair Housing violations, and no language about who should live there. If you need anything before you write, ask me first.

**Example output** (one of the three ad variations plus the targeting plan):

> **Ad primary text:** New to the market and open this Saturday. Three bed, two bath in [Neighborhood], with the backyard everyone on the street wishes they had. Walk through it in person Saturday from 12 to 3, or tap below and I will send you the photos, the price, and the details. [link]
>
> **Headline:** Open Saturday in [Neighborhood]
> **Description:** See the home, the price, and the details.
>
> **Targeting plan:** Center on the listing's ZIP or city. Set a radius around it (Meta's minimum under the Housing category is 15 miles, so start there and widen only if your area is rural). Objective: Leads or Traffic to the landing page. Remember: under the Housing Special Ad Category you cannot narrow by age, gender, or interests. Location and radius only.

**Your turn:** run the prompt for your next open house, set up the ad with a small budget ($50 to $100), and point it at a landing page or your site. Post in Wins & Results what your cost per lead came in at. Next lesson we work the phones and turn the neighborhood around your listing into your next seller with circle prospecting.

## Notes for Ryan (not for the post)

- The Housing Special Ad Category is a real Meta policy: housing ads must use it, and it removes detailed and demographic targeting, leaving location plus a radius (Meta's minimum radius under housing has been 15 miles). Platform rules change, so verify the current policy and radius before you shoot, and keep the Fair Housing framing front and center.
- Coach attributions: Jason Pantana (geo-targeted Meta and YouTube ads, Housing category) and Jimmy Mackin / Curaytor (single-property landing page plus paid boost). Both accurate. The ~$10 per lead and the $500 over 10 days figures are Curaytor's own documented numbers; present cost-per-lead only as a benchmark, never as a guarantee.
- Do not teach or imply demographic targeting. The whole point of the Housing category is anti-discrimination; the lesson should reinforce that.
