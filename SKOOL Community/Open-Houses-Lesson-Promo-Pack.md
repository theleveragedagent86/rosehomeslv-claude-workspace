## Post Body (paste under the video)

Most agents promote an open house with one graphic and a prayer. "Open house Sunday, 12 to 3." Posted once, the day before, on one platform. Then they blame foot traffic.

The top listing agents do not do that. They treat a single listing like an asset and market it in phases: Coming Soon, Just Listed, then Open House, the way a studio rolls out a movie. That is Jimmy Mackin's whole philosophy. Jason Pantana teaches the same thing from the marketing side: go value-first and video-first, and be everywhere your local audience is. The idea was never the hard part. The time was. Building seven pieces of promo for every open house is an hour you do not have on a Thursday night.

So you post the one graphic and move on.

In this lesson you build the entire kit from one prompt in about five minutes, then spend a few more making it sound like you.

**What you'll build in this one:**

- Why one graphic is not a marketing plan, and what "market a listing like a movie" actually means (Mackin)
- The single prompt that turns your listing details into a full promotion kit
- The three social posts: a Coming Soon teaser, a Just Listed post, and an Open House event post with a real reason to show up
- A 5-line text-on-screen Reel script you can film in one walkthrough of the house (Pantana's video-first play)
- An email to your database, plus a short text blast for your sphere
- A Nextdoor post written for neighbors, not buyers
- How to make this a 10-minute routine you run before every open house

**The reusable prompt** (copy this, paste it into Claude or ChatGPT, fill in your listing):

> You are my real estate marketing team. I have an open house coming up and I want a full promotion kit, because right now I post one graphic and call it marketing.
>
> Here is the listing:
> - Address or area:
> - Price:
> - Three things that make this house special:
> - Open house date and time:
> - My name, brokerage, and how people reach me:
>
> Treat this listing like an asset I market in phases (Coming Soon, then Just Listed, then Open House), the way Jimmy Mackin teaches. Make it value-first and built for video, the way Jason Pantana teaches. Give me all of the following, each one ready to post:
>
> 1. A Coming Soon teaser post that builds curiosity without revealing the address
> 2. A Just Listed post for the day it goes live
> 3. An Open House event post with the date, time, and a real reason to stop by
> 4. A 5-line text-on-screen script for a walkthrough Reel, hook line first
> 5. A short email to my database inviting them and asking them to forward it to one person
> 6. A text blast under 300 characters for my past clients and sphere
> 7. A Nextdoor post written for the neighbors, not for buyers
>
> Rules: sound like a real person, not a brochure. Do not use the words "nestled," "boasts," or "stunning." Short sentences. One clear call to action per piece. If you need anything before you write, ask me first.

**Example output** (the event post and the Reel script it wrote off one listing):

> **Open House event post:** Open house this Saturday, 12 to 3, in [Neighborhood]. Three bed, two bath, with the backyard everyone else on the street wishes they had. Come walk it, ask me anything, grab a coffee. No pressure and no sign-in guilt, just come see it. [Address]. See you Saturday.
>
> **5-line Reel script (text on screen over your walkthrough):**
> 1. POV: you finally found the backyard
> 2. 3 bed / 2 bath in [Neighborhood]
> 3. the kitchen actually has counter space
> 4. open house Saturday, 12 to 3
> 5. come see it before it's gone

That is the pattern to look for: each piece has one job, it sounds like a person, and none of it reads like the same tired listing template every other agent posts.
