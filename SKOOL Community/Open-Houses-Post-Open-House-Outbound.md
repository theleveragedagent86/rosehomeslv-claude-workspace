**How to use the prompt:**
1. Download both files below: the Open House Lead Reconnection Prompt and the How to Use PDF.
2. Open the PDF first. It shows what you'll need: Claude or ChatGPT, a phone photo of your sign-in sheet, and about two minutes to answer questions.
3. Open the prompt file and copy everything inside the box.
4. Start a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT, paste it and send it. Don't add anything else yet.
5. Upload the photo of your sign-in sheet when it asks. Handwriting is fine.
6. Answer the quick interview: your name, brokerage, market, CRM, texting voice and what contact info you have for the lead.
7. Get your plan. Send the first message, then load the rest of the sequence into your CRM.

No Claude Code needed. It works in regular Claude chat or ChatGPT.

#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

🎙️ **Talk Your Prompt Instead of Typing It** by using the microphone button in Claude Code, so you get every thought out instead of filtering it down to one tidy sentence.

🧭 **Plan Before Claude Touches Anything** by switching into Plan Mode and telling Claude to ask you questions instead of guessing, so the plan is built on facts and not assumptions.

📸 **Read a Handwritten Sign-In Sheet in Seconds** by handing Claude a phone photo of the form, so it pulls the name, number, email and agent status and flags anything it can't read.

🏡 **Lead With the Buyer Conversation** by opening on what they want to buy and only moving to "do you need to sell first?" once rapport is back, because the relationship started at an open house.

📲 **Get a Full Touch Sequence Off One Sheet** covering the day-zero text, a voicemail script, follow-up texts, a value touch, a personal video and a handwritten note, so nobody sits on your desk again.

⚡ **Turn It Into a Skill That Runs Every Time** so the next open house is one photo and one command, and the contacts land in your CRM without you typing a single name.

#### In this video, Ryan covers:

The problem is the stack of open house sign-in sheets sitting on every agent's desk. Real names, real phone numbers, real people who walked through a house, and nobody ever follows up because typing them into the CRM one by one takes forever. The sheets I use in this video came from a coworker who never touched them.

The system is **the Open House Reconnect**, a Plan Mode prompt that reads the sheet, builds a cold reconnect plan grounded in Jimmy Mackin, Jason Pantana, Tom Ferry, Alex Hormozi and Sharran Srivatsaa style outreach, and leads with the buy side before it ever brings up selling.

The practical steps are taking a photo of the sheet, turning on Plan Mode, talking the prompt in with the microphone, telling Claude to ask questions instead of inferring, reviewing the day-zero text and full touch sequence it builds, and then turning the whole thing into a skill that adds each contact to your CRM for you.

#### Who this is for:

- **Agents holding open houses regularly** who have a pile of sign-in sheets they never followed up on and want every one of them worked without the data entry.

- **Team leads and agents with a backlog** who want one repeatable process that turns any stack of old sheets into CRM contacts and a real outreach plan.
