### SHORT 2: THE QUICK WIN

**Script:**
Here is how to make prospecting calls stop feeling like cold calls. You need a reason for the call, and a new listing is the best one there is. Instead of, hi, do you know anyone selling, you say, I'm about to bring a home on your street to the market, and I'm letting the neighbors know first. Now you are the messenger, not a telemarketer. Then you get to two questions. Who do you know that has thought about moving into the neighborhood, and have you thought about what your own home is worth right now. That is the whole play. If you do not know how to word it, hand AI your listing details and ask for a warm Coming Soon call script. Try it and comment how many calls you made this week.

### SHORT 3: THE BEFORE/AFTER

**Script:**
Here is the call most agents are scared to make. Uh, hi, I'm a realtor, do you know anyone thinking about selling? Click. Of course they hang up. You gave them no reason to care. Now here is the same call with a reason. Hi, I'm calling because I'm about to bring a home on your street to the market in the next few days, and I'm letting the neighbors know before it goes live. Homes like this do not come up often here. Do you know anyone who has thought about moving into the neighborhood? Completely different call. One is a cold pitch. The other is news about their street. That reframe is the entire game, and AI writes the script for your exact listing in a minute. I teach it inside The Leveraged Agent. Link in bio.

### SHORT 5: THE MATH

**Script:**
Let's do the math on circle prospecting. You take one listing. You call fifty neighbors around it over a couple of focused hours. Most will not answer, so you leave a voicemail. Of the fifteen or so who pick up, a few will know someone thinking about moving, and one or two will admit they have thought about selling themselves. That is one listing turning into your next conversation, and sometimes your next listing, without spending a dime on leads.
