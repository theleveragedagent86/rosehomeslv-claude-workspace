### SHORT 2: THE QUICK WIN

**Script:**
Here is the one question that turns door-knocking from awkward to profitable. When you invite a neighbor to your open house, before you leave, ask this. Who do you know that has talked about moving into the neighborhood? It feels friendly, not salesy, and it gets people naming names. Then, if it fits, add the soft one. Have you had any thoughts about selling your own place in the next year? That second question is how an open house invite turns into a listing appointment. This is straight out of Tom Ferry's playbook. And if you do not know how to word your version, tell AI the neighborhood and the listing and ask it to write you a natural four-step invite. Try it this week and comment how many doors you knocked.

### SHORT 3: THE BEFORE/AFTER

**Script:**
Here is a nervous agent knocking a door before an open house. Um, hi, sorry to bother you, I'm having an open house down the street, if you want to come, no pressure, okay bye. We have all done it. Now here is the same knock with a real script. Hi, I'm Ryan with my brokerage. I'm hosting an open house Saturday just down the street, and I promised the owner I'd personally invite the neighbors. Who do you know that's thought about moving into the area? And have you had any thoughts about selling yourself? One of those knocks fills a room and finds a seller. The other one wastes your Saturday. The difference is a script, and AI will write yours for the exact neighborhood in about a minute. I show you how inside The Leveraged Agent. Link in bio.
