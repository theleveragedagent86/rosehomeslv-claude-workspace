### SHORT 2: THE QUICK WIN

**Script:**
Here is a 30-second fix for your real estate ads. If you are running any ad about a listing or an open house, you have to set the Special Ad Category to Housing. It is not optional, it is Meta's rule for real estate, and if you skip it your ad can get shut off. But here is the catch. Housing category strips out most of your targeting. You cannot pick an audience by age or interests, only by location and a radius. So the copy has to do the heavy lifting. Open ChatGPT or Claude and ask it to write you three versions of an open house ad, short, scroll-stopping, one clear call to action each. Pick the best one. Try it and comment if you have been missing that Housing setting this whole time.
