### SHORT 2: THE QUICK WIN

**Script:**
Here is something you can do before your next open house that almost nobody does. Three days out, post a Coming Soon teaser. Not the full listing. Just a hint. "Something special is about to hit the market in this neighborhood. Open house this weekend." It builds curiosity before the house is even live. Jimmy Mackin calls this treating your listing like an asset, you market it in phases instead of dumping everything the day before. And you do not have to write it. Open ChatGPT or Claude and say, "Write me a Coming Soon teaser for an open house this weekend in my area, keep it short, build curiosity, do not reveal the address." Ten seconds. Post it. Try it this week, then drop a comment and tell me if your Coming Soon post beat your usual open house graphic.

### SHORT 5: THE MATH

Writing a Coming Soon post, a Just Listed post, an event post, a Reel script, a database email, a text blast, and a Nextdoor post from scratch, thoughtfully, that is easily an hour per listing. Most agents do not have that hour, so they post one graphic and move on. With one prompt, that same seven-piece kit takes about five minutes to generate and another five to personalize and schedule. So call it ten minutes instead of sixty. Across twenty open houses a year, that is over sixteen hours back. But here is the real math. One graphic might get you three people through the door. A full promotion sequence might get you fifteen. More bodies means more sign-ins, means more follow-up, means more deals. The ten minutes is not the win. The extra people are.
