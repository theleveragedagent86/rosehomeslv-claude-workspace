# 24-Reel Posting Schedule (two slots a day, sets swap every 3 days)

Start 2026-09-27. Posting days are Mon, Tue, Wed, Sat, Sun. Thu and Fri are skipped, weakest buckets.

Slot 1 = 5:00am PST (East Coast 8am scroll, schedule the night before).
Slot 2 = that day's evening peak: Mon 5:15pm, Tue 6:15pm, Wed 6:15pm, Sat 5:15pm, Sun 6:15pm PST.

Both series run every day, so no follower gets three straight weeks of one topic. Which series takes the
morning slot flips every 3 posting days, so neither one always sits in the weaker 5am bucket.

W = Weekly-Seller-Update-Reel-NN (black cover). OH = Open-House-Reel-NN (white cover).
The grid alternates black/white in posting order on its own, no cover re-render needed for reordering.

## YouTube Shorts (same Reels, same days, different clock)

Original 24-slot pairing (W 01-03 included, since they were never on YouTube), but the morning Reel goes up at **9:00am PT** and the
evening Reel at **2:00pm PT**. Why, from [YOUTUBE-POSTING-TIMES-RESEARCH.md](YOUTUBE-POSTING-TIMES-RESEARCH.md):
Shorts posted 3 to 7am PT did worst (0.87x the channel's median views), 7am to 2pm is baseline and
2 to 5pm was best with a real sample (1.10x). Times are in yt_manifest.json.
