# When to post YouTube Shorts for agents (research, 2026-09-25)

**Answer: weekdays and weekends both fine, post between 9am and 2pm PT, never before 7am.**
YouTube timing matters far less than Instagram. The whole spread is roughly plus or minus 15% of normal.

## Method

- 11 agent-coach channels: Tom Ferry, Jimmy Mackin, Jason Pantana, Mike Sherrard, Kevin Ward,
  Ricky Carruth, Brian Icenhower, Byron Lazine, Lab Coat Agents, Knolly Williams, Ryan Serhant.
- Their latest 30 long-form and 30 Shorts each (628 uploads), exact upload timestamps from YouTube,
  486 inside the last 12 months. Converted to Pacific time.
- Performance: each upload's views divided by that channel's median for that format, so big and small
  channels count equally. Median of those ratios per time window.
- Comments: YouTube only shows "N hours ago" for comments under 24 hours old, then "N days ago".
  Only 26 hour-precise comments came back, too few to use for hours. 293 day-level comments used for days.

## What the coaches do

- Long-form: 7am PT is the single most common upload hour (54 of 241), almost all 5am to 11am.
  Mon (68) and Thu (89) are their days. Almost nothing on weekends.
- Shorts: again 7am PT (53 of 245), spread 4am to 2pm. Weekdays, very little Sat/Sun.
- Channel habits: Mike Sherrard 5am, Tom Ferry 7am long-form / 11am Shorts, Kevin Ward and
  Icenhower 7am, Ryan Serhant Shorts 9am, Byron Lazine long-form 11am.

## What actually performed (views index, 1.00 = that channel's normal)

| Upload window PT | Shorts | Long-form |
|---|---|---|
| 3 to 7am | 0.87 (n=57) | 0.92 (n=35) |
| 7 to 11am | 1.02 (n=106) | 1.00 (n=122) |
| 11am to 2pm | 0.99 (n=51) | 0.99 (n=46) |
| 2 to 5pm | 1.10 (n=21) | 1.18 (n=28) |
| 5 to 9pm | 1.18 (n=6, thin) | 0.94 (n=9) |

Shorts by day: Tue 1.14, Fri 1.05, Wed 1.01, Mon 0.99, Sat 0.99, Thu 0.97, Sun 0.92 (n=7).
Long-form by day: Fri is the one clear loser at 0.63; Mon to Thu all about 1.0.

Comment activity by day (approx): Mon 78, Wed 64, Thu 57, Tue 49, Sat 20, Fri 16, Sun 9.

## Takeaways

1. The pre-dawn slot that works on Instagram (East Coast scroll) is the worst slot on YouTube.
2. Posting in the afternoon, after the coaches' 7am wave, did slightly better, likely less competition.
3. Shorts keep getting pushed for days, so the day of week barely matters for them. For long-form
   videos, publish Mon to Thu and skip Friday.
