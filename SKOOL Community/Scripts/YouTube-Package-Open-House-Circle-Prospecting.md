## Description

### First 2 lines (visible above "Show More")

Your next listing is not on Zillow. It is on your current listing's street. Here is how I use AI to write the circle-prospecting scripts that turn one listing into my next three.

### Body

Every time you take a listing, you have a reason to call the whole neighborhood, and that reason is what makes it warm instead of cold. You are not asking strangers if they want to sell. You are telling the neighbors that a home is coming to the market on their street. That is news about their biggest asset, and it is a welcome call.

The top listing agents build pipeline this way. Jimmy Mackin teaches a sharp version of it: use the short Coming Soon window to create genuine urgency before the house is even live. Tom Ferry has agents personally call and invite the neighbors around every listing. The tactic works. The reason most agents skip it is they do not know what to say and they are scared of sounding like a telemarketer.

In this video I hand a listing to AI and get every script back: a Coming Soon call, a Just Listed call, a Just Sold call, an Open House invite, objection handling for the pushback you will hear, and a voicemail for the majority who will not pick up. Then I talk through calling the street the right way, including scrubbing your list against the Do Not Call registry and staying inside legal calling hours.

If you want the prompts and the full open house system, that is what I teach inside The Leveraged Agent, my Skool community for real estate agents.
