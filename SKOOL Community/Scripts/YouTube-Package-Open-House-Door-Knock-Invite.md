## Description

### First 2 lines (visible above "Show More")

The best traffic at your open house lives on the listing's street, not on Zillow. Here is how I use AI to write the exact door-knock invite that fills the room and finds my next seller.

### Body

Top agents do not wait for buyers to find their open house. They knock the neighbors' doors and personally invite them. Tom Ferry teaches a 4-step version of this: introduce yourself and the open house, ask who they know who might want to move into the neighborhood, reiterate the invite, and softly ask whether they have thought about selling their own home. That last question is how an open house invite quietly becomes a listing appointment. Andy Tse, one of the highest-volume agents in the country, works the street in person the same way.

Most agents skip all of it for one reason: they freeze at the door because they do not know what to say. That is a script problem, not a courage problem.

In this video I hand AI the listing and the neighborhood and have it write the whole thing: a short, natural invite I can say from memory, a door-hanger card for the houses where nobody answers, and a follow-up text for the neighbors who give me their number. Then I practice it so it sounds like a person, not a pitch.

If you want the prompts and the full open house system, that is what I teach inside The Leveraged Agent, my Skool community for real estate agents.
