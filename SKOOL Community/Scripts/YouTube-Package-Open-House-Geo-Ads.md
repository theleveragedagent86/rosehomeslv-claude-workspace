## Description

### First 2 lines (visible above "Show More")

Boosted an open house post and got nothing? The problem is you boosted a post instead of running an ad. Here is how I write a full geo-targeted open house campaign with AI in about ten minutes.

### Body

Real estate ads have to run under Meta's Housing Special Ad Category. It exists for Fair Housing reasons, and it removes detailed and demographic targeting, so you can only target by location and a radius. That is why a lazy boost gets you likes from other agents and zero real leads: nothing about the setup is built to bring in buyers.

The marketing coaches work inside that rule. Jason Pantana runs geo-targeted Meta and YouTube ads to become the agent people see everywhere in their market. Jimmy Mackin's team at Curaytor sends paid traffic to a single-property landing page and brings in leads at a real cost per lead. The common thread is that the creative does the work, because the targeting cannot.

In this video I hand a listing to AI and have it write the whole campaign: three ad copy variations, headlines and descriptions, a location-based targeting plan I can paste into Ads Manager, a short video-ad script I film walking the house, and the landing-page copy the ad points to. Then I set it up with a small budget and show you what a lead should roughly cost, using Curaytor's reported benchmark as a sanity check.

If you want the prompts and the full open house system, that is what I teach inside The Leveraged Agent, my Skool community for real estate agents.
