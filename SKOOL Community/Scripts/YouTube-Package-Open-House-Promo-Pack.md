## Description

### First 2 lines (visible above "Show More")

Most agents promote an open house with one graphic the day before, then wonder why nobody shows up. Here is how I build a full week of open house promotion from a single AI prompt in about five minutes.

### Body

The top listing agents do not market an open house with one post. They treat the listing like an asset and roll it out in phases, Coming Soon, then Just Listed, then Open House, the way a studio markets a movie. That is Jimmy Mackin's philosophy, and Jason Pantana teaches the marketing side of it: go value-first, build for video, and be everywhere your local audience already is.

The catch was always time. Writing a Coming Soon teaser, a Just Listed post, an event post, a Reel script, a database email, a text blast, and a Nextdoor post from scratch is an hour per listing, so most agents skip it and post the one graphic.

In this video I hand a single listing to AI with one prompt and get the entire kit back: seven ready-to-post pieces, each written to sound like a person instead of a brochure. Then I walk through personalizing it, filming the Reel in one pass through the house, and scheduling it all so your next open house gets promoted like a top team runs it, without a marketing team.

If you want the exact prompts and the full open house system, that is what I teach inside The Leveraged Agent, my Skool community for real estate agents.
