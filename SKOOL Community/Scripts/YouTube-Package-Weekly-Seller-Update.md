## Description

### First 2 lines (visible above "Show More")

Sellers don't fire their agent over price. They fire them over silence. Here's the weekly seller update I built in Claude Code that pulls the numbers, writes the report and drafts the email for me.

### Body

Every listing agent promises weekly updates. Most stop after a few weeks because pulling views from Zillow, Redfin, Homes.com and Realtor.com, collecting showing feedback and writing it all up takes too long. So the seller hears nothing, and silence is what gets agents fired.

In this video I walk through the system I use on my own listings, built on a real one: 29 Amber Rock St in Henderson. By the first 30 days it had 21 showings and 5,500+ online views, and the seller saw every number, every week.

Here's what it produces each week from one command:
- A branded seller report: online views by site, a showings summary, marketing activity and a plain-English summary of the week
- CRM-ready copy for the listing's landing page
- A seller email with the link, sent every Friday

I also show how I publish it as a private landing page in Lofty in about a minute, and why Claude should be asking you questions, not the other way around.

The full setup, the Plan Mode prompt that interviews you and builds this for your own listings, lives inside The Leveraged Agent, my Skool community for real estate agents. Link below.
