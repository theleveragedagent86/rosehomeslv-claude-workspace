### 2. Lesson 5 Short 2 The Quick Win
Your Instagram post and your Facebook post should not be the same caption.

Instagram: hook in the first line. That sentence is the only thing people see before "more", so it has to stop the scroll.

Facebook: lead with lifestyle and neighborhood. Nobody is searching Facebook for square footage.

Same listing. Two different posts. Two different engagement levels.
