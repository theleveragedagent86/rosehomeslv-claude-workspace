**How to use the prompt:**
1. Download both files below: the prompt (weekly-seller-update-plan-prompt.md) and the How to Use PDF.
2. Open the PDF first. It shows what you'll need before you start: your listing address, MLS number, the seller's email, and your Zillow, CRM, Meta Ads and showing service logins open in Chrome.
3. Open Claude Code inside your Claude folder.
4. Switch to Plan Mode. Press Shift+Tab until the bottom of the screen says "plan mode."
5. Open the prompt file, copy everything in it, paste it into Claude Code and hit Enter.
6. Answer Claude's questions. It asks in 10 short rounds. If you don't know an answer, say so and it marks it "NOT FOUND" instead of guessing.
7. Read the plan, approve it, and let Claude build it.
8. Every week after that, type /weekly-update plus the property address.

The first run takes about 20 minutes. Every week after that is about 5.

#### Hey, in this video, you're gonna learn:

📊 **Build a Weekly Seller Report That Writes Itself** by having Claude Code assemble your headshot, the hero photo, an executive summary, online views, showing feedback and listing activities into one branded page, so your seller gets a real update every Friday without you writing it.

🎯 **Pick the Tone Before Claude Writes a Word** by choosing momentum, hold steady, subtle repositioning or custom each week, so the summary sets up the conversation you actually need to have, including the price talk, without ever saying "reduce" in writing.

📈 **Pull Every Portal's Views Into One Number** by pointing Claude at your listing's Zillow, Redfin, Homes.com and Realtor.com pages plus your CRM landing page, so the seller sees total exposure instead of guessing.

💬 **Turn Scattered Showing Feedback Into One Honest Summary** by feeding Claude what came in through ShowingTime, texts and calls, so buyer comments reach the seller in plain words and in the tone you picked.

📬 **Show the Seller the Work You're Doing** by listing mailers sent, Meta ad impressions and reverse prospecting reach as three simple cards, so they stop wondering what you're doing to sell the house.

🗺️ **Build the Whole System in Plan Mode First** by talking your prompt out and reading the plan before approving it, so Claude asks you questions instead of guessing and gets it right the first time.

⚡ **Run It Every Week With One Command** by saving it as a `/weekly-update` skill that takes the property address, so each new report is one line, one paste into your CRM and one email draft.

#### In this video, Ryan covers:

Sellers fire agents over silence, not over price. You promised at the listing appointment that you'd keep them in the loop, then week three hits, showings slow down, and the only update they get is the one they had to ask for. Most agents know they should send a weekly report. Almost nobody does it, because pulling views from four portals, chasing showing feedback and writing it up nicely takes an hour a listing.

The system is **the Weekly Seller Update**, a "don't fire me" report that Claude Code builds for you every Friday. One branded page with your face and the property up top, an executive summary written in the tone you choose, total online views by portal, a plain-English showing feedback summary, and the listing activities you've done. It goes out with a short email and a link to the page.

The practical steps are opening Claude Code in your Claude folder, switching to Plan Mode, talking out the prompt (or pasting the one attached below), and reading the plan so you know which skill files, report template and page layout it will build. You add your brand colors, approve it, and it saves as a `/weekly-update` skill. From then on you type `/weekly-update` plus the address, paste the HTML into a Lofty landing page, publish, and the seller email is ready to go.

#### Who this is for:

- **Listing agents with one to ten active listings** who promised weekly communication at the listing appointment and need it to happen every Friday without losing an hour per listing.

- **Team leads and listing coordinators** who want every seller on the team getting the same professional update, built the same way, no matter which agent owns the listing.
