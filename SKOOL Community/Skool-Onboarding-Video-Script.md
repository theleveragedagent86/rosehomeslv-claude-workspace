# Skool onboarding video, 60 second script

Plays once, right after someone joins. Skool's own guidance on that field: "Videos under 2 mins work best."
This is NOT the About page VSL. The VSL sells the join. This plays after they already joined, so it does
one job: get them to the pinned thread and get one sentence out of them.

**Setup:** phone, vertical or horizontal both fine, eye level, window in front of you. One take. If you
flub a word keep going, this is not a produced asset. Do not add an intro sting, do not add music.

---

## The read

Hey, you're in. I'm Ryan.

Quick on me: I'm a licensed agent in Las Vegas, I still sell houses, and I build AI systems to do the
parts of the job I hate. Listing launches, MLS input, transaction follow up, marketing. That's what
gets taught here. Everything I show you is something I actually run in my own business.

Here's the only thing I want you to do right now.

Go to the pinned post on the community page. There's a thread in there. Drop a comment with the one
task in your week you'd hand off if you could. Not a big vision thing. The boring one. The thing you
do every single time and hate every single time.

That's it, one comment.

I read all of them, and the ones that keep coming up are what I build next and walk through on camera.
So the faster you tell me yours, the faster it turns into a system you can use.

Go post it. I'll see you in there.

---

**Word count:** 189. At a normal on-camera 165 to 175 wpm that is 65 to 68 seconds. If you want it
tighter, cut the "Not a big vision thing / the boring one" pair, which takes it to 172 words and about
60 seconds.

## Why it's built this way

- **No re-sell.** They already bought. Selling again after the yes is the most common onboarding mistake.
- **One instruction, repeated three times.** Go to the thread, post the one task, go post it.
- **Lowers the ask to a comment, not a post.** Matches the system in `Skool-Welcome-DM.md`: ask ONE
  question, never ask for a post cold, never say "pain point".
- **Names the payoff.** Their comment turns into the thing that gets built. That's the whole flywheel.
- **Credibility in one sentence,** because a stranger who just joined does not need a bio.
