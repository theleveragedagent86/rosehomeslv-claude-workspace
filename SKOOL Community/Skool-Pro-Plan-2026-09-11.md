## The plan

Four phases. The order matters more than the contents. Do not jump to phase 3.

### Phase 1, today, roughly 40 minutes, no recording required

The goal is to stop selling something you cannot deliver, and to stop the manual welcome work.

1. **Decide Premium.** Two options, pick one now.
   - **A. Close the paid tiers.** Switch Pricing back to Free, or use "Close doors" on the paid
     tiers, until the classroom can back Premium up. Zero risk, zero revenue, and the honest
     position while Level 0 is unrecorded.
   - **B. Rewrite the Premium bullets down to what actually exists today.** Bullets become the
     Open Houses module, the Listing Launch Package once published, and direct answers in Q&A.
     Then publish Listing Launch Package out of Draft so there are two things behind the
     paywall instead of one.

   **Recommendation: B, but only if Listing Launch Package ships out of Draft in the same
   sitting.** If it is not ready to publish today, do A today and B when it is.

2. **Decide VIP.** At $1,500/m with Level 0 unrecorded, toggle it off until you have the
   capacity to serve one. It is one switch and it is reversible.

3. **Turn on Auto DM new members** and load the Group A message you already wrote in
   [Skool-Welcome-DM.md](Skool-Welcome-DM.md). The question-first opener is already proven in
   your own DMs. This ends the hand-sending, and it means Charlotte, Christopher, and Amanda
   are the last three people who arrive to silence.

4. **Turn on Instant approval.** With Auto DM live and a free Standard tier, manual approval
   only adds delay between the click and the message.

5. **Turn on "New customer email"** in Notifications. If somebody buys Premium you need to
   know in the hour, not at the next payout.

6. **Turn on Links** and put three in the sidebar: the YouTube channel, the Instagram, and the
   pinned task thread.

7. **Claim the custom URL.** Try `skool.com/leveraged-agent` or `skool.com/theleveragedagent`.
   The `-7674` suffix is the first thing that reads as unfinished on every link you share, and
   it is on every carousel and Reel caption in the funnel that is about to go out.

8. **Fix the three em-dashes** on the About page and the Open Houses course description.

9. **Replace or confirm the onboarding video.** If the loaded clip is Skool's stock demo, cut a
   sixty second phone video pointing at the pinned thread. Sixty seconds, one take, your face.
   It runs for every new member from now on.

### Phase 2, this week, no recording required

11. **Read all 15 membership-question answers.** Write them into one list next to the two
