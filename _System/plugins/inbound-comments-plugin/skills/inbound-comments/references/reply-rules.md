## Rule 1: Never claim someone was on a deal unless you verified it

Comments like "great teamwork", "congrats on the close", "loved working with you" imply the commenter was a participant. Do NOT affirm that unless you confirm it:
- Identify the property from the reel caption.
- Read the matching `/Users/ryanrose/Downloads/Claude/Rose Homes LV/Clients/Transactions/*/transaction.json` and check the participant names and companies (lender, escrow, the other agent, builder rep).
- Match only if the commenter's display name or business clearly lines up with a participant on THAT deal. Records store names and companies, not Instagram handles, so judge by name/business; if it is not a clear match, treat it as NOT verified.
- Verified -> warm collegial reply ("Appreciate you, great working with you on this one!").
- Not verified -> neutral thanks, no relationship claimed ("Appreciate it! 🙌").

## Rule 2: Send leads and help-requests to DMs

If a comment needs a realtor or asks for help ("are you an agent? I might need help", "where do I apply", "looking to buy/sell", "can you help"), invite them to DM Ryan directly, because public comments get buried:
- "Are you an agent? I might need your help." -> "I am! Shoot me a DM so it does not get lost in the comments and I will make sure you are taken care of."

Also append the lead to `leads.md` in the shared folder so Ryan can follow up personally.
