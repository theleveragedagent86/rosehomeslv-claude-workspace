# New-Follower DM Templates

Rotate these, never the same one twice in a row. Fill `[Name]` with the follower's first name; if there is no usable personal first name (missing, business name, all emoji), use a **no-name** variant. One emoji max. **No em-dashes.**

**Base (Ryan's original):** "Hi [Name], thanks for following me! My name is Ryan Rose and I am a local Real Estate agent in Las Vegas. I love connecting with my new followers and always have people reaching out with questions about their upcoming move...are you thinking about moving in the next year?"

## Variants (with name)

1. Hi [Name], thanks for following me! My name is Ryan Rose and I am a local real estate agent here in Las Vegas. I love connecting with new followers, and I always have people reaching out with questions about their upcoming move. Are you thinking about moving in the next year?

2. Hey [Name], thanks so much for the follow! I'm Ryan Rose, a local Vegas real estate agent. I really enjoy getting to know new followers, and a lot of folks here are weighing a move. Any chance you're thinking about one in the next year or so?

3. Hi [Name], appreciate you following along! Ryan Rose here, I help people buy and sell around the Las Vegas valley. Connecting with new followers is one of my favorite things. Are you possibly looking to make a move sometime in the next year?

4. [Name], thanks for the follow! I'm Ryan, a Las Vegas real estate agent. I always like to say hi to new followers, since so many end up having questions about a future move. Is a move on your radar at all for the next year?

5. Hey [Name], glad to have you following! My name is Ryan Rose and I do real estate here in Las Vegas. Getting to know new followers is the best part. Are you thinking about buying or selling in the next year?

## When they reply

These ask a question on purpose. When someone replies, that is a live lead, do NOT auto-converse. Flag the thread in `leads.md` in the shared folder so Ryan handles it personally.
