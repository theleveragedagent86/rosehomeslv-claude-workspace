# Liker DM Templates

Rotate these, never the same one twice in a row. Fill `[Name]` with the liker's first name; if there is no usable personal first name, use a **no-name** variant. The 🙌 is fine (one emoji max). **No em-dashes.**

**Base (Ryan's original):** "Hi [Name], noticed you liked one of my recent posts! Just wanted to say thanks for the support 🙌. By the way, any big plans for the future as far as real estate goes? Thinking about making a move in the next year?"

## Variants (with name)

1. Hi [Name], noticed you liked one of my recent posts! Just wanted to say thanks for the support 🙌 By the way, any big plans on the real estate front? Thinking about making a move in the next year?

2. Hey [Name], saw you liked one of my recent reels, appreciate the support! 🙌 Quick question while I have you, any real estate plans on the horizon? Maybe a move in the next year?

3. Hi [Name], thanks for liking my recent post, always means a lot 🙌 Curious, do you have anything coming up real estate wise? Thinking about moving in the next year or so?

4. [Name], caught that you liked one of my recent videos, thank you! 🙌 Any chance real estate is on your mind lately? Maybe a move in the next year?

5. Hey [Name], appreciate you liking my recent post! 🙌 By the way, any big plans real estate wise coming up? Possibly a move within the next year?

## When they reply

When someone replies, that is a live lead, do NOT auto-converse. Flag the thread in `leads.md` in the shared folder so Ryan handles it personally.
