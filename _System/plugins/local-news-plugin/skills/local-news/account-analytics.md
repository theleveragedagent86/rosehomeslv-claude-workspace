# @rosehomeslv Account Analytics

Verified performance data for Ryan Rose's Instagram account. This is the evidence base for every posting time, day, and trial reel decision the skill makes. Do not substitute generic social media best practice for the numbers in this file.

**Snapshot pulled:** 2026-09-08, from the Instagram professional dashboard and the follower active times charts, timezone PDT.

---

## Refresh policy

Re-pull this data roughly every 90 days, or sooner if follower count moves more than about 25 percent. When you refresh it, update the snapshot date above and revise the derived guidance below to match. Guidance that no longer matches the numbers is worse than no guidance.

Two fields cannot be read from Instagram's web dashboard and can only be pulled from the mobile app:
- Exact publish time of each reel
- Reel duration and watch time or retention

Both are marked NOT VERIFIED below. Do not infer them.

**Follower versus non-follower split**
- Views: 10.5 percent followers, 89.5 percent non-followers
- Interactions: 45.3 percent followers, 54.7 percent non-followers

This split is the single most important number in this file. Distribution to non-followers is already excellent, so reach expansion is not the account's bottleneck. At the same time the 10.5 percent follower base produces 45.3 percent of all interactions, so followers are disproportionately valuable per person. Anything that spends a slot without reaching followers has a high hidden cost.

## Marked NOT VERIFIED

Never invent values for these. If a decision depends on one of them, say so and make the decision on other grounds.

- Exact publish time of any individual reel
- Reel duration, watch time, retention curves
- Any trial reel metric. Trial reels do not appear on the profile grid or in the web dashboard at all.
- Topics for reels ranked 13 through 20
- Per-day-of-week active time curves, as opposed to the aggregate buckets above
