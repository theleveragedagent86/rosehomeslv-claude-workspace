# The strategy brief

Answer these **in writing, before designing anything**. The answers go in
`brief.md` next to the spec. A thumbnail built without them is decoration.

## The five questions

1. **What is the video's real argument?**
   Not the topic. The claim. "New construction in Las Vegas" is a topic.
   "Builders price the upgrades so the base price is a lie" is an argument.

2. **What should the viewer feel in the first half-second?**
   Pick one: caught out, reassured, curious, warned, greedy. One. Feelings do not stack.

3. **What visual evidence proves it?**
   A number, a place, a before/after, a document. If there is no evidence, the video is
   an opinion piece and the thumbnail should sell the person, not the claim.

4. **What image would work with no text at all?**
   If the answer is "none", the plate is wrong. Fix the plate before writing copy.

5. **What expression does that emotion wear?**
   Write it as a physical description, not a mood word: "brows drawn together, mouth
   closed, head tilted forward" beats "concerned". The physical description is what gets
   pasted into the Higgsfield prompt.

## The competitive check

Search YouTube for the query the video is targeting. Look at the top 6 thumbnails that
actually rank, and write down:

- **The dominant colour.** If they are all one temperature, go the other way.
- **The dominant layout.** If they are all face-right with big text, a `big-number` or
  `vs-split` will break the pattern.
- **What every one of them is claiming.** The gap in that list is the video worth making.

Record the answer as three lines in `brief.md`. This is the step that makes the
difference between a thumbnail that is well made and one that gets clicked, and it is the
step that gets skipped.

## Titles ship with the thumbnail

Produce **2–3 titles engineered to pair with the finished thumbnail**, never separately.
The pairing rule: the thumbnail states the emotional claim, the title supplies the
specific and the search terms.

- Thumbnail: `THE NEW BUILD TRAP` → Title: "The New Construction Trap Nobody Warns Las Vegas Buyers About"
- Thumbnail: `$38K LOST` → Title: "How Las Vegas New Build Buyers Lose $38,000 Without Noticing"

If the title and thumbnail say the same words, one of them is wasted.
