Patterns in the data

What the numbers show

  1. HOA dues vary enormously and are not reflected in asking price. Twenty-three of the 102 carry an HOA; the median is $875 a month. Fourteen are at $800 a month or higher. Those fourteen have a median in-place cap of 3.15 percent against 3.80 percent for the no-HOA buildings in the same $500,000 to $700,000 asking range. At the extremes: 2201 James Bilbray Drive carries $1,884 a month and returns 1.68 percent; 2732 Alicialynn Way carries $975 a month and produces a negative NOI on its collected rent.
  2. Six listings report no collected rent at all. 2527 Carroll Street, 2529 Valley Street, 736 North 10th Street, 728 North 11th Street, 732 North 11th Street, and 3608 East Cheyenne Avenue. Four of those are in 89101, listed 203 days, three of them at exactly $498,000. With no rent there is no cap rate, no GRM, and no debt-service break-even to compute.
  3. Four identical buildings share one asking price. 4771, 4781, 4791 and 4851 East Craig Road are all listed at exactly $815,000, with in-place caps of 4.23, 4.31, 4.22 and 4.45 percent. Collected rents run $4,780 to $4,980 a month.
  4. The two most expensive listings return less than the median. 1153 and 1165 Sierra Vista Drive are asking $1,310,000 each, the highest on the list, with in-place caps of 3.84 and 3.96 percent against a median of 3.79 percent.
  5. Advertised income and unit-level rent lines frequently disagree. Where the two conflicted I used the lower figure. Six buildings needed a further correction from language in their own remarks. 905 East Bonanza Road advertises $43,200 of annual gross income while its remarks state that current monthly rent collected is $1,800, which is $21,600 a year. 3232 Figler Court's agent income sheet and its unit rent lines differ by $12,000 a year. 510 South 10th Street lists a single $4,200 rent that is the building total, not a per-unit figure.
  6. Thirty-two buildings predate 1965 and twenty have been listed more than 180 days. Two have been listed more than a year: 2761 Mountain Vista Street at 551 days and 300 North 14th Street at 447 days.

How this was measured

Everything here comes from the two MLS exports you sent, 110 Cross Property Agent Full detail sheets, parsed field by field.

  1. Geography. Each listing was placed by the township, range and section in its legal description, fit against known zip centroids, and measured from Sahara Avenue and Las Vegas Boulevard. Eight fell outside 15 miles and were removed: seven in Pahrump at 47 miles and one in Caliente at 108. That leaves the 102 buildings shown. Nothing in Mesquite or Boulder City appeared in the export.
  2. Income is collected rent. Where the unit-level rent lines and the agent-stated gross income disagreed, the lower figure was used. Six buildings were corrected further from language in their own remarks. Vacant units contribute zero. No pro forma or market-rent figure appears anywhere on this page.
  3. Expenses are built from parts, not from a ratio. Taxes from the listed assessment grossed up 8 percent, insurance by construction vintage, HOA from the posted dues, utilities based on which ones the listing says are separately metered, 9 percent vacancy and credit loss, 8 to 10 percent management, a per-unit capital reserve, and turnover cost. The result runs a 46 percent median operating expense ratio. Published cap rates were not used; one listing in the original 110 claimed a 92 percent NOI margin.
  4. Cap rate is in-place NOI divided by asking price. Nothing is adjusted for condition, and no growth is assumed.
  5. Break-even is arithmetic from stated loan terms. In-place NOI divided by 6.14 percent, which is annual debt service as a share of price at 25 percent down and 7.25 percent over 30 years. Change any of those inputs and the number changes. It describes the loan, not the building's worth.

The one structural observation the data supports: median in-place cap across these 102 buildings is 3.79 percent while debt at the terms above costs 6.14 percent of price per year. Two of the 96 buildings reporting rent produce enough in-place NOI to cover that at their asking price.

Sources: LVR MLS Cross Property Agent Full detail exports, 110 listings, parsed 2026-08-25.
