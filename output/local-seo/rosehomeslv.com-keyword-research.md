## How to use this
1. **Validate volumes** in a keyword tool; promote the highest-volume/lowest-difficulty terms to primary H1s.
2. **One primary per page** — the bold "Primary keyword" above. Longtail goes in H2s, body, and FAQs, not stuffed in the H1.
3. **Geo pages inherit the parent intent's structure** (see interlinking.md): hub → community versions, community version links back to its area page + parent hub.
4. **FAQs** double as on-page content and FAQPage schema fuel, and get cited by AI answer engines. Keep answers factual; mark anything you can't verify as unknown.
5. Feed community-specific longtail to `blog-writer` for the supporting blog cluster.
